Limburgfestival (Theater Festival)

Kulturfestival · Roermond, Regionaal · 11.–16. August 2026

Wann11.–16. August 2026
WoMultiple venues throughout Midden-Limburg including Roermond, Weert, Nederweert, and surrounding municipalities, Regionaal, 6040 Roermond
ArtKulturfestival

One of the largest open-air theater festivals in the Netherlands featuring 50 performances including acrobatics, cabaret, and theater. Theme: Tribute to Women. Performances at scenic locations including castles and farms.
