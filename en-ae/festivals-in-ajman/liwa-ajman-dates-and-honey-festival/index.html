Liwa Ajman Dates and Honey Festival

Funfair · Ajman, Al Jurf · 30 July – 3 August 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When30 July – 3 August 2027
WhereEmirates Hospitality Centre, Al Khail Street, Al Jurf, Ajman
TypeFunfair

Annual dates and honey festival in Ajman, organised by the emirate's tourism authority and held at the Emirates Hospitality Hall (also called Emirates Hospitality Centre). The programme includes competitions for dates, honey and fruit with prize money, workshops, heritage performances, educational sessions on palm cultivation and beekeeping, and daily auctions of dates and honey.
