Al Ain Flower Festival

Funfair · Al Ain, Al Sarooj · 30 January – 13 February 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When30 January – 13 February 2027
WhereAl Sarooj Park, Khalid Bin Sultan Street, Al Ain
TypeFunfair

Annual outdoor flower festival at Al Sarooj Park in Al Ain, organised by Al Ain City Municipality. The official listing for the 2026 edition describes more than ten million flowers from 23 species across about 50,000 square metres, with floral sculptures and themed displays.
