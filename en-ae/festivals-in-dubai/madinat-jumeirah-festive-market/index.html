Madinat Jumeirah Festive Market

Christmas market · Dubai, Umm Suqeim · 4–27 December 2026

When4–27 December 2026
WhereFort Island, Madinat Jumeirah, Umm Suqeim, Dubai
TypeChristmas market

Free-entry Christmas market on Fort Island at Madinat Jumeirah, with stalls, a large festive tree and seasonal activities. It is open from 3pm Monday to Thursday and from noon Friday to Sunday.
