Sikka Art and Design Festival

Culture festival · Dubai, Al Shindagha · 23 January – 1 February 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When23 January – 1 February 2027
WhereAl Shindagha Historic District, Dubai
TypeCulture festival

Art and design festival in Dubai's historic neighbourhoods of Al Shindagha and Al Fahidi, with public art placed across heritage houses, courtyards and alleyways. The programme includes workshops, panel discussions, live performances and food and beverage concepts. Recent editions have been held at Al Shindagha in late January and early February.
