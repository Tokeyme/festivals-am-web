Winter City

Christmas market · Dubai, Expo City · 11–31 December 2026

When11–31 December 2026
WhereAl Wasl Plaza, Expo City Dubai, Dubai
TypeChristmas market

Winter village on Al Wasl Plaza in Expo City Dubai. Its fifth edition, in 2026, has a Disney+ theme, 'Once upon a holiday'. It has themed zones inspired by Frozen, Toy Story and Home Alone, snowfall under the Al Wasl Dome, Santa meet-and-greets and a festive market. It opens daily in the evening and requires a paid ticket.
