Ras Al Khaimah Art Festival

Culture festival · Ras Al Khaimah, Al Jazeera Al Hamra · 22 January – 14 February 2027

When22 January – 14 February 2027
WhereAl Jazeera Al Hamra Heritage Village, Ras Al Khaimah
TypeCulture festival

A contemporary art festival staged in Al Jazeera Al Hamra Heritage Village, a former pearling village on the Ras Al Khaimah coast, running for several weeks. The 2027 edition has the theme 'Lost in Translation' and features 131 artists of 54 nationalities. The programme includes workshops and masterclasses, art, heritage and food tours and the Hidden Table dining experience.
