Sharjah Heritage Days

Historical festival · Sharjah, Heart of Sharjah · 4–15 February 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When4–15 February 2027
WhereHeritage Area, Heart of Sharjah, Sharjah
TypeHistorical festival

A heritage festival run by the Sharjah Institute for Heritage, with its main programme in the Heart of Sharjah. Families and children take part in cultural and heritage activities, and events are also held in other towns across the emirate. The 23rd edition in 2026 is reported to have drawn around 390,000 visitors.
