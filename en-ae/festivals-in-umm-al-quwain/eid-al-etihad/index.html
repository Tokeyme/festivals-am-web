Eid Al Etihad

Town festival · Umm Al Quwain, Al Rawdah · 29 November – 3 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When29 November – 3 December 2026
WhereAl Khor Waterfront, King Faisal Road, Al Rawdah, Umm Al Quwain
TypeTown festival

Public celebrations of the UAE National Day in Umm Al Quwain, organised by the emirate's Higher Committee for Celebrations. The 2025 programme at the Creek Waterfront included folklore performances, a photography exhibition and a productive families market, with several government bodies taking part. The 2024 programme at Al Khor Waterfront ran for five days.
