Bregenzer Festspiele (Seebühne) – La traviata

Music festival · Bregenz, Zentrum · 22 July – 23 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When22 July – 23 August 2026
WhereSeebühne Bregenz, Festspielhaus, Platz der Wiener Symphoniker 1, Zentrum, 6900 Bregenz
TypeMusic festival

80th Bregenzer Festspiele with Verdi's La traviata in world premiere on the lake stage (July 22 to Aug 23), Leoš Janáček's The Excursions of Mr. Brouček (world premiere July 23), orchestra concerts, chamber plays, and singalong at the lake for the 80th anniversary (Aug 1).
