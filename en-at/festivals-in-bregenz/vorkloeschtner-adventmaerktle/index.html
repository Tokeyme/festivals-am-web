Vorklöschtner Adventmärktle

Market · Bregenz, Vorklösterle · 21–22 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When21–22 November 2026
WhereKirche Mariahilf, Clemens-Holzmeister-Gasse 2, Bregenz-Vorklösterle, 6900 Bregenz
TypeMarket

25th advent market around the Mariahilf Church with approximately 60 lovingly decorated vendor stalls by hobby artists (wood decorations, pottery, Christmas ornaments, textiles), culinary offerings (roasted chestnuts, sausages, mulled wine, coffee).
