Weihnachtsmarkt Bregenzer Oberstadt (Martinsplatz)

Christmas market · Bregenz, Oberstadt · 28 November – 20 December 2026

When28 November – 20 December 2026
WhereMartinsplatz, Bregenzer Oberstadt (2.–4. Adventwochenende), 6900 Bregenz
TypeChristmas market

Atmospheric handicraft market in the historic old town featuring glassblowers, gingerbread bakers, woodcarvers, and wax casters presented on Sat+Sun from the 2nd to 4th advent weekends from 2:00 PM.
