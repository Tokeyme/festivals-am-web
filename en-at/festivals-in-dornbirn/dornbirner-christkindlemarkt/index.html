Dornbirner Christkindlemarkt

Christmas market · Dornbirn · 20 November – 23 December 2026

When20 November – 23 December 2026
WhereMarktplatz, 6850 Dornbirn
TypeChristmas market

Weihnachtsmarkt (20.11. 17:00-21:00; from 21.11. daily 10:00-21:00) with a Christmas tree, Advent decorations, arts and crafts, a children's paradise (125 m²), an ice rink with ice stock lanes and a supporting programme.
