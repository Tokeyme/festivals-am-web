Martini Kirtag

Funfair · Eisenstadt · 11 November 2026

When11 November 2026
WhereHauptplatz und Dom Eisenstadt, 7000 Eisenstadt
TypeFunfair

A traditional St. Martin's festival on the provincial holiday, with a wine encounter, a Kirtag market in the pedestrian zone, farm produce and an evening lantern procession with a St. Martin's play for children.
