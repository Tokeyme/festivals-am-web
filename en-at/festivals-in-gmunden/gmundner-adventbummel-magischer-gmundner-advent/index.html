Gmundner Adventbummel – Magischer Gmundner Advent

Christmas market · Gmunden, Altstadt · 28 November – 21 December 2026

When28 November – 21 December 2026
WhereAltstadt Gmunden (Marktplatz, Traungasse und umliegende Gassen), Altstadt, 4810 Gmunden
TypeChristmas market

Christmas market over four weekends (Fri 17:00–20:00, Sat 10:00–20:00, Sun 11:00–19:00) with handcrafted products, punch and culinary treats. Highlights: a trail of lights on the Grünberg reached by cable car, carriage rides, alpaca walks and a children's baking room.
