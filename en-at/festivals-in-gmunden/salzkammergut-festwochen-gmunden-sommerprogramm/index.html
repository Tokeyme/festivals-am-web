Salzkammergut Festwochen Gmunden – Sommerprogramm

Culture festival · Gmunden, Gmunden · 2 July – 31 August 2026

When2 July – 31 August 2026
WhereDiverse Orte (Stadttheater, Toscanapark, Galerie 422 u.a.), Gmunden, 4810 Gmunden
TypeCulture festival

An international summer culture festival under the motto 'Kommen härtere Tage?', with literature, music, theatre and visual art based on works by Ingeborg Bachmann and Thomas Bernhard.
