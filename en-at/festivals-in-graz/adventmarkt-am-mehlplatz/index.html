Adventmarkt am Mehlplatz

Christmas market · Graz, Innere Stadt · 20 November – 24 December 2026

When20 November – 24 December 2026
WhereMehlplatz, Innere Stadt, 8010 Graz
TypeChristmas market

At Graz's Mehlplatz, you can browse a special selection of arts and crafts while stars and snowflakes dance across the ground. The small market lies right in the heart of Graz's old town.
