Ball der Technik

Event · Graz, Innere Stadt · 29 January 2027

When29 January 2027
WhereGrazer Congress, Albrechtgasse 1, Innere Stadt, 8010 Graz
TypeEvent

The Ball der Technik is a social highlight for engineering, the technical sciences and research in Styria, and is hosted by the Technische Universität Graz and FH Joanneum. It ranks among the most tradition-rich and elegant balls in Graz and looks back on a history spanning more than 160 years. The venue is the Grazer Congress.
