Christkindlmarkt am Südtiroler Platz

Christmas market · Graz, Lend · 20 November – 23 December 2026

When20 November – 23 December 2026
WhereSüdtiroler Platz, Lend, 8020 Graz
TypeChristmas market

The Christkindlmarkt am Südtiroler Platz is one of the city's newer Christmas markets and lies right next to the Kunsthaus Graz on the right bank of the Mur. The square is located at the western end of the Hauptbrücke in the district of Lend.
