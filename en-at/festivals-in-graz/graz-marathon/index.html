Graz Marathon

Sports event · Graz, Innere Stadt · 9–11 October 2026

When9–11 October 2026
WhereStart/Ziel Oper Graz, Opernring, Innere Stadt, 8010 Graz
TypeSports event

The Graz Marathon is the city's largest running event, with its start and finish in front of the Oper Graz. The route runs through the city centre, across the Hauptplatz and the Herrengasse, and along the Mur. In addition to the marathon over 42.195 kilometres, the events on offer include a half marathon and a family run, among others.
