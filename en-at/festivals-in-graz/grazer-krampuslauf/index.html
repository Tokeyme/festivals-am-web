Grazer Krampuslauf

Event · Graz, Innere Stadt · 8 November 2026

When8 November 2026
WhereFreiheitsplatz, Innere Stadt, 8010 Graz
TypeEvent

At the Grazer Krampuslauf, numerous Krampus groups parade through the city centre, creating a striking display of tradition with thundering footsteps, blazing flames and eerie masks. The run takes place at Freiheitsplatz and is accompanied by a music and street-food programme as well as a children's programme.
