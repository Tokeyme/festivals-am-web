Grazer Opernredoute

Event · Graz, Innere Stadt · 30 January 2027

When30 January 2027
WhereOper Graz, Kaiser-Josef-Platz 10, Innere Stadt, 8010 Graz
TypeEvent

The Opernredoute is regarded as the social and cultural highlight of the Styrian Ballsaison and traditionally takes place at the Oper Graz. The opening in the grand ballroom combines music, song and dance and is accompanied by the Grazer Philharmoniker. The ball ranks among the most important ball events in Austria.
