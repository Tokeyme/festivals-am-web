Grieskindlmarkt am Nikolaiplatz

Christmas market · Graz, Gries · 20 November – 20 December 2026

When20 November – 20 December 2026
WhereNikolaiplatz, Gries, 8020 Graz
TypeChristmas market

The Grieskindlmarkt on Nikolaiplatz in Graz's Gries district is known as a sustainable Christmas market and showcases the diversity of Styria's design scene. On offer are handmade products spanning fashion, home accessories, natural cosmetics, jewellery and accessories. Since 2019 the market has been an official part of the Grazer Advent.
