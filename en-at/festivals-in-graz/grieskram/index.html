Grieskram

Neighbourhood festival · Graz, Gries · 26 September 2026

When26 September 2026
WhereBezirk Gries, Gries, 8020 Graz
TypeNeighbourhood festival

The Grieskram is a neighbourhood festival open to everyone in the Graz district of Gries. Local residents, clubs and institutions put together a colourful programme centred on art, culture and cuisine. The festival's motto is 'A district better than its reputation'.
