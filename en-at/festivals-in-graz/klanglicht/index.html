Klanglicht

Culture festival · Graz, Eggenberg · 23 October – 7 November 2026

When23 October – 7 November 2026
WhereSchloss Eggenberg, Eggenberger Allee 90, Eggenberg, 8020 Graz
TypeCulture festival

Klanglicht is the art festival of Bühnen Graz and is regarded as Austria's largest festival of light and sound. Public spaces are brought to life with light and sound installations. The 2026 edition is hosted at Schloss Eggenberg, where the installations are shown in the palace gardens and inside the palace.
