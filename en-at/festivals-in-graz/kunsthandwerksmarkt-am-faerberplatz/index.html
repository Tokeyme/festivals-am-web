Kunsthandwerksmarkt am Färberplatz

Christmas market · Graz, Innere Stadt · 20 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November – 23 December 2026
WhereFärberplatz, Innere Stadt, 8010 Graz
TypeChristmas market

The Kunsthandwerksmarkt am Färberplatz offers arts and crafts from around 50 local and international artisans and designers. The market is located in Graz's old town, not far from the Hauptplatz.
