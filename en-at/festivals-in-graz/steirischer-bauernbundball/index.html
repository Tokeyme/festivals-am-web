Steirischer Bauernbundball

Event · Graz, Jakomini · 5 February 2027

When5 February 2027
WhereStadthalle Graz, Messeplatz 1, Jakomini, 8010 Graz
TypeEvent

The Steirischer Bauernbundball is one of the largest balls in Styria and in Europe and is held at the Grazer Stadthalle. The ball is dedicated to traditional Styrian culture, with music, dance and regional cuisine.
