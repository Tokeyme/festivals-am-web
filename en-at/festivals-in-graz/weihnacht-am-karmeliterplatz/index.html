Weihnacht am Karmeliterplatz

Christmas market · Graz, Innere Stadt · 20 November – 23 December 2026

When20 November – 23 December 2026
WhereKarmeliterplatz, Innere Stadt, 8010 Graz
TypeChristmas market

At the Karmeliterplatz, with a view of the Uhrturm and a glass of punch or mulled wine in hand, visitors are drawn into the pre-Christmas spirit. The offering includes high-quality gourmet products, arts and crafts and Christmas items. Light projections and colourful façade projections create a festive atmosphere.
