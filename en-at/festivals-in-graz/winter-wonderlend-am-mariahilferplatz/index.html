Winter Wonderlend am Mariahilferplatz

Christmas market · Graz, Lend · 20 November – 23 December 2026

When20 November – 23 December 2026
WhereMariahilferplatz, Lend, 8020 Graz
TypeChristmas market

At the heart of the Christmas market on Mariahilferplatz stands a large, illuminated Christbaum, around which stalls offering drinks and handicrafts are arranged. The programme includes daily music, live performances on Saturdays, children's activities and a visit from the Krampus.
