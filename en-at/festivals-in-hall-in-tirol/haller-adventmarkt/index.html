Haller Adventmarkt

Christmas market · Hall in Tirol, Altstadt · 20 November – 24 December 2026

When20 November – 24 December 2026
WhereOberer Stadtplatz, Unterer Stadtplatz (Altstadt), Altstadt, 6060 Hall in Tirol
TypeChristmas market

One of the most beautiful Weihnachtsmärkte in Tyrol, with an Advent calendar on medieval house façades. Live music daily from 18:00, an ice rink, Nikolaus on 6.12., pony rides, a petting zoo and a fairy-tale stage for children.
