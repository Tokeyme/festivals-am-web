Haller Töpfermarkt

Market · Hall in Tirol, Altstadt · 21–23 August 2026

When21–23 August 2026
WhereBachlechnerstraße, Altstadtpark, Marktanger, Altstadt, 6060 Hall in Tirol
TypeMarket

Tyrol's largest Töpfermarkt with around 35 master potters from Austria and neighbouring countries. Handmade ceramics and arts and crafts over three days. Held regularly since 1997.
