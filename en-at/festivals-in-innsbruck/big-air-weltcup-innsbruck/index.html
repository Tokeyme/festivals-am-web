Big Air Weltcup Innsbruck

Sports event · Innsbruck, Wilten · 26–28 November 2026

When26–28 November 2026
WhereBergiselstadion, Wilten, 6020 Innsbruck
TypeSports event

For the first time since 2017, a major freestyle event returns to Innsbruck: the World Cup opener in freeski and snowboard Big Air comes to the Tyrolean capital. The world's best freeskiers and snowboarders perform spectacular jumps and tricks on the Big Air ramp. The Bergiselstadion is planned as the venue.
