BMW IBSF Weltcup Bob & Skeleton

Sports event · Innsbruck, Igls · 4–6 December 2026

When4–6 December 2026
WhereOlympia Eiskanal, Heiligwasserwiese 1, Igls, Igls, 6080 Innsbruck
TypeSports event

The Weltcup in bobsleigh and skeleton comes to the Olympia Eiskanal in Innsbruck-Igls. The world's best bobsleigh and skeleton athletes take to the start on the rebuilt 1964 Olympic track. The programme includes races in the monobob, two-man bob and four-man bob, as well as skeleton.
