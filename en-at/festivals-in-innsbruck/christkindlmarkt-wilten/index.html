Christkindlmarkt Wilten

Christmas market · Innsbruck, Wilten · 20 November – 23 December 2026

When20 November – 23 December 2026
WhereWiltener Platzl, Wilten, 6020 Innsbruck
TypeChristmas market

The small Christkindlmarkt at the Wiltener Platzl is considered particularly romantic and distinctive. Its roughly ten stalls offer handcrafted jewellery, one-off artistic pieces and regional and international specialities. The market lies in the Wilten district, a little away from the bustle of the city centre.
