FIL Rennrodel-Weltmeisterschaften

Sports event · Innsbruck, Igls · 1–7 February 2027

When1–7 February 2027
WhereOlympia Eiskanal, Heiligwasserwiese 1, Igls, 6080 Innsbruck
TypeSports event

Innsbruck-Igls is the venue for the luge World Championships. In a world first, the title contests in artificial-track luge and alpine luge are held in parallel. The racing takes place on the newly renovated 1964 Olympic track, which is hosting a luge World Championship for the sixth time.
