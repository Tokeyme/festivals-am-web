Innsbruck Nature Film Festival

Culture festival · Innsbruck, Mariahilf · 7–11 October 2026

When7–11 October 2026
WhereMetropol Kino, Innstraße 5, Mariahilf, 6020 Innsbruck
TypeCulture festival

The Innsbruck Nature Film Festival is a renowned nature and environmental film festival, taking place for the 25th time in 2026. Around 60 curated films on nature, the environment, climate and sustainability are shown. Alongside this there are film talks, lectures and a supporting programme around Innsbruck.
