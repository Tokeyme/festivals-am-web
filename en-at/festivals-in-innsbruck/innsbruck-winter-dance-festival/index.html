Innsbruck Winter Dance Festival

Culture festival · Innsbruck, Innenstadt · 29 January – 28 February 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When29 January – 28 February 2027
WhereCongress Innsbruck, Rennweg 3, Innenstadt, 6020 Innsbruck
TypeCulture festival

At the Innsbruck Winter Dance Festival, the provincial capital becomes the alpine capital of dance. The programme includes dance theatre at the Congress Innsbruck as well as choreographies and live music at various locations around the city. The festival celebrates the art of dance in all its many facets.
