Neujahrskonzert Tiroler Symphonieorchester Innsbruck

Event · Innsbruck, Innenstadt · 1 January 2027

When1 January 2027
WhereCongress Innsbruck, Rennweg 3, Saal Tirol, Innenstadt, 6020 Innsbruck
TypeEvent

The Tiroler Symphonieorchester Innsbruck invites audiences to a festive New Year's concert in the Saal Tirol at the Congress Innsbruck. The programme features melodies from the Viennese Classical period, Romanticism and operetta. The concert sets the musical tone for the new year.
