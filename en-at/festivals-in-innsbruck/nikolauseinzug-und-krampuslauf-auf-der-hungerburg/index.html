Nikolauseinzug und Krampuslauf auf der Hungerburg

Event · Innsbruck, Hungerburg · 6 December 2026

WhereHungerburg, Hermann-Buhl-Platz, Hungerburg, 6020 Innsbruck
TypeEvent

On the Hungerburg, the Nikolaus rides up on the funicular and hands out little treat bags to the children. This is followed by a Krampuslauf. The event is part of the Weihnachtsmarkt on the Hungerburg.
