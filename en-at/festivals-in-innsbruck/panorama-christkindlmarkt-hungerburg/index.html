Panorama Christkindlmarkt Hungerburg

Christmas market · Innsbruck, Hungerburg · 20 November 2026 – 6 January 2027

When20 November 2026 – 6 January 2027
WhereHermann-Buhl-Platz, Hungerburg, 6020 Innsbruck
TypeChristmas market

Innsbruck's highest-lying Christkindlmarkt sits on the Hungerburg and can be reached from the city centre in just a few minutes on the Hungerburgbahn. On Hermann-Buhl-Platz, visitors can look forward to artisan products, Christmas gifts and regional delicacies. From the market there is a panoramic view over Innsbruck and the surrounding mountains.
