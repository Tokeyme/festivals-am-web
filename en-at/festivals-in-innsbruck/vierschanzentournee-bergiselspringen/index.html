Vierschanzentournee Bergiselspringen

Sports event · Innsbruck, Wilten · 3 January 2027

When3 January 2027
WhereBergiselstadion, Bergiselweg 3, Wilten, 6020 Innsbruck
TypeSports event

The Bergiselspringen is the Austrian leg of the Vierschanzentournee and one of the highlights of the ski jumping World Cup. On the Bergiselschanze (HS128), the world's best ski jumpers compete as part of the tradition-rich tournament. Tens of thousands of spectators follow the competition in the Bergiselstadion above the city.
