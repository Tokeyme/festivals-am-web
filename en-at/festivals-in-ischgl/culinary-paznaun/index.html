Culinary Paznaun

Culture festival · Ischgl, Paznaun region · 3 July – 31 October 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When3 July – 31 October 2026
WhereMultiple alpine huts in Paznaun valley (Friedrichshafener Hütte, Heilbronner Hütte, Larein Alpe, others), Paznaun region, 6561 Ischgl
TypeCulture festival

A summer gourmet series in which renowned top chefs take over mountain huts to create exclusive regional dishes. The gourmet market opens on July 3, 2026, with hut festivals on opening night and on selected Sundays.
