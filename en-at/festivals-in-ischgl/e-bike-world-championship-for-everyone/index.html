E-Bike World Championship for Everyone

Sports event · Ischgl, Ischgl/Galtür · 3–5 September 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When3–5 September 2026
WhereIschgl and Galtür, Ischgl/Galtür, 6561 Ischgl
TypeSports event

E-bike championship with four routes (30-50 km, 600-1500 m elevation) for professionals and hobby riders. Routes named 'Everyone', 'Jedermann', 'Jedermann Advanced' and 'Elite', commercial e-bikes with pedal assistance up to 25 km/h permitted.
