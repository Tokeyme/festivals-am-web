Kitzbüheler Advent / Christkindlmarkt

Christmas market · Kitzbühel, Kitzbühel · 25 November – 24 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When25 November – 24 December 2026
WhereInnenstadt / Stadtpark Kitzbühel, Kitzbühel, 6370 Kitzbühel
TypeChristmas market

Christmas market with roasted chestnut and mulled wine aromas, regional delicacies, and crafts. Atmospheric wooden stalls, light installations, and music from choirs and groups. For children: star painting, carousel, Christ Child post office.
