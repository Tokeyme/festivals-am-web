St. Johanner Knödelfest

Funfair · Kitzbühel, St. Johann in Tirol · 19 September 2026

When19 September 2026
WhereSpeckbacherstraße, Hauptplatz St. Johann in Tirol, St. Johann in Tirol, 6380 Kitzbühel
TypeFunfair

44th Knödel Festival with the world's longest knödel table (595 m). 26,000 knödels with 27 recipes from 20 restaurants. Festival procession at 11:00 AM, live music until 8:00 PM. Tickets 12 EUR (10.50 EUR group).
