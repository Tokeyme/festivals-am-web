Advent am Stadtpfarrturm

Event · Klagenfurt, Innere Stadt · 28 November – 26 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28 November – 26 December 2026
WhereStadtpfarrturm St. Egid, Pfarrplatz, Innere Stadt, 9020 Klagenfurt
TypeEvent

"Advent am Stadtpfarrturm" turns the Stadtpfarrturm (parish church tower) of St. Egid church into a festive spectacle through the light installation "Leuchtende Klangwelten" (glowing worlds of sound). During Advent the illuminated show is presented several times every evening, and admission is free. The tower in the heart of the old town thereby becomes an eye-catcher of the Klagenfurt Advent season.
