Alpen-Adria Genussmeile

Market · Klagenfurt, Innere Stadt · 17–19 September 2026

When17–19 September 2026
WhereNeuer Platz, Alter Platz und Ossiacher Hof, Innere Stadt, 9020 Klagenfurt
TypeMarket

At the Alpen-Adria Genussmeile (culinary mile), around 50 hand-picked producers from Carinthia, Styria, Slovenia, Friuli, the Veneto and Istria present their specialities in the historic town centre. Between Neuer Platz, Alter Platz and Ossiacher Hof, olive oils, cheese, truffles, wild herbs, wines, meat, sweets and coffee are offered for tasting and buying. Admission is free, and the Genussmeile marks the close of the Tage der Alpen-Adria Küche (Days of Alpine-Adriatic Cuisine).
