Brauchtumsmesse

Market · Klagenfurt · 13–15 November 2026

When13–15 November 2026
WhereKlagenfurter Messe, Messeplatz 1, 9020 Klagenfurt
TypeMarket

The Brauchtumsmesse (folk-customs fair) at the Klagenfurt exhibition grounds puts Carinthian folk culture and tradition at its centre. The programme features Tracht (traditional-costume) fashion shows as well as performances by choirs, folk-music groups, hunting-horn players and brass bands, alongside regional crafts, hunting and cuisine from the 'Genussland Kärnten' (Carinthia culinary region). Visitors can also explore a lovingly designed traditions village and a nativity-scene exhibition.
