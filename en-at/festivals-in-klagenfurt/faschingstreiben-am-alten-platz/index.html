Faschingstreiben am Alten Platz

Carnival · Klagenfurt, Innere Stadt · 9 February 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When9 February 2027
WhereAlter Platz, Innere Stadt, 9020 Klagenfurt
TypeCarnival

On Faschingsdienstag (Shrove Tuesday), the innkeepers of the Alter Platz turn Klagenfurt's city centre into a Fasching (carnival) meeting point. In the afternoon there is a family programme with children's entertainment and a bouncy castle, while in the evening live music and DJs provide the atmosphere; guests in costume are expressly welcome. The Stadtrichter zu Clagenfurth run a charity bar in aid of a good cause.
