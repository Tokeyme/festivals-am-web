Stiller Advent im und um den Dom

Event · Klagenfurt, Innere Stadt · 28 November – 24 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28 November – 24 December 2026
WhereDom zu Klagenfurt und Landhaushof, Innere Stadt, 9020 Klagenfurt
TypeEvent

At Stiller Advent (Quiet Advent) in and around the cathedral, well-known Carinthian choirs sing reflective Advent and Christmas songs. The settings are Klagenfurt Cathedral and the arcades of the Landhaushof, one of the oldest buildings in the city. The free programme is rounded off by Advent melodies played by Weisenbläser (traditional folk-melody brass players).
