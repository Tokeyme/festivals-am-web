Viktringer Adventmarkt

Christmas market · Klagenfurt, Viktring · 4–5 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When4–5 December 2026
WhereHexnStubn, Keutschacher Straße 72, Viktring, 9073 Klagenfurt
TypeChristmas market

Around the HexnStubn on Keutschacher Straße, the associations Kulturleben Viktring and Initiatives Viktring host an Adventmarkt (Christmas market) in the Viktring district. The stalls offer handmade regional products, gift ideas and a varied range of food and drink. The programme includes the primary school's children's choir, a fire show and a magician, as well as a visit from the Krampus group 'Glanfurter Moarbartl' with Nikolaus (Saint Nicholas).
