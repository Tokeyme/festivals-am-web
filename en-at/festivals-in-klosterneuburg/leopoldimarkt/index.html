Leopoldimarkt

Funfair · Klosterneuburg, Altstadt · 12–15 November 2026

When12–15 November 2026
WhereRathausplatz, Altstadt, 3400 Klosterneuburg
TypeFunfair

Traditional Leopoldifest held for over 500 years, with around 30 fairground rides and 70 market stalls on the Rathausplatz. On offer are textiles, jewellery, toys and sweets as well as mulled wine, punch and culinary specialities.
