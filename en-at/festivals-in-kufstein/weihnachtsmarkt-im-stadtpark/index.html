Weihnachtsmarkt im Stadtpark

Christmas market · Kufstein, Stadtpark · 20 November – 20 December 2026

When20 November – 20 December 2026
WhereStadtpark Kufstein, Stadtpark, 6330 Kufstein
TypeChristmas market

An Advent market with around 30 stalls in the Stadtpark. A carousel, a nostalgic railway and a fairy-tale trail. Regional specialities, punch and mulled wine.
