Lienzer Adventmarkt (Weihnachtsmarkt)

Christmas market · Lienz, Lienz · 21 November – 24 December 2026

When21 November – 24 December 2026
WhereHauptplatz, Lienz, Lienz, 9900 Lienz
TypeChristmas market

One of the most beautiful Christmas markets in Austria. The Liebburg (town hall) becomes a large Advent calendar, with over 30 stalls offering crafts, mulled wine and traditional specialities.
