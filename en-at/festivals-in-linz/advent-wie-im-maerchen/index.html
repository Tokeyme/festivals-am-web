Advent wie im Märchen

Event · Linz, Pöstlingberg · 29 November – 24 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When29 November – 24 December 2026
WhereAm Pöstlingberg 16 (Grottenbahn), Pöstlingberg, 4040 Linz
TypeEvent

On the Sundays of Advent, the Grottenbahn (grotto railway) on the Pöstlingberg opens its festively decorated fairy-tale world. At the Märchenhauptplatz (fairy-tale main square), a nostalgic Standlmarkt (stall market) offers sweets and small gifts, alongside fairy-tale storytelling and interactive theatre. The dragon train Lenzibald and the dwarves create a pre-Christmas atmosphere.
