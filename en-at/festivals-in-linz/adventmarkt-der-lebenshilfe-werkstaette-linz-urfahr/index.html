Adventmarkt der Lebenshilfe-Werkstätte Linz-Urfahr

Christmas market · Linz, Urfahr · 27 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 November 2026
WhereKlausenbachstraße 46, Urfahr, 4040 Linz
TypeChristmas market

The workers of the Lebenshilfe-Werkstätte Linz-Urfahr, a workshop for people with disabilities, host a self-organised Advent market (Christmas market) with handmade products such as nativity scenes, candles, Advent wreaths, leather goods and Christmas decorations. Refreshments include punch, fried potatoes and goulash soup, accompanied by live music.
