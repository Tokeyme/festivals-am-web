Eisrausch

Event · Linz, Pöstlingberg · 27 November 2026 – 6 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 November 2026 – 6 January 2027
WhereOK Kunstwald, Am Pöstlingberg, Pöstlingberg, 4040 Linz
TypeEvent

At the OK Kunstwald on the Pöstlingberg, an ice rink is created among art installations during Advent, supplemented by two Stockschießen (ice stock sport) lanes. Ice skating is possible with free admission. The facility ranks among the most unusual Advent offerings in the city.
