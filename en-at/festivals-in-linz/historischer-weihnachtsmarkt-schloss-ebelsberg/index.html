Historischer Weihnachtsmarkt Schloss Ebelsberg

Christmas market · Linz, Ebelsberg · 4–6 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When4–6 December 2026
WhereEbelsberger Schloßweg 7, Ebelsberg, 4030 Linz
TypeChristmas market

In the historic setting of Ebelsberg Castle, around 70 exhibitors offer traditional handicrafts and delicacies in the castle courtyard, the garden and the historic rooms. The accompanying programme includes Nachtwächter (night watchman) guided tours, a fire show, performances by children's and music groups, and a visit from Nikolaus (Saint Nicholas) with his Krampusse (Krampus companions).
