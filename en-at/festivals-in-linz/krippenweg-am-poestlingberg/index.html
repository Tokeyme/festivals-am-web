Krippenweg am Pöstlingberg

Event · Linz, Pöstlingberg · 11 December 2026 – 10 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When11 December 2026 – 10 January 2027
WherePöstlingbergkirche, Am Pöstlingberg, 4040 Linz
TypeEvent

A circular walk leads from the church nativity scene past several stations with Christmas nativity scenes at clubs, public buildings and private houses on the Pöstlingberg. Along the route, children can discover the Christmas story presented in several parts. The trail is wheelchair accessible.
