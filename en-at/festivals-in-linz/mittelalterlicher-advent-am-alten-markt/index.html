Mittelalterlicher Advent am Alten Markt

Christmas market · Linz, Innere Stadt · 11–13 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When11–13 December 2026
WhereAlter Markt, Innere Stadt, 4020 Linz
TypeChristmas market

At the Alter Markt in Linz's old town, Advent turns into a journey back in time to the Middle Ages. Craftsmen such as blacksmiths, bowyers and potters demonstrate old techniques, and there is food from the wood-fired oven, grilled dishes and sweets as well as live music. The market takes place on one weekend in the run-up to Christmas.
