Weihnacht im Botanischen Garten

Event · Linz, Froschberg · 21 November 2026 – 2 February 2027

When21 November 2026 – 2 February 2027
WhereRoseggerstraße 20, Froschberg, 4020 Linz
TypeEvent

In the show houses and outdoor grounds of the Botanical Garden, the Linzer Krippenfreunde (a Linz nativity scene society) present a traditional Christmas nativity scene exhibition. Various nativity scenes can be seen along a circular walk. Only the regular garden admission is required to visit.
