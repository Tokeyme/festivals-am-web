Weinlesefest Mörbisch

Wine festival · Neusiedl am See · 25–27 September 2026

When25–27 September 2026
WhereMörbisch am See (Region), 7072 Neusiedl am See
TypeWine festival

Wine harvest festival with freshly fermented wines, the crowning of a wine harvest queen and a procession. The Hauerstraße offers brass band music, wine tastings and regional specialities.
