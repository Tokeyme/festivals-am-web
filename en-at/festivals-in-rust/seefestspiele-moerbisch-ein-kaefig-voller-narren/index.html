Seefestspiele Mörbisch - Ein Käfig voller Narren

Culture festival · Rust, Mörbisch am See · 16 July – 22 August 2026

When16 July – 22 August 2026
WhereSeebühne Mörbisch, 7072 Mörbisch am See, Mörbisch am See, 7072 Rust
TypeCulture festival

Musical comedy on Europe's largest lake stage, featuring Alfons Haider and Drew Sarich in alternating roles. Performances on various dates from July to August, with the wildlife scenery of the Neusiedlersee as a backdrop.
