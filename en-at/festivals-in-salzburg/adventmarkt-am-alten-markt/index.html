Adventmarkt am Alten Markt

Christmas market · Salzburg, Altstadt · 20 November – 22 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November – 22 December 2026
WhereAlter Markt, Altstadt, 5020 Salzburg
TypeChristmas market

The charitable Adventmarkt am Alten Markt in Salzburg's old town has been run on a voluntary basis by numerous social organisations and aid groups for more than 25 years. The stalls offer homemade goods, crafts and food in aid of charitable causes. The market is regarded as a particularly atmospheric meeting place in the heart of the old town.
