Adventmarkt am Salzachsee

Christmas market · Salzburg, Liefering · 20 November – 20 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November – 20 December 2026
WhereSalzachsee, Saalachspitz, Liefering, 5020 Salzburg
TypeChristmas market

The Adventmarkt am Salzachsee invites visitors to stroll in the run-up to Christmas along the shore of the bathing lake, in the Salzachsee-Saalachspitz protected landscape area in the Liefering district. At around 15 market stalls, handmade regional products are on offer, such as goods made of wool, wood, marble or ceramics, along with jewellery and natural cosmetics. Admission is free; the programme is accompanied by Perchten runs and brass music.
