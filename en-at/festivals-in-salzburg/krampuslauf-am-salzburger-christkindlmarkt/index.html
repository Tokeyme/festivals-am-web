Krampuslauf am Salzburger Christkindlmarkt

Event · Salzburg, Altstadt · 5 December 2026

When5 December 2026
WhereDom- und Residenzplatz, Altstadt, 5020 Salzburg
TypeEvent

At the traditional Krampuslauf at the Salzburger Christkindlmarkt, several Krampus troupes parade across Domplatz and Residenzplatz. With wooden masks, fur costumes and bells, the groups keep an old Salzburg tradition alive. The run takes place on 5 December 2026.
