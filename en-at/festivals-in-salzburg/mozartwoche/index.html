Mozartwoche

Music festival · Salzburg · 21–31 January 2027

When21–31 January 2027
WhereStiftung Mozarteum und weitere Spielstätten, 5020 Salzburg
TypeMusic festival

The Mozartwoche is an international classical-music festival held each year by the Stiftung Mozarteum around Mozart's birthday on 27 January. From 21 to 31 January 2027, under the motto 'Mozart und Mozarts', the programme features more than 50 events, including concerts, opera, chamber music, talks and a family programme. One highlight is the new production of Mozart's 'Così fan tutte' under Rolando Villazón.
