ORF Lange Nacht der Museen

Culture festival · Salzburg, Altstadt · 3 October 2026

When3 October 2026
WhereMax-Reinhardt-Platz (Treffpunkt) und Museen stadtweit, Altstadt, 5020 Salzburg
TypeCulture festival

At the ORF Lange Nacht der Museen, numerous museums and galleries in the city of Salzburg open their doors until midnight with a single shared ticket. The Austria-wide event takes place on 3 October 2026; the ticket also serves as a travel pass for the shuttle buses. The central meeting point and ticket collection is the Max-Reinhardt-Platz.
