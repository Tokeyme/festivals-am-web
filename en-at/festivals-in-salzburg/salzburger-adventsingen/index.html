Salzburger Adventsingen

Culture festival · Salzburg, Altstadt · 27 November – 13 December 2026

When27 November – 13 December 2026
WhereGroßes Festspielhaus, Hofstallgasse 1, Altstadt, 5020 Salzburg
TypeCulture festival

The Salzburger Adventsingen at the Großes Festspielhaus combines music, song and staged performance into an evening of pre-Christmas tradition. In 2026, the production 'Das große Geheimnis' is presented, placing the miracle of the birth at its centre and addressing the diversity of religions. Founded in 1946, the event celebrates its 80th anniversary.
