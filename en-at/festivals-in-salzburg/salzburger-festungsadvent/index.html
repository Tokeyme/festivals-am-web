Salzburger Festungsadvent

Christmas market · Salzburg, Altstadt · 20 November – 20 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November – 20 December 2026
WhereFestung Hohensalzburg, Mönchsberg 34, Altstadt, 5020 Salzburg
TypeChristmas market

The Salzburger Festungsadvent takes place in the courtyard of the Festung Hohensalzburg, high above the city. On the weekends of Advent, the market offers arts and crafts and culinary delicacies as well as a supervised children's programme with crafts and biscuit baking. The fortress is easily reached by the Festungsbahn.
