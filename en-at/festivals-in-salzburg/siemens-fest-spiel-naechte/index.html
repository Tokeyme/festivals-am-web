Siemens Fest>Spiel>Nächte

Culture festival · Salzburg, Altstadt · 25 July – 30 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When25 July – 30 August 2026
WhereKapitelplatz, Altstadt, 5020 Salzburg
TypeCulture festival

At the Siemens Fest>Spiel>Nächte, opera, concert and theatre productions from the Salzburger Festspiele are shown free of charge on an LED wall at Kapitelplatz. In 2026 the open-air broadcasts run from 25 July to 30 August. They are staged by the Salzburger Festspiele together with ORF Salzburg, Siemens and Unitel.
