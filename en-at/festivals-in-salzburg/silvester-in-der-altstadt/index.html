Silvester in der Altstadt

Event · Salzburg, Altstadt · 31 December 2026 – 1 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When31 December 2026 – 1 January 2027
WhereKapitelplatz, Altstadt, 5020 Salzburg
TypeEvent

For the turn of the year, Salzburg's old town around the Kapitelplatz offers a free programme featuring regional artists and cultural practitioners, live music and firework broadcasts on an LED screen. At midnight, the bells of the Salzburger Dom ring in the new year, accompanied by a music-synchronised firework display over the Festung Hohensalzburg.
