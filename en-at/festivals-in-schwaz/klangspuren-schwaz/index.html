Klangspuren Schwaz

Culture festival · Schwaz, Altstadt · 10–26 September 2026

When10–26 September 2026
WhereVerschiedene Venues (SZentrum, Gleis 4, Museum der Völker, Wohnzimmer), Altstadt, 6130 Schwaz
TypeCulture festival

33rd edition of the festival for new music with the motto 'Love is Louder'. 15 world premieres, over 20 Austrian premieres, musical theatre, Club Concerts, living-room concerts and Sound Walks. Artistic director: Marco Blaauw.
