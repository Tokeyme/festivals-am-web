Schwazer Adventmarkt

Christmas market · Schwaz, Altstadt · 20 November – 23 December 2026

When20 November – 23 December 2026
WhereMaximilianplatz, Altstadt, 6130 Schwaz
TypeChristmas market

A Weihnachtsmarkt with Tyrolean specialities (Kiachl, Zillertaler Krapfen), Advent concerts with local brass bands and choirs, and a free Christkindlwerkstatt for children (crafts, baking, games). Free sleigh rides at weekends.
