Tiroler Silberpfad Trophy

Event · Schwaz, Altstadt · 19 September 2026

When19 September 2026
WhereAltstadt Schwaz (Start/Ziel), Altstadt, 6130 Schwaz
TypeEvent

A demanding trail-running competition with four distances (11km, 23km, 42km, 86km). Start and finish in the heart of the Silberregion Karwendel. A new TST 11K power loop with 600 metres of elevation gain.
