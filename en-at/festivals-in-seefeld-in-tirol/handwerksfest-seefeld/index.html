Handwerksfest Seefeld

Event · Seefeld in Tirol, Seefeld · 12–13 September 2026

When12–13 September 2026
WhereFußgängerzone Seefeld, Klosterstraße, Seefeld, 6100 Seefeld in Tirol
TypeEvent

A festival more than 25 years old with around 1,500 participants: craftspeople, musicians and choirs present traditional crafts such as basket-weaving, saddlery, violin-making and blacksmithing. Wood-fired bread baking, brass music at seven locations, regional cuisine and a vintage tractor parade.
