Klingender Advent

Music festival · Seefeld in Tirol, Seefeld · 9–10 December 2026

When9–10 December 2026
WhereWM Halle Seefeld, Möserer Straße 632, Seefeld, 6100 Seefeld in Tirol
TypeMusic festival

High-quality Advent concerts: on 9 December, 'Alpenklänge im Advent' with Lechufer, Martin Locher and Tschentig; on 10 December, the 'Happy Christmas Tour' with Nik P. & Band. Admission: 9 Dec from 15 euros, 10 Dec from 55 euros. Doors open at 19:00.
