Regional Music Festival (District Music Festival)

Music festival · Seefeld in Tirol, Leutasch · 14–16 August 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When14–16 August 2026
WhereMusikpavillon Leutasch, Leutasch, 6105 Seefeld in Tirol
TypeMusic festival
