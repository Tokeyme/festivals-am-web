Seefeld Christmas Market (Weihnachtsmarkt Seefeld)

Christmas market · Seefeld in Tirol, Seefeld · 20 November 2026 – 6 January 2027

When20 November 2026 – 6 January 2027
WhereFußgängerzone, Klosterstr. 43, Seefeld, 6100 Seefeld in Tirol
TypeChristmas market

Charming Advent market with traditional wooden chalets in Tyrolean log-cabin style, mulled wine, regional specialities and handcrafted gift items. In the evenings, brass music and Christmas choirs, and a children's post office for letters to the Christ Child. Daily 14:00-21:00.
