Seefeld Krampus Display (Krampuslauf Seefeld)

Funfair · Seefeld in Tirol, Seefeld · 4 December 2026

When4 December 2026
WhereFußgängerzone, Klosterstr. 43, Seefeld, 6100 Seefeld in Tirol
TypeFunfair

Tyrol's largest Krampuslauf, with 30 Tuifel groups from across the Alpine region. A tradition of elaborate wooden masks, horns and bells. Fast-paced scenes with playful interactions. With an after-show party. 18:00–19:00.
