Seefeld Magic of Lights (Lichterinstallationen)

Event · Seefeld in Tirol, Seefeld · 1 November 2026 – 6 January 2027

When1 November 2026 – 6 January 2027
WhereGesamte Fußgängerzone, Kurpark bis Seekirchl und Olympia-Sport-Congress-Zentrum, Seefeld, 6100 Seefeld in Tirol
TypeEvent

Artistic light sculptures transform the village into a sea of light: magical animal figures, illuminated swings, a 100,000-light trail across the Pfarrhügel, cross-country and biathlon figures, and a festive Christmas tree. Daily 16:00–22:00.
