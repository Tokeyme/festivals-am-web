Seefeld Tuesday Market (Seefelder Dienstagsmarkt)

Market · Seefeld in Tirol, Seefeld · 5 May – 29 September 2026

When5 May – 29 September 2026
WhereDorfplatz Seefeld, Klosterstraße, Seefeld, 6100 Seefeld in Tirol
TypeMarket

A weekly farmers' market every Tuesday from May to the end of September, offering mountain cheese, butter, yoghurt, meat, sausage, smoked trout, baked goods, handmade candles, soaps and larch-wood products. 10:00–16:00.
