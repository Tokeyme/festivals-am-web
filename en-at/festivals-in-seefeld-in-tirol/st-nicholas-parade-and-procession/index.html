St. Nicholas Parade & Procession

Culture festival · Seefeld in Tirol, Seefeld · 6 December 2026

When6 December 2026
WhereFußgängerzone bis Pfarrkirche, Klosterstraße, Seefeld, 6100 Seefeld in Tirol
TypeCulture festival

A St Nicholas and Krampus procession through the pedestrian zone to the parish church at 3pm, followed by a St Nicholas celebration in the parish church at around 4.45pm. A traditional custom, with the giving of a blessing.
