Frequency Festival

Music festival · St. Pölten, St. Pölten · 20–22 August 2026

When20–22 August 2026
WhereGreen Park VAZ St. Pölten, St. Pölten, 3100 St. Pölten
TypeMusic festival

The FM4 Frequency Festival, one of the largest festivals in Austria with around 140,000 visitors. 80 bands from hip-hop, pop and indie-pop. Artists include Twenty One Pilots, Lorde, Kraftklub and Paul Kalkbrenner.
