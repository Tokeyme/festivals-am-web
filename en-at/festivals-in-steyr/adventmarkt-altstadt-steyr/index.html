Adventmarkt Altstadt Steyr

Christmas market · Steyr, Altstadt · 13 November – 24 December 2026

When13 November – 24 December 2026
WhereStadtplatz, Leopoldibrunnen, Altstadt, 4400 Steyr
TypeChristmas market

A romantic Adventmarkt with Adventblasen daily at 17:00 and Christkind fairy tales on Advent Saturdays from 15:00. A blacksmith show, Punsch and crafts. Free admission.
