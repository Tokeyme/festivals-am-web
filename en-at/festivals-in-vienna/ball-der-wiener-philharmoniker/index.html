Ball der Wiener Philharmoniker

Event · Vienna, Innere Stadt · 21 January 2027

When21 January 2027
WhereWiener Musikverein, Musikvereinsplatz 1, Innere Stadt, 1010 Vienna
TypeEvent

The Ball der Wiener Philharmoniker takes place at the Wiener Musikverein and is ceremonially opened by the Wiener Philharmoniker. It is one of the most prominent balls of the Viennese Ballsaison.
