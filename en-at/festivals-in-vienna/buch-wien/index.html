BUCH WIEN

Culture festival · Vienna, Leopoldstadt · 25–29 November 2026

When25–29 November 2026
WhereMesse Wien, Messeplatz 1, Leopoldstadt, 1020 Vienna
TypeCulture festival

BUCH WIEN is an international book fair and, at the same time, a reading festival week at the Messe Wien. More than 500 exhibitors present current novels as well as non-fiction, children's and young adult books; the fair is accompanied by an extensive programme of readings.
