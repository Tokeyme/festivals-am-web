ernte.dank.festival

Funfair · Vienna, Innere Stadt · 12–13 September 2026

When12–13 September 2026
WhereHeldenplatz, Innere Stadt, 1010 Vienna
TypeFunfair

The ernte.dank.festival on Vienna's Heldenplatz is the largest celebration of Austrian agriculture. The programme features a farmers' market and a village of regional delicacies, traditional folk-costume fashion, brass music and folk dancing, a ceremonial tapping of the beer barrel, as well as the blessing of the harvest produce and a parade of harvest wagons.
