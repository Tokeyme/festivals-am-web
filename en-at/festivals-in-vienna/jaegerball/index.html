Jägerball

Event · Vienna, Innere Stadt · 25 January 2027

When25 January 2027
WhereHofburg und Spanische Hofreitschule, Innere Stadt, 1010 Vienna
TypeEvent

The Jägerball – Ball vom Grünen Kreuz takes place in the Wiener Hofburg and the Spanische Hofreitschule and, with around 5,000 guests, is one of the largest Vienna balls. Traditionally, it is held on the last Monday in January in Tracht and hunting attire.
