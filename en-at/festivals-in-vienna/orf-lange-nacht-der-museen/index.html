ORF-Lange Nacht der Museen

Culture festival · Vienna, Innere Stadt · 3 October 2026

When3 October 2026
WhereMaria-Theresien-Platz (Treffpunkt Museum), Innere Stadt, 1010 Vienna
TypeCulture festival

At the ORF-Lange Nacht der Museen, numerous museums and galleries across Vienna open their doors on a single evening until midnight. With one ticket, visitors can explore a wide range of venues; the central meeting point is the Maria-Theresien-Platz.
