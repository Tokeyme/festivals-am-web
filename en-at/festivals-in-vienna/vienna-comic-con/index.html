Vienna Comic Con

Event · Vienna, Leopoldstadt · 21–22 November 2026

When21–22 November 2026
WhereMesse Wien, Messeplatz 1, Leopoldstadt, 1020 Vienna
TypeEvent

The Vienna Comic Con (VIECC) at the Messe Wien is a large public convention devoted to comics, pop culture and cosplay. Highlights include a Cosplay Village with a catwalk, photo ops with stars and numerous exhibitor stands.
