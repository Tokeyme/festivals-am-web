Weihnachtsdorf Campus Universität Wien

Christmas market · Vienna, Alsergrund · 13 November – 23 December 2026

When13 November – 23 December 2026
WhereAltes AKH, Spitalgasse 2-4, Alsergrund, 1090 Vienna
TypeChristmas market

The Christmas village on the campus of the Universität Wien is set in the courtyards of the former general hospital (Altes AKH). At its centre stands an unusual Christbaum made of sledges, surrounded by Alpine-style chalets offering regional specialities.
