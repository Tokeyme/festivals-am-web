Weihnachtsmarkt am Hof

Christmas market · Vienna, Innere Stadt · 13 November – 23 December 2026

When13 November – 23 December 2026
WhereAm Hof, Innere Stadt, 1010 Vienna
TypeChristmas market

The Weihnachtsmarkt am Hof is set on one of the oldest squares in Vienna's city centre. At more than 70 stalls, visitors will find handmade and artistic items and a selection of gift ideas; catering businesses provide culinary classics and hot drinks.
