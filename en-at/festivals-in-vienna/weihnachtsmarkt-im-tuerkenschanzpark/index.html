Weihnachtsmarkt im Türkenschanzpark

Christmas market · Vienna, Währing · 13 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When13 November – 23 December 2026
WhereTürkenschanzpark, Währing, 1180 Vienna
TypeChristmas market

The Weihnachtsmarkt im Türkenschanzpark is an Adventmarkt set in the park-like surroundings of the Türkenschanzpark in the Währing district. The market offers craftwork, gifts and culinary treats in a festive Christmas atmosphere.
