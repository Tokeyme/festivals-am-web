Weihnachtszauber Schwarzenbergplatz

Christmas market · Vienna, Landstraße · 13 November – 26 December 2026

When13 November – 26 December 2026
WhereSchwarzenbergplatz, Landstraße, 1030 Vienna
TypeChristmas market

The Weihnachtszauber am Schwarzenbergplatz is one of Vienna's Christmas markets and is located on the Schwarzenbergplatz near the Ringstraße. The Advent market offers festive arts and crafts, gift ideas and food and drink.
