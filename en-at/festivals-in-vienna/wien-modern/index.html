Wien Modern

Music festival · Vienna, Landstraße · 30 October – 30 November 2026

When30 October – 30 November 2026
WhereWiener Konzerthaus, Lothringerstraße 20, Landstraße, 1030 Vienna
TypeMusic festival

Wien Modern is the largest festival for contemporary music in Austria and one of the most significant of its kind worldwide. It was founded in 1988 on the initiative of Claudio Abbado and each autumn takes over numerous venues across Vienna, including the Wiener Konzerthaus.
