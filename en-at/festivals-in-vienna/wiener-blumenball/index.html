Wiener Blumenball

Event · Vienna, Innere Stadt · 15 January 2027

When15 January 2027
WhereFesträume des Wiener Rathauses, Eingang Lichtenfelsgasse 2, Innere Stadt, 1010 Vienna
TypeEvent

The City of Vienna's Blumenball takes place in the ceremonial halls of the Wiener Rathaus and is known for its elaborate floral decoration. It is one of the popular balls of the Viennese ball season.
