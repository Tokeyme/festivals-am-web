Wiener Eistraum

Sports event · Vienna, Innere Stadt · 22 January – 7 March 2027

When22 January – 7 March 2027
WhereRathausplatz, Innere Stadt, 1010 Vienna
TypeSports event

The Wiener Eistraum transforms the square in front of Vienna's City Hall into one of the largest ice-skating surfaces in Europe, with over 9,000 square metres of ice. Alongside the expansive ice surfaces, there are winding ice paths through the Rathauspark, a children's area, and food and music on offer.
