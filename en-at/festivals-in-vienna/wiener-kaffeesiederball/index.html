Wiener Kaffeesiederball

Event · Vienna, Innere Stadt · 22 January 2027

When22 January 2027
WhereHofburg, Heldenplatz, Innere Stadt, 1010 Vienna
TypeEvent

The Wiener Kaffeesiederball is the ball of Vienna's coffee-house proprietors and takes place in the Wiener Hofburg. For the occasion, the Hofburg is transformed into the largest and most beautiful coffee house in the world.
