Wiener Opernball

Event · Vienna, Innere Stadt · 4 February 2027

When4 February 2027
WhereWiener Staatsoper, Opernring 2, Innere Stadt, 1010 Vienna
TypeEvent

The Wiener Opernball is the best-known ball of the Wiener Ballsaison and takes place in the festively converted home of the Wiener Staatsoper. It is regarded as the social highlight of the Wiener Fasching and is traditionally held on the last Thursday before Ash Wednesday.
