Wiener Sicherheitsfest

Event · Vienna, Innere Stadt · 25–26 October 2026

When25–26 October 2026
WhereRathausplatz, Innere Stadt, 1010 Vienna
TypeEvent

At the Wiener Sicherheitsfest on Rathausplatz, the Helfer Wiens present themselves together with Vienna's aid and emergency-response organisations and numerous departments of the City of Vienna. As Austria's largest showcase of safety and emergency services, it offers a programme for the whole family, with demonstrations and hands-on stations.
