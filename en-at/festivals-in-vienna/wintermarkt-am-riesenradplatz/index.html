Wintermarkt am Riesenradplatz

Christmas market · Vienna, Leopoldstadt · 20 November 2026 – 6 January 2027

When20 November 2026 – 6 January 2027
WhereRiesenradplatz, Wiener Prater, Leopoldstadt, 1020 Vienna
TypeChristmas market

The Wintermarkt am Riesenradplatz in Vienna's Prater combines an Advent and Christmas market with fairground rides and a live music programme. Alongside numerous street-food stalls, there are free concerts by local artists and a large New Year's Eve programme.
