ZuckerBäckerball

Event · Vienna, Innere Stadt · 14 January 2027

When14 January 2027
WhereHofburg, Heldenplatz, Innere Stadt, 1010 Vienna
TypeEvent

The ZuckerBäckerball is the traditional ball of Vienna's confectioners and pastry chefs, held in the Wiener Hofburg. It is one of the social highlights of the Wiener Ballsaison.
