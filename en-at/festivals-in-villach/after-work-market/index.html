After Work Market

Market · Villach, Innere Stadt · 24 July 2026

When24 July 2026
WhereHans-Gasser-Platz, Innere Stadt, 9500 Villach
TypeMarket

The Alpen-Adria After Work Market invites visitors to Hans-Gasser-Platz for relaxed evening hours. From 5 pm to 11 pm, there are regional specialities from the Alpine-Adriatic region, selected organic products, refreshing drinks and musical entertainment. Admission is free.
