Christbaummarkt

Market · Villach, Innere Stadt · 15–23 December 2026

When15–23 December 2026
WhereKaiser-Josef-Platz, Innere Stadt, 9500 Villach
TypeMarket

On Kaiser-Josef-Platz in the pedestrian zone, the Christbaummarkt (Christmas-tree market) offers a wide selection of Christmas trees in all sizes and varieties. Held each year in the days before Christmas, the market is open daily.
