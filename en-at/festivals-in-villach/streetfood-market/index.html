Streetfood Market

Market · Villach, Innere Stadt · 10–11 September 2026

When10–11 September 2026
WhereNikolaiplatz, Innere Stadt, 9500 Villach
TypeMarket

At the Streetfood Market, Nikolaiplatz becomes a meeting point for food lovers. Chefs from the region invite guests on a culinary journey featuring a variety of dishes, aromas and flavours from the Alpine-Adriatic region. Admission is free.
