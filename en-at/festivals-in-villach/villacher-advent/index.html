Villacher Advent

Christmas market · Villach, Altstadt · 20 November – 31 December 2026

When20 November – 31 December 2026
WhereRathausplatz und Altstadt Villach, Altstadt, 9500 Villach
TypeChristmas market

Christmas market with over 50 wooden chalets, traditional handicrafts, Christmas decorations, Austrian specialities and mulled wine. It transforms the old town into a sparkling sea of lights.
