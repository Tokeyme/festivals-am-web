Villacher Faschingsumzug

Carnival · Villach, Innere Stadt · 6 February 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When6 February 2027
WhereHauptplatz, Innere Stadt, 9500 Villach
TypeCarnival

The Villacher Faschingsumzug (Villach carnival parade) is the highlight of the Villacher Fasching (Villach carnival) and one of the largest carnival parades in Austria. On Carnival Saturday, hundreds of masked groups and lavishly decorated floats move from Hans-Gasser-Platz across the Hauptplatz and the Draubrücke bridge to the Bahnhofsplatz, accompanied by tens of thousands of masked spectators. The parade is traditionally broadcast on ORF.
