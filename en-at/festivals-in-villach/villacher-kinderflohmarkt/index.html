Villacher Kinderflohmarkt

Flea market · Villach, Innere Stadt · 19 September 2026

When19 September 2026
WhereHans-Gasser-Platz, Innere Stadt, 9500 Villach
TypeFlea market

At the Villacher Kinderflohmarkt (children's flea market) on Hans-Gasser-Platz, children aged between 4 and 14 sell their no-longer-needed toys, books, clothes and much more themselves. Through selling, swapping and haggling, the children discover how exciting it is to make small deals and to take responsibility for their own belongings.
