Villacher Kirchtag

Funfair · Villach, Altstadt · 26 July – 2 August 2026

When26 July – 2 August 2026
WhereAltstadt Villach, rund um Hauptplatz und Kirchenplatz, Altstadt, 9500 Villach
TypeFunfair

Austria's largest traditional folk festival, now in its 81st edition. The old town becomes a festival mile with music, traditional fare (Kirchtag-Suppe) and Kirchtag atmosphere for around 450,000 visitors.
