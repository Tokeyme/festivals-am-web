Villacher Krampuslauf

Event · Villach, Innere Stadt · 27 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 November 2026
WhereHauptplatz, Innere Stadt, 9500 Villach
TypeEvent

At the Villacher Krampuslauf (Krampus run), numerous Krampus and Perchten groups pass through the city centre. Wearing eerie masks and curved horns, accompanied by the dull sound of bells, the groups make their way across the Villacher Hauptplatz (main square), providing a highlight of the pre-Christmas period.
