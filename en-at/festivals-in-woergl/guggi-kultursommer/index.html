Guggi Kultursommer

Culture festival · Wörgl, Zentrum · 22 July – 2 September 2026

When22 July – 2 September 2026
WhereBegegnungszone vor dem CityCenter Wörgl, Bahnhofstraße, Zentrum, 6300 Wörgl
TypeCulture festival

Seven cultural evenings on Wednesdays (22 July, 29 July, 5 August, 12 August, 19 August, 26 August, 2 September), each from 18:00 to 22:00 in the open air. Music, theatre, cabaret and karaoke from clubs, bands and artists. A free offering with Guggi culture vouchers.
