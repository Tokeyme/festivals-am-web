Public Viewing Fußball-Weltmeisterschaft

Event · Wörgl, Zentrum · 17 June – 20 July 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When17 June – 20 July 2026
WhereParkplatz hinter dem City Center Wörgl, Bahnhofstraße, Zentrum, 6300 Wörgl
TypeEvent

Public screening of 12 World Cup matches over 9 event days on a big screen, free of charge. Including the Austrian national team's matches (17 June from 6:00 with breakfast), the round of 16, the semi-finals (14–15 July) and the final (19 July).
