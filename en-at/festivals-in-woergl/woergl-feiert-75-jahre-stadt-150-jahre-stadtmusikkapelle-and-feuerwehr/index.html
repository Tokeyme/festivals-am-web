Wörgl feiert – 75 Jahre Stadt, 150 Jahre Stadtmusikkapelle & Feuerwehr

Town festival · Wörgl, Zentrum · 19–21 June 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When19–21 June 2026
WhereSchopperfeld (Festgelände), Bahnhofstraße, Stadtzentrum Wörgl, Zentrum, 6300 Wörgl
TypeTown festival

A weekend of anniversary celebrations: 150 years of the Stadtmusikkapelle Wörgl, 150 years of the volunteer fire brigade, and 75 years of the town of Wörgl. Festive evening on Friday 19 June from 17:30 with a ceremonial procession, brass music and a vehicle display; on Sunday, a blessing of vehicles, field mass and brunch.
