Wörgler Christkindlmarkt

Christmas market · Wörgl, Zentrum · 1–30 December 2026

When1–30 December 2026
WhereGradlplatz (hinter der Pfarrkirche), Bahnhofstraße, Zentrum, 6300 Wörgl
TypeChristmas market

A traditional Christkindlmarkt with festively decorated stalls, regional specialities, Glühwein, Punsch and children's entertainment. Wednesday–Saturday 15:00–21:00, Sunday 15:00–19:00. Closed: 24 December & 31 December.
