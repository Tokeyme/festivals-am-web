Ballarat Heritage Weekend

Historical festival · Ballarat, Ballarat Central · 8–9 May 2027

When8–9 May 2027
WhereBallarat Railway Station, Lydiard Street North, Ballarat Central VIC 3350, Ballarat Central, 3350 Ballarat
TypeHistorical festival

A heritage weekend held across central Ballarat. Past editions have used the Mining Exchange as an exhibition space, and vintage trains and a preserved tramway have featured at the railway station and the Botanical Gardens.
