The Ballarat Makers Edit

Market · Ballarat, Ballarat Central · 12–13 December 2026

When12–13 December 2026
WhereThe Mining Exchange, 12-16 Lydiard Street North, Ballarat Central VIC 3350, 3350 Ballarat
TypeMarket

A quarterly showcase of design-led makers, with objects, fashion, art and small-batch pantry goods, held at the Mining Exchange in Ballarat Central. Entry is free, and gold coin donations are requested for the Ballarat Animal Shelter.
