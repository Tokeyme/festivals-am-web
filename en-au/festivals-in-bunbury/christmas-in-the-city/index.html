Christmas in the City

Town festival · Bunbury · 13 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When13 December 2026
WhereBicentennial Square, 6230 Bunbury
TypeTown festival

Free family festival in Bunbury's city centre each December, with a street parade, a carols concert, market stalls and food vendors. It is held on a Sunday evening at Bicentennial Square and in the surrounding CBD.
