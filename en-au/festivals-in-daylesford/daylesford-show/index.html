Daylesford Show

Funfair · Daylesford · 28 November 2026

When28 November 2026
WhereVictoria Park, cnr Ballan Road & Burrall Street, 3460 Daylesford
TypeFunfair

The 152nd Daylesford Show is a one-day agricultural show at Victoria Park on Saturday 28 November 2026, running from 9am to 3pm. The program includes free children's activities such as a petting zoo and puppet show, showground rides, live music, horse events, sheep, poultry and dog judging, and arts, crafts and horticulture entries. The Victorian state election is held on the same day.
