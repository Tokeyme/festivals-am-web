Dark Mofo

Culture festival · Hobart · 10–21 June 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When10–21 June 2027
WhereHobart waterfront, Sullivans Cove, 7000 Hobart
TypeCulture festival

Dark Mofo is a midwinter arts festival in Hobart, held around the June solstice. It brings art, music and public events to the city, and its audience includes many visitors from interstate and overseas.
