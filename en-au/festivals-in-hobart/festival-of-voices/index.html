Festival of Voices

Culture festival · Hobart · 2–11 July 2027

When2–11 July 2027
WhereHobart Town Hall, 50 Macquarie Street, 7000 Hobart
TypeCulture festival

Festival of Voices is a winter singing festival held across Tasmania, with a strong programme in Hobart. It combines concerts, workshops and community singing, including The Big Sing, a free annual singalong for families.
