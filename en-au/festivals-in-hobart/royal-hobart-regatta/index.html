Royal Hobart Regatta

Sports event · Hobart · 6–8 February 2027

When6–8 February 2027
WhereRoyal Hobart Regatta Grounds, McVilly Drive, 7000 Hobart
TypeSports event

The Royal Hobart Regatta is a regatta held on the Derwent at the Regatta Grounds on the Queens Domain. It runs over a long weekend of racing in summer.
