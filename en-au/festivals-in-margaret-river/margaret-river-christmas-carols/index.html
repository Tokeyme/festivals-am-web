Margaret River Christmas Carols

Event · Margaret River · 11 December 2026

When11 December 2026
WhereMargaret River Senior High School Oval, 158 Bussell Highway, 6285 Margaret River
TypeEvent

The Margaret River Christmas Carols is a free, all-ages community evening of carols sung by local choirs, with bands and food and drink vendors on site. It is presented by a local church with support from the Shire.
