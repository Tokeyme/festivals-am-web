Melbourne Cup Carnival

Sports event · Melbourne, Flemington · 30 October – 6 November 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When30 October – 6 November 2027
Where448 Epsom Road, Flemington, 3031 Melbourne
TypeSports event

The Melbourne Cup Carnival is the Victoria Racing Club's four-day festival at Flemington, opening with Victoria Derby Day on a Saturday and continuing through Melbourne Cup Day, Oaks Day and Stakes Day. The Cup is run on the first Tuesday in November, which is a public holiday in Victoria. The carnival brings together racing, fashion and a large social programme.
