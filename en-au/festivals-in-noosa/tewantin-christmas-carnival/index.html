Tewantin Christmas Carnival

Funfair · Noosa, Tewantin · 6 December 2026

When6 December 2026
WhereTewantin Noosa RSL, Memorial Park, 4565 Noosa
TypeFunfair

A free community carnival at RSL Memorial Park in Tewantin on Sunday 6 December 2026, from 3.30pm to 8pm, run by Tewantin Noosa RSL. The program includes free rides, food stalls, stage entertainment and Carols by Candlelight, and the evening ends with a fireworks display over the river.
