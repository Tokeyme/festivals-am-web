Golden Slipper Day

Sports event · Parramatta, Rosehill · 20 March 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 March 2027
WhereJames Ruse Drive, Rosehill, 2142 Parramatta
TypeSports event

Golden Slipper Day is the highlight of the Sydney Autumn Carnival at Rosehill Gardens, built around the world's richest race for two-year-old horses. It is staged by the Australian Turf Club and draws large crowds with a fashion and social side programme. The meeting usually falls in the second half of March.
