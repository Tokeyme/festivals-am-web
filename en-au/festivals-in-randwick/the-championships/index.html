The Championships

Sports event · Randwick · 3–10 April 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When3–10 April 2027
WhereAlison Road, 2031 Randwick
TypeSports event

The Championships are the finale of the Sydney Autumn Carnival, run on two Saturdays in April at Royal Randwick. The first day features mile, sprint and Derby races, while the second day closes with the Queen Elizabeth Stakes and the Sydney Cup. Both days combine top-class racing with a fashion and hospitality programme.
