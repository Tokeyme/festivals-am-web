Illawarra Folk Festival

Music festival · Wollongong, Bulli · 15–17 January 2027

When15–17 January 2027
WhereBulli Showground, Grevillea Park Road, Bulli NSW 2516, 2516 Wollongong
TypeMusic festival

A long-running folk festival marking its 40th anniversary in January 2027, held at Bulli Showground. The organisers promise more than 100 local and international artists across folk, roots, contemporary and world music, plus workshops, poetry, markets, food trucks and activities for children. Visitors can camp on site or come for the day, and children under 12 go free.
