The Banff Christmas Market

Christmas market · Banff, Warner Stables · 19 November – 13 December 2026

When19 November – 13 December 2026
WhereWarner Stables, 132 Banff Avenue, Warner Stables, T1L 1B8 Banff
TypeChristmas market

Alpine-style Christmas market at Warner Stables in the heart of Banff National Park. Over 200 artisans across four weekends, an outdoor fire lounge, live music and Santa's workshop against a Rocky Mountain backdrop.
