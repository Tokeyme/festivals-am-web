The Banff Christmas Market

Christmas market · Banff, Warner Stables · 19 November – 13 December 2026

When19 November – 13 December 2026
WhereBanff Train Station, 327 Railway Avenue, Warner Stables, T1L 1B8 Banff
TypeChristmas market

Alpine-style Christmas market at the historic Banff Train Station in the heart of Banff National Park. Over 200 artisanal vendors, an outdoor fire lounge and Santa’s House & Workshop against a Rocky Mountain backdrop, Thursday to Sunday.
