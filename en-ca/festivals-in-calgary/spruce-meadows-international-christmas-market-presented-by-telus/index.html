Spruce Meadows International Christmas Market, presented by TELUS

Christmas market · Calgary, Spruce Meadows · 13 November – 6 December 2026

When13 November – 6 December 2026
Where18011 Spruce Meadows Way SW, Spruce Meadows, T2X 4B7 Calgary
TypeChristmas market

A festive European-inspired Christmas market at the world-renowned Spruce Meadows equestrian grounds in south Calgary. Held over four weekends, it fills heated halls and grounds with international artisans and vendors, Christmas decor, seasonal treats, mulled wine and family activities in a cozy winter-wonderland setting.
