Evergreen Festival (Downtown Halifax)

Christmas market · Halifax, Downtown / Grand Parade & Spring Garden · 27 November – 20 December 2026

When27 November – 20 December 2026
WhereGrand Parade, 1841 Argyle Street, Downtown / Grand Parade & Spring Garden, B3J 3A5 Halifax
TypeChristmas market

Downtown Halifax's month-long holiday festival with a Christmas market, glow lights, skating and celebrations of Nova Scotian food, art and culture, centred on the Grand Parade and the Spring Garden district.
