Kitchener-Waterloo Christkindl Market

Christmas market · Kitchener, Downtown Kitchener · 3–13 December 2026

When3–13 December 2026
WhereKitchener City Hall, 200 King Street West, Downtown Kitchener, N2G 1B6 Kitchener
TypeChristmas market

Canada's largest authentic German-style Christmas market, held at Kitchener City Hall in Ontario's German-heritage twin cities. Gluhwein, bratwurst, Stollen, Old-World crafts, carollers and a live Christkindl. Expanded to two full weekends in 2026.
