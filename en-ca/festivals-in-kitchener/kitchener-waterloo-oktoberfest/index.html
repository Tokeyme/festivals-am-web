Kitchener-Waterloo Oktoberfest

Funfair · Kitchener, Downtown Kitchener · 25 September – 17 October 2026

When25 September – 17 October 2026
WhereWillkommen Platz, Carl Zehr Square, 200 King Street West, Downtown Kitchener, N2G 4G7 Kitchener
TypeFunfair

Canada's Great Bavarian Festival and one of the largest Oktoberfest celebrations in the world outside Munich, rooted in the deep German heritage of the Kitchener-Waterloo region. Running from late September through Thanksgiving, it features Festhallen with live oompah bands, Bavarian food and beer, the free outdoor Willkommen Platz on King Street, KIDtoberfest family programming, and the traditional Thanksgiving Day Parade.
