London Oktoberfest (German Canadian Club)

Funfair · London, The Coves / Old South · 23–24 October 2026

When23–24 October 2026
WhereGerman Canadian Club, 1 Cove Road, The Coves / Old South, N6J 1H7 London
TypeFunfair

The German Canadian Club of London hosts the city's authentic Oktoberfest with imported German beer, schnitzel and bratwurst, oompah bands and dancing over two evenings.
