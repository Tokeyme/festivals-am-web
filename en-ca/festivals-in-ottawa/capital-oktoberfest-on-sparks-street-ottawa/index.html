Capital Oktoberfest on Sparks Street (Ottawa)

Funfair · Ottawa, Downtown / Sparks Street · 1–4 October 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When1–4 October 2026
WhereSparks Street Pedestrian Mall (Munich-style tent, Bier Markt), Downtown / Sparks Street, K1P 5A5 Ottawa
TypeFunfair

A large Munich-style Oktoberfest tent takes over Sparks Street for four days of Paulaner beer, live music and Bavarian food, run in partnership with the University of Ottawa Heart Institute.
