Oktoberfest on the Prairies (Saskatoon)

Funfair · Saskatoon, Prairieland Park · 3–4 October 2026

When3–4 October 2026
WherePrairieland Park (outdoor pavilion), 503 Ruth Street West, Prairieland Park, S7J 5H2 Saskatoon
TypeFunfair

Saskatoon's Oktoberfest on the Prairies blends Bavarian tradition with prairie charm at Prairieland Park, offering beer, homemade pretzels, food trucks and live entertainment.
