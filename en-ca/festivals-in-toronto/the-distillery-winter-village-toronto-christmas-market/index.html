The Distillery Winter Village (Toronto Christmas Market)

Christmas market · Toronto, Distillery District · 12 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12 November 2026 – 3 January 2027
Where55 Mill Street, Distillery District, M5A 3C4 Toronto
TypeChristmas market

Toronto's original and most famous Christmas market, set among the cobblestone streets and Victorian-era brick buildings of the historic Distillery District. The Winter Village features Canada's most photographed Christmas tree, thousands of twinkling lights, artisan vendors, mulled wine, seasonal food and festive entertainment. Admission is free on weekdays, with timed tickets on weekends.
