Toronto Caribbean Carnival (Caribana)

Culture festival · Toronto, Exhibition Place · 31 July – 3 August 2026

When31 July – 3 August 2026
WhereExhibition Place & Lake Shore Blvd W, Exhibition Place, M6K 3C3 Toronto
TypeCulture festival

North America's largest Caribbean carnival, culminating in the spectacular Grand Parade of masqueraders in elaborate costumes along Lake Shore Boulevard. Soca, calypso, steelpan and Caribbean cuisine fill the August long weekend.
