Toronto International Film Festival (TIFF)

Culture festival · Toronto, Entertainment District · 10–20 September 2026

When10–20 September 2026
WhereTIFF Lightbox, 350 King St W, Entertainment District, M5V 3X5 Toronto
TypeCulture festival

One of the world's premier film festivals, TIFF brings star-studded premieres and hundreds of international films to downtown Toronto each September. The 51st edition is anchored at TIFF Lightbox and Roy Thomson Hall.
