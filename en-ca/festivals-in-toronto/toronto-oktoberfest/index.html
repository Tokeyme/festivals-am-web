Toronto Oktoberfest

Funfair · Toronto, Fort York / Garrison Common · 25–26 September 2026

When25–26 September 2026
WhereGarrison Common, Fort York National Historic Site, 100 Garrison Road, Fort York / Garrison Common, M5V 1A9 Toronto
TypeFunfair

Toronto's largest and most authentic Oktoberfest, with Bavarian bands, Schuhplattler dancers, German and European beers and hearty festival fare. For 2026 the festival moves to a new home on Garrison Common at historic Fort York.
