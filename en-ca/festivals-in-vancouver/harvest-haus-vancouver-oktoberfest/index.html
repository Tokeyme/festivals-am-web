Harvest Haus Vancouver Oktoberfest

Funfair · Vancouver, Hastings-Sunrise (PNE) · 2–3 October 2026

When2–3 October 2026
WherePNE Forum, Pacific National Exhibition, 2901 East Hastings Street, Hastings-Sunrise (PNE), V5K 5J1 Vancouver
TypeFunfair

Vancouver's biggest Oktoberfest is a Bavarian-style beer hall in the PNE Forum, with imported German brews, live oompah and party bands, long communal tables and Alpine food.
