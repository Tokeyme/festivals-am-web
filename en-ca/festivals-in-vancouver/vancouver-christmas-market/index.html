Vancouver Christmas Market

Christmas market · Vancouver, Coal Harbour / Downtown · 12 November – 24 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12 November – 24 December 2026
WhereJack Poole Plaza, 1055 Canada Place, Coal Harbour / Downtown, V6C 0C3 Vancouver
TypeChristmas market

An authentic German-style Christmas market on the downtown waterfront at Jack Poole Plaza, modelled on the traditional Weihnachtsmaerkte of Germany. Visitors find handcrafted gifts from local and German artisans, gluehwein, bratwurst and stollen, a walk-through Christmas Alley, an Alpine carousel and a giant German Christmas pyramid. Now one of Vancouver's signature holiday traditions.
