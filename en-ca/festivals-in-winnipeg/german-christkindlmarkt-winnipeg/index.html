German Christkindlmarkt Winnipeg

Christmas market · Winnipeg, Downtown · 27–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27–29 November 2026
WhereMall of Fort Garry Place, 85 Garry Street, Downtown, R3C 3H8 Winnipeg
TypeChristmas market

Authentic German market run by the German-Canadian Congress (Manitoba) since 1985, held indoors at the Mall of Fort Garry Place with Stollen, deli fare, Gluhwein, crafts and German entertainment.
