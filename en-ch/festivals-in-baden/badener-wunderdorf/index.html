Badener Wunderdorf

Event · Baden, Baden-Stadt · 29 October – 23 December 2026

When29 October – 23 December 2026
WhereTheaterplatz, 5400 Baden, Baden-Stadt, 5400 Baden
TypeEvent

Outdoor Weihnachtsmarkt with over 100 huts, street food, drinks, lights and mulled wine. The most popular Weihnachtsmarkt in the Aargau region, with autumn pumpkin weeks until November and Advent magic in December.
