Cortège

Carnival · Basel, Altstadt Grossbasel · 15–17 February 2027

When15–17 February 2027
WhereInnere und äussere Route (Barfüsserplatz, Marktplatz, Steinenberg), Altstadt Grossbasel, 4051 Basel
TypeCarnival

The Cortège is the main Fasnacht parade, which winds through the city centre on Monday and Wednesday afternoons from around 1.30 pm. Cliquen, Guggenmusiken, floats, chaises and individual masked figures move along an inner and an outer route around Barfüsserplatz, Marktplatz and Steinenberg. Organised by the Fasnachts-Comité, the parade continues until the early evening.
