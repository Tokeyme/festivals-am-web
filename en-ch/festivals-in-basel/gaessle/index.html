Gässle

Carnival · Basel, Altstadt Grossbasel · 15–17 February 2027

When15–17 February 2027
WhereGassen der Altstadt, Altstadt Grossbasel, 4051 Basel
TypeCarnival

Gässle refers to Fasnacht revellers wandering freely through the alleyways of Basel's old town. Especially on the parade-free Tuesday, Cliquen along with individual pipers and drummers play away from fixed routes, turning the entire town centre into a stage. This free-flowing activity is regarded as a particularly atmospheric part of the Basler Fasnacht.
