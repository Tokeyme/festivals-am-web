Guggekonzärt

Carnival · Basel · 16 February 2027

When16 February 2027
WhereBarfüsserplatz, Marktplatz, Claraplatz, 4051 Basel
TypeCarnival

On Fasnacht Tuesday, the Guggenmusiken take centre stage. From about 7.30 pm, numerous Guggen play well-known and lesser-known tunes in a discordant, brassy style on Barfüsserplatz, Marktplatz and Claraplatz. Tuesday has no official parade and is regarded as an especially popular Fasnacht day.
