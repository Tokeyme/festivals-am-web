Museumsnacht Basel

Culture festival · Basel · 22 January 2027

When22 January 2027
Whereverschiedene Museen; Infopunkt Münsterplatz, 4051 Basel
TypeCulture festival

At the Museumsnacht Basel, around 40 museums and cultural institutions open their doors for one night from 6 pm to 2 am. A single ticket gives access to exhibitions, guided tours, performances and workshops, while shuttle buses connect the venues from the information point on the Münsterplatz. The event is aimed equally at families, young people and adults.
