Regionale

Culture festival · Basel · 26 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

When26 November 2026 – 3 January 2027
Whereverschiedene Ausstellungsräume, 4051 Basel
TypeCulture festival

The Regionale is a tri-national exhibition project that presents current art from the Basel region, Alsace and southern Baden over the festive period. Around two dozen exhibition spaces, a large share of them in Basel, show works selected through an open call. For more than 25 years the event has fostered exchange between artists, curators and the public across national borders.
