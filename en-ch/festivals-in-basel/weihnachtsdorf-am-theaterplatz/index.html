Weihnachtsdorf am Theaterplatz

Christmas market · Basel · 26 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When26 November 2026 – 3 January 2027
WhereTheaterplatz / Elisabethenkirche, 4051 Basel
TypeChristmas market

A new Christmas village is to be created around Theaterplatz and the Elisabethenkirche, complementing Basel's existing Christmas markets. The focus is on local design, social projects and a curated culinary offering. The project was most recently going through the permit process; its staging has not yet been definitively confirmed.
