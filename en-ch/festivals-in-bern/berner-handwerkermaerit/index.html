Berner Handwerkermärit

Market · Bern, Altstadt · 5 September 2026

When5 September 2026
WhereMünsterplattform, Altstadt, 3011 Bern
TypeMarket

The Berner Handwerkermärit, a market for craftspeople, is held monthly on the Münsterplattform in Bern's old town. Around a hundred stalls offer handmade, one-of-a-kind pieces from their own workshops. Additional market days are held during Advent.
