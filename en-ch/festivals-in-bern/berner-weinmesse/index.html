Berner Weinmesse

Wine festival · Bern, Wankdorf · 9–18 October 2026

When9–18 October 2026
WhereBERNEXPO, Mingerstrasse 6, Wankdorf, 3014 Bern
TypeWine festival

The Berner Weinmesse is regarded as the largest wine fair in the Espace Mittelland region and is held on the BERNEXPO grounds. Exhibitors present a large selection of wines to taste, enjoy and buy.
