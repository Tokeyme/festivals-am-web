Bümplizer-Chilbi

Church / parish fest · Bern, Bümpliz · 14–16 August 2026

When14–16 August 2026
WhereBümpliz, Bümpliz, 3018 Bern
TypeChurch / parish fest

The Bümplizer-Chilbi is regarded as the largest Chilbi in the canton of Bern and has been organised each year by an association since 1948. Some 35 showmen and market stalls, along with a funfair, provide entertainment. The event supports social projects in the city's District VI.
