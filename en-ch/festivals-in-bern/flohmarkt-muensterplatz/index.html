Flohmarkt Münsterplatz

Flea market · Bern, Altstadt · 19 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When19 September 2026
WhereMünsterplatz, Altstadt, 3011 Bern
TypeFlea market

On the Münsterplatz in Bern's old town, a flea market is held from spring to autumn on the third Saturday of each month. In front of the Berner Münster, second-hand goods and bric-a-brac are on offer.
