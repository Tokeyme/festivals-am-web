Lorrainechilbi

Neighbourhood festival · Bern, Lorraine · 15 August 2026

When15 August 2026
WhereLorrainestrasse, Lorraine, 3013 Bern
TypeNeighbourhood festival

The Lorrainechilbi is a district festival in Bern's Lorraine quarter. Around the Lorrainestrasse and the small park there are concerts and culture, a market and flea market, disco and dancing, as well as food and drink.
