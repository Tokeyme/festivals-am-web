Neubrückstrassenfest

Street festival · Bern, Länggasse · 29 August 2026

When29 August 2026
WhereNeubrückstrasse, Länggasse, 3012 Bern
TypeStreet festival

The Neubrückstrassenfest takes place along Neubrückstrasse in Bern's Länggasse district, which is closed to traffic for the occasion. In the afternoon there is a street festival with street performers, magicians and music. In the evening a shared Tavolata with live music follows.
