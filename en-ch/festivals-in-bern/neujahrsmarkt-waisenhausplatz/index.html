Neujahrsmarkt Waisenhausplatz

Market · Bern, Altstadt · 4–9 January 2027

When4–9 January 2027
WhereWaisenhausplatz, Altstadt, 3011 Bern
TypeMarket

The New Year market on the Waisenhausplatz is held in early January, following the Christmas and year-end market. On this central square in Bern's old town, market stalls offer their goods.
