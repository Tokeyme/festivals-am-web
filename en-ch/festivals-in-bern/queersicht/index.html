Queersicht

Culture festival · Bern · 5–11 November 2026

When5–11 November 2026
WhereKinos in der Stadt Bern, 3011 Bern
TypeCulture festival

Queersicht is Bern's LGBTIAQ* film festival. Over several days, feature films and documentaries on queer themes are shown in cinemas around Bern. The coming edition is the 30th.
