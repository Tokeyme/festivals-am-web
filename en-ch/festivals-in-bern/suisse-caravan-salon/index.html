Suisse Caravan Salon

Event · Bern, Wankdorf · 22–26 October 2026

When22–26 October 2026
WhereBERNEXPO, Mingerstrasse 6, Wankdorf, 3014 Bern
TypeEvent

The Suisse Caravan Salon is the largest camping and caravanning fair in Switzerland and is held on the BERNEXPO grounds. Around 350 exhibitors present new products and trends relating to caravans, motorhomes and camping accessories.
