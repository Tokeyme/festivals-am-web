Untermattfest

Neighbourhood festival · Bern, Bethlehem · 29 August 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When29 August 2026
WhereGalenicaplatz, Untermatt, Bethlehem, 3027 Bern
TypeNeighbourhood festival

The Untermattfest is a neighbourhood festival in the Untermatt district of Bern-Bümpliz. The Galenica car park is transformed into a bazaar with numerous stalls and small stages.
