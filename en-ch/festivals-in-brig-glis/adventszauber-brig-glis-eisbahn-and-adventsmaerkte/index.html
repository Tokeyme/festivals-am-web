Adventszauber Brig-Glis (Eisbahn & Adventsmärkte)

Christmas market · Brig-Glis, Brig · 28 November 2026 – 4 January 2027

When28 November 2026 – 4 January 2027
WhereVerschiedene Orte in Brig-Glis (Eisbahn St. Sebastian, Marktplätze), 3900 Brig-Glis
TypeChristmas market

Wintry Advent celebration with an ice rink, market stalls (drinks, delicacies) and an artificial rink using synthetic ice. Open Tue-Sun 13:00-19:00.
