Francomanias Festival

Music festival · Bulle, Centre · 26–29 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When26–29 August 2026
WhereEbullition (ancienne cour du château), Bulle, Centre, 1630 Bulle
TypeMusic festival

One of the largest music festivals in French-speaking Switzerland since 1990. Four days of international and Swiss artists, free of charge. In 2026 mainly at the Ebullition cultural center rather than the castle.
