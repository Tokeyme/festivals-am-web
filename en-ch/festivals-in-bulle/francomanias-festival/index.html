Francomanias Festival

Music festival · Bulle, Centre · 26–29 August 2026

When26–29 August 2026
WhereEbullition (ancienne cour du château), Bulle, Centre, 1630 Bulle
TypeMusic festival

One of the largest music festivals in French-speaking Switzerland since 1990. Four days of international and Swiss artists, free of charge. In 2026 mainly at the Ebullition cultural centre rather than the castle.
