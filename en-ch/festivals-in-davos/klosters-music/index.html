Klosters Music

Music festival · Davos, Klosters · 31 July – 9 August 2026

When31 July – 9 August 2026
WhereLandstrasse 177, Klosters, Klosters, 7250 Davos
TypeMusic festival

8th edition with the motto 'Icons.' Ten days of chamber music festival with German Chamber Philharmonic Bremen, La Cetra Baroque Orchestra Basel, and Camerata Salzburg. Artists: Bryn Terfel, András Schiff, Miloš, Maurice Steger.
