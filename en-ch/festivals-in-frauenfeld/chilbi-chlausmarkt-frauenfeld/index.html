Chilbi Chlausmarkt Frauenfeld

Funfair · Frauenfeld · 5–7 December 2026

When5–7 December 2026
WhereMarktplatz Frauenfeld, 8500 Frauenfeld
TypeFunfair

A tradition featuring the Chilbi fairground on the Marktplatz from Saturday to Monday. On Sunday 6 December at 14:00 the Chlaus procession leaves from the Soldatendenkmal and moves through the old town; the traditional Chlausmarkt takes place on Monday 7 December.
