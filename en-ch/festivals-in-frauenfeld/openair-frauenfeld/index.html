Openair Frauenfeld

Music festival · Frauenfeld · 9–11 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When9–11 July 2026
WhereGrosse Allmend, Waffenplatzstrasse, 8500 Frauenfeld
TypeMusic festival

Europe's largest hip-hop festival, with over 40 confirmed acts such as Ken Carson, Yeat, Don Toliver and Gunna. Featuring a Clubville section across several stages (Arena Stage, Park Stage, Red Bull Homebase, Heineken Rooftop, Coca-Cola Skyclub).
