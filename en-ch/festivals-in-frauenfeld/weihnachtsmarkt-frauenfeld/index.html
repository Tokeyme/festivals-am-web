Weihnachtsmarkt Frauenfeld

Christmas market · Frauenfeld · 18–20 December 2026

When18–20 December 2026
WhereFrauenfeld Altstadt/Innenstadt, 8500 Frauenfeld
TypeChristmas market

33rd edition of the region's largest Weihnachtsmarkt, with around 160 exhibitors and around 35,000 visitors. Over 200 specialist shops and shopping centres in the town centre, with a camel caravan as a special highlight.
