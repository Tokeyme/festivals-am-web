20 Heures de Musiques de Romont

Music festival · Fribourg, Romont · 19–20 September 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When19–20 September 2026
WhereRomont Old Town and Plateau d'Arruffens, Romont, 1680 Fribourg
TypeMusic festival

For 20 hours non-stop, Romont hosts over 45 concerts of 45 minutes each in concert halls spread across the old town and the Plateau d'Arruffens, spanning every music style. Children's workshops and shows are offered separately.
