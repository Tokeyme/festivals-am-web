Estivale Open Air

Music festival · Fribourg, Estavayer-le-Lac · 29 July – 1 August 2026

When29 July – 1 August 2026
WherePlace Nova Friburgo peninsula, Estavayer-le-Lac, 1400 Fribourg
TypeMusic festival

The 34th edition of this major music festival on the shores of Lake Neuchâtel, with a diverse line-up. Daily opening at 4 PM; 100% cashless payment by card or wristband. Artists include Bob Sinclar, Youssoupha, GIMS and Christophe Maé.
