Morat-Fribourg Race (92nd edition)

Sports event · Fribourg, Murten/Fribourg · 2–4 October 2026

When2–4 October 2026
WhereMurten/Morat to Fribourg, Murten/Fribourg, 1700 Fribourg
TypeSports event

A classic long-distance running race from Murten to Fribourg (17.17 km main distance), with additional courses including the Trail Moitié-Moitié, the Mini Morat-Fribourg and family runs. It opens on the Friday evening with the After Work Run.
