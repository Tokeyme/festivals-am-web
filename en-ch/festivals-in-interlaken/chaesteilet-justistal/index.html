Chästeilet Justistal

Funfair · Interlaken, Sigriswil · 18 September 2026

When18 September 2026
WhereSpycherberg, Justistal, Sigriswil, 3800 Interlaken
TypeFunfair

Traditional cheese distribution ceremony dating back almost 300 years. Alpine farmers distribute cheese according to ancient tradition. Alpine festivities with local musicians, Swiss accordions (Schwyzerörgeli) and yodeling performances.
