Inferno Triathlon

Sports event · Interlaken, Interlaken · 14–16 August 2026

When14–16 August 2026
WhereThun to Schilthorn, Alpine Region, Interlaken, 3800 Interlaken
TypeSports event

One of the toughest triathlons in the world with 5,500 m elevation gain. The Inferno Triathlon combines swimming (3.1 km), road cycling (97 km), mountain biking (30 km) and fell running (25 km) to Schilthorn.
