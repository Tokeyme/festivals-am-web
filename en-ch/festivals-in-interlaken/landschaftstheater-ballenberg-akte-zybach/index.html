Landschaftstheater Ballenberg - Akte Zybach

Culture festival · Interlaken, Hofstetten · 1 July – 15 August 2026

When1 July – 15 August 2026
WhereBallenberg Freilichtmuseum, Hofstetten, Hofstetten, 3858 Interlaken
TypeCulture festival

Open-air theater production 'Akte Zybach' at Ballenberg open-air museum focusing on Alpine history. The play takes place in the Grimsel region around innkeeper Peter Zybach with regional actors.
