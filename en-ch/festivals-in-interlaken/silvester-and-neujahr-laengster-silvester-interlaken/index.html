Silvester & Neujahr - Längster Silvester Interlaken

Funfair · Interlaken, Interlaken · 31 December 2026 – 2 January 2027

When31 December 2026 – 2 January 2027
WhereVarious locations, Höhematte Park, Interlaken, 3800 Interlaken
TypeFunfair

Switzerland's longest New Year's Eve celebration spanning 3 days (Dec 31 - Jan 2) with the 'Touch the Mountains' Festival on January 1st, New Year's swim in Lake Thun and the traditional Harder-Potschete with wooden mask parade.
