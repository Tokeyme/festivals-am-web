Thun Water Magic - Wasserzauber

Culture festival · Interlaken, Thun · 25 September – 25 October 2026

When25 September – 25 October 2026
WhereHotel Aare Thun, River Aare Front, Thun, 3600 Interlaken
TypeCulture festival

Spectacular light, water and music show on the Aare with water fountains reaching up to 30 m in height. Two shows daily (7:30 pm and 9:15 pm) with regional specialties at food and drink stalls. Free admission.
