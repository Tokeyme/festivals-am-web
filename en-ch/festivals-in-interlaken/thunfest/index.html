Thunfest

Town festival · Interlaken, Thun · 6–9 August 2026

When6–9 August 2026
WhereThun City Center, Rathausplatz, Thun, 3600 Interlaken
TypeTown festival

50th edition of Thunfest, Switzerland's largest city festival with live music, culinary delicacies and refreshing drinks. Around 25,000 visitors celebrate this grand festival under the motto 'Together 250 Years'.
