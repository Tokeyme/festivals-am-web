William Tell Open-Air Theatre Interlaken

Culture festival · Interlaken, Matten · 25 July – 5 September 2026

When25 July – 5 September 2026
WhereRugenwald, Matten bei Interlaken, Matten, 3800 Interlaken
TypeCulture festival

Open-air theater tradition since 1912 with over 140 actors from the region, horses, and animals on a natural open-air stage. The play 'Tell unser Held' recreates the legendary apple-shooting scene against an Alpine backdrop.
