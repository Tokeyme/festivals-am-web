Weihnachtsmarkt im Schloss Köniz

Christmas market · Köniz, Köniz · 12–13 December 2026

When12–13 December 2026
WhereKulturhof – Schloss Köniz, Muhlernstrasse 11, Köniz, 3098 Köniz
TypeChristmas market

Weihnachtsmarkt with arts and crafts, an Advent atmosphere and handmade work. Saturday 10:00-18:00, Sunday 10:00-17:00. With performances by the Musikschule Köniz, Samichlaus, a carousel and gingerbread decorations.
