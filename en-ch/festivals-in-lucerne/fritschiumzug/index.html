Fritschiumzug

Carnival · Lucerne, Altstadt · 4 February 2027

When4 February 2027
WhereKapellplatz und Altstadt, 6004 Lucerne
TypeCarnival

The Fritschiumzug is the first big parade on Schmutziger Donnerstag (Dirty Thursday) and starts at 2 pm. Organized by the Zunft zu Safran guild, it passes above all across the Kapellplatz in honor of Bruder Fritschi, where the Fritschibrunnen fountain is circled three times. The route runs via Luzernerhof, Schweizerhofquai, Schwanenplatz, Kapellplatz, Seebrücke and Bahnhofplatz.
