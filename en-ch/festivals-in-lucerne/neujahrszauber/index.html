Neujahrszauber

Funfair · Lucerne · 1 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When1 January 2027
WhereSeebecken / Seebrücke, 6004 Lucerne
TypeFunfair

The Neujahrszauber lights up the Lucerne sky over the lake basin with fireworks and marks the festive start to the new year. The best view is from the Seebrücke and along the lakeside promenade. The event is backed by the city of Lucerne, local hotels and the tourism organisation.
