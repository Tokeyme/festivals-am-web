Seeüberquerung Luzern

Sports event · Lucerne, Tribschen · 16 August 2026

When16 August 2026
WhereStrandbad Lido bis Tribschen, Tribschen, 6006 Lucerne
TypeSports event

With around 600 participants, the Seeüberquerung Luzern (lake crossing) is one of the largest and most popular amateur sporting events on Lake Lucerne (Vierwaldstättersee). The swimming course covers around 1.1 kilometres from the Lido lakeside bathing area to Tribschen.
