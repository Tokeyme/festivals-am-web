SwissCityMarathon Luzern

Sports event · Lucerne · 25 October 2026

When25 October 2026
WhereVerkehrshaus / Seeuferweg, 6006 Lucerne
TypeSports event

The SwissCityMarathon Luzern is run over the distances of 10 km, half marathon and marathon. The start and finish are at the Verkehrshaus, and the course runs along Lake Lucerne, across the Horw peninsula, through the football stadium, past the KKL and through the old town. Some 13,000 participants take part.
