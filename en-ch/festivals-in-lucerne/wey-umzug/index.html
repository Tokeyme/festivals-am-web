Wey-Umzug

Carnival · Lucerne, Altstadt · 8 February 2027

When8 February 2027
WhereAltstadt und Neustadt, Altstadt, 6004 Lucerne
TypeCarnival

The Wey-Umzug on Güdismontag (Shrove Monday) is the second large parade of the Lucerne Fasnacht and starts at 2 pm. Led by the Wey-Frosch, the frog that serves as the symbol of the Wey guild, it makes its way through the city centre. The route runs via Luzernerhof, Schweizerhofquai, Schwanenplatz, Seebrücke, Bahnhofplatz, Pilatusstrasse and Viktoriaplatz.
