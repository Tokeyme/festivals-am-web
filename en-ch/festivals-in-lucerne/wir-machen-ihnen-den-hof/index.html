Wir machen Ihnen den Hof

Christmas market · Lucerne · 11 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When11 December 2026
WhereHofkirche St. Leodegar, 6006 Lucerne
TypeChristmas market

The Quartier am Hof neighbourhood holds a small Christmas market at the foot of Lucerne's Hofkirche. The market stalls offer handicrafts, art, culinary specialities and gift items, with the involvement of local businesses and the parish of St. Leodegar. The programme includes fairy-tale storytelling for children and adults, as well as a nostalgic carousel.
