Festa d'Autunno

Funfair · Lugano, Lugano Centro · 2–4 October 2026

When2–4 October 2026
WhereCentro storico di Lugano (Via Nassa, piazze, ristoranti), Lugano Centro, 6900 Lugano
TypeFunfair

Three-day autumn festival featuring local gastronomic specialties, wine tastings of Ticino wines, and natural products. Grottini (temporary eating areas) in the historic center with traditional dishes.
