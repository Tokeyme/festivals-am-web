Mercatini di Natale di Lugano

Christmas market · Lugano, Lugano Centro · 26 November 2026 – 6 January 2027

When26 November 2026 – 6 January 2027
WhereVia Nassa, Piazza Dante, Piazza Manzoni, Piazza della Riforma, Parco Ciani, Lugano, Lugano Centro, 6900 Lugano
TypeChristmas market

Christmas market with over 60 artisan stands, elegant decorations, and local specialties. Gastronomic huts as gathering points, ice skating rink in Parco Ciani, Christmas tree on Piazza della Riforma, live music, and DJ sets.
