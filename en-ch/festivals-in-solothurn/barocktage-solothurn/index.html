Barocktage Solothurn

Culture festival · Solothurn, verschiedene · 8–16 August 2026

When8–16 August 2026
WhereSolothurn Stadt und Umgebung, verschiedene, 4500 Solothurn
TypeCulture festival

Nine-day festival celebrating Baroque culture, with guided tours through magnificent Baroque buildings, concerts, theatre performances, a Baroque parade, a market stroll and culinary specialities. Participants in Baroque costume make their way through the old town.
