Chlausemäret Solothurn

Christmas market · Solothurn, Altstadt · 2–3 December 2026

When2–3 December 2026
WhereFriedhofplatz, Altstadt, 4500 Solothurn
TypeChristmas market

Legendary Nikolausmarkt held since 1981 with over 70 stalls (tree decorations, Glühwein, wooden decorations, Lebkuchen, nativity scenes). Samichlaus wanders through the market stalls and delights children and adults alike.
