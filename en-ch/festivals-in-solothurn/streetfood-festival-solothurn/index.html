Streetfood Festival Solothurn

Funfair · Solothurn, Vorstadt · 28–30 August 2026

When28–30 August 2026
WhereLeporellobrücke (neben Kulturfabrik Kofmehl), Vorstadt, 4500 Solothurn
TypeFunfair

The 10th Streetfood Festival with international food stalls serving world cuisine on the Aarepromenade. Free admission, a children's area with activities and a bouncy castle, live music and covered areas.
