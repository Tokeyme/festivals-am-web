Thuner Wasserzauber

Culture festival · Thun · 25 September – 25 October 2026

When25 September – 25 October 2026
WhereAarebecken vor Restaurant Freienhof, Thun Altstadt, 3600 Thun
TypeCulture festival

A daily water, light and laser show (daily at 19:30 and 21:15) with water fountains up to 30m high in the Aare basin. Free admission.
