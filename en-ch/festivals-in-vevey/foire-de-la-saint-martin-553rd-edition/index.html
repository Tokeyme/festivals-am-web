Foire de la Saint-Martin (553rd edition)

Funfair · Vevey, Vevey · 30 October – 1 November 2026

When30 October – 1 November 2026
WherePlace du Marché, Vevey, Vevey, 1800 Vevey
TypeFunfair

A traditional Saint Martin's fair over 3 days, with a festive parade, local specialities (rum cake), a cattle vigil on the Friday and a 'Brunch du Terroir' on the Sunday. An important regional autumn festival.
