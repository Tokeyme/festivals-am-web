Winterthurer Weihnachtsmarkt (Altstadt)

Christmas market · Winterthur, Altstadt · 26 November – 23 December 2026

When26 November – 23 December 2026
WhereNeumarkt / Altstadt, Winterthur, Altstadt, 8400 Winterthur
TypeChristmas market

The 29th edition of the Winterthur Christmas market, with a daily programme: face painting, storytelling, gingerbread decorating and musical performances. A highlight is the enchanted fairy-tale forest with animated scenes; free admission.
