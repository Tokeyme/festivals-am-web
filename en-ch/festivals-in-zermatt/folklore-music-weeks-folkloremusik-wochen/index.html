Folklore Music Weeks (Folkloremusik-Wochen)

Culture festival · Zermatt, Zermatt · 27 June – 10 October 2026

When27 June – 10 October 2026
WhereKirchplatz (Kirchenplatz), Zermatt, Zermatt, 3920 Zermatt
TypeCulture festival

Daily folklore performances with alternating traditional costume groups, alphorn players and folk dance groups. Performances daily at 4 pm in the church square, spontaneous performances throughout the village and mountains.
