Circus Conelli

Event · Zurich · 21 November – 31 December 2026

When21 November – 31 December 2026
WhereBauschänzli, Stadthausquai, 8001 Zurich
TypeEvent

The Conelli Christmas circus performs every year during Advent on the Bauschänzli. The programme combines acrobatics, poetry, clowning and music. The line-up also includes a dinner spectacle with a multi-course menu.
