Dörflifäscht

Street festival · Zurich, Niederdorf · 28–30 August 2026

When28–30 August 2026
WhereNiederdorf (zwischen Central und Bellevue), Niederdorf, 8001 Zurich
TypeStreet festival

The Dörflifäscht is the street festival in Zürich's Niederdorf between Central and Bellevue. During the day there is a programme for families, and in the evening live concerts and a festive atmosphere. Street food and international cuisine are on offer.
