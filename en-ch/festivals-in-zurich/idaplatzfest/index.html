Idaplatzfest

Neighbourhood festival · Zurich, Wiedikon · 15 August 2026

When15 August 2026
WhereIdaplatz, Wiedikon, 8003 Zurich
TypeNeighbourhood festival

The Idaplatzfest is a neighbourhood festival in Zurich's District 3, held on Idaplatz in Wiedikon. In 2026 it is deliberately being kept smaller and run only during the day, in order to preserve its neighbourhood character. It begins with a brunch and offers music and food.
