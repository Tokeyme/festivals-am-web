Lethargy Festival

Music festival · Zurich, Wollishofen · 7–9 August 2026

When7–9 August 2026
WhereRote Fabrik, Seestrasse 395, Wollishofen, 8038 Zurich
TypeMusic festival

The Lethargy Festival is a festival of electronic music and visual art at the Rote Fabrik on Lake Zurich. Concerts and parties take place over several days. It is regarded as one of the artistically most relevant electronic festivals in Switzerland.
