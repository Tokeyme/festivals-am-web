Lindenplatzfest

Neighbourhood festival · Zurich, Altstetten · 19 September 2026

When19 September 2026
WhereLindenplatz, Altstetten, 8048 Zurich
TypeNeighbourhood festival

The Lindenplatzfest is the neighbourhood festival of Zurich-Altstetten, held on Lindenplatz and the adjoining Chilehügel. It offers music, food and drink as well as a family programme. It sees itself as a festival for young and old.
