Radar - Festival for New Music

Music festival · Zurich, Aussersihl · 12 September 2026

When12 September 2026
Whererund um die Langstrasse, Aussersihl, 8004 Zurich
TypeMusic festival

Radar is a one-day music festival around the Langstrasse in Zürich's Kreis 4. Promising Swiss and international newcomers from various genres are presented at several venues. The festival hub is the 25hours Hotel Langstrasse.
