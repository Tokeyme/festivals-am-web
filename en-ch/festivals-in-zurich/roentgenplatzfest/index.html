Röntgenplatzfest

Neighbourhood festival · Zurich, Industriequartier · 21–22 August 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When21–22 August 2026
WhereRöntgenplatz, Röntgenstrasse 56, Industriequartier, 8005 Zurich
TypeNeighbourhood festival

The Röntgenplatzfest is a neighbourhood festival in Zurich's District 5, held on Röntgenplatz, and has existed since 1980. It offers music, talks, a children's programme and food in the Industriequartier district.
