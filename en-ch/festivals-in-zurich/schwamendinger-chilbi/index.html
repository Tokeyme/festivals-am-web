Schwamendinger Chilbi

Church / parish fest · Zurich, Schwamendingen · 5–6 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When5–6 September 2026
WhereSchwamendingerplatz, Schwamendingen, 8051 Zurich
TypeChurch / parish fest

The Schwamendinger Chilbi is the traditional district fair of Zurich-Schwamendingen and has existed since the 1970s. It is organised by the Quartierverein Schwamendingen (district association) and offers a festival for the whole family.
