Silvesterzauber

Funfair · Zurich · 31 December 2026

When31 December 2026
WhereSeebecken / Bürkliplatz, 8001 Zurich
TypeFunfair

The Silvesterzauber is the public New Year's Eve celebration at Zurich's lake basin around Bürkliplatz, Schifflände and General-Guisan-Quai. The programme features music, food stalls and bar areas, with free admission. At midnight a large firework display is set off over the lake.
