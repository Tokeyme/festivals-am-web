Weltklasse Zürich

Sports event · Zurich · 26–27 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When26–27 August 2026
WhereStadion Letzigrund, Baslerstrasse 44, 8048 Zurich
TypeSports event

Weltklasse Zürich is the athletics meeting of the Wanda Diamond League at the Letzigrund stadium. National and international top athletes compete across numerous disciplines. Part of the programme, such as the pole vault, takes place beforehand in the middle of the city.
