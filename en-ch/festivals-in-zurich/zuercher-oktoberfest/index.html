Zürcher Oktoberfest

Funfair · Zurich · 8 October – 7 November 2026

When8 October – 7 November 2026
WhereBauschänzli, Stadthausquai 2, 8001 Zurich
TypeFunfair

The Zürcher Oktoberfest transforms the Bauschänzli, an islet in the middle of the Limmat, into a Bavarian marquee decked out in white and blue. On offer are live music, speciality beers and traditional dishes. The heated marquee has room for around 800 guests.
