Züri-Wiesn

Funfair · Zurich · 24 September – 17 October 2026

When24 September – 17 October 2026
WhereHauptbahnhof Zürich, Bahnhofplatz 15, 8001 Zurich
TypeFunfair

The Züri-Wiesn is the Oktoberfest held in Zurich's main railway station. In the festively decorated tent, guests celebrate with hearty live music and Bavarian specialities. It traditionally opens with the call «O'zapft is!».
