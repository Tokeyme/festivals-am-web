ZüriCarneval

Carnival · Zurich, Altstadt · 12–14 February 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12–14 February 2027
WhereInnenstadt / Niederdorf, Altstadt, 8001 Zurich
TypeCarnival

The ZüriCarneval is the Fasnacht of the city of Zurich, with a large parade through the city centre. In the old town, Guggenmusiken provide the atmosphere in heated tents. Zurich traditionally celebrates its Fasnacht on the weekend after Ash Wednesday.
