Zürich liest

Culture festival · Zurich · 20–25 October 2026

When20–25 October 2026
Whereverschiedene Orte, 8001 Zurich
TypeCulture festival

Zürich liest is the book and literature festival of the city of Zürich. More than 200 events featuring authors, readings and discussions take place at over 100 locations. The programme is aimed at all age groups.
