Altstadtflohmarkt Aachen

Flea market · Aachen · 18 October 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When18 October 2026
WhereKatschhof, 52062 Aachen, 52062 Aachen
TypeFlea market
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

Four times a year, the historic setting between the cathedral and the town hall makes room for the Altstadtflohmarkt (old town flea market). On the Katschhof square, antiques and secondhand goods are traded. Pavilions and tents are not permitted.
