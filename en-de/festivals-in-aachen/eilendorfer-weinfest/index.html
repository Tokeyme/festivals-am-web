Eilendorfer Weinfest

Wine festival · Aachen, Eilendorf · 18–19 September 2026

When18–19 September 2026
WhereSeverinusplatz, 52080 Aachen, Eilendorf, 52080 Aachen
TypeWine festival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

In early autumn, the Eilendorf district holds a small wine festival on Severinusplatz. Winegrowers and traders offer wines, along with cocktails, soft drinks and various dishes. Some of the seating is set up under tents. The event is organised by the Bezirksamt Eilendorf (district office) and the Interessengemeinschaft Eilendorfer Vereine (association of local clubs).
