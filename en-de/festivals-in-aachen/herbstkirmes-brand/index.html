Herbstkirmes Brand

Funfair · Aachen, Brand · 23–26 October 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When23–26 October 2026
WhereMarktplatz, 52078 Aachen, Brand, 52078 Aachen
TypeFunfair
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

In autumn, the Aachen district of Brand celebrates its traditional Herbstkirmes (autumn funfair) on the marketplace. Rides such as the dodgems and a nostalgic children's carousel, together with stalls selling food and drink, welcome young and old.
