Kinderkostümzug des Märchenprinzen

Carnival · Aachen · 7 February 2027

When7 February 2027
WhereInnenstadt, 52062 Aachen, 52062 Aachen
TypeCarnival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

On Carnival Sunday, the children's costume parade of the Märchenprinz (fairy-tale prince) moves through Aachen's city center. Costumed children, clubs and groups take part. It is the child-friendly parade held ahead of the Rosenmontagszug, the main Rose Monday procession.
