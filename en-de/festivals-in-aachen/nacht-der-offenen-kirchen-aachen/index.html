Nacht der offenen Kirchen Aachen

Culture festival · Aachen · 16 October 2026

When16 October 2026
WhereInnenstadt, 52062 Aachen, 52062 Aachen
TypeCulture festival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

During the Night of Open Churches, numerous Aachen churches open their doors in the evening and at night. Music, readings, films, installations and prayers are on offer. The parishes invite people to special experiences inside the churches.
