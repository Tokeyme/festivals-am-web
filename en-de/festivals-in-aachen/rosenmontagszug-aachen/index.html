Rosenmontagszug Aachen

Carnival · Aachen · 8 February 2027

When8 February 2027
WhereInnenstadt, 52062 Aachen, 52062 Aachen
TypeCarnival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Rosenmontagszug (Rose Monday parade) is the highlight of the Aachen carnival and one of the largest parades in the region. With numerous walking groups, bands and themed floats, it moves through the city centre. Along the route, Kamelle (sweets) are thrown.
