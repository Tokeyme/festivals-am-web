Sessionseröffnung Aachener Karneval

Carnival · Aachen · 11 November 2026

When11 November 2026
WhereHolzgraben, 52062 Aachen, 52062 Aachen
TypeCarnival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

On the eleventh of the eleventh, the Aachen carnival opens its new season. On an open-air stage at the Holzgraben, the Narren (carnival revellers) celebrate the public season launch 'Vür wecke Öcher Jecke'. Street carnival in the city centre begins.
