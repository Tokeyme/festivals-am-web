Weihnachtsmarkt Kornelimünster

Christmas market · Aachen, Kornelimünster · 28–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28–29 November 2026
WhereKunsthaus, 52076 Aachen, Kornelimünster, 52076 Aachen
TypeChristmas market
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

On the first weekend of Advent, the parish together with local clubs hosts a Christmas market in the historic old core of Kornelimünster. In the courtyard of the Kunsthaus there are food offerings and mulled wine as well as small gift and decorative items.
