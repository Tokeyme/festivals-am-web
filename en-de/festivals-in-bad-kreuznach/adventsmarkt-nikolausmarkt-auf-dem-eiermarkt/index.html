Adventsmarkt / Nikolausmarkt auf dem Eiermarkt

Christmas market · Bad Kreuznach · 20 November – 20 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November – 20 December 2026
WhereEiermarkt rund um die Nikolauskirche, 55545 Bad Kreuznach
TypeChristmas market
StateRhineland-Palatinate

Traditional Christmas market in the old town around the Nikolauskirche with numerous sales and snack stalls, handicrafts, a large nativity scene and a children's programme in the Christmas tent. The market remains closed on Totensonntag.
