Bad Kreuznach tanzt

Town festival · Bad Kreuznach · 4–5 June 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When4–5 June 2027
WhereKornmarkt, 55543 Bad Kreuznach
TypeTown festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The dance event takes place over a weekend in early June, on Friday and Saturday, in the centre of Bad Kreuznach, with the Kornmarkt as its venue. It runs at the same time as the wine festival on the Eiermarkt.
