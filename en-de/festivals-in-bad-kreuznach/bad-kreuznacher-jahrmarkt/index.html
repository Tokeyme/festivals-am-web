Bad Kreuznacher Jahrmarkt

Funfair · Bad Kreuznach · 21–25 August 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When21–25 August 2026
WherePfingstwiese an der Nahe, 55545 Bad Kreuznach
TypeFunfair
StateRhineland-Palatinate

One of the largest and oldest folk festivals in Germany, with around 180 fairground rides, a large Naheweinzelt, party tents and a traditional Dippemarkt. It concludes on Tuesday evening with a large aerial fireworks display.
