Fischerstechen auf der Roseninsel

Village & club fest · Bad Kreuznach · 4–6 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When4–6 September 2026
WhereRoseninsel an der Nahe, 55545 Bad Kreuznach
TypeVillage & club fest
StateRhineland-Palatinate

Traditional jousting on flat boats on the Nahe, in which youth and adult teams compete against each other. The competition is accompanied by a lively folk festival with music and culinary offerings, with free admission.
