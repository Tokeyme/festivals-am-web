Kreuznach leuchtet

Event · Bad Kreuznach · 28 November 2026

When28 November 2026
WhereInnenstadt, 55543 Bad Kreuznach
TypeEvent
StateRhineland-Palatinate

"Kreuznach leuchtet" is an illumination event run by the "Wir sind Kreuznach" initiative. It lights up various spots in the town centre and in the spa district. In previous years the programme included a mulled wine trail with winegrowers from the Nahe, music in churches, light installations and activities put on by local shops.
