Oktoberfest mit Live Musik im Oktoberfestzelt

Funfair · Bad Kreuznach · 3 October 2026

When3 October 2026
WhereBrauwerk, Saline Karlshalle 11, 55543 Bad Kreuznach
TypeFunfair
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Brauwerk at the Saline Karlshalle puts on an Oktoberfest with live music in its own marquee on the evening of 3 October. Bands play from 6 p.m. and the evening is scheduled to end at 11.30 p.m. The organiser is Braun & Röth GmbH, which runs the Brauwerk.
