Weihnachtsmarkt Bad Münster am Stein-Ebernburg

Christmas market · Bad Kreuznach, Bad Münster am Stein-Ebernburg · 28 November – 20 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28 November – 20 December 2026
WhereKurpark, Bad Münster am Stein-Ebernburg, 55583 Bad Kreuznach
TypeChristmas market
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Christmas market opens in the Kurpark of Bad Münster am Stein-Ebernburg on all four Advent weekends, on Saturdays and Sundays. Around 100 stalls offer craft work, tree decorations and gifts as well as food such as Flammkuchen, freshly roasted chestnuts and mulled wine. Thousands of lights illuminate the trees in the park and the historic Kurmittelhaus, with the floodlit Rheingrafenstein rock towering above.
