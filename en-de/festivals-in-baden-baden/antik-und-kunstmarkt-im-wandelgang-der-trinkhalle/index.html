Antik- und Kunstmarkt im Wandelgang der Trinkhalle

Market · Baden-Baden · 10–11 October 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When10–11 October 2026
WhereTrinkhalle, Kaiserallee 1B, 76530 Baden-Baden
TypeMarket
StateBaden-Württemberg

In the covered promenade of the listed Trinkhalle (Pump Room) in the Kurpark, high-quality antiques, old and new art, pictures and prints, sculptures, jewellery, furniture, porcelain, glassware as well as coins and stamps are on offer. The market is held between the classical Corinthian columns of the Trinkhalle. Admission is free for visitors.
