Baden-Badener Christkindelsmarkt

Christmas market · Baden-Baden · 26 November 2026 – 6 January 2027

When26 November 2026 – 6 January 2027
WhereKurgarten vor dem Kurhaus / Lichtentaler Allee, 76530 Baden-Baden
TypeChristmas market
StateBaden-Württemberg

Traditional Christmas market in front of the historic Kurhaus with crafts, culinary specialties, fairy tale street, carousel, Ferris wheel, and live programs. Open daily 11 AM to 9 PM (shortened hours on Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve, New Year's Day, and January 6).
