Eisarena Baden-Baden

Event · Baden-Baden · 13 November 2026 – 10 January 2027

When13 November 2026 – 10 January 2027
WhereAugustaplatz, 76530 Baden-Baden
TypeEvent
StateBaden-Württemberg

In winter, a covered ice rink of around 525 square metres is set up on Augustaplatz for skating on real ice. The offering includes skate and skating-aid hire, Eisstockschießen (Bavarian curling) as well as refreshment stands with mulled wine, soups and warm dishes. The Kitzbüheler Hütte serves Alpine specialities such as fondue. The enclosed design and green electricity from hydropower ensure energy-efficient operation.
