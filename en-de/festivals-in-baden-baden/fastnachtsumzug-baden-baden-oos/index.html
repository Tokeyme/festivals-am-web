Fastnachtsumzug Baden-Baden-Oos

Carnival · Baden-Baden, Oos · 9 February 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When9 February 2027
WhereOoser Hauptstraße, Oos, 76532 Baden-Baden
TypeCarnival
StateBaden-Württemberg

On Fastnachtsdienstag (Shrove Tuesday), the large Fastnacht carnival parade passes through the Oos district. The organiser is the Ooser Carneval-Verein 1932; for many decades the parade has ranked among the city's largest Fastnacht events, at times affecting traffic in the district. Numerous groups of Narren (carnival revellers) and spectators take part.
