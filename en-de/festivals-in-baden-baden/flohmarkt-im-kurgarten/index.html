Flohmarkt im Kurgarten

Flea market · Baden-Baden · 15 August 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When15 August 2026
WhereKurgarten, rund um das Kurhaus, 76530 Baden-Baden
TypeFlea market
StateBaden-Württemberg

Around the historic Kurhaus and the arcades in the Kurgarten, one of the best-known flea markets in southern Germany is held on selected Saturdays. Up to 150 exhibitors, mostly from Baden-Baden and the surrounding area, offer second-hand goods and antiques. The market takes place several times a year.
