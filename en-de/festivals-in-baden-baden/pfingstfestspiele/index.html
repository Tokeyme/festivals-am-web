Pfingstfestspiele

Music festival · Baden-Baden · 5–17 May 2027

When5–17 May 2027
WhereBeim Alten Bahnhof 2, 76530 Baden-Baden
TypeMusic festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

The Pfingstfestspiele (Whitsun Festival) is dedicated to the SWR Symphonieorchester. Alongside international soloists, there are curated chamber music programmes, performed in the Festspielhaus and the Trinkhalle, among other venues.
