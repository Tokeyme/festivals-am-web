Steinbacher Moped- und Motorradtreffen

Event · Baden-Baden, Steinbach · 13 September 2026

When13 September 2026
WhereARAL-Tankstelle Karcher, an der B3, Steinbach, Steinbach, 76534 Baden-Baden
TypeEvent
StateBaden-Württemberg

On the grounds of the Karcher ARAL petrol station in the Rebland district of Steinbach, two-wheeler enthusiasts gather for petrolhead talk, making contacts and swapping spare parts. Two-wheelers up to and including the 1980 model year and older are admitted, as are scooters, sidecar combinations and historic bicycles. Around 1,500 visitors attended the last edition; admission and participation are free.
