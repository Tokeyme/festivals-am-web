Steinbacher Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Baden-Baden, Steinbach · 12 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12 December 2026
WhereBürgergarten, Steinbach, Steinbach, 76534 Baden-Baden
TypeChristmas market
StateBaden-Württemberg

A small, family-friendly Christmas market is held in the Bürgergarten park of Steinbach, a district in the Rebland wine region. Local clubs provide a festive atmosphere, food and entertainment. The market is a firmly established tradition on the Saturday before the third Sunday of Advent.
