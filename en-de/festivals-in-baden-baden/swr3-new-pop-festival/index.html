SWR3 New Pop Festival

Music festival · Baden-Baden · 17–19 September 2026

When17–19 September 2026
WhereKaiserallee 1, 76530 Baden-Baden
TypeMusic festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

The festival presents up-and-coming national and international artists who are tipped for a great career. The concerts are spread across the Kurhaus, the Festspielhaus and the Theater Baden-Baden. There is also a free supporting programme with performances at the Konzertmuschel bandstand, interviews and an illuminated red carpet running through the town centre. All concerts are additionally relayed within the town and shown by live stream.
