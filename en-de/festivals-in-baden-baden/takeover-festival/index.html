Takeover Festival

Culture festival · Baden-Baden · 29 January – 7 February 2027

When29 January – 7 February 2027
WhereBeim Alten Bahnhof 2, 76530 Baden-Baden
TypeCulture festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

At the Takeover Festival, young artists take over the Festspielhaus Baden-Baden. The programme ranges from concerts and dance to talks and workshops, and some events are free of charge.
