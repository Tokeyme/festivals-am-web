The World of John Neumeier

Culture festival · Baden-Baden · 23 September – 3 October 2027

When23 September – 3 October 2027
WhereBeim Alten Bahnhof 2, 76530 Baden-Baden
TypeCulture festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

The dance festival at the Festspielhaus has been curated by John Neumeier since 2021. Besides the Hamburg Ballett and the Bundesjugendballett, further companies appear as guests every year.
