Weihnachtsmarkt im Klosterhof in Lichtental

Christmas market · Baden-Baden, Lichtental · 4–6 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When4–6 December 2026
WhereKlosterhof, Kloster Lichtenthal, Hauptstraße 40, Lichtental, 76534 Baden-Baden
TypeChristmas market
StateBaden-Württemberg

In the courtyard of Lichtenthal Abbey, the non-profit association Baden-Lichtental hosts a small, romantic Christmas market. Only non-profit stallholders and the local associations of Lichtental create a family atmosphere, with food such as Bratwurst, Merguez and Flammkuchen as well as Glühwein (mulled wine). The proceeds go to charitable causes.
