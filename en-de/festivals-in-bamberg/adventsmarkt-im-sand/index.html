Adventsmarkt im Sand

Christmas market · Bamberg · 27–29 November 2026

When27–29 November 2026
WhereElisabethenplatz / Obere Sandstraße, Bamberg, 96049 Bamberg
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

Enchanting advent market around the medieval Elisabethenkirche in the Sand district, distinguished by high-quality handicrafts and craft demonstrations rather than mass-produced goods. Traditionally held on the first advent weekend.
