Bamberger Sandkerwa

Funfair · Bamberg · 20–24 August 2026

When20–24 August 2026
WhereSandstraße / Klein Venedig, Bamberg, 96049 Bamberg
TypeFunfair
StateBavaria

One of Bavaria's largest and most famous folk festivals, originating from the church dedication festival of the Sand church St. Elisabeth. Celebrated in the narrow lanes of the Sand district with Franconian specialties and the traditional Fischerstechen. The 76th Sandkerwa takes place Thursday through Monday.
