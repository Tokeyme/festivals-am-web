Bamberger Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Bamberg · 24 November – 23 December 2026

When24 November – 23 December 2026
WhereMaximiliansplatz / Grüner Markt / Obstmarkt, Bamberg, 96047 Bamberg
TypeChristmas market
StateBayern

The city's main Christmas market stretches from the pedestrian zone at Maximiliansplatz through Grüner Markt to Obstmarkt. Traditional wooden stalls offer handicrafts, Christmas goods, and food and beverages. Opening hours: Friday/Saturday until 9 pm, Sunday from 11 am.
