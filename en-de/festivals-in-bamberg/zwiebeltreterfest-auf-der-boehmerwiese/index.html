Zwiebeltreterfest auf der Böhmerwiese

Neighbourhood festival · Bamberg · 10–13 September 2026

When10–13 September 2026
WhereBöhmerwiese, Gärtnerstadt Bamberg, 96047 Bamberg
TypeNeighbourhood festival
StateBayern

Four-day festival in Bamberg's historic gardener district featuring regional and seasonal food, drinks, and free live music under the open sky. It celebrates the traditional gardening culture of the UNESCO World Heritage site.
