Almabtrieb Da Summa is umma

Historical festival · Berchtesgaden · 26 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When26 September 2026
WhereBerchtesgaden und Umgebung, 83471 Berchtesgaden
TypeHistorical festival
StateBavaria

When the cattle return from the alpine pastures to the valley in autumn, Berchtesgaden celebrates the Almabtrieb Da Summa is umma. If the alpine summer has gone well, the animals are elaborately decorated; the exact date depends on the weather.
