Berchtesgadener Weihnachtsschützen

Historical festival · Berchtesgaden · 17–24 December 2026

When17–24 December 2026
WhereOrtskern und umliegende Anhöhen, 83471 Berchtesgaden
TypeHistorical festival
StateBavaria

Around a week before Christmas, the Berchtesgadener Weihnachtsschützen welcome the Christ Child with hand-held gun salutes. The custom is part of Bavaria's intangible cultural heritage; the highlight is the ceremonial firing on Christmas Eve.
