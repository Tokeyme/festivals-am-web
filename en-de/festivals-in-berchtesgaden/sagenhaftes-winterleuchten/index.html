Sagenhaftes Winterleuchten

Event · Berchtesgaden · 5 February 2027

When5 February 2027
WhereWeihnachtsschützenplatz, 83471 Berchtesgaden
TypeEvent
StateBavaria

The Winterleuchten (winter lights) turns the historic centre of Berchtesgaden into an illuminated festive mile for one evening. Façades are lit up, and light installations and stilt walkers line streets and squares. Restaurateurs and shops provide street food and extended opening hours for refreshments and shopping. The organiser is the association Aktive Unternehmen Berchtesgaden.
