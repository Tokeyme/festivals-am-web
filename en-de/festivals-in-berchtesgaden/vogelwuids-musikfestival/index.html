Vogelwuids Musikfestival

Music festival · Berchtesgaden · 1 August 2026

When1 August 2026
WhereHistorischer Ortskern, 83471 Berchtesgaden
TypeMusic festival
StateBavaria

At the Vogelwuids street music festival, around 20 acts spanning folk music, indie, rock, pop, jazz and classical play on several stages in the historic center of Berchtesgaden. The backyard concerts with their special atmosphere are popular, accompanied by regional street food. In 2026, the Vogelwuide Kids-Festival premieres with its own children's program.
