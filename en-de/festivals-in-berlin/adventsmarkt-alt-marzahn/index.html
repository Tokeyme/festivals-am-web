Adventsmarkt Alt-Marzahn

Christmas market · Berlin, Marzahn · 29 November 2026

When29 November 2026
WhereAlt Marzahn 63, Dorfanger, 12685 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On the village green of Alt-Marzahn around the church and near the Bockwindmill, a small, family-friendly Advent market has taken place for over twenty years. With mulled wine, fire bowls, shadow play, and craft activities for children.
