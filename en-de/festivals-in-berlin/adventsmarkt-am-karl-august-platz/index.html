Adventsmarkt am Karl-August-Platz

Christmas market · Berlin, Charlottenburg · 29 November – 13 December 2026

When29 November – 13 December 2026
WhereKarl-August-Platz, Charlottenburg, 10625 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On the first Advent Sundays, Karl-August-Platz invites visitors to a small artisan market. Here visitors find unusual and individual gifts and a cozy Advent atmosphere in the neighborhood.
