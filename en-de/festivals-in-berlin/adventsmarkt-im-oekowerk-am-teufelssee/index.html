Adventsmarkt im Ökowerk am Teufelssee

Christmas market · Berlin, Grunewald · 6 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When6 December 2026
WhereTeufelsseechaussee 22, Grunewald, 14193 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

The Ökowerk nature conservation center on the shores of Teufelssee invites visitors to a small, sustainably-oriented Advent market. The stands offer gift ideas made from natural materials, organic and fair-trade treats, plus hands-on activities for children and craft enthusiasts.
