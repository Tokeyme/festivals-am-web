Adventsmarkt in Alt-Buckow

Christmas market · Berlin, Buckow · 28–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28–29 November 2026
WhereGutshof Alt-Buckow, Buckow, 12349 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

The over-30-year-old Advent market at the Buckow manor traditionally opens the Christmas season on the first Advent weekend in the village center. Homemade goods, regional delicacies, and contemplative mood in historic ambiance offer a peaceful festival away from the hustle.
