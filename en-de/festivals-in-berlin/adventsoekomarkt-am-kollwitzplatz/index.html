Adventsökomarkt am Kollwitzplatz

Christmas market · Berlin, Prenzlauer Berg · 29 November – 20 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When29 November – 20 December 2026
WhereKollwitzplatz, Prenzlauer Berg, 10435 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On Advent Sundays, Kollwitzplatz transforms into a fair and sustainable Christmas market. Over sixty exhibitors offer ecologically and socially responsible toys, textiles, arts and crafts, and delicacies from around the world.
