Alt-Marzahner Erntefest mit Umweltfest

Historical festival · Berlin, Marzahn · 11–13 September 2026

When11–13 September 2026
WhereDorf Alt-Marzahn, Marzahn, 12685 Berlin
TypeHistorical festival
StateBerlin

In the listed historic village center of Alt-Marzahn around the Bockwindmühle, the harvest is traditionally celebrated. Three days of craftsmanship, stage performances, farmers' market, and an environmental festival on Saturday.
