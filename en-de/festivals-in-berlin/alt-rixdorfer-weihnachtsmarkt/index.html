Alt-Rixdorfer Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Berlin, Rixdorf · 4–6 December 2026

When4–6 December 2026
WhereRichardplatz, Rixdorf, 12055 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On the second Advent weekend, the historic Richardplatz in the Bohemian Village transforms into one of Berlin's most atmospheric Christmas markets. Over 200 stalls run by charitable organizations offer handmade goods, arts and crafts, and delicacies, illuminated by the warm glow of oil lamps. Camel rides, the Three Wise Men, and a witch enchant guests.
