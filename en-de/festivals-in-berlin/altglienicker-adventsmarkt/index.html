Christmas market · Berlin, Altglienicke · 5–6 December 2026

In the village center of Altglienicke near the S-Bahn station, a small neighborhood Advent market emerges on the second Advent weekend. Craft stalls, Christmas fare, and a stage program bring neighborhood spirit to southeastern Berlin.
