Berliner Volksfestsommer

Funfair · Berlin, Tegel · 9 July – 2 August 2026

When9 July – 2 August 2026
WhereZentraler Festplatz, Kurt-Schumacher-Damm 207, Tegel, 13405 Berlin
TypeFunfair
StateBerlin

On the Central Festival Grounds at Kurt-Schumacher-Damm, the major summer fair unfolds with numerous rides, food stands, and attractions for the whole family. Formerly known as the German-French Folk Festival, it ranks among the city's largest folk festivals.
