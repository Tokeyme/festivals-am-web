Berliner Wintertraum Niederschöneweide

Christmas market · Berlin, Niederschöneweide · 13 November – 28 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When13 November – 28 December 2026
WhereSchnellerstraße 145, Niederschöneweide, 12439 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On Schnellerstrasse in Niederschöneweide, Berliner Wintertraum unites Christmas market and fairground fun with over 60 attractions. Rides from a Ferris wheel to a Free-Fall Tower, mulled wine, and winter treats create fairground atmosphere.
