BMW Berlin-Marathon

Event · Berlin · 27 September 2026

When27 September 2026
WhereStraße des 17. Juni / Brandenburger Tor, 10117 Berlin

One of the world's largest and fastest marathons leads around 55,000 runners from around the globe through the city center. The flat course ends spectacularly through the Brandenburg Gate. Along the route, hundreds of thousands of spectators and musical groups celebrate.
