Bölschestraßenfest Friedrichshagen (Herbst)

Street festival · Berlin, Friedrichshagen · 18–20 September 2026

When18–20 September 2026
WhereBölschestraße, Friedrichshagen, 12587 Berlin
TypeStreet festival
StateBerlin

In fall, Bölschestraße in Friedrichshagen becomes a festival street again with arts and crafts, stage performances, and carousels. Along the beautiful Art Nouveau backdrop between the S-Bahn station and Müggelsee, neighborhood life, music, and dining blend.
