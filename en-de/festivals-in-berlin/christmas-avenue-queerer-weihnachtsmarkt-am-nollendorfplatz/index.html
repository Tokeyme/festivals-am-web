Christmas Avenue - Queerer Weihnachtsmarkt am Nollendorfplatz

Christmas market · Berlin, Schöneberg · 23 November – 23 December 2026

When23 November – 23 December 2026
WhereNollendorfplatz, Schöneberg, 10777 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

A queer Christmas market at Nollendorfplatz features daily stage programming with drag shows, DJs, and live acts. Lovingly designed stalls offer mulled wine, hot cocktails, and unusual gift ideas. Admission is free.
