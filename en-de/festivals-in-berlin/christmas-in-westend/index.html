Christmas in Westend

Christmas market · Berlin, Westend · 19–20 December 2026

When19–20 December 2026
WherePreußenallee, Westend, 14052 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

Shortly before Christmas, Preußenallee in Westend hosts an atmospheric Advent weekend. The green median strip features handcrafted wares, festive treats, and a small stage program in the neighborhood.
