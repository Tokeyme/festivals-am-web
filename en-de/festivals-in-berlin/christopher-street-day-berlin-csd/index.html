Christopher Street Day Berlin (CSD)

Town festival · Berlin · 24–25 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When24–25 July 2026
WhereLeipziger Straße, 10117 Berlin
TypeTown festival
StateBerlin

Berlin's major Pride highlight with a demonstration and parade across the city center. In 2026, CSD takes place for the first time over two days: opening with a Democracy Night at Brandenburg Gate, followed Saturday by the colorful parade from Mitte through Schöneberg. Hundreds of thousands celebrate diversity and equal rights.
