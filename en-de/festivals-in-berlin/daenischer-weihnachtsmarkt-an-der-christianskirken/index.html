Dänischer Weihnachtsmarkt an der Christianskirken

Christmas market · Berlin, Wilmersdorf · 28–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28–29 November 2026
WhereBrienner Straße 12, Christianskirken, Wilmersdorf, 10713 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

In the courtyard of the Danish Christianskirken, a cozy Nordic Christmas market emerges. Visitors discover Danish culinary delights like Æbleskiver and Smørrebrød, plus Julebryg Christmas beer and Akvavit, as well as stalls with design and Christmas decorations.
