Dein Sommer im Park – Rudow

Neighbourhood festival · Berlin, Rudow · 2–23 August 2026

When2–23 August 2026
WherePark am Ritterburgspielplatz, Ursulinenstraße 14, Rudow, 12357 Berlin
TypeNeighbourhood festival
StateBerlin

On four Sundays in August, the Park am Ritterburgspielplatz in Rudow becomes a summer stage with free concerts. Storytelling, children's theater, and musical performances round out the program. Free admission for the whole family.
