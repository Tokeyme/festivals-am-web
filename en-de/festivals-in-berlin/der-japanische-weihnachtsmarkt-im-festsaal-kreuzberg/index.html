Der Japanische Weihnachtsmarkt im Festsaal Kreuzberg

Christmas market · Berlin, Kreuzberg · 6 December 2026

When6 December 2026
WhereFestsaal Kreuzberg, Am Flutgraben 2, Kreuzberg, 12435 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

The Japanese Christmas Market in Festsaal Kreuzberg at the Flutgraben brings Japanese culture to the pre-Christmas season. Visitors find arts and crafts, fashion, specialties, and gifts from Japan in a day filled with Far Eastern flair.
