Erntetag im Museumsdorf Düppel

Historical festival · Berlin, Zehlendorf · 26–27 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When26–27 September 2026
WhereMuseumsdorf Düppel, Clauertstraße 11, Zehlendorf, 14163 Berlin
TypeHistorical festival
StateBerlin

In the medieval museum village, the harvest is celebrated as it was 800 years ago. Fresh bread bakes in the historic clay oven, craftspeople demonstrate old techniques, and families can weave harvest wreaths.
