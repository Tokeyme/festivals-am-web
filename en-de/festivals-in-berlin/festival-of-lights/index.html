Festival of Lights

Event · Berlin · 9–18 October 2026

When9–18 October 2026
Where10117 Berlin
TypeEvent
StateBerlin

For ten evenings, light art, projections, and illuminations transform Berlin's landmarks into glowing artworks. Brandenburg Gate, Berlin Cathedral, Potsdamer Platz, and many more sites glow from 7 p.m. to 11 p.m. The 22nd edition's theme is Colours of Love. Admission is free.
