Gauklerfest auf der Zitadelle Spandau

Historical festival · Berlin, Haselhorst · 3–4 October 2026

When3–4 October 2026
WhereZitadelle Spandau, Am Juliusturm 64, Haselhorst, 13599 Berlin
TypeHistorical festival
StateBerlin

Medieval festivities fill the historic Citadel: jesters, musicians, stilt theater, and comedians showcase their art. On the market grounds, handmade wares, hearty fare, and a carousel for the little ones beckon.
