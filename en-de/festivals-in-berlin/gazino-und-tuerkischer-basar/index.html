Gazino und Türkischer Basar

Street festival · Berlin, Schöneberg · 4 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When4 July 2026
WhereSteinmetzstraße zwischen Bülow- und Alvenslebenstraße, Schöneberg, 10783 Berlin
TypeStreet festival

Music and street festival on Steinmetzstraße, recalling the legendary Gazino music club and the Turkish bazaar of the 1970s and 1980s at Bülowstraße station. A diverse music program, plus foods, drinks, and goods from the Middle East.
