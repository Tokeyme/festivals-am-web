Grunewalder Weihnachtsmarkt rund um die Grunewaldkirche

Christmas market · Berlin, Grunewald · 29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When29 November 2026
WhereBismarckallee / Grunewaldkirche, Grunewald, 10711 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

Since 1976, the area around Grunewald Church transforms into a cozy neighborhood market on the first Advent. Around 50 stalls run by charities offer sweet and savory treats and gift ideas, including mulled wine brewed to a traditional recipe at triangular stalls.
