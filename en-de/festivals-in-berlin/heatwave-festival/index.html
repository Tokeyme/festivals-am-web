Heatwave Festival

Neighbourhood festival · Berlin, Märkisches Viertel · 3–4 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When3–4 July 2026
WhereFestwiese am Seggeluchbecken, Märkisches Viertel, 13435 Berlin
TypeNeighbourhood festival
StateBerlin

Organized by young people from Reinickendorf, this festival brings over 30 live acts of hip-hop, pop, afrobeats, and techno to the festival meadow at Seggeluchbecken. From programming to stage technology, everything is in the hands of local youth. Admission is free.
