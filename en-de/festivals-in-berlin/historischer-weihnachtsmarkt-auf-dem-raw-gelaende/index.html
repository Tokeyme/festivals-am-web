Historischer Weihnachtsmarkt auf dem RAW-Gelände

Christmas market · Berlin, Friedrichshain · 12 November – 22 December 2026

When12 November – 22 December 2026
WhereRevaler Straße 99, Friedrichshain, 10245 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On the historic grounds of the former Imperial Railways repair works, a medieval Christmas market emerges in torchlight. Blacksmiths, potters, and woodcarvers demonstrate their crafts, with mead, mulled wine, hearty fare, jesters, fire shows, and historical music.
