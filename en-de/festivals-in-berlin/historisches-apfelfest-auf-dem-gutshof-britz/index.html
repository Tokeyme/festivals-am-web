Historisches Apfelfest auf dem Gutshof Britz

Historical festival · Berlin, Britz · 26–27 September 2026

When26–27 September 2026
WhereGutshof Britz, Alt-Britz 73, Britz, 12359 Berlin
TypeHistorical festival
StateBerlin

For fall's opening, theater troupe Cocolorus Budenzauber transforms the historic Britz estate farm into a place of playful fun. Acrobatics, juggling, fairy tale plays, fire shows, and a William Tell tournament delight young and old, while an apple variety exhibition and autumn delicacies add flavor. A festive family celebration centered on the apple.
