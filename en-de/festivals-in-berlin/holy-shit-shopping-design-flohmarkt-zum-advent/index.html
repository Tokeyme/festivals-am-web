Holy Shit Shopping – Design-Flohmarkt zum Advent

Flea market · Berlin, Neukölln · 28–29 November 2026

When28–29 November 2026
WhereCANK, Karl-Marx-Straße 95, Neukölln, 12043 Berlin
TypeFlea market
StateBerlin

On the first Advent, the former department store at Karl-Marx-Strasse transforms into a lively design and lifestyle market. Hundreds of young creatives present fashion, jewelry, art, and gift ideas, with drinks, street food, and DJ sounds.
