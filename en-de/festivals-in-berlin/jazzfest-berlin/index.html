Jazzfest Berlin

Event · Berlin, Wilmersdorf · 29 October – 1 November 2026

When29 October – 1 November 2026
WhereSchaperstraße 24, Wilmersdorf, 10719 Berlin
TypeEvent
StateBerlin

One of Europe's oldest jazz festivals annually crowns the jazz year. At the House of Berlin Festivals and clubs like A-Trane and Quasimodo, style-defining greats and young talents perform. The program combines creative boundary-crossing with collective visions.
