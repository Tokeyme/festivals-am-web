Karlshorster Weihnachtsmarkt "Die Welt zuhause in Karlshorst"

Christmas market · Berlin, Karlshorst · 28–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28–29 November 2026
WhereJohannes-Fest-Platz / Kulturhaus Karlshorst, Treskowallee 112, Karlshorst, 10318 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On Johannes-Fest-Platz and in the neighboring Kulturhaus Karlshorst, the intercultural Christmas Market celebrates the festive diversity of many cultures. Alongside crafts and Christmas treats, a colorful stage program offers music and theater.
