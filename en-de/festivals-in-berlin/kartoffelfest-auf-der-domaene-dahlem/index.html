Kartoffelfest auf der Domäne Dahlem

Village & club fest · Berlin, Dahlem · 19–20 September 2026

When19–20 September 2026
WhereDomäne Dahlem, Königin-Luise-Straße 49, Dahlem, 14195 Berlin
TypeVillage & club fest
StateBerlin

At the historic Domäne Dahlem estate farm, this weekend revolves around potatoes. Potato dishes from the farm shop, hands-on harvest activities, plus a market with local producers and arts and crafts.
