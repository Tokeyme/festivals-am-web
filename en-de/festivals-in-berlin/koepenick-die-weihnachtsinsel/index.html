Köpenick, die Weihnachtsinsel

Christmas market · Berlin, Köpenick · 11–13 December 2026

When11–13 December 2026
WhereSchlossplatz und Schlossinsel Köpenick, Köpenick, 12555 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On the castle island and at the castle square in historic Altköpenick, one of Berlin's most atmospheric Christmas markets takes place. Between cobblestones and baroque architecture, handcrafted wares, regional delicacies, and mulled wine invite leisurely browsing.
