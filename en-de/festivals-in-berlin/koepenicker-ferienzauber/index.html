Köpenicker Ferienzauber

Town festival · Berlin, Köpenick · 31 July – 2 August 2026

When31 July – 2 August 2026
WhereAltstadt Köpenick / Schlossinsel, Köpenick, 12555 Berlin
TypeTown festival
StateBerlin

In the midst of summer holidays, the Köpenick old town invites a whimsical family festival with games, food, and hands-on activities. Between Luisenhain and Schlossinsel, stage performances, arts and crafts, and street food ensure an enjoyable weekend.
