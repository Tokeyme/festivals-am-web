Köpenicker Herbst

Town festival · Berlin, Köpenick · 30 September – 4 October 2026

When30 September – 4 October 2026
WhereLuisenhain am Rathaus Köpenick, Köpenick, 12555 Berlin
TypeTown festival
StateBerlin
