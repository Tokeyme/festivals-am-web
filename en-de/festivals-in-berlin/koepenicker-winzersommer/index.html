12. Köpenicker Winzersommer

Wine festival · Berlin, Köpenick · 21–23 August 2026

When21–23 August 2026
WhereSchlossinsel und Luisenhain, Altstadt Köpenick, Köpenick, 12555 Berlin
TypeWine festival
StateBerlin

Between Luisenhain and Schlossinsel, the Köpenick old town becomes a gourmet destination with over 300 wines from around 40 winemakers. Accompanied by live music on two stages and culinary delights, the wine festival invites leisurely strolling and tasting.
