Krempel de Luxe – Flohmarkt in der Inspektor Villa

Flea market · Berlin, Wedding · 13 September 2026

When13 September 2026
WhereGarten der Inspektor Villa, Gerichtstraße 36, Wedding, 13347 Berlin
TypeFlea market
StateBerlin

In the idyllic garden of the listed Inspector Villa in Wedding, around 25 stalls display their treasures under blooming trees. This charming neighborhood flea market offers dishes, décor, secondhand fashion, jewelry, and lovely finds, free admission.
