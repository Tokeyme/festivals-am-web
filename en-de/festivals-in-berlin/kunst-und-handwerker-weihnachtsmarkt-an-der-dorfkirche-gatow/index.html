Kunst- und Handwerker-Weihnachtsmarkt an der Dorfkirche Gatow

Christmas market · Berlin, Gatow · 13 December 2026

When13 December 2026
WhereDorfkirche Gatow, Alt-Gatow, Gatow, 14089 Berlin
TypeChristmas market

In historic Gatow's village center, a small, cozy Christmas market surrounds the village church. About 40 artisans and craftspeople offer handmade goods, with music, a dance workshop for children, and Advent singing.
