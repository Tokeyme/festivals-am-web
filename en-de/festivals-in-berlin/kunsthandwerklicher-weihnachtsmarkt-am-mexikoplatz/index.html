Kunsthandwerklicher Weihnachtsmarkt am Mexikoplatz

Christmas market · Berlin, Zehlendorf · 29 November – 20 December 2026

When29 November – 20 December 2026
WhereMexikoplatz 4, Zehlendorf, 14163 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On the four Advent Sundays, Mexikoplatz at the distinctive S-Bahn station transforms into a special Christmas market. Genuine arts and crafts take center stage, complemented by mulled wine and Christmas delicacies.
