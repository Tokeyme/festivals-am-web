Kunsthandwerklicher Weihnachtsmarkt Frohnau

Christmas market · Berlin, Frohnau · 5–6 December 2026

When5–6 December 2026
WhereZeltinger Platz, Frohnau, 13465 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

For over 20 years, the artisan Christmas market has invited visitors on the second Advent weekend to Zeltinger Platz. Around 120 artisans present handcrafted individual pieces in an atmospheric setting.
