Lesbisch-schwules Stadtfest am Nollendorfplatz

Town festival · Berlin, Schöneberg · 18–19 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When18–19 July 2026
WhereNollendorfplatz, Motzstraße, Eisenacher Straße, Fuggerstraße, Schöneberg, 10777 Berlin
TypeTown festival

Europe's largest lesbian and gay street festival transforms the rainbow district around Nollendorfplatz into a colorful festival grounds. Over two days, visitors enjoy stage programs, information booths, dining, and festive atmosphere, all free of charge.
