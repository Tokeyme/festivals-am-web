Lichtenberger Winterzeit an der Landsberger Allee

Christmas market · Berlin, Fennpfuhl · 31 October – 27 December 2026

When31 October – 27 December 2026
WhereLandsberger Allee 104 (vor IKEA), Fennpfuhl, 10367 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

The major Christmas fair at Landsberger Allee combines festive-winter atmosphere with fairground fun. A Ferris wheel, numerous rides, plus mulled wine and sweet specialties draw families throughout Advent.
