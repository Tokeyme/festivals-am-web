Lichtenrader Lichtermarkt

Christmas market · Berlin, Lichtenrade · 29 November 2026

When29 November 2026
WhereDorfteich an der Alten Dorfkirche, Alt-Lichtenrade, Lichtenrade, 12309 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

Traditional light market on the first Advent Sunday around the village pond and old village church in Alt-Lichtenrade. Nearly a hundred stalls offer handmade goods from wood, ceramic, wool, and metal, with mulled wine, children's punch, and sweet and savory specialties.
