Lichtermarkt am Rathaus Lichtenberg

Christmas market · Berlin, Lichtenberg · 29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When29 November 2026
WhereMöllendorffstraße 6, Rathaus Lichtenberg, Lichtenberg, 10367 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

Around Lichtenberg Town Hall, the social light market takes place on the first Advent. Associations, schools, and charitable organizations offer homemade goods, craft projects, and Advent wreaths at their stalls, with mulled wine and grilled fare.
