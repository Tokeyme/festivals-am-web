MachMit-Markt bei Mampe

Christmas market · Berlin, Kreuzberg · 19–20 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When19–20 December 2026
WhereMampe, Am Tempelhofer Berg 6, Kreuzberg, 10965 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

The small MachMit market at Mampe on Tempelhofer Berg invites leisurely browsing shortly before Christmas. Local manufacturers and creatives offer gift ideas, with mulled wine and winter snacks in a neighborly atmosphere.
