Märchenhafter Weihnachtsmarkt am Jagdschloss Grunewald

Christmas market · Berlin, Grunewald · 5–6 December 2026

When5–6 December 2026
WhereHüttenweg 100, Jagdschloss Grunewald, Grunewald, 14193 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

In the courtyard of the Grunewald hunting lodge on the shores of Grunewaldsee, a bewitched Christmas market emerges deep in the forest. Fairy-tale figures like Mother Holle, Hansel and Gretel, and the Frog Prince mingle among guests, with narrators, musical stage programming, and special gifts.
