Manufakturen-Weihnachtsmarkt am Schloss Schönhausen

Christmas market · Berlin, Niederschönhausen · 27–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27–29 November 2026
WhereSchlosspark Schönhausen, Tschaikowskistraße 1, Niederschönhausen, 13156 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

In the atmospheric Schönhausen Palace Park, the sustainable Manufakturen Christmas Market invites browsing on the first Advent weekend. Arts and crafts, fashion, mulled wine, and Christmas pastries meet a colorful stage program with choirs.
