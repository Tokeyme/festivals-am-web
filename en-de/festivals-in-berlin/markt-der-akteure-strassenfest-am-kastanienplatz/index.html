Markt der Akteure - Straßenfest am Kastanienplatz

Street festival · Berlin, Kreuzberg · 12 September 2026

When12 September 2026
WhereKastanienplatz, Wassertorstraße, Kreuzberg, 10969 Berlin
TypeStreet festival
StateBerlin

Around Kastanienplatz in the Wassertorkiez, the Market of Actors hosts a colorful street festival for everyone. Associations, initiatives, and institutions from the district present themselves with information booths, hands-on activities, and small stage performances for all ages.
