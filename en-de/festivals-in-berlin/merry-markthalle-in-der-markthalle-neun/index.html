Merry Markthalle in der Markthalle Neun

Christmas market · Berlin, Kreuzberg · 13 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When13 December 2026
WhereMarkthalle Neun, Eisenbahnstraße 42-43, Kreuzberg, 10997 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On Advent Sundays, the historic Markthalle Neun becomes a Christmas market. Among the scents of mulled wine and gingerbread, visitors discover ceramics, jewelry, wine, tea, and fine chocolates from small local manufacturers.
