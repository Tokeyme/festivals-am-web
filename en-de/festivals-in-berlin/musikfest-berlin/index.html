Musikfest Berlin

Event · Berlin, Tiergarten · 28 August – 23 September 2026

When28 August – 23 September 2026
WhereHerbert-von-Karajan-Straße 1, Tiergarten, 10785 Berlin
TypeEvent
StateBerlin

Major festival of classical and contemporary music by the Berlin Festival. International top orchestras and ensembles perform at the Philharmonie, Konzerthaus, and other venues. This season opens with György Ligeti's opera Le Grand Macabre.
