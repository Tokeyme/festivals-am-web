Nikolausmarkt auf dem Kinderbauernhof Pinke-Panke

Christmas market · Berlin, Pankow · 6 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When6 December 2026
WhereKinderbauernhof Pinke-Panke, Am Bürgerpark 15-18, Pankow, 13189 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

The small St. Nicholas market at the Pinke-Panke children's farm near Bürgerpark is especially designed for families on the second Sunday of Advent. Animals, campfire, crafts, and Christmas decorations create a warm atmosphere surrounded by nature.
