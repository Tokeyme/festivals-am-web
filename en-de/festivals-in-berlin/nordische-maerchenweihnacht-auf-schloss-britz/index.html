Nordische Märchenweihnacht auf Schloss Britz

Christmas market · Berlin, Britz · 27 November – 13 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 November – 13 December 2026
WhereGutshof Britz, Alt-Britz 73, Britz, 12359 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On the first Advent weekends, the Britz manor farm transforms into a historically and Nordic-influenced Christmas market. The Snow Queen with her trolls and Mother Holle mingle among guests, with fire shows, fairy-tale theater, and Glögg. A magical Advent experience for the whole family.
