Novemberlicht - Kiezfest Niederschönhausen

Neighbourhood festival · Berlin, Niederschönhausen · 21 November 2026

The atmospheric neighborhood festival on the central promenade in Niederschönhausen ushers in the dark season. Activities include lantern crafting, toasted bread over the fire, fire bowls, music, and winter treats for all ages.
