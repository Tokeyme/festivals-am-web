Nowkölln Flowmarkt 4. Oktober

Flea market · Berlin, Neukölln · 4 October 2026

When4 October 2026
WhereMaybachufer 32-33, Neukölln, 12047 Berlin
TypeFlea market
StateBerlin

Nowkoelln Flowmarkt is a flea market on the Maybachufer in the Reuterkiez district of Neukoelln, right on the Landwehr Canal. It is held every second Sunday, selling second-hand goods and handmade items.
