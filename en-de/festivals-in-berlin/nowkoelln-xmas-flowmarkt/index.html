Nowkoelln Xmas Flowmarkt

Flea market · Berlin, Reuterkiez · 13 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When13 December 2026
WhereMaybachufer 32-33, Reuterkiez, 12047 Berlin
TypeFlea market
StateBerlin

The festive Advent edition of the Nowkoelln Flowmarkt at Maybachufer invites pre-Christmas browsing. Alongside secondhand items, there are numerous handmade creations, art, and design pieces, plus mulled wine and music by the canal. The ideal place for special Christmas gifts from Neukölln.
