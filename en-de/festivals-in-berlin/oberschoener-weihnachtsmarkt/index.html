Oberschöner Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Berlin, Oberschöneweide · 5–6 December 2026

When5–6 December 2026
WhereMaHalla, Wilhelminenhofstraße 76, Oberschöneweide, 12459 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

In a former industrial hall in Oberschöneweide, an artisan Christmas market takes shape on the second Advent weekend. Regional design, sustainable fare, live music, and a fire show characterize the special atmosphere.
