Oktoberfest am Alexanderplatz

Funfair · Berlin, Mitte · 25 September – 4 October 2026

When25 September – 4 October 2026
WhereAlexanderplatz, Mitte, 10178 Berlin
TypeFunfair
StateBerlin

In the heart of the city, Alexanderplatz transforms into an Oktoberfest with Bavarian flair. A Paulaner beer garden, hearty fare, daily live stage programs from brass bands to party sounds, plus stands with arts and crafts.
