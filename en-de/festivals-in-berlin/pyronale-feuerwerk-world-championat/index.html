Pyronale - Feuerwerk-World-Championat

Event · Berlin, Westend · 25–26 September 2026

When25–26 September 2026
WhereMaifeld am Olympiastadion, Westend, 14053 Berlin
TypeEvent
StateBerlin

On the Maifeld at the Olympic Stadium, pyrotechnic teams from various countries compete in a fireworks championship. Choreographed light shows set to music illuminate the night sky. In 2026, Pyronale celebrates its 20th anniversary.
