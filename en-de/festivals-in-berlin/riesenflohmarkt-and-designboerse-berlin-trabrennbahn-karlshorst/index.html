Riesenflohmarkt & Designbörse Berlin – Trabrennbahn Karlshorst

Flea market · Berlin, Karlshorst · 7–8 November 2026

When7–8 November 2026
WhereTrabrennbahn Karlshorst, Treskowallee 129, Karlshorst, 10318 Berlin
TypeFlea market
StateBerlin

The large flea market at Trabrennbahn Karlshorst is complemented by the Berlin Design Exchange. Alongside classic flea-market finds, furniture, lamps, and twentieth-century design classics take center stage for collectors and enthusiasts.
