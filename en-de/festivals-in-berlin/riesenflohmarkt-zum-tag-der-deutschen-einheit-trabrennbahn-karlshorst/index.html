Riesenflohmarkt zum Tag der Deutschen Einheit – Trabrennbahn Karlshorst

Flea market · Berlin, Karlshorst · 2–4 October 2026

When2–4 October 2026
WhereTrabrennbahn Karlshorst, Treskowallee 129, Karlshorst, 10318 Berlin
TypeFlea market
StateBerlin

Around the national holiday, Trabrennbahn Karlshorst transforms into a giant flea and antique market with over 700 exhibitors. On the expansive grounds, antiques, collector's items, design classics, and a colorful flea market mix await.
