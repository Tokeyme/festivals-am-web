Rudower Septembermeile

Street festival · Berlin, Rudow · 5–6 September 2026

When5–6 September 2026
WhereKrokusstraße / Alt-Rudow, Rudow, 12357 Berlin
TypeStreet festival
StateBerlin

The family-friendly street festival around Krokusstraße in Alt-Rudow offers a colorful stage program with live music, arts and crafts, and a street food market. Children enjoy carousels, bounce houses, and face painting. Admission is free.
