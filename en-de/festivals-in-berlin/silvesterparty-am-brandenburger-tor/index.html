Silvesterparty am Brandenburger Tor

Town festival · Berlin, Mitte · 31 December 2026

When31 December 2026
WhereStraße des 17. Juni / Brandenburger Tor, Mitte, 10117 Berlin
TypeTown festival
StateBerlin

Berlin's official New Year's Eve open-air celebration transforms Straße des 17. Juni into a vast dance floor. Top DJs perform until midnight, when a spectacular fireworks display illuminates the Brandenburg Gate. Free entry and live television broadcast.
