Solidarischer Wintermarkt im Görlitzer Park

Christmas market · Berlin, Kreuzberg · 12–13 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12–13 December 2026
WhereGörlitzer Park, Skalitzer Straße 47, Kreuzberg, 10997 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

As an alternative to large commercial markets, the solidarity winter market in Görlitzer Park invites the neighborhood to celebrate together. About 30 stands offer mulled wine, waffles, clothing swap, and handmade goods on a donation basis—with proceeds supporting social projects.
