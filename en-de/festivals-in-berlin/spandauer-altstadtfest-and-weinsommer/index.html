Spandauer Altstadtfest & WeinSommer

Wine festival · Berlin, Altstadt Spandau · 27–30 August 2026

When27–30 August 2026
WhereMarkt / Reformationsplatz, Altstadt Spandau, Altstadt Spandau, 13597 Berlin
TypeWine festival
StateBerlin

The largest summer festival of the old town transforms the historic market square into one of Berlin's largest wine gardens. Winemakers from various regions pour their wines, plus two show stages, a food street, and a children's play area at Reformationsplatz.
