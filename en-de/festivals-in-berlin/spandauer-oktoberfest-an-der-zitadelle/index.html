Spandauer Oktoberfest an der Zitadelle

Funfair · Berlin, Haselhorst · 18 September – 24 October 2026

When18 September – 24 October 2026
WhereFestplatz an der Zitadelle, Zitadellenweg, Haselhorst, 13599 Berlin
TypeFunfair
StateBerlin

In the large festival tent at the Citadel, authentic Oktoberfest celebration unfolds: Fridays and Saturdays feature live music, party atmosphere, and Bavarian fare like pork knuckles, pretzels, and white sausages.
