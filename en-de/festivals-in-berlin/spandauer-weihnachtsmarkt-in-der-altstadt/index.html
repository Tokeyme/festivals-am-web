Spandauer Weihnachtsmarkt in der Altstadt

Christmas market · Berlin, Altstadt Spandau · 23 November – 22 December 2026

When23 November – 22 December 2026
WhereAltstadt Spandau, vom Rathaus bis St. Nikolai, Altstadt Spandau, 13597 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

One of Berlin's largest Christmas markets stretches from the Town Hall to the Nikolai Church. Around 120 stalls offer arts and crafts and specialties, over 100 illuminated fir trees decorate the old town, and ice stock shooting awaits at Reformationsplatz.
