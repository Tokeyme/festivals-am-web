Spooky Christmas Market im Goerzwerk

Christmas market · Berlin, Lichterfelde · 5–6 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When5–6 December 2026
WhereGoerzwerk, Goerzallee 299, Lichterfelde, 14167 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

In the historic Goerzwerk industrial area, an indoor Christmas market with a tongue-in-cheek touch takes place, with an eerie Krampus roaming through. The surrounding program remains rather cozy than scary, aimed at the whole family.
