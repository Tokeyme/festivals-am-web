Stadtteilfest Falkenhagener Feld

Neighbourhood festival · Berlin, Falkenhagener Feld · 12 September 2026

When12 September 2026
WhereWesterwaldstraße 13, Falkenhagener Feld, 13583 Berlin
TypeNeighbourhood festival

For many years, the district festival in Falkenhagener Feld has been a fixed neighborhood date. At Westerwaldstraße, guests enjoy stage performances, read-aloud activities, a musical carousel, and diverse family offerings.
