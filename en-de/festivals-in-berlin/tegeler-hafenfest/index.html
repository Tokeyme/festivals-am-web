Tegeler Hafenfest

Funfair · Berlin, Tegel · 16–19 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When16–19 July 2026
WhereGreenwichpromenade am Tegeler See, Borsigdamm, Tegel, 13507 Berlin
TypeFunfair
StateBerlin

Along the Tegeler Lake shore, the Greenwichpromenade transforms into a colorful family festival with water sports, live music, arts and crafts, and food stands. The highlight is a large fireworks display over the lake. Admission is free.
