Traditioneller Finnischer Weihnachtsbasar

Christmas market · Berlin, Kreuzberg · 14–15 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When14–15 November 2026
WherePassionskirche, Marheinekeplatz 2, Kreuzberg, 10961 Berlin
TypeChristmas market

At the Passionskirche at Marheinekeplatz, the Finnish community invites visitors to their traditional Christmas bazaar. Finnish specialties, baked goods, handicrafts, and Nordic gift ideas create a pre-Christmas mood.
