Umwelt- und Weihnachtsmarkt Koppenplatz und Sophienstraße

Christmas market · Berlin, Spandauer Vorstadt · 28 November – 21 December 2026

When28 November – 21 December 2026
WhereKoppenplatz / Sophienstraße, Spandauer Vorstadt, 10115 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On Advent weekends, the market stretches from Koppenplatz over Sophienstrasse to the Hackesche Höfe. The focus is on ecological products, arts and crafts, and sustainable gift ideas in atmospheric old-building setting.
