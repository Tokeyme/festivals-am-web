Weihnachten an der Spree am Spreespeicher

Christmas market · Berlin, Friedrichshain · 16 November – 30 December 2026

When16 November – 30 December 2026
WhereStralauer Allee 2b, Friedrichshain, 10245 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

Directly on the Spree riverbank near Oberbaumbrücke, an atmospheric Christmas market emerges with views of the water. Stalls with mulled wine, winter fare, and gift ideas create pre-Christmas atmosphere at the Spreespeicher.
