Weihnachtsbasar in Britz

Christmas market · Berlin, Britz · 5–6 December 2026

On the second Advent weekend, the allotment garden at Marienfelder Weg opens its Christmas bazaar. Between winter-decorated garden huts, homemade goods, mulled wine, and neighborhood gatherings prevail. A neighborhood festival in green Britz.
