Weihnachtsmarkt am Schloss Charlottenburg

Christmas market · Berlin, Charlottenburg · 23 November – 27 December 2026

When23 November – 27 December 2026
WhereSpandauer Damm 10, vor dem Schloss Charlottenburg, 14059 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

Against the magnificent Baroque backdrop of Charlottenburg Palace, an atmospheric Christmas market emerges with high-quality arts and crafts and culinary delicacies. Over 200 illuminated trees, small children's carousels, and a Ferris wheel create festive brilliance.
