Weihnachtsmarkt an der Dorfkirche Marienfelde

Christmas market · Berlin, Marienfelde · 4–13 December 2026

When4–13 December 2026
WhereAlter Dorfanger an der Dorfkirche Marienfelde, Marienfelde, 12277 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

A contemplative Christmas market on the second and third Advent weekends around the medieval Marienfelde village church. On the old village green, stalls with arts and crafts, gift ideas, mulled wine, and homemade cake invite lingering, accompanied by musical performances in the church.
