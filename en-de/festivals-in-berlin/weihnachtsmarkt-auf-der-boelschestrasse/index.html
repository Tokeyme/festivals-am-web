Weihnachtsmarkt auf der Bölschestraße

Christmas market · Berlin, Friedrichshagen · 4–6 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When4–6 December 2026
WhereBölschestraße / Marktplatz Friedrichshagen, Friedrichshagen, 12587 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On the second Advent weekend, Bölschestrasse in Friedrichshagen becomes a romantic Christmas market with over a hundred decorated stalls. Against Jugendstil architecture, unusual gift ideas, bratwurst, mulled wine, and roasted almonds attract visitors.
