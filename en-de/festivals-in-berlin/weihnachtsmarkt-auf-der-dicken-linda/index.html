Weihnachtsmarkt auf der Dicken Linda

Around the "Dicke Linda" weekly market at Kranoldplatz, a small, community-minded Christmas market emerges. Regional producers and handcrafts define the offerings, alongside mulled wine and winter delicacies. A charming neighborhood market in the southern district.
