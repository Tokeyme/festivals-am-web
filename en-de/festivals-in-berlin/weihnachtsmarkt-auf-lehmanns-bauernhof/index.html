Weihnachtsmarkt auf Lehmanns Bauernhof

Christmas market · Berlin, Marienfelde · 4–13 December 2026

When4–13 December 2026
WhereLehmanns Bauernhof, an der Marienfelder Dorfaue, Marienfelde, 12277 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

Traditional farm Christmas market on the second and third Advent weekends at the romantic Marienfelde village meadow. Warm light, handcrafted Christmas ornaments, pony rides, farm animals, and hearty fare like pea soup, kale, and flammkuchen await.
