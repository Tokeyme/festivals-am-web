Weihnachtsmarkt im Winterquartier Berlin

Christmas market · Berlin, Friedrichshain · 28 November 2026 – 28 February 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28 November 2026 – 28 February 2027
WhereModersohnstraße 35-45, Friedrichshain, 10245 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

At Winterquartier at the Napoleon Complex, Christmas market and winter sports combine: alongside stalls with mulled wine and snacks, an ice rink, ice stock shooting, and a cultural program await. A gathering place for the cold season in Friedrichshain.
