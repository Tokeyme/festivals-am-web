Weihnachtsmarkt in den Späth'schen Baumschulen

Christmas market · Berlin, Baumschulenweg · 27 November – 13 December 2026

When27 November – 13 December 2026
WhereSpäthstraße 80/81, Baumschulenweg, 12437 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On the first three Advent weekends, the historic gardens of Späth's Plant Nursery transform into an atmospheric Christmas market. Arts and crafts, stage programming, angel visits, and winter delicacies delight families among the display gardens.
