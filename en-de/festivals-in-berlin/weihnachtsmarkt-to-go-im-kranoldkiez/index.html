Weihnachtsmarkt to go im Kranoldkiez

Christmas market · Berlin, Lichterfelde-Ost · 12 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12 December 2026
Whererund um den Kranoldplatz, Lichterfelde-Ost, 12209 Berlin
TypeChristmas market

On the Saturday before the third Sunday of Advent, the streets around Kranoldplatz transform into a decentralized Christmas market with regional flair. Mulled wine, festive treats, trumpet music outside Petruskirche, and participating shops with extended hours.
