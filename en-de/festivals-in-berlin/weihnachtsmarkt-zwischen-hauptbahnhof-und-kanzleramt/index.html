Weihnachtsmarkt zwischen Hauptbahnhof und Kanzleramt

Christmas market · Berlin, Moabit · 23 November – 26 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When23 November – 26 December 2026
WhereWashingtonplatz, Moabit, 10557 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On Washingtonplatz south of the main train station, a market emerges with Christmas treats and products from designer workshops and organic manufacturers. A small Ferris wheel, carousel, and choir performances round out the visit.
