Weihnachtsrodeo - Design-Weihnachtsmarkt im Colosseum

Christmas market · Berlin, Prenzlauer Berg · 12–13 December 2026

When12–13 December 2026
WhereColosseum Berlin, Schönhauser Allee 123, Prenzlauer Berg, 10437 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

At the Colosseum on Schönhauser Allee, over 200 designers and creatives present handmade unique pieces, jewelry, art, and textiles on an Advent weekend. Street food, drinks, and DJs complete this indoor design market.
