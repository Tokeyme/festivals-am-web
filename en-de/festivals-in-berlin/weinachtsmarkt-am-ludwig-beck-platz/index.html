WEINachtsmarkt am Ludwig-Beck-Platz

Christmas market · Berlin, Steglitz · 12–13 December 2026

When12–13 December 2026
WhereLudwig-Beck-Platz, Steglitz, 12165 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

Inspired by the neighborhood's summer wine festivals, this culinary Christmas market showcases wines from carefully selected, family-run vineyards across Germany. Ludwig-Beck-Platz becomes a warm, wine-focused festive gathering place.
