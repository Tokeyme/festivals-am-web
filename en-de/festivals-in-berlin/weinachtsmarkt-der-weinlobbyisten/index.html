WEINachtsmarkt der Weinlobbyisten

Christmas market · Berlin, Schöneberg · 27 November – 30 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 November – 30 December 2026
WhereKolonnenstraße 62, Schöneberg, 10827 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

A small, refined Christmas market in the courtyard of the Schöneberg wine bar Der Weinlobbyist. The focus is on selected vintner mulled wines and self-created dishes like goose stew and goose strudel alongside classics.
