Weinmarkt im Späth'schen Weingarten

Wine festival · Berlin, Baumschulenweg · 24–26 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When24–26 July 2026
WhereSpäthstraße 80/81, Baumschulenweg, 12437 Berlin
TypeWine festival
StateBerlin

In the historic vineyard of the Späth'schen plant nursery, family wineries from several German wine regions present their wines. Among blooming display gardens, the market invites with wine, regional specialties, and a relaxed summer atmosphere.
