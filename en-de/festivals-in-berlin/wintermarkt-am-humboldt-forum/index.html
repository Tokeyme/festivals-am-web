Wintermarkt am Humboldt Forum

Christmas market · Berlin, Mitte · 18 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

When18 November 2026 – 3 January 2027
WhereSchlossplatz / Schlüterhof, Mitte, 10178 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

In front of Berlin Castle and in the Schlüterhof of the Humboldt Forum, an atmospheric winter market takes shape. Arts and crafts, historic rides, and culinary stalls blend with the special ambiance of the reconstructed palace.
