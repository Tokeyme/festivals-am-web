Wintermarkt an der Alten Frachtkantine (TXL)

Christmas market · Berlin, Tegel · 28–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28–29 November 2026
WhereUrban Tech Republic, Alte Frachtkantine, Flughafen Tegel 1, Tegel, 13405 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

On the first Advent, the old freight station on the grounds of the former Tegel Airport transforms into a cozy winter market. Mulled wine, music, and gift stalls create an open, welcoming atmosphere.
