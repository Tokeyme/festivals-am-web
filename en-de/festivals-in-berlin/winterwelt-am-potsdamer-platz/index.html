Winterwelt am Potsdamer Platz

Christmas market · Berlin, Tiergarten · 2 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When2 November 2026 – 3 January 2027
WherePotsdamer Platz, Tiergarten, 10785 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

At Potsdamer Platz, Christmas market and winter sports combine into an unusual experience. Alongside mulled wine and stalls, a large toboggan run and ice rink invite visitors right between the skyscrapers, with winter magic beginning particularly early here.
