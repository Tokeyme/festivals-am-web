Zehlendorfer Weihnachtsmarkt an der Dorfaue

Christmas market · Berlin, Zehlendorf · 23 November – 30 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When23 November – 30 December 2026
WhereDorfaue / vor dem Rathaus Zehlendorf, Teltower Damm, Zehlendorf, 14163 Berlin
TypeChristmas market
StateBerlin

In the old village center directly at Zehlendorf Town Hall, a cozy Christmas market unfolds over several weeks. An ice rink, mulled wine, sweet and savory specialties, and children's attractions create neighborhood Advent spirit.
