Pützchens Markt

Funfair · Bonn, Pützchen · 11–15 September 2026

When11–15 September 2026
WhereMarkt-Wiese, Bonn-Pützchen, Pützchen, 53229 Bonn
TypeFunfair
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

One of the largest and most well-known folk festivals in the region. Over five days, the market meadows in Bonn-Pützchen transform into a large amusement park with rides, carousels, games, and culinary offerings, concluding with fireworks.
