Bremer Freimarkt (Ischa Freimaak)

Funfair · Bremen · 16 October – 1 November 2026

When16 October – 1 November 2026
WhereBürgerweide, 28215 Bremen
TypeFunfair
StateBremen

The 991st Free Market—one of Germany's oldest and largest folk festivals on Bürgerweide with numerous amusement rides and exhibitors. The highlight is the grand Free Market parade on Saturday, October 24, 2026; there's also a small Free Market in the city center.
