Burgfrieden auf Burg Blomendal

Christmas market · Bremen, Blumenthal · 12–13 December 2026

When12–13 December 2026
WhereBurg Blomendal, Blumenthal, 28779 Bremen
TypeChristmas market
StateBremen

The Burgfrieden transforms the Wasserburg Blomendal in Bremen-Blumenthal into a pre-Christmas village. Amid the lively bustle there is food and drink, coffee and cake, as well as a Weihnachtsmarkt where craftspeople present tailoring, woodwork, fine art, natural cosmetics and decorations. The event sees itself as a sign of peaceful togetherness.
