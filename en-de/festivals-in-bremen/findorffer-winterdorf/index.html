Findorffer Winterdorf

Christmas market · Bremen, Findorff · 6 November 2026 – 31 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When6 November 2026 – 31 January 2027
WhereJan-Reiners-Park, Fürther Straße, Findorff, 28215 Bremen
TypeChristmas market
StateBremen

Findorffer Winterdorf is a neighbourhood winter festival in Jan-Reiners-Park on Fürther Straße, near the Schlachthof. In its local, community atmosphere, wooden stalls, open fires, mulled wine and vegan varieties of punch beckon, along with fair-trade and regional gift ideas. The programme also includes after-work parties.
