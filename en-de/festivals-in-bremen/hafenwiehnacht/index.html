HafenWiehnacht

Christmas market · Bremen, Überseestadt · 3–6 December 2026

When3–6 December 2026
WhereAm Speicher XI, Überseestadt, 28217 Bremen
TypeChristmas market
StateBremen

HafenWiehnacht is a small Weihnachtsmarkt in Bremen's Überseestadt, on the square between Speicher XI and the Energieleitzentrale. In a rustic, maritime atmosphere, a village of huts offers food, drinks and regional, handmade products.
