Lesumer Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Bremen, Lesum · 5–6 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When5–6 December 2026
WhereAlt-Lesum, Lesum, 28717 Bremen
TypeChristmas market
StateBremen

The Lesumer Weihnachtsmarkt takes place in the heart of Bremen-Lesum on the Lesumer Marktplatz. On offer are culinary specialities, handicrafts and festive activities. A highlight is the communal Advent singing in the St.-Martini-Kirche.
