Lichter der Neustadt

Christmas market · Bremen, Neustadt · 26 November – 20 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When26 November – 20 December 2026
WhereAm Leibnizplatz, Neustadt, 28199 Bremen
TypeChristmas market
StateBremen

The Lichter der Neustadt is an alternative Weihnachtsmarkt in Bremen's Neustadt district, organised by KulturKraken e.V. Between the bremer shakespeare company and the Südbad, a festive mood combines with art, culture and music. At stalls that change daily there are handmade gift ideas and culinary offerings; admission is free.
