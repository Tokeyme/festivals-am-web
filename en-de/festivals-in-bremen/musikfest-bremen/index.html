Musikfest Bremen

Music festival · Bremen, Mitte · 15 August – 4 September 2026

When15 August – 4 September 2026
WhereMarktplatz, Mitte, 28195 Bremen
TypeMusic festival
StateBremen

Musikfest Bremen is an international festival of classical music with over 45 events in Bremen and the surrounding north-west German region. The programme features symphonic music, choral and chamber music, solo recitals and early music, for example at the Arp-Schnitger-Festival. The opening event, "Eine große Nachtmusik", is staged across numerous venues around the Marktplatz.
