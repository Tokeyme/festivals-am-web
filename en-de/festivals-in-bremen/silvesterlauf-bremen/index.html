Silvesterlauf Bremen

Sports event · Bremen, Hastedt · 31 December 2026

When31 December 2026
WhereHastedter Osterdeich, Hastedt, 28207 Bremen
TypeSports event
StateBremen

Silvesterlauf Bremen brings the running year to a close in the east of Bremen and is one of the city's most popular running events. On offer are courses ranging from the Bambini and Kids run through 2.5 and 5 kilometres to the 10-kilometre run, as well as walking and Nordic walking distances. The route runs along the Weser, the Werdersee and the Weserwehr.
