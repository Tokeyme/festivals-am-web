Sixdays Bremen

Sports event · Bremen, Findorff · 15–18 January 2027

When15–18 January 2027
WhereÖVB-Arena, Bürgerweide, Findorff, 28215 Bremen
TypeSports event
StateBremen

The Sixdays Bremen is a long-established track cycling event at the ÖVB-Arena that combines sport, show and party. Over several days, the cyclists compete on the track, accompanied by an extensive supporting programme. The event has been held since 1965.
