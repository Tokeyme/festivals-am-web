Skandinavischer Weihnachtsmarkt in der Lloyd Passage

Christmas market · Bremen, Mitte · 23 November – 28 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When23 November – 28 December 2026
WhereLloyd Passage, Mitte, 28195 Bremen
TypeChristmas market
StateBremen

A Scandinavian-style Weihnachtsmarkt takes place in the covered Lloyd Passage in Bremen city centre. Along around 250 metres, some 20 exhibitors offer culinary delicacies, original gift ideas and warming accessories beneath the glass roof.
