swb-Marathon

Sports event · Bremen, Mitte · 13 September 2026

When13 September 2026
WhereBremer Innenstadt, Mitte, 28195 Bremen
TypeSports event
StateBremen

The swb-Marathon is the city of Bremen's major running event. Alongside the marathon distance of 42.195 kilometres, a half marathon and a 10-kilometre run are also on offer. The route passes through numerous districts, including the Neustadt, past the Werdersee and Weserwehr, and through the Bürgerpark.
