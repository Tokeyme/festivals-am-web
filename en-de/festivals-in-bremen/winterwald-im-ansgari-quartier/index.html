Winterwald im Ansgari-Quartier

Christmas market · Bremen, Mitte · 23 November – 23 December 2026

When23 November – 23 December 2026
WhereAnsgarikirchhof, Mitte, 28195 Bremen
TypeChristmas market
StateBremen

Winterwald im Ansgari-Quartier invites visitors to enjoy winter entertainment on the Ansgarikirchhof in Bremen city center. Illuminated fir trees and lovingly decorated wooden huts create a special atmosphere against the historic backdrop of the Handwerkskammer. The culinary offering ranges from mulled wine and coffee to hearty grilled dishes, crêpes and desserts.
