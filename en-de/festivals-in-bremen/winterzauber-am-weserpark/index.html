Winterzauber am Weserpark

Christmas market · Bremen, Osterholz · 20 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November 2026 – 3 January 2027
WhereWeserpark, Hans-Bredow-Straße, Osterholz, 28327 Bremen
TypeChristmas market
StateBremen

The Winterzauber am Weserpark is a Christmas-themed market at the Weserpark shopping center in the east of Bremen. Its attractions include a nostalgic carousel and a fairy-tale world featuring motifs such as 'Frau Holle' and 'Die Schneekönigin'. Festively decorated stalls offer roasted almonds, Glühwein and waffles.
