Cochemer Burgweihnacht

Historical festival · Cochem · 12–13 December 2026

When12–13 December 2026
WhereReichsburg Cochem, 56812 Cochem
TypeHistorical festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Cochemer Burgweihnacht brings the Christmas story to life on the medieval Reichsburg with performers and live animals. Against the backdrop of the castle enthroned high above the Moselle, a festive Christmas atmosphere emerges on one Advent weekend.
