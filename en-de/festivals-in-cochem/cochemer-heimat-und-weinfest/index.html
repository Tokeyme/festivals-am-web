Cochemer Heimat- und Weinfest

Wine festival · Cochem · 27–31 August 2026

When27–31 August 2026
WhereMoselpromenade / Marktplatz, 56812 Cochem
TypeWine festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Cochemer Heimat- und Weinfest on the last weekend in August is one of the largest wine festivals on the Moselle. Moselle wines are served on the Moselle promenade and the market square; the highlights are the large festival parade on Sunday and a closing fireworks display over the river.
