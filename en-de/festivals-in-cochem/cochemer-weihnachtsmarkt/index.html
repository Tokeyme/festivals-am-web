Cochemer Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Cochem · 27 November – 20 December 2026

When27 November – 20 December 2026
WhereEndertplatz / Altstadt, 56812 Cochem
TypeChristmas market
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Cochem Christmas market fills the Endertplatz as well as the squares and winding lanes of the old town below the Reichsburg. At festively decorated wooden huts, regional delicacies and handicrafts are on offer, while the lighting along the river promenade and in the town center creates a pre-Christmas atmosphere.
