Cochemer Weinherbst

Wine festival · Cochem · 30–31 October 2026

When30–31 October 2026
WhereEndertplatz, 56812 Cochem
TypeWine festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

To round off the season, Cochem celebrates once more at the Federweißenfest. In the large marquee on the Endertplatz, the young wine takes centre stage. The town of Cochem gives the first two November weekends for it; the first of these already begins on the last weekend in October.
