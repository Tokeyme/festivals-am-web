Cochemer Weinlagenfest

Wine festival · Cochem, Cond · 3–5 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When3–5 July 2026
WhereMoselufer Cond, Cond, 56812 Cochem
TypeWine festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

At the Cochemer Weinlagenfest, local winemakers present their wines on the banks of the Moselle in the Cond district, with a view of the old town and the Reichsburg. In a summer atmosphere, regional wines and culinary specialties are on offer right by the river.
