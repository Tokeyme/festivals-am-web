Nikolausmarkt

Market · Cochem · 9 December 2026

When9 December 2026
WhereMarkt 1, 56812 Cochem
TypeMarket
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Nikolausmarkt is one of the traditional Krammärkte (general markets) of the town of Cochem. The town lists it in its events calendar under the Krammärkte category and gives the Markt in the old town as its location.
