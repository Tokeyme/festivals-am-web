Oster- & Frühlingsmarkt

Market · Cochem · 13–14 March 2027

When13–14 March 2027
WhereEndertplatz, 56812 Cochem
TypeMarket
StateRhineland-Palatinate

Shortly after the start of spring, the town of Cochem holds a market themed around spring and Easter. Exhibitors offer seasonal goods as well as food and drink. On Sunday, the shops in the town centre are open for a verkaufsoffener (shopping) afternoon.
