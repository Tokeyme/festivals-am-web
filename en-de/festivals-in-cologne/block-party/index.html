Block-Party

Street festival · Cologne, Neustadt-Süd · 12–13 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12–13 September 2026
WhereLindenstraße, Neustadt-Süd, 50674 Cologne
TypeStreet festival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Block-Party is a neighbourhood festival in the Kwartier Latäng around Lindenstraße, under the motto 'we know each other, we help each other'. There is a BBQ, drinks and cake, live music and DJ sets, a flea market and activities for children; the proceeds go to charitable causes.
