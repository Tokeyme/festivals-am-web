Deutzer Adventsklaaf

Christmas market · Cologne, Deutz · 4–7 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhereKirchplatz St. Heribert, Deutzer Freiheit, Deutz, 50679 Cologne
TypeChristmas market
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Deutzer Adventsklaaf (Düxer Adventsklaaf) is the Christmas market in Köln-Deutz on the church square of St Heribert on the Deutzer Freiheit. Deutz's clubs offer Glühwein, Kinderpunsch, Reibekuchen and arts and crafts, as well as a programme for young and old.
