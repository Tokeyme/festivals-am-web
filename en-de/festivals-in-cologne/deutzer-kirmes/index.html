Deutzer Kirmes

Funfair · Cologne, Deutz · 24 October – 1 November 2026

When24 October – 1 November 2026
WhereDeutzer Werft, Deutz, 50679 Cologne
TypeFunfair
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

An autumn funfair on the Deutz quayside on the right bank of the Rhine, between the Deutz and Severin bridges. Around 80 showmen set up rides and stalls there. Food and drink come from snack and beer stands and a beer garden with a view of the Rhine.
