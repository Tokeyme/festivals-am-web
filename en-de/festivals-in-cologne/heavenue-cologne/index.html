HEAVENUE Cologne

Christmas market · Cologne, Neustadt-Nord · 16 November – 23 December 2026

When16 November – 23 December 2026
WhereFriesenplatz, Neustadt-Nord, 50672 Cologne
TypeChristmas market
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

A Christmas market on Friesenplatz with stands from local brands and independent makers selling gifts, home accessories and candles. It includes the Heaven&Hell mulled wine house and a stage with a daily programme of live acts, drag shows and DJs. Light installations, street food and music shape the site.
