Heinzels Wintermärchen

Christmas market · Cologne, Altstadt-Nord · 23 November – 23 December 2026

When23 November – 23 December 2026
WhereAlter Markt / Heumarkt, Altstadt-Nord, 50667 Cologne
TypeChristmas market
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

Heinzels Wintermärchen is the Christmas market on the Alter Markt and the Heumarkt in Cologne's old town. The lanes are arranged by theme, among them a crafts lane, a sweets lane and a toy lane, and the Heumarkt also has a large ice rink.
