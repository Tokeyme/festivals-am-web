Kölner Geisterzug

Carnival · Cologne · 30 January 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When30 January 2027
Wherewechselnder Zugweg, Cologne
TypeCarnival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Kölner Geisterzug is an alternative, non-commercial carnival procession that winds through different Cologne districts on the Saturday evening before Weiberfastnacht. Participants dress up as ghosts and mythical creatures; the procession sees itself as the continuation of an old tradition and leaves room for socially critical undertones.
