Kölner Rosenmontagszug

Carnival · Cologne, Innenstadt · 8 February 2027

When8 February 2027
WhereZugweg Innenstadt, Innenstadt, 50667 Cologne
TypeCarnival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Kölner Rosenmontagszug is the highlight of Cologne's street carnival and one of the largest carnival processions in Germany. With floats, marching groups on foot, dance troupes and bands, it moves through the city centre; each year more than a million people watch it from the roadside.
