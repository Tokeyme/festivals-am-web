Markt der Engel

Christmas market · Cologne, Altstadt-Nord · 16 November – 23 December 2026

When16 November – 23 December 2026
WhereNeumarkt, Altstadt-Nord, 50667 Cologne
TypeChristmas market
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Markt der Engel on the Neumarkt is one of Cologne's best-known Christmas markets. The stalls stand in rows beneath a starry sky of white lights, while choirs, the Nikolaus and Christmas angels create a festive mood in the run-up to Christmas.
