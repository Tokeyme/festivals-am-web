Nikolausdorf am Rudolfplatz

Christmas market · Cologne, Neustadt-Süd · 16 November – 23 December 2026

When16 November – 23 December 2026
WhereRudolfplatz, Neustadt-Süd, 50674 Cologne
TypeChristmas market
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Nikolausdorf am Rudolfplatz is a cosy Christmas market in the shadow of the historic Hahnentorburg. Arts and crafts, food and drink are offered in small half-timbered huts.
