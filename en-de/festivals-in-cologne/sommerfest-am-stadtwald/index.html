Sommerfest am Stadtwald

Neighbourhood festival · Cologne, Braunsfeld · 3–6 September 2026

TypeNeighbourhood festival

The Sommerfest am Stadtwald is a culinary summer festival in the Kölner Stadtwald in Braunsfeld. Food trucks, drinks and wines, a stage programme, a children's carousel and comfortable seating invite you to linger.
