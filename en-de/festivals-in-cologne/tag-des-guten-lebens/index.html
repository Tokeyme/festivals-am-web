Tag des guten Lebens

Neighbourhood festival · Cologne, Neustadt-Süd · 13 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When13 September 2026
Whererund um den Rathenauplatz, Neustadt-Süd, 50674 Cologne
TypeNeighbourhood festival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Tag des guten Lebens is a car-free day of action run by the Agora Köln initiative, on which a Veedel belongs to its residents and local clubs for one day. It is planned for the area around Rathenauplatz and Roonstraße, where streets become spaces for meeting, play and ideas.
