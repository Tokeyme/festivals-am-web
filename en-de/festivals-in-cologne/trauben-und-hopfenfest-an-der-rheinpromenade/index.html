Trauben- und Hopfenfest an der Rheinpromenade

Wine festival · Cologne, Altstadt-Nord · 14–16 August 2026

When14–16 August 2026
WhereRheinpromenade, Konrad-Adenauer-Ufer, Altstadt-Nord, 50667 Cologne
TypeWine festival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

At the Trauben- und Hopfenfest on the Rheinpromenade between the Hohenzollernbrücke and the Bastei, regional and international breweries and wineries present selected specialities. There are also Cologne-style Rievkooche, Alsatian Flammkuchen and homemade quiches against the backdrop of the Rhine panorama.
