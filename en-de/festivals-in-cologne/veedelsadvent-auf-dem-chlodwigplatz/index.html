Veedelsadvent auf dem Chlodwigplatz

Christmas market · Cologne, Altstadt-Süd · 19 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When19 November – 23 December 2026
WhereChlodwigplatz, Altstadt-Süd, 50678 Cologne
TypeChristmas market
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Veedelsadvent on Chlodwigplatz is the Christmas market of Cologne's Südstadt and is organised by the association of Südstadt business owners. Around 20 stalls in the shadow of the Severinstorburg offer mulled wine, arts and crafts, and design; a children's carousel and local clubs round off the programme.
