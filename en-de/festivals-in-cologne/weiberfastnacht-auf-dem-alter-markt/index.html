Weiberfastnacht auf dem Alter Markt

Carnival · Cologne, Altstadt-Nord · 4 February 2027

When4 February 2027
WhereAlter Markt, Altstadt-Nord, 50667 Cologne
TypeCarnival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

On Weiberfastnacht, the street carnival is opened in Cologne. On the Alter Markt in the old town, a large open-air carnival session takes place, traditionally hosted by Altstädter Köln 1922 e.V. Costumed revellers fill the squares and lanes of the city centre.
