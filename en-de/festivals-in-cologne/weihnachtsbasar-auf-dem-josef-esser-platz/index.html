Weihnachtsbasar auf dem Josef-Esser-Platz

Christmas market · Cologne, Bickendorf · 28–29 November 2026

When28–29 November 2026
WhereJosef-Esser-Platz, Bickendorf, 50827 Cologne
TypeChristmas market
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

A Christmas bazaar on Josef-Esser-Platz in the Cologne district of Bickendorf. It runs over one Advent weekend and is one of the neighborhood Christmas markets listed by the city portal koeln.de.
