Weihnachtsmarkt im Stadtgarten

Christmas market · Cologne, Neustadt-Nord · 16 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When16 November – 23 December 2026
WhereStadtgarten, Venloer Straße 40, Neustadt-Nord, 50672 Cologne
TypeChristmas market
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Weihnachtsmarkt im Stadtgarten in the Belgisches Viertel is one of Cologne's popular smaller Weihnachtsmärkte. In decorated huts, more than 80 stalls offer design items, delicacies, jewellery, regional products and homemade mulled wine.
