Advents-Galopprennen

Sports event · Dresden, Seidnitz · 6 December 2026

When6 December 2026
WhereOskar-Röder-Straße 1, Seidnitz, 01237 Dresden
TypeSports event
StateSaxony

With this race day in Advent, the Dresdener Rennverein 1890 closes the flat racing season at its course in Seidnitz. It is a winter date for anyone who wants to experience horse racing in the cold season. Advance sales have already begun, and the supporting programme and visitor information are to follow on the club's website.
