Augustusmarkt - Neustädter Advent

Christmas market · Dresden, Innere Neustadt · 25 November – 31 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When25 November – 31 December 2026
WhereInnere Neustadt, 01097 Dresden
TypeChristmas market
StateSachsen

Dresden's second-largest Christmas market as a 500-meter-long strolling market on Hauptstraße in the Neustadt with international flair, Saxon handicrafts, and gold and white illuminated pagodas. Open until New Year's Eve (closed December 24–26).
