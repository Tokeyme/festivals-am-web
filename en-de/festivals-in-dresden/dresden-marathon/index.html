Dresden-Marathon

Sports event · Dresden · 24–25 October 2026

When24–25 October 2026
Where01069 Dresden
TypeSports event
StateSaxony

The Dresden-Marathon runs along a flat, fast course through the Saxon state capital, passing well-known landmarks. Alongside the marathon, a marathon relay, a half marathon, a 10-km run, a 5-km run and children's runs are also on offer.
