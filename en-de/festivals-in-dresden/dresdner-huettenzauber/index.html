Dresdner Hüttenzauber

Christmas market · Dresden, Wilsdruffer Vorstadt · 19 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When19 November 2026 – 3 January 2027
WhereOstra-Allee 18, Wilsdruffer Vorstadt, 01067 Dresden
TypeChristmas market
StateSaxony

The Dresdner Hüttenzauber is a Weihnachtsmarkt with an après-ski atmosphere at the Haus der Presse on Ostra-Allee, near Postplatz. Across more than 2,000 square metres there is catering, a chalet bar, an event chalet and a Bavarian curling rink. The market is especially popular with a younger crowd.
