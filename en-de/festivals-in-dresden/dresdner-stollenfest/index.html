Dresdner Stollenfest

Historical festival · Dresden, Altstadt · 5 December 2026

When5 December 2026
WhereAltmarkt, Altstadt, 01067 Dresden
TypeHistorical festival
StateSaxony

The Dresdner Stollenfest is a highlight of the Striezelmarkt. A giant Stollen weighing around three tonnes is pulled on a historic horse-drawn cart in a festive procession through the historic old town and then ceremonially cut.
