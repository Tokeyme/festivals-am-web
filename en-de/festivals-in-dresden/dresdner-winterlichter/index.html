Dresdner Winterlichter

Christmas market · Dresden, Seevorstadt · 25 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When25 November – 23 December 2026
WherePrager Straße, Seevorstadt, 01069 Dresden
TypeChristmas market
StateSaxony

The Weihnachtsmarkt Dresdner Winterlichter stretches along Prager Straße between the Hauptbahnhof and the Altmarkt. Between the Hotel Pullman and the Centrum Galerie, visitors will find traders, food outlets and hot drinks, as well as a Ferris wheel, a carousel and an ice rink.
