Finn Village – Finnischer Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Dresden, Altstadt · 26 November – 22 December 2026

When26 November – 22 December 2026
WhereDr.-Külz-Ring, Altstadt, 01067 Dresden
TypeChristmas market
StateSaxony

The Finnish Weihnachtsmarkt Finn Village is located on the Dr.-Külz-Ring on the south side of the Altmarkt-Galerie. Visitors can expect a Nordic atmosphere with Finnish specialities such as smoked salmon and Finnish mulled wine (Glögi), a Nordic tipi and a campfire.
