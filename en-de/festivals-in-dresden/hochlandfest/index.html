Hochlandfest

Village & club fest · Dresden, Schönfeld · 12–13 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12–13 September 2026
WhereSchloss Schönfeld, Schönfeld, 01328 Dresden
TypeVillage & club fest
StateSaxony

Hochlandfest is a festival in the Schönfelder Hochland in the east of Dresden, set against the backdrop of the Renaissance Schloss Schönfeld. The programme includes, among other things, tours of the castle as well as music and activities for the whole family.
