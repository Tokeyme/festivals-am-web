Jazztage Dresden

Music festival · Dresden · 2 October – 29 November 2026

When2 October – 29 November 2026
WhereDresden
TypeMusic festival
StateSaxony

Jazztage Dresden is a music festival with concerts at various venues around the city. Over several weeks in autumn, national and international artists perform, offering a varied program of jazz and related styles.
