Keramikmarkt Dresden

Market · Dresden, Innere Neustadt · 5–6 September 2026

When5–6 September 2026
WhereHauptstraße / Goldener Reiter, Innere Neustadt, 01097 Dresden
TypeMarket
StateSaxony

The Keramikmarkt Dresden takes place around the Goldener Reiter on the Hauptstraße. Around 70 selected workshops present the full range of ceramic design, from simple everyday ceramics through finely printed porcelain to experimental surfaces, figurative garden art and jewellery.
