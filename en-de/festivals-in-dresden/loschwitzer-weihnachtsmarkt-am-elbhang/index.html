Loschwitzer Weihnachtsmarkt am Elbhang

Christmas market · Dresden, Loschwitz · 5–20 December 2026

When5–20 December 2026
WhereFriedrich-Wieck-Straße, Loschwitz, 01326 Dresden
TypeChristmas market
StateSaxony

The Loschwitzer Weihnachtsmarkt am Elbhang takes place right by the Blaues Wunder on Friedrich-Wieck-Straße in the Loschwitz district. Selected artisans and caterers present creative wares; choirs, a cappella Advent concerts and music accompany the market.
