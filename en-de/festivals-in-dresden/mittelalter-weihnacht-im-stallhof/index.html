Mittelalter-Weihnacht im Stallhof

Christmas market · Dresden, Altstadt · 25 November – 23 December 2026

When25 November – 23 December 2026
WhereAltstadt, 01067 Dresden
TypeChristmas market
StateSaxony

Medieval Christmas market in the historic Stallhof courtyard of the Residenzschloss palace, featuring jesters, stilt-walkers, storytellers, and harp and fanfare players.
