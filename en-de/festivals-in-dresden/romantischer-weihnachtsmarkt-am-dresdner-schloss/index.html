Romantischer Weihnachtsmarkt am Dresdner Schloss

Christmas market · Dresden, Altstadt · 26 November – 24 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When26 November – 24 December 2026
WhereTaschenberg / Schloßstraße, Altstadt, 01067 Dresden
TypeChristmas market
StateSaxony

The Romantischer Weihnachtsmarkt takes place on the Piazza on the south side of the Dresdner Residenzschloss. Historically styled market stalls in the manner of the 19th century offer Erzgebirge folk art and Dresdner Christstollen in a festive atmosphere.
