SemperOpernball

Event · Dresden, Altstadt · 5 February 2027

When5 February 2027
WhereTheaterplatz 2, Altstadt, 01067 Dresden
TypeEvent
StateSaxony

The SemperOpernball is a ball in the Semperoper with classical music and artistic performances. On the Theaterplatz in front of the opera house, a free open-air ball takes place at the same time, with dancing and celebration.
