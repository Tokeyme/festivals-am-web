Bilker Martinszug

Event · Düsseldorf, Bilk · 10 November 2026

When10 November 2026
WhereSiegplatz, Bilk, 40223 Düsseldorf
TypeEvent
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Bilker Martinszug is one of Düsseldorf's long-established Martinszüge. Led by St Martin on horseback, children parade through the Bilk district with lanterns they have made themselves. The starting point is Siegplatz.
