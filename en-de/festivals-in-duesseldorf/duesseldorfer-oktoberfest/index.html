Düsseldorfer Oktoberfest

Funfair · Düsseldorf, Eller · 10–26 September 2026

When10–26 September 2026
WhereSchützenplatz Eller, Heidelberger Straße 4, Eller, 40229 Düsseldorf
TypeFunfair
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

A large marquee on the Schuetzenplatz in Duesseldorf-Eller hosts an Oktoberfest with Bavarian specialities, live music and party and entertainment acts. VIP and table packages are available for groups.
