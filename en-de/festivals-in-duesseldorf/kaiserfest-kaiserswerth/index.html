Kaiserfest Kaiserswerth

Neighbourhood festival · Düsseldorf, Kaiserswerth · 12–13 September 2026

When12–13 September 2026
WhereKaiserswerther Markt, Kaiserswerth, 40489 Düsseldorf
TypeNeighbourhood festival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Kaiserfest is the district festival in the historic old centre of Düsseldorf-Kaiserswerth. Around the Kaiserswerther Markt, the Klemensviertel and the banks of the Rhine, there is a programme of stalls and music. It recalls the town's imperial history.
