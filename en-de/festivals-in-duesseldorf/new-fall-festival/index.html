New Fall Festival

Music festival · Düsseldorf, Pempelfort · 7–11 October 2026

When7–11 October 2026
WhereRheinterrasse, Joseph-Beuys-Ufer 33, Pempelfort, 40479 Düsseldorf
TypeMusic festival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The New Fall Festival showcases pop and indie music at distinctive venues across Düsseldorf, including the Rheinterrasse, the Schauspielhaus and the Henkel-Saal. Rather than a conventional open-air stage, the festival makes use of unusual cultural spaces.
