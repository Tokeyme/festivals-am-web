Tonnenrennen Niederkassel

Carnival · Düsseldorf, Niederkassel · 7 February 2027

When7 February 2027
WhereNiederkasseler Straße, Niederkassel, 40547 Düsseldorf
TypeCarnival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

On Carnival Sunday, a procession and the traditional Tonnenrennen take place in the Niederkassel district. Runners compete against one another with barrels along Niederkasseler Straße. The custom is staged by the Tonnengarde Niederkassel 1887.
