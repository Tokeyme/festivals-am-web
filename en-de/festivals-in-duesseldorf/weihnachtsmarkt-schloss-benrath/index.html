Weihnachtsmarkt Schloss Benrath

Christmas market · Düsseldorf, Benrath · 27 November – 20 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 November – 20 December 2026
WhereBenrather Schlossallee 100-108, Benrath, 40597 Düsseldorf
TypeChristmas market
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Christmas market in front of the Baroque Schloss Benrath takes place in the Benrath district. Against the backdrop of the palace, there are arts and crafts, festive lighting and food stalls. For children, a little train runs.
