Weihnachtsmarkt vor den Düsseldorf Arcaden in Bilk

Christmas market · Düsseldorf, Bilk · 20 November – 30 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November – 30 December 2026
WhereFriedrichstraße 133, Bilk, 40217 Düsseldorf
TypeChristmas market
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

A small Christmas market on the forecourt of the Duesseldorf Arcaden in Bilk. Around 15 nostalgic-style huts offer crafts and food, and there is a carousel for children.
