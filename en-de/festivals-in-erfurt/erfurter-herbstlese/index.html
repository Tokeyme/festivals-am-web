Erfurter Herbstlese

Culture festival · Erfurt, Altstadt · 22 September – 4 December 2026

When22 September – 4 December 2026
WhereHaus Dacheröden, Anger 37, Altstadt, 99084 Erfurt
TypeCulture festival
StateThuringia

The Erfurter Herbstlese is a literary festival, taking place in 2026 for its 30th edition. Well-known authors read from their work and discuss it at various venues around the city; the anniversary programme comprises around 53 events. The central venue is the Haus Dacheröden on the Anger.
