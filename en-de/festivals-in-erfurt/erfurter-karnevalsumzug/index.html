Erfurter Karnevalsumzug

Carnival · Erfurt, Altstadt · 7 February 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When7 February 2027
WhereDomplatz (Start und Ziel), Altstadt, 99084 Erfurt
TypeCarnival
StateThuringia

On Rosensonntag (the Sunday before Ash Wednesday), the Erfurter Karnevalsumzug (carnival parade) moves with floats and walking groups from the Domplatz through the city centre and back again to the Domplatz. The colourful parade forms the highlight of Erfurt's Fastnacht (the pre-Lenten carnival season) and is free of charge for spectators.
