Erfurter Martinsfest

Event · Erfurt, Altstadt · 10 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When10 November 2026
WhereDomplatz, Altstadt, 99084 Erfurt
TypeEvent
StateThuringia

For the Martinsfest, thousands of children and adults gather with glowing lanterns on Erfurt's Domplatz (Cathedral Square). Against the backdrop of the cathedral and the Severikirche (Church of St Severus), an ecumenical St Martin's celebration is held in memory of Martin of Tours and Martin Luther. The event includes a Martinsspiel (St Martin's play) with a lantern procession as well as a Martinsmarkt selling Martinshörnchen (St Martin's pastries), lanterns and candles.
