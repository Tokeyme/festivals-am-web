Erfurter Winterberg

Event · Erfurt, Altstadt · 23 November 2026 – 8 January 2027

When23 November 2026 – 8 January 2027
WhereZitadelle Petersberg (Ostterrasse), Altstadt, 99084 Erfurt
TypeEvent
StateThuringia

High above the rooftops of Erfurt, the Erfurter Winterberg takes shape on the eastern terrace of the Petersberg, featuring an open-air ice rink. Around the ice surface, which measures 15 by 30 metres, several wooden huts serving warm food and drinks form a wintry village. The dining areas offer a wide view across the city.
