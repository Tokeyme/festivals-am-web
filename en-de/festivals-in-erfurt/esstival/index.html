Esstival

Event · Erfurt, Altstadt · 18 September 2026

When18 September 2026
WhereWenigemarkt / Krämerbrücke, Altstadt, 99084 Erfurt
TypeEvent
StateThuringia

Around the Krämerbrücke and on the Wenigemarkt, the Esstival combines culinary highlights, light installations and live music into a food festival in the heart of the old town. Following its premiere in 2024, Erfurt's restaurants and caterers are inviting visitors to feast and linger for the third time.
