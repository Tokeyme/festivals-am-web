Florales zur Weihnachtszeit

Event · Erfurt, Altstadt · 12 November – 30 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12 November – 30 December 2026
WhereFelsenkeller am Domberg, Altstadt, 99084 Erfurt
TypeEvent
StateThuringia

For more than 30 years, florists from central Germany have decorated the historic Felsenkeller at Erfurt's Domberg during the Christmas season. Elaborate floral Christmas decorations are created in the medieval vaulted niches. The egapark exhibition is one of the city's established Advent attractions.
