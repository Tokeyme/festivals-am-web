Herbst- und Cerealienmarkt

Market · Erfurt, Altstadt · 18–19 September 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When18–19 September 2026
WhereDomplatz, Altstadt, 99084 Erfurt
TypeMarket
StateThuringia

On the Domplatz (Cathedral Square), nurseries and traders from Erfurt and Thuringia offer everything for autumn and winter planting of gardens, balconies and terraces. The market is complemented by a farmers' market with regional fruit and vegetables as well as Thuringian sausages from the charcoal grill. The market's name goes back to Ceres, the Roman goddess of agriculture. Live music and a children's program accompany the market weekend.
