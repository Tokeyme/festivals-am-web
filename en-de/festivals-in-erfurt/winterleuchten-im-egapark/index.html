Winterleuchten im egapark

Event · Erfurt · 27 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 November 2026 – 3 January 2027
Whereegapark, Gothaer Straße 38, 99094 Erfurt
TypeEvent
StateThuringia

On the evenings of the Advent season, the egapark is transformed into a world of light and colour. Along a circular route of roughly three kilometres, hundreds of spotlights, video projections and artful light objects illuminate the wintry park. The Winterleuchten is one of the highlights of Erfurt's winter season.
