Brucker Faschingszug

Carnival · Erlangen, Bruck · 7 February 2027

When7 February 2027
WhereMarktplatz Bruck, Bruck, 91058 Erlangen
TypeCarnival
StateBavaria

The carnival society Die Brucker Gasshenker holds its procession on Carnival Sunday through the Erlangen district of Bruck. It sets off at 2 p.m. from the market square in Bruck. More than 50 groups take part and around 60,000 visitors are expected.
