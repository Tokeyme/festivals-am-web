Berger Markt

Market · Frankfurt am Main, Bergen-Enkheim · 29 August – 2 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhereFestplatz Bergen-Enkheim, Marktstraße, Bergen-Enkheim, 60388 Frankfurt am Main
TypeMarket
StateHesse

A traditional market in the Frankfurt district of Bergen-Enkheim and one of the oldest markets in the Rhine-Main region. The Berger Markt begins on the Saturday before the first Tuesday in September and ends five days later with a large firework display. The festival features a festival procession, fairground rides, a Krammarkt and the appointment of the Stadtschreiber of Bergen-Enkheim.
