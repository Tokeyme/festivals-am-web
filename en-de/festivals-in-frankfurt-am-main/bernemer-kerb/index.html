Bernemer Kerb

Church / parish fest · Frankfurt am Main, Bornheim · 7–12 August 2026

When7–12 August 2026
WhereBornheim, rund um die Johanniskirche, Bornheim, 60385 Frankfurt am Main
TypeChurch / parish fest
StateHesse

Traditional church dedication festival (Kerwe) in the Bornheim district with a festival procession, kerwe tree, and traditional customs like Gickelschmiss. One of Frankfurt's oldest district festivals.
