Großer Frankfurter Fastnachtszug

Carnival · Frankfurt am Main · 7 February 2027

When7 February 2027
WhereUntermainkai, 60329 Frankfurt am Main
TypeCarnival
StateHesse

The main Frankfurt carnival procession moves through the city center. It assembles and sets off at the Untermainkai, and there is a grandstand for spectators on the Roemerberg. The organizer is the Grosser Rat der Karnevalvereine Frankfurt am Main.
