Höchster Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Frankfurt am Main, Höchst · 28–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28–29 November 2026
WhereSchlossplatz und Justinusplatz, Höchst, 65929 Frankfurt am Main
TypeChristmas market
StateHesse

A Weihnachtsmarkt in the old town of Frankfurt-Höchst around Schlossplatz and Justinusplatz. Against the backdrop of the castle and the Justinuskirche, traders and local clubs offer mostly handmade goods. A medieval Weihnachtsmarkt is also held in the castle moat.
