Mainova Frankfurt Marathon

Sports event · Frankfurt am Main · 25 October 2026

When25 October 2026
WhereFriedrich-Ebert-Anlage, 60327 Frankfurt am Main
TypeSports event
StateHesse

An international city marathon over 42.195 kilometres, starting at the Friedrich-Ebert-Anlage and finishing in the Festhalle. The flat, fast course runs through the city centre, across the Eiserner Steg and through the Frankfurter Stadtwald.
