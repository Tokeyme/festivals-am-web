Sachsenhäuser Brunnenfest

Neighbourhood festival · Frankfurt am Main, Sachsenhausen · 14–16 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When14–16 August 2026
WhereParadiesplatz, Sachsenhausen, 60594 Frankfurt am Main
TypeNeighbourhood festival
StateHesse

One of Frankfurt's oldest festivals, traditionally held on the third weekend of August on Paradiesplatz in Alt-Sachsenhausen. The programme includes the crowning of the Brunnenkönigin, the Brunnengang and a children's festival on the Sunday. Clubs and caterers from Sachsenhausen provide the food and drink.
