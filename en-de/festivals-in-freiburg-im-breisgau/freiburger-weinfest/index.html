Freiburger Weinfest

Wine festival · Freiburg im Breisgau, Altstadt · 2–7 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When2–7 July 2026
WhereMünsterplatz, Altstadt, 79098 Freiburg im Breisgau
TypeWine festival
StateBaden-Württemberg
