Freiburger Weinfest

Wine festival · Freiburg im Breisgau, Altstadt · 2–7 July 2026

When2–7 July 2026
WhereMünsterplatz, Altstadt, 79098 Freiburg im Breisgau
TypeWine festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

For six days, Münsterplatz celebrates Baden wine. Around 60 regional businesses offer over 400 wines and sparkling wines, plus regional cuisine. Opening on July 2 at 6 PM in front of the Historical Warehouse.
