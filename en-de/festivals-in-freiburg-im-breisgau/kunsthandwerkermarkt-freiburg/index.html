Kunsthandwerkermarkt Freiburg

Market · Freiburg im Breisgau, Altstadt · 3–5 September 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When3–5 September 2026
WhereObere Altstadt zwischen Münster und Augustinerplatz, Altstadt, 79098 Freiburg im Breisgau
TypeMarket
StateBaden-Württemberg

The Kunsthandwerkermarkt (arts and crafts market) is set up in Freiburg's upper old town between the Münster and Augustinerplatz. Craftspeople show their work there and offer it for sale.
