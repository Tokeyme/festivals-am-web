Festival vielsaitig

Music festival · Füssen · 2–9 September 2026

When2–9 September 2026
WhereKaisersaal, Kloster St. Mang, Lechhalde 3, 87629 Füssen
TypeMusic festival
StateBavaria

International chamber music festival in the baroque Kaisersaal of St. Mang Abbey. Seven concerts combine classical music and jazz, early and new music, connecting to Füssen's heritage as the cradle of European lute and violin making.
