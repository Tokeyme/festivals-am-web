Füssener Stadtfest (Streetfestival)

Town festival · Füssen · 31 July – 1 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When31 July – 1 August 2026
WhereAltstadt, 87629 Füssen
TypeTown festival
StateBavaria

For two days, Füssen's old town turns into a festival mile with international street performers. In the squares of the old town there is theater, acrobatics and dance, plus music ranging from DJ sets through rock, pop and Schlager to Latin, as well as walking acts. The food vendors offer regional specialties and international cuisine; admission is free.
