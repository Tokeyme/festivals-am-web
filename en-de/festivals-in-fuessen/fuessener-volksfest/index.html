Füssener Volksfest

Funfair · Füssen · 3–14 June 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

WhereFestplatz an der Kemptener Straße, 87629 Füssen

The Füssener Volksfest begins on the eve of Corpus Christi and lasts twelve days. A festival tent, fairground rides and a varied supporting program with senior and family days provide entertainment.
