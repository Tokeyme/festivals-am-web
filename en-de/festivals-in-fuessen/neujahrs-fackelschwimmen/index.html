Neujahrs-Fackelschwimmen

Event · Füssen · 1 January 2027

When1 January 2027
WhereLechufer an der Theresienbrücke, 87629 Füssen
TypeEvent
StateBavaria

At the start of the year, lifeguards from the Wasserwachten (water rescue services) drift down the Lech past the old town by torchlight. As they pass the Theresienbrücke, Böllerschützen (ceremonial gun-salute shooters) fire a salute. Spectators can watch from the bank of the Lech or from the bridge below St Mang's Abbey.
