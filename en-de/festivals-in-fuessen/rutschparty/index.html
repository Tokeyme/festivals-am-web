Rutschparty

Event · Füssen · 30 December 2026

When30 December 2026
WhereKaiser-Maximilian-Platz, 87629 Füssen
TypeEvent
StateBavaria

Open-air party shortly before the turn of the year on the Kaiser-Maximilian-Platz in front of the Tourist Info. DJs and a live band provide the music, and admission is free. Drinks and snacks are available around the dance floor.
