Fuldaer Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Fulda · 20 November – 23 December 2026

When20 November – 23 December 2026
WhereUniversitätsplatz, 36037 Fulda
TypeChristmas market
StateThuringia

Fulda's town centre turns into a Christmas city with several themed markets. Besides the traditional Christmas market there is the Kinder-Weihnachtsland with a Ferris wheel and carousel, a medieval Christmas village, the festive regio'markt with food from the Rhön, and the Winterwald. The markets are spread across Uniplatz, Museumshof, Unterm Heilig Kreuz and Buttermarkt.
