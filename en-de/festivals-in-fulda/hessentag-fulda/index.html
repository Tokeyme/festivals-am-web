Hessentag Fulda

Town festival · Fulda · 12–21 June 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When12–21 June 2026
Where36037 Fulda
TypeTown festival

The 63rd Hessentag turns the baroque city of Fulda into a large festival stage for ten days under the motto FD - Foll hessisch. On offer are around 1,200 experiences with stage programmes, a festival parade, experience worlds and city-centre celebrations.
