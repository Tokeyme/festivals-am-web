Weihnachtsmarkt Fulda

Christmas market · Fulda · 20 November – 23 December 2026

When20 November – 23 December 2026
Where36037 Fulda
TypeChristmas market
StateThuringia

Fulda's Christmas market comprises five themed areas: a traditional market with a large pyramid, a children's Christmas land, a medieval Christmas village, a regional food market with Rhön specialities and an illuminated winter forest. Closed on 22 November.
