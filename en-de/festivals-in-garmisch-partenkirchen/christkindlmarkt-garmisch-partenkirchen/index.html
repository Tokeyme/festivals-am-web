Christkindlmarkt Garmisch-Partenkirchen

Christmas market · Garmisch-Partenkirchen · 20 November 2026 – 5 January 2027

When20 November 2026 – 5 January 2027
WhereMichael-Ende-Kurpark, 82467 Garmisch-Partenkirchen
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

The Christkindlmarkt in the Michael-Ende-Kurpark offers stalls with handicrafts, an ice-skating rink and atmospheric light installations. Evening performances by regional music groups accompany the Advent season.
