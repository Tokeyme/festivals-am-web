Garmischer Festwoche

Funfair · Garmisch-Partenkirchen, Garmisch · 25 July – 2 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When25 July – 2 August 2026
WhereWittelsbacher Park, Garmisch, 82467 Garmisch-Partenkirchen
TypeFunfair
StateBavaria

The festival week of the traditional costume association transforms the Wittelsbacher Park into a large festival-tent event with brass music, Schuhplattln and Bavarian tradition. In 2026, the festival is combined with a Gaufest of the Oberländer Trachtenvereinigung.
