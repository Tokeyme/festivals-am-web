Mountain Roots

Music festival · Garmisch-Partenkirchen · 26 September 2026

When26 September 2026
WhereMichael-Ende-Kurpark, 82467 Garmisch-Partenkirchen
TypeMusic festival
StateBavaria

A festival in the Michael-Ende-Kurpark with live bands and a western atmosphere. There is a bull-riding contest and lasso throwing, plus face painting and a treasure hunt for children. Food trucks and a straw-bale bar take care of the catering.
