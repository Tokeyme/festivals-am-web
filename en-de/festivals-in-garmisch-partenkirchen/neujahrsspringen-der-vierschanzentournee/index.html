Neujahrsspringen der Vierschanzentournee

Sports event · Garmisch-Partenkirchen, Partenkirchen · 1 January 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When1 January 2026
WhereGroße Olympiaschanze, Partenkirchen, 82467 Garmisch-Partenkirchen
TypeSports event
StateBavaria

The New Year's ski jump on the Große Olympiaschanze is the second stop of the Vierschanzentournee and traditionally takes place on 1 January. Tens of thousands of spectators watch the World Cup ski jumping competition, which is broadcast live on television.
