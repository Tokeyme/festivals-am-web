Partenkirchener Festwoche

Funfair · Garmisch-Partenkirchen, Partenkirchen · 8–17 August 2026

When8–17 August 2026
WhereFestzelt am Schützenhaus, Partenkirchen, 82467 Garmisch-Partenkirchen
TypeFunfair
StateBavaria

In the festival tent at the Schützenhaus in Partenkirchen, the town celebrates its traditional costume and folk culture with brass music, dance and regional cuisine. The festival week follows directly after the Garmischer Festwoche.
