Richard-Strauss-Tage

Music festival · Garmisch-Partenkirchen · 6–14 June 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When6–14 June 2026
WhereRichard-Strauss-Platz und weitere Spielorte, 82467 Garmisch-Partenkirchen
TypeMusic festival
StateBavaria

The festival commemorates the composer Richard Strauss, who lived in Garmisch-Partenkirchen for many years. Over several days, chamber and symphony concerts, song recitals and open-air programs are performed at various venues in the town. In 2026, the program concludes with a chamber version of the opera Salome.
