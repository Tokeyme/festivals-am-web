Straßen.Kunst.Festival

Culture festival · Garmisch-Partenkirchen, Partenkirchen · 19–20 September 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When19–20 September 2026
WherePartenkirchen, 82467 Garmisch-Partenkirchen
TypeCulture festival
StateBavaria

International street performers take to Partenkirchen with acrobatics, music and theater. Admission is free; the performers receive no fixed fee and depend on what the audience puts in the hat. The event is organized by Werbegemeinschaft Partenkirchen erleben e.V. with support from the market town of Garmisch-Partenkirchen.
