Adventsmarkt auf der Vienenburg

Christmas market · Goslar, Vienenburg · 13 December 2026

When13 December 2026
WhereBurgweg 2, Vienenburg, 38690 Goslar
TypeChristmas market
StateLower Saxony

On the third Sunday of Advent, Burg Vienenburg opens its decorated castle courtyard for an Advent market, with hundreds of lights glowing and the keep shining out across the countryside like a candle. Traders offer their wares in the BurgSaal, while the BurgCafé serves coffee and cake. There is also a children's programme and Advent music. Admission is free and no registration is needed.
