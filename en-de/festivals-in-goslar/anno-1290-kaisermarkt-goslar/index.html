ANNO 1290 – Kaisermarkt Goslar

Market · Goslar, Altstadt · 2–4 October 2026

When2–4 October 2026
WhereHistorische Altstadt Goslar (Marktplatz, Schuhhof, Stiftskirche), Altstadt, 38640 Goslar
TypeMarket
StateLower Saxony

Medieval market with period costumes, music and medieval theater, marking the 1290 anniversary (Goslar's elevation to a Free Imperial City). Free admission, shops open on Sunday 4 October 1–6 p.m.
