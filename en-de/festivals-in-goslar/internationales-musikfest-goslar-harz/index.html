Internationales Musikfest Goslar-Harz

Music festival · Goslar, Altstadt/Wöltingerode · 7–23 August 2026

When7–23 August 2026
WhereVerschiedene Spielstätten in Goslar und Wöltingerode (Kaiserpfalz, Stephanikirche, Kloster), Altstadt/Wöltingerode, 38640 Goslar
TypeMusic festival
StateLower Saxony

24th music festival with classical and jazz sections. Concerts ranging from chamber orchestra to jazz (Till Brönner). Opening concert on 7 August, closing concert with an open-air on 23 August at the Kaiserpfalz.
