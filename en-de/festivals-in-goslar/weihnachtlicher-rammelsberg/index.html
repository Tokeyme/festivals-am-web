Weihnachtlicher Rammelsberg

Christmas market · Goslar · 12–13 December 2026

When12–13 December 2026
WhereBergtal 19, 38640 Goslar
TypeChristmas market
StateLower Saxony

On one Advent weekend, Christmas magic spreads above and below ground at the Rammelsberg World Heritage Site. On the grounds of the museum and the visitor mine there is a Christmas market with regional crafts and food and drink. The family programme includes alpacas, story readings with Father Christmas and demonstrations of glassblowing and pewter figure casting.
