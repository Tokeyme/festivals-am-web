Weihnachtsmarkt & Weihnachtswald Goslar

Christmas market · Goslar, Altstadt · 25 November – 30 December 2026

When25 November – 30 December 2026
WhereHistorische Altstadt Goslar (Marktplatz, Kaiserpfalzwiese), Altstadt, 38640 Goslar
TypeChristmas market
StateLower Saxony

One of the most beautiful Christmas markets in Europe, with a Christmas forest (60 illuminated trees, 160,000 lights), 70 wooden huts with specialties and handicrafts, a Ferris wheel and a children's railway. Mon–Sun 10 a.m.–8 p.m.
