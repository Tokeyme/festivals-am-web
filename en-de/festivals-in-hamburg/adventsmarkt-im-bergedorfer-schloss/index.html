Adventsmarkt im Bergedorfer Schloss

Christmas market · Hamburg, Bergedorf · 28–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28–29 November 2026
WhereBergedorfer Schloss, Bergedorfer Schlossstraße 4, 21029 Hamburg, Bergedorf, 21029 Hamburg
TypeChristmas market
StateHamburg

On the first Advent weekend, Bergedorf Castle's hall transforms into an atmospheric Advent market. Local craftspeople display handwork, jewelry, woodwork, honey, and more, while the large Christmas market outside draws visitors. Admission is free.
