Afrikafestival Hamburg Alafia

Event · Hamburg, Altona-Altstadt · 28–30 August 2026

When28–30 August 2026
WhereGroße Bergstraße, 22767 Hamburg, Altona-Altstadt, 22767 Hamburg
TypeEvent
StateHamburg

The Alafia Africa Festival brings African joie de vivre to Große Bergstraße in Altona. The open-air stage features live music, dance, and drums; a large African market with crafts, fashion, and specialties invites browsing and enjoying. The colorful program celebrates cultural exchange in the heart of the neighborhood.
