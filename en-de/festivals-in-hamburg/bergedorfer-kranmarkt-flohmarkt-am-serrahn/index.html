Bergedorfer Kranmarkt (Flohmarkt am Serrahn)

Flea market · Hamburg, Bergedorf · 25 July 2026

When25 July 2026
WhereSerrahnstraße / Alte Holstenstraße, Bergedorfer Hafen, 21029 Hamburg, Bergedorf, 21029 Hamburg
TypeFlea market
StateHamburg

Around the freshly restored harbor crane on Serrahn, haggling and bargaining happen in the charming waterside location. Tables display clothing, books, household goods, and toys; waterside dining adds to the mood.
