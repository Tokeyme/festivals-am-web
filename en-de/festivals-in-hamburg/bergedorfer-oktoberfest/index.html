Bergedorfer Oktoberfest

Funfair · Hamburg, Bergedorf · 18–20 September 2026

When18–20 September 2026
WhereFrascatiplatz, 21029 Hamburg, Bergedorf, 21029 Hamburg
TypeFunfair
StateHamburg

Hamburg's largest Oktoberfest takes place in a large festival tent on Frascati Square. Alongside authentic live music and Paulaner beer, there's a funfair with rides, a children's festival in the afternoon, and fireworks Friday evening.
