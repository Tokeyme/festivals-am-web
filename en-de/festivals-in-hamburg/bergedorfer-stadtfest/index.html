Bergedorfer Stadtfest

Town festival · Hamburg, Bergedorf · 10–12 July 2026

When10–12 July 2026
WhereFrascatiplatz / Bergedorfer Markt / Bahnhofsvorplatz, 21029 Hamburg, Bergedorf, 21029 Hamburg
TypeTown festival
StateHamburg

Hamburg's largest district festival transforms Bergedorf's city center into a colorful festival mile for three days. Multiple stages feature live music, plus food stands, dining, and the popular Festival of Nations. Admission is free.
