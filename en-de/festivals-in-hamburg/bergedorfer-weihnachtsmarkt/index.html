Bergedorfer Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Hamburg, Bergedorf · 20 November – 30 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November – 30 December 2026
WhereAlte Holstenstraße / Kirchenvorplatz / Schlosswiese, 21029 Hamburg, Bergedorf, 21029 Hamburg
TypeChristmas market
StateHamburg

Hamburg's oldest Christmas market lies around Bergedorf Castle and ranks among the city's most beautiful. Cozy wooden houses among small fir groves, bonfires, and Nordic lights create Scandinavian atmosphere from Alte Holstenstraße to the castle lawn.
