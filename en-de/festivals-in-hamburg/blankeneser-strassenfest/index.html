Blankeneser Straßenfest

Neighbourhood festival · Hamburg, Blankenese · 22 August 2026

When22 August 2026
WhereBahnhofstraße / Elbchaussee, 22587 Hamburg, Blankenese, 22587 Hamburg
TypeNeighbourhood festival
StateHamburg

The Blankenese street festival transforms the town center between Bahnhofstraße and Elbchaussee into a colorful festival mile. Local shops, societies, and schools provide live music, stage programs, a flea market, and regional cuisine for lively fun. A family day with children's programs rounds out the action at the Elbe hillside.
