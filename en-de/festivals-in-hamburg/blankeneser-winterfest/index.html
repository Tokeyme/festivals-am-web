Blankeneser Winterfest

Christmas market · Hamburg, Blankenese · 23 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When23 November – 23 December 2026
WhereErik-Blumenfeld-Platz 29, 22587 Hamburg, Blankenese, 22587 Hamburg
TypeChristmas market
StateHamburg

At Blankenese Station on Erik-Blumenfeld Square, the Blankenese Winter Festival invites cozy pre-Christmas time. The mulled wine lounge and stands offer culinary variety from crêpes to bratwurst to Tuscan delicacies. A small train takes children through a lovingly designed winter wonderland.
