ChristGrindelMarkt

Christmas market · Hamburg, Rotherbaum · 16 November – 22 December 2026

When16 November – 22 December 2026
WhereAllendeplatz, 20146 Hamburg, Rotherbaum, 20146 Hamburg
TypeChristmas market
