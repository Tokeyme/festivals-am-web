Eppendorfer Weihnachtsbummel

Christmas market · Hamburg, Eppendorf · 19 November – 30 December 2026

When19 November – 30 December 2026
WhereMarie-Jonas-Platz, Kümmellstraße, 20249 Hamburg, Eppendorf, 20249 Hamburg
TypeChristmas market
StateHamburg

On Marie-Jonas-Platz in the heart of Eppendorf, the Christmas stroll invites a varied program and treats for all ages. Thursdays, a Christmas angel visits children with stories; on St. Nicholas Day, Santa arrives. The centerpiece is a cozy punch hut with mulled wine and hot chocolate.
