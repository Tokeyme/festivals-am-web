Erntedankfest Kirchwerder

Historical festival · Hamburg, Kirchwerder · 3–4 October 2026

When3–4 October 2026
WhereSt.-Severini-Kirche, Kirchwerder Hausdeich, 21037 Hamburg, Kirchwerder, 21037 Hamburg
TypeHistorical festival
StateHamburg

At Germany's largest Thanksgiving parade, elaborately decorated wagons, tractors, and carriages adorned with flowers, fruit, and vegetables process through Kirchwerder. After the service at St. Severini Church, the festival parade starts and draws tens of thousands of visitors to the marshland landscape.
