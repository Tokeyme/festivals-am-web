Flohmarkt auf dem Hof im Goldbekhaus (September-Termin)

Flea market · Hamburg, Winterhude · 6 September 2026

When6 September 2026
WhereGoldbekhaus, Moorfuhrtweg 9, 22301 Hamburg (am Goldbekkanal), Winterhude, 22303 Hamburg
TypeFlea market
StateHamburg

One of Hamburg's most beautiful courtyard flea markets right on the Goldbek Canal. In a charming atmosphere, browsing and haggling runs from 11 a.m. to 4 p.m., with bratwurst and coffee on offer.
