Flohmarkt Neue Große Bergstraße (Altona)

When30 August 2026
WhereNeue Große Bergstraße, 22767 Hamburg, Altona-Altstadt, 22767 Hamburg
TypeFlea market

The late-summer flea market on Neue Große Bergstraße brings secondhand fashion, furniture, and rarities from private sellers to the street. A lively neighborhood stroll with street food between the shops.
