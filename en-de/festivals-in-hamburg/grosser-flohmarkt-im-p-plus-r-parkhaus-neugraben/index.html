Großer Flohmarkt im P+R Parkhaus Neugraben

Flea market · Hamburg, Neugraben-Fischbek · 27 September 2026

When27 September 2026
WhereP+R Parkhaus am Bahnhof Neugraben, 21149 Hamburg, Neugraben-Fischbek, 21149 Hamburg
TypeFlea market
StateHamburg

Twice a year, the weatherproof P+R parking garage right at Neugraben Station becomes a major flea market. On multiple levels, private and commercial stands invite browsing, haggling, and secondhand shopping.
