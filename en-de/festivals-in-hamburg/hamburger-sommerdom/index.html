Hamburger Sommerdom

Funfair · Hamburg, St. Pauli · 24 July – 30 August 2026

When24 July – 30 August 2026
WhereHeiligengeistfeld, 20359 Hamburg, St. Pauli, 20359 Hamburg
TypeFunfair
StateHamburg

Northern Germany's largest folk festival attracts visitors for five weeks at Heiligengeistfeld. Amusement rides, food stalls, and game booths create excitement; every Friday at 10:30 PM there's a large fireworks display. Wednesdays are family days with reduced prices.
