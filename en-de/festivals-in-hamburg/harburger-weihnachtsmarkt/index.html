Harburger Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Hamburg, Harburg · 19 November – 29 December 2026

When19 November – 29 December 2026
WhereHarburger Rathausplatz 1, 21073 Hamburg, Harburg, 21073 Hamburg
TypeChristmas market
StateHamburg

Before the historic backdrop of Harburg's neo-Renaissance Town Hall, a festive Christmas market emerges each year. Lovingly decorated booths, a Ferris wheel, carousel, a giant Advent calendar, and nightly bell-tower trumpet playing create pre-Christmas spirit.
