Holi Festival of Colours Hamburg

Event · Hamburg, Horn · 22 August 2026

When22 August 2026
WhereGalopprennbahn Horn, 22111 Hamburg, Horn, 22111 Hamburg
TypeEvent
StateHamburg

Colorful color festival modeled after the Indian tradition at the Horn racing track. To electronic music from national and international DJs, visitors throw colored powder into the air every hour and turn the grounds into a rainbow sea.
