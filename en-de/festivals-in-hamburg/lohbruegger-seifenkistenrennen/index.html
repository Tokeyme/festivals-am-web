Lohbrügger Seifenkistenrennen

Neighbourhood festival · Hamburg, Lohbrügge · 6 September 2026

When6 September 2026
WhereLeuschnerpark, zwischen Lohbrügger Landstraße und Lohbrügger Markt, 21031 Hamburg, Lohbrügge, 21031 Hamburg
TypeNeighbourhood festival
StateHamburg

At Leuschner Park, homemade soap box cars race down the track while a colorful family festival celebrates all around. The spectacle is organized by the Lohbrügge neighborhood association in collaboration with the LOLA cultural center.
