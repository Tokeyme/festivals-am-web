Marmstorfer Vogelschießen

Funfair · Hamburg, Marmstorf · 4–7 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When4–7 July 2026
WhereFeuerteichweg, 21077 Hamburg-Marmstorf, Marmstorf, 21077 Hamburg
TypeFunfair
StateHamburg

This traditional marksmen's and folk festival of the Marmstorf Marksmen's Association attracts many visitors the first weekend in July. The festival grounds feature a festival tent, amusement rides, shooting galleries, disco, and fireworks as the marksmen crown their new king.
