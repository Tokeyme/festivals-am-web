Nachtflohmarkt am Altonaer Balkon (Cruise Center)

Flea market · Hamburg, Altona-Altstadt · 5 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When5 September 2026
WhereVan-der-Smissen-Straße / Altonaer Balkon, 22767 Hamburg, Altona-Altstadt, 22767 Hamburg
TypeFlea market
StateHamburg

With views of the Elbe and harbor, the area at Cruise Center and Altonaer Balkon transforms into an atmospheric night flea market. In a summer and evening atmosphere, visitors browse vintage finds, secondhand fashion, and rarities while food stands and drinks keep the mood festive. A special seasonal edition of the beloved open-air flea market series.
