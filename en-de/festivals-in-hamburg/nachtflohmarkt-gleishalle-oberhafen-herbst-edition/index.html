Nachtflohmarkt Gleishalle Oberhafen (Herbst-Edition)

Flea market · Hamburg, HafenCity · 16–17 October 2026

When16–17 October 2026
WhereStockmeyerstraße 43, 20457 Hamburg, HafenCity, 20457 Hamburg
TypeFlea market
StateHamburg

The covered night flea market in the historic Oberhafen Rail Hall draws visitors on Friday and Saturday evenings with private secondhand stalls, live music, and cozy atmosphere. A popular spot for strolling and bargain hunting after dark.
