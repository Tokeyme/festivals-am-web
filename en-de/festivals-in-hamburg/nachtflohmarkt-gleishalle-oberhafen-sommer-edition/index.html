Nachtflohmarkt Gleishalle Oberhafen (Sommer-Edition)

Flea market · Hamburg, HafenCity · 31 July – 1 August 2026

When31 July – 1 August 2026
WhereStockmeyerstraße 43, 20457 Hamburg, HafenCity, 20457 Hamburg
TypeFlea market
StateHamburg

In the atmospheric old Gleishalle of the Oberhafen Quarter, evening browsing and haggling happen by string lights and music. Private stalls offer vintage, clothing, and oddities, plus street food and drinks.
