Nachtflohmarkt vor der Rindermarkthalle St. Pauli (Herbst-Special)

Flea market · Hamburg, St. Pauli · 22 October 2026

When22 October 2026
WhereNeuer Kamp 31, 20359 Hamburg, St. Pauli, 20359 Hamburg
TypeFlea market
StateHamburg

To close out the season in October, the evening flea market in front of the Rindermarkt Hall opens one last time. With food trucks and music, you can browse for vintage fashion, records, and rarities.
