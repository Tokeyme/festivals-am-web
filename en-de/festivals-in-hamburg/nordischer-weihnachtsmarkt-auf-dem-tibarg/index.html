Nordischer Weihnachtsmarkt auf dem Tibarg

Christmas market · Hamburg, Niendorf · 26 November – 24 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When26 November – 24 December 2026
WhereTibarg, Fußgängerzone, 22459 Hamburg, Niendorf, 22459 Hamburg
TypeChristmas market
StateHamburg

The Tibarg pedestrian zone becomes a Nordic Christmas wonderland with glowing tipi tents, evergreen trim, and lights. Around a decorated fir tree, craft goods, mulled wine, fresh baked goods, and children's programming welcome visitors. A tipi lounge provides cozy hygge.
