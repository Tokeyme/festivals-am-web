Poppenbütteler Sommerfest

Neighbourhood festival · Hamburg, Poppenbüttel · 4–6 September 2026

When4–6 September 2026
WherePoppenbüttler Markt, 22399 Hamburg, Poppenbüttel, 22399 Hamburg
TypeNeighbourhood festival
StateHamburg

Around Poppenbüttel Market, the summer festival invites visitors to linger with music, stalls, and relaxed late-summer vibes. Bouncy castles, a children's flea market, and craft goods make it a family gathering place.
