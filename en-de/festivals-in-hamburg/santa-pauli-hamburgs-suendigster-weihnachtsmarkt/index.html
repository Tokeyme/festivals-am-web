Santa Pauli - Hamburgs sündigster Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Hamburg, St. Pauli · 9 November – 23 December 2026

When9 November – 23 December 2026
WhereSpielbudenplatz, 20359 Hamburg, St. Pauli, 20359 Hamburg
TypeChristmas market
StateHamburg

Arguably Hamburg's most unconventional Christmas market on Spielbuden Square combines classic mulled wine flair with the cheeky charm of St. Pauli. Alongside booths and stage shows, there's adult entertainment and spirited fun in the heart of the Reeperbahn.
