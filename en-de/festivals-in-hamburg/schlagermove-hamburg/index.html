Schlagermove Hamburg

Street festival · Hamburg, St. Pauli · 3–4 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When3–4 July 2026
WhereHeiligengeistfeld / Reeperbahn, 20359 Hamburg, St. Pauli, 20359 Hamburg
TypeStreet festival
StateHamburg

Colorful hit music parade with around 50 trucks traveling from Heiligengeistfeld via Millerntor, Landing Bridges, and Reeperbahn. Tens of thousands celebrate in costume to pop music, plus an open-air concert at Heiligengeistfeld. One of Germany's largest pop-music street festivals.
