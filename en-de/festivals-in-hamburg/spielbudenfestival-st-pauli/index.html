Spielbudenfestival St. Pauli

Street festival · Hamburg, St. Pauli · 24–26 July 2026

When24–26 July 2026
WhereSpielbudenplatz, 20359 Hamburg, St. Pauli, 20359 Hamburg
TypeStreet festival
StateHamburg

For three days, Spielbudenplatz becomes a stage for international street art. Acrobatics, clownery, music, and performance merge into a free, accessible spectacle in the middle of the red-light district.
