Stadtfest Harburg

Town festival · Hamburg, Harburg · 19–20 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When19–20 September 2026
WhereSchloßmühlendamm / Herbert-und-Greta-Wehner-Platz, 21073 Hamburg, Harburg, 21073 Hamburg
TypeTown festival
StateHamburg

In late summer, Harburg's downtown celebrates a festival for the whole family around Schloßmühlendamm. On a car-free strolling mile, there's stage programming with local artists, dining, a children's area, and many participatory offerings.
