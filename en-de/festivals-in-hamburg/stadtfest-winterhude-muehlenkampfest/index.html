Stadtfest Winterhude (Mühlenkampfest)

Neighbourhood festival · Hamburg, Winterhude · 5–6 September 2026

When5–6 September 2026
WhereMühlenkamp, Gertigstraße, Prey- und Schinkelstraße, 22299 Hamburg, Winterhude, 22299 Hamburg
TypeNeighbourhood festival
StateHamburg

Large neighborhood festival around Mühlenkamp and Gertig Street with live music, children's and family programming at Schinkel Square, a flea market on Prey and Schinkel Streets, and numerous culinary stands. It extends from Goldbek Square to the Mühlenkamp dock.
