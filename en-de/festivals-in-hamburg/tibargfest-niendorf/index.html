Tibargfest Niendorf

Neighbourhood festival · Hamburg, Niendorf · 3–5 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When3–5 July 2026
WhereTibarg, vom Tibarg Center bis zum Niendorfer Marktplatz, 22459 Hamburg, Niendorf, 22459 Hamburg
TypeNeighbourhood festival
StateHamburg

The pedestrian zone at Tibarg transforms into a vibrant experience mile for the whole family. Food stands, participatory activities, retailers, and a stage program; local societies present themselves and children enjoy carousels and games. Sunday concludes with a large flea market.
