Weihnachtsmarkt Altona-Ottensen

Christmas market · Hamburg, Ottensen · 23 November – 23 December 2026

When23 November – 23 December 2026
WhereOttenser Hauptstraße / Spritzenplatz, 22765 Hamburg, Ottensen, 22765 Hamburg
TypeChristmas market
StateHamburg

Between Altona Station, the Mercado, and Spritzen Square stretches one of the city's most atmospheric Christmas markets. Over 40 lovingly decorated huts offer international street food, craft goods, and mulled wine, plus a colorful children's program. The market is known as particularly authentic and cozy away from downtown crowds.
