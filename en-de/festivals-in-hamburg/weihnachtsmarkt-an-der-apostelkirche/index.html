Weihnachtsmarkt an der Apostelkirche

Christmas market · Hamburg, Eimsbüttel · 13 November – 21 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When13 November – 21 December 2026
WhereBei der Apostelkirche, 20259 Hamburg, Eimsbüttel, 20259 Hamburg
TypeChristmas market

Around the Apostel Church, a small, cozy Christmas village emerges in the heart of Eimsbüttel. There's vintner mulled wine, culinary stands, numerous concerts, and a dedicated children's program. A contemplative Advent atmosphere in the church's shadow.
