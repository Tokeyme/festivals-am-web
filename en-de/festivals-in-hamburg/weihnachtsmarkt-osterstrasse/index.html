Weihnachtsmarkt Osterstraße

Christmas market · Hamburg, Eimsbüttel · 12 November – 30 December 2026

When12 November – 30 December 2026
WhereFanny-Mendelssohn-Platz, an der U-Bahn Osterstraße, 20259 Hamburg, Eimsbüttel, 20259 Hamburg
TypeChristmas market
StateHamburg

Fanny Mendelssohn Square transforms into a cozy Christmas village with Scandinavian-style wooden houses and romantic light ambiance. Bonfires, many candles, and a large culinary selection make it a popular neighborhood gathering place.
