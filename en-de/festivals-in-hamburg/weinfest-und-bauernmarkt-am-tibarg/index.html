Weinfest und Bauernmarkt am Tibarg

Wine festival · Hamburg, Niendorf · 25–27 September 2026

When25–27 September 2026
WhereTibarg, Fußgängerzone, 22459 Hamburg, Niendorf, 22459 Hamburg
TypeWine festival
StateHamburg

The Tibarg pedestrian zone becomes a fall market mile with a wine village and farmers' market. Vintners and wine merchants pour fine wines, while flammkuchen, cheese varieties, and live afternoon music round out the experience. Around Dorf Square, families enjoy a straw jump castle, pumpkin carving, and a pedal tractor course.
