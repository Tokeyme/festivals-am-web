Winterhuder Weihnachtstreff

Christmas market · Hamburg, Winterhude · 13 November – 23 December 2026

When13 November – 23 December 2026
WhereWinterhuder Marktplatz, 22299 Hamburg, Winterhude, 22299 Hamburg
TypeChristmas market

A small, cozy Christmas gathering on Winterhude Market Square with the central punch hut 'Lütte Eck'. The focus is on contemplative neighborhood hours, mulled wine and homemade punch, plus charitable activities for good causes.
