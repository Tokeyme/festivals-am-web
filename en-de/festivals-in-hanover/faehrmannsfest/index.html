Fährmannsfest

Music festival · Hanover, Linden-Nord · 31 July – 2 August 2026

When31 July – 2 August 2026
WhereFaust-Wiese / Weddigenufer, Linden-Nord, 30449 Hanover
TypeMusic festival
StateLower Saxony

Fährmannsfest is the largest alternative open-air festival in the Hannover region and has been held since 1983 at the confluence of the Ihme and the Leine. On several stages between the Leine and the Ihme, as well as on the Fährmannsinsel, there is live music of various genres. The programme is complemented by the Bunte Bühne on the Faust-Wiese, with a children's festival, poetry slam and further attractions.
