Karnevalsumzug Hannover

Carnival · Hanover, Mitte · 6 February 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When6 February 2027
WhereInnenstadt (Platz der Weltausstellung), Mitte, 30159 Hanover
TypeCarnival
StateLower Saxony

At the Karnevalsumzug, several thousand carnival revellers make their way through Hannover's city centre on floats, in convertibles and on foot. On the stage at Platz der Weltausstellung there is music, singing and performances by music corps. The organiser is the Komitee Hannoverscher Karneval.
