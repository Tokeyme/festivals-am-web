Opernplatzfest

Culture festival · Hanover, Mitte · 29 August 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When29 August 2026
WhereOpernplatz, Mitte, 30159 Hanover
TypeCulture festival
StateLower Saxony

For the Opernplatzfest, the Opernplatz is transformed with several stages into a summer festival mile. Members of the opera and ballet ensembles, along with the orchestra and costume department, showcase their skills, complemented by hands-on activities ranging from open ballet training and workshops to singalongs. In the evening, the festival winds down with DJ music.
