Roncalli's Weihnachtsdorf

Christmas market · Hanover, Mitte · 23 November – 30 December 2026

When23 November – 30 December 2026
WhereErnst-August-Platz, Mitte, 30159 Hanover
TypeChristmas market
StateLower Saxony

Roncalli's Weihnachtsdorf is located on the Ernst-August-Platz in front of the main railway station. More than fifty elaborately decorated Christmas huts, a nostalgic Ferris wheel and daily light projections onto the station building define the market. The drinks served include organic Glühwein.
