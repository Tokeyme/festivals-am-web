Silvesterlauf Hannover

Sports event · Hanover, Südstadt · 31 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When31 December 2026
WhereMaschsee, Nordufer (Fackelträger), Südstadt, 30169 Hanover
TypeSports event
StateLower Saxony

The Silvesterlauf Hannover is a public fun run around the Maschsee, starting and finishing on the north shore by the Fackelträger. The main course measures around 5.8 kilometres; for children there are shorter runs over 600 and 1,200 metres. The event places importance on sustainability and does without fireworks.
