Südstadtfest auf dem Stephansplatz

Neighbourhood festival · Hanover, Südstadt · 13–14 June 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When13–14 June 2026
WhereStephansplatz, 30171 Hannover, Südstadt, 30171 Hanover
TypeNeighbourhood festival
StateLower Saxony

Neighborhood festival of Hannover's Südstadt on Stephansplatz, organized by restaurateurs from the district. Over two days, food and drink stalls, live music, and an open-air program draw visitors young and old; the offerings also include a wine tasting (featuring the wine bar REBE, among others).
