Weihnachtsdorf auf dem Lindener Berg

Christmas market · Hanover, Linden-Süd · 27 November – 20 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 November – 20 December 2026
WhereAm Lindener Berge, Linden-Süd, 30449 Hanover
TypeChristmas market
StateLower Saxony

The Weihnachtsdorf auf dem Lindener Berg is a small, cosy Weihnachtsmarkt by the Lindener Turm beer garden, open on the Advent weekends from Friday to Sunday. Atmospherically decorated log cabins offer handicrafts as well as Christmas food and drink. The market supports a good cause.
