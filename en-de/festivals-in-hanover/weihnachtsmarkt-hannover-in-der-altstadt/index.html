Weihnachtsmarkt Hannover in der Altstadt

Christmas market · Hanover · 23 November – 22 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When23 November – 22 December 2026
WhereHistorische Altstadt / Am Markte (Marktkirche), 30159 Hanover
TypeChristmas market
StateLower Saxony

The city's largest Christmas market in the historic old town, organized into traditional market sections, a Finnish Christmas village, and a medieval market. Features a large illuminated tree and children's programs.
