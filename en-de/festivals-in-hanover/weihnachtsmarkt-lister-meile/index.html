Weihnachtsmarkt Lister Meile

Christmas market · Hanover, Oststadt · 23 November – 22 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When23 November – 22 December 2026
WhereLister Meile (Lister Platz bis Wedekindstraße), Oststadt, 30161 Hanover
TypeChristmas market
StateLower Saxony

The traditional Weihnachtsmarkt on the Lister Meile stretches along the pedestrian zone from Lister Platz to Wedekindstraße. The stalls offer wooden toys, Christmas tree decorations, candles and ceramic and glass wares, along with festive treats; and various fairground rides turn for the children. The organiser is Aktion Lister Meile.
