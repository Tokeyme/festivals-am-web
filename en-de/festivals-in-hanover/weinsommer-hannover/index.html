WeinSommer Hannover

Wine festival · Hanover · 20–23 August 2026

When20–23 August 2026
WhereOpernplatz, 30159 Hanover
TypeWine festival
StateLower Saxony

On the Opernplatz in front of the opera house, winemakers and wine merchants offer their wines for tasting. Visitors enjoy wine and food outdoors in the centre of Hannover, from Thursday through Sunday until late in the evening.
