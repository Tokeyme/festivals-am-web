Winterzauber Herrenhausen

Christmas market · Hanover, Herrenhausen · 28 October – 1 November 2026

When28 October – 1 November 2026
WhereHerrenhäuser Str. 3, Schloss Herrenhausen, Herrenhausen, 30419 Hanover
TypeChristmas market
StateLower Saxony

The Winterzauber transforms Schloss Herrenhausen and the Galerie into a festive winter market with arts and crafts, decorations and culinary offerings. The event is organised by Evergreen GmbH and takes place in the stately setting of the palace and in the palace courtyard.
