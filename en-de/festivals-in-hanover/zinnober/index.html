ZINNOBER

Culture festival · Hanover · 5–6 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When5–6 September 2026
Where Hanover
TypeCulture festival
StateLower Saxony

ZINNOBER is a city-wide art weekend in Hannover, during which numerous studios, galleries, project spaces and art institutions open their doors. Artists present contemporary art, complemented by special events, guided tours and an art-education programme. The so-called Kunstvolkslauf spreads across many locations throughout the city.
