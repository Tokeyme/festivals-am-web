Heidelberger Herbst

Town festival · Heidelberg, Altstadt · 26–27 September 2026

When26–27 September 2026
WhereAltstadt, 69117 Heidelberg
TypeTown festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

Large old-town festival in Heidelberg with over 40 stages and bands, a historical market at University Square, a large flea market, and a medieval procession. Sunday features "Family Autumn" with programs for children. 2026 marks the 55th edition.
