Casseler Herbst-Freyheit

Town festival · Kassel · 29 October – 1 November 2026

When29 October – 1 November 2026
Where34117 Kassel
TypeTown festival
StateHesse

For four days Kassel's city centre turns into one long festival mile. On offer are a colourful autumn market with regional specialities, food and drink from the region, street art and activities for the whole family.
