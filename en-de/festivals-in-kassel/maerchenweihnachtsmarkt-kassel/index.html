Märchenweihnachtsmarkt Kassel

Christmas market in Kassel's city centre between Königsplatz and Friedrichsplatz with a Ferris wheel, a fairy-tale forest and the world's largest fairy-tale pyramid. Open daily from 11 am to 8 pm, food and drink until 10 pm; closed from 24 to 26 December.
