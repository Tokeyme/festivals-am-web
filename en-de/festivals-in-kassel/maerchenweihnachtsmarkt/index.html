Märchenweihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Kassel · 23 November – 30 December 2026

When23 November – 30 December 2026
WhereKönigsplatz, 34117 Kassel
TypeChristmas market
StateHesse

Kassel's Christmas market stretches between Königsplatz and Friedrichsplatz and along the Treppenstraße. There is a Ferris wheel on Friedrichsplatz and a fairy-tale slide in the Treppenstraße. The market stays closed from 24 to 26 December. In 2026 its theme is "Das tapfere Schneiderlein" (The Brave Little Tailor).
