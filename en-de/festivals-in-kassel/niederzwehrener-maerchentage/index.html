Niederzwehrener Märchentage

Culture festival · Kassel, Niederzwehren · 18–27 September 2026

When18–27 September 2026
WhereNiederzwehren, 34134 Kassel
TypeCulture festival
StateHesse

The Kassel district of Niederzwehren hosts its Märchentage (fairy-tale days) over ten days. A large programme of events on the theme of fairy tales is put on for young and old alike.
