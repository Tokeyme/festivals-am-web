Koblenzer Oktoberfest

Funfair · Koblenz · 11 September – 10 October 2026

When11 September – 10 October 2026
WhereMesseplatz Koblenz, 56070 Koblenz
TypeFunfair
StateRhineland-Palatinate

Wiesn funfair in a festival tent on the Messeplatz, running over several weekends. On offer are Oktoberfest and party music, Bavarian specialities and freshly tapped beer; on several Fridays and Saturdays.
