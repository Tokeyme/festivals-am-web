Koblenzer Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Koblenz · 20 November 2026 – 10 January 2027

When20 November 2026 – 10 January 2027
WhereKoblenzer Altstadt, 56068 Koblenz
TypeChristmas market
StateRhineland-Palatinate

Christmas market at several squares in the old town, including Jesuitenplatz, Münzplatz, Am Plan, Josef-Görres-Platz and Liebfrauenkirche, with festively decorated stalls, arts and crafts and culinary offerings.
