Rhein in Flammen Koblenz

Funfair · Koblenz · 7–9 August 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When7–9 August 2026
WhereDeutsches Eck und Rheinanlagen, 56068 Koblenz
TypeFunfair
StateRhineland-Palatinate

Three-day summer festival around the Deutsches Eck with a stage programme and music. The highlight on Saturday evening is the large aerial fireworks display with a ship convoy against the backdrop of Ehrenbreitstein Fortress.
