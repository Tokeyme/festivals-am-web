Schaengelmarkt Koblenz

Town festival · Koblenz · 18–20 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When18–20 September 2026
WhereKoblenzer Innenstadt, 56068 Koblenz
TypeTown festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

One of the largest city and folk festivals in Rhineland-Palatinate. Over three days, the city centre transforms into a large festival mile with live music, a Burgunderfest, arts and crafts and a family programme.
