Weinfestival Koblenz

Wine festival · Koblenz · 8 May – 11 July 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When8 May – 11 July 2026
WhereKoblenz Innenstadt und Rheinpromenade, 56068 Koblenz
TypeWine festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

A weeks-long series of events centred on the wine culture of the wine city of Koblenz and the growing regions of Mosel, Mittelrhein, Ahr and Rheinhessen, with wine tastings, a stage programme and wine bars. Part of the series falls in the summer period from mid-June.
