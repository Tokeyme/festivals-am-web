AWO Chérisy Herbstflohmarkt

Flea market · Konstanz, Fürstenberg · 26 September 2026

When26 September 2026
WhereChérisystraße, Fürstenberg, 78467 Konstanz
TypeFlea market
StateBaden-Württemberg

The AWO hosts an autumn flea market at the Treffpunkt Chérisy. It runs from 10am to 4pm and is one of the Konstanz markets of the year. The Treffpunkt Chérisy is in the Fürstenberg district.
