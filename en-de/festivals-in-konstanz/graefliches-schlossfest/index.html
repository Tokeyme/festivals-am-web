Gräfliches Schlossfest

Market · Konstanz, Litzelstetten · 1–4 October 2026

When1–4 October 2026
WhereInsel Mainau, Litzelstetten, 78465 Konstanz
TypeMarket
StateBaden-Württemberg

The Gräfliches Schlossfest on Mainau island is an autumn festival with an arts and crafts market around the palace. Selected exhibitors show fashion, jewellery and accessories in palace rooms opened specially for the occasion. The supporting programme includes music, talks and the Gräfliche Hutmodenschau, a hat fashion show with creations from the atelier of Diana Gräfin Bernadotte. There are also experiences in the historic wine cellar.
