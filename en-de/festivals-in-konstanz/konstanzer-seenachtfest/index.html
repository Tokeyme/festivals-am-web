Konstanzer Seenachtfest

Funfair · Konstanz · 14 August 2027

When14 August 2027
WhereUferpromenade Konstanz (Stadtgarten, Konzilvorplatz, Hafenstraße), 78462 Konstanz
TypeFunfair
StateBaden-Württemberg

Large lake festival along the promenade with stage programs, live bands, and DJs. Starting at 3 PM; evening highlight with a preshow (drone show/light installations) and pyrotechnics around 10:15 PM over Lake Constance.
