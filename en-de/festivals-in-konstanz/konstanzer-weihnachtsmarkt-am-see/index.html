Konstanzer Weihnachtsmarkt am See

Christmas market · Konstanz · 26 November – 23 December 2026

When26 November – 23 December 2026
WhereMarktstätte und Seeufer (Konzertmuschel), 78462 Konstanz
TypeChristmas market
StateBaden-Württemberg

The largest Christmas market in the Lake Constance region around the bandshell, along the lakeside, and at Marktplatz with numerous stalls, crafts, and dining right on the lake. Daily 11 AM to 8 PM; Friday and Saturday until 9:30 PM.
