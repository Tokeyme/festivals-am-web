Kunst- & Designmarkt Konstanz

Market · Konstanz · 17–18 October 2026

When17–18 October 2026
WhereReichenaustraße 21, 78467 Konstanz
TypeMarket
StateBaden-Württemberg

Around 110 exhibitors, most of them young labels, show their work at the Bodenseeforum. The range runs from fashion, jewellery and art through interiors and accessories to bags and stationery. The labels say they make their products to a high standard and with sustainably chosen materials. There is street food and a view of the lake; open from 11am to 6pm on both days.
