Adventsmarkt des Bürgervereins Achdorf

Christmas market · Landshut, Achdorf · 27 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 November 2026
WhereÄußere Münchener Straße 83 (Zollhaus), Achdorf, 84036 Landshut
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

The Bürgerverein Achdorf (local residents' association) hosts this Advent market at the Zollhaus in Landshut's Achdorf district. The festively decorated beer garden offers Advent wreaths and arrangements, ceramics, knitwear, as well as bratwurst and mulled wine. Musical performances and stories create a Christmas mood, and a visit from Nikolaus (St Nicholas) is also planned.
