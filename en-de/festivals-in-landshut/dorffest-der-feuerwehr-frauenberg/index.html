Dorffest der Feuerwehr Frauenberg

Village & club fest · Landshut, Frauenberg · 8 August 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When8 August 2026
WhereFrauenberger Straße (Marienkirche), Frauenberg, 84036 Landshut
TypeVillage & club fest
StateBavaria

The Frauenberg fire-fighting unit (Löschzug) of the Landshut volunteer fire brigade holds its traditional village festival around the Marienkirche and the fire station in the Frauenberg district. On offer are Steckerlfisch (fish grilled on a stick), grilled dishes, beer as well as coffee and cake; the Frauenberger Blaskapelle (brass band) provides the musical entertainment. For more than 40 years, the festival has been a draw for young and old.
