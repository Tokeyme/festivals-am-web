Kunst- und Schauhandwerkermarkt

Christmas market · Landshut · 27 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 November – 23 December 2026
WhereAltstadt, Fußgängerzone zwischen Theaterstraße und Stadtresidenz, 84028 Landshut
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

The Kunst- und Schauhandwerkermarkt (arts and live-crafts market) is held in the pedestrian zone of the old town between Theaterstraße and Stadtresidenz and complements the Christkindlmarkt (Christ Child Christmas market). At twelve wooden market stalls, along with further stands, arts and live crafts are displayed and sold. The neighbouring Adventstreffs (Advent meeting points) provide the drinks and snacks on offer.
