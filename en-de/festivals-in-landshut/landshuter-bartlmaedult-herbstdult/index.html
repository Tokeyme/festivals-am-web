Landshuter Bartlmädult (Herbstdult)

Funfair · Landshut · 21–30 August 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When21–30 August 2026
WhereGrieserwiese, Landshut, 84034 Landshut
TypeFunfair
StateBavaria

One of the oldest folk festivals in Germany, first mentioned in 1339. It begins with the tapping of the first keg in front of the town hall and a procession to the Grieserwiese; on offer are festival tents, fairground rides, market stalls and fireworks.
