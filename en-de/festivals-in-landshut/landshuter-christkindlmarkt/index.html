Landshuter Christkindlmarkt

Christmas market · Landshut · 19 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When19 November – 23 December 2026
WhereRingelstecherwiese, Landshut, 84028 Landshut
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

Christmas market with around 50 stalls, an ice rink, Bavarian curling, a children's carousel and a 38-metre-high Ferris wheel with views of the old town and Burg Trausnitz. Accompanied by the show and crafts market in the old town.
