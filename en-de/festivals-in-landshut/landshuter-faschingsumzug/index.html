Landshuter Faschingsumzug

Carnival · Landshut · 7 February 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When7 February 2027
WhereAltstadt, 84028 Landshut
TypeCarnival
StateBavaria

At the Landshuter Faschingsumzug, themed floats and walking groups move through the old town. The city of Landshut organises the parade together with local Fasching carnival societies. Around the town hall, a stage programme with music accompanies the colourful festivities.
