Landshuter Haferlmarkt

Market · Landshut · 11–13 September 2026

When11–13 September 2026
WhereFreyung, Altstadt, 84028 Landshut
TypeMarket
StateBavaria

The Landshuter Haferlmarkt is a ceramics market in the Freyung in the old town. Graduates of the Landshut ceramics college and potters from the region display and sell handmade pottery ranging from traditional to modern. In front of the Jodokskirche, a beer and coffee garden with music invites visitors to linger, while a special exhibition in the town hall rounds off the programme. Admission is free.
