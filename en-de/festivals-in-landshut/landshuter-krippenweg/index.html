Landshuter Krippenweg

During the Advent and Christmas season, the Landshuter Krippenweg (nativity-scene trail) leads to nativity scenes at more than 30 locations in churches, monasteries, shops and cafes across the city, under the motto 'Folge dem Stern' (Follow the Star). A star with a tail marks each nativity-scene site. The trail is accompanied by guided tours and concerts, and a large nativity-scene exhibition forms its centrepiece.
