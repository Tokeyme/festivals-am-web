Landshuter Kunstnacht

Culture festival · Landshut · 11–13 September 2026

When11–13 September 2026
WhereAltstadt, 84028 Landshut
TypeCulture festival
StateBavaria

At the Landshuter Kunstnacht (Landshut art night), around a dozen of the city's galleries, museums and art associations open their doors. The event opens on Friday evening with the art night and its private views; on the following weekend the exhibition venues are open during the day as part of the art weekend. A yellow footprint marks the route between the art stations. Admission is free.
