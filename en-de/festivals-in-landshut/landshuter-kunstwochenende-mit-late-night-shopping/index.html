Landshuter Kunstwochenende mit Late-Night-Shopping

Culture festival · Landshut · 11–13 September 2026

When11–13 September 2026
WhereAltstadt, 84028 Landshut
TypeCulture festival
StateBavaria

For the 21st Kunstwochenende, 14 Landshut galleries and other art venues open their doors, among them the municipal museums and the Keramikschule (ceramics school); yellow footprints show the way. Opening times are Friday from 7pm to 11pm and throughout the weekend from 2pm to 6pm. Running alongside it, the Late-Night-Shopping stretches from Dreifaltigkeitsplatz through the narrow lanes down into the lower Altstadt. The organiser had not yet published the detailed programme for 2026.
