Lasershow in der Silvesternacht

Event · Landshut · 31 December 2026 – 1 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When31 December 2026 – 1 January 2027
WhereAltstadt, Fußgängerzone zwischen Rathaus und Martinskirche, 84028 Landshut
TypeEvent
StateBavaria

At the turn of the year, an approximately 15-minute laser show transforms the pedestrian zone of the old town between the town hall and the Martinskirche into a play of light, music and fog. Late in the evening a DJ performs on the stage in front of the town hall, while several food stalls provide refreshments. The city offers the laser show as an alternative to fireworks in the historic old town, partly for fire-safety reasons.
