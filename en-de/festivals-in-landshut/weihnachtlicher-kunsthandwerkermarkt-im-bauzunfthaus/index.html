Weihnachtlicher Kunsthandwerkermarkt im Bauzunfthaus

Christmas market · Landshut · 4–6 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When4–6 December 2026
WhereDreifaltigkeitsplatz 2 (Bauzunfthaus), 84028 Landshut
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

The Christmas arts and crafts market in the historic Bauzunfthaus at Dreifaltigkeitsplatz is organised by Bauzunfthaus Landshut e.V. Association members and other exhibitors present handmade one-off pieces. Sweet and savoury treats (Schmankerl), mulled wine and coffee are also available.
