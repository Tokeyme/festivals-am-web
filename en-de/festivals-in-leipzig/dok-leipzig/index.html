DOK Leipzig

Culture festival · Leipzig, Zentrum · 26 October – 1 November 2026

When26 October – 1 November 2026
Whereverschiedene Kinos (u.a. Passage Kinos, Hainstraße 19a), Zentrum, 04109 Leipzig
TypeCulture festival
StateSaxony

DOK Leipzig is an international festival for documentary and animated film. It shows films from all over the world and is regarded as one of the most important festivals of its kind. The programme is rounded off by an industry gathering (DOK Industry).
