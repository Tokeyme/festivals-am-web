Lachmesse

Culture festival · Leipzig, Zentrum · 25 October – 1 November 2026

When25 October – 1 November 2026
Whereverschiedene Spielstätten (u.a. Kabarett Leipziger Pfeffermühle), Zentrum, 04109 Leipzig
TypeCulture festival
StateSaxony

The Lachmesse is an international festival of cabaret and variety arts in Leipzig. Cabaret artists and comedians perform at numerous venues such as the Leipziger Pfeffermühle, the academixer and the Moritzbastei. On offer is a mix of political satire, music and comedy.
