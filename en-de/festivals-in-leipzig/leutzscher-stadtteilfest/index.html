Leutzscher Stadtteilfest

Neighbourhood festival · Leipzig, Leutzsch · 5 September 2026

When5 September 2026
WhereGeorg-Schwarz-Straße / Zentrum Leutzsch, Leutzsch, 04179 Leipzig
TypeNeighbourhood festival

The Leutzscher Stadtteilfest is a community festival in the heart of the Leipzig district of Leutzsch. The BürgerVerein Leutzsch invites neighbours to meet, enjoy the programme and celebrate together.
