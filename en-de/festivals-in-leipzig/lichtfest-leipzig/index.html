Lichtfest Leipzig

Historical festival · Leipzig, Zentrum · 9 October 2026

When9 October 2026
WhereAugustusplatz, Zentrum, 04109 Leipzig
TypeHistorical festival
StateSaxony

Each year on 9 October, the Lichtfest Leipzig commemorates the Monday demonstration of 1989 and the Peaceful Revolution. Light installations and a stage programme are presented in the city centre around the Ring.
