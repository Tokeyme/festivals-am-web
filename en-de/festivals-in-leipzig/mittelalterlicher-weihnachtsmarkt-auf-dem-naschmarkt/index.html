Mittelalterlicher Weihnachtsmarkt auf dem Naschmarkt

Christmas market · Leipzig, Zentrum · 25 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When25 November – 23 December 2026
WhereNaschmarkt, Zentrum, 04109 Leipzig
TypeChristmas market
StateSaxony

The medieval Christmas market 'Alt Leipzig' on the Naschmarkt brings visitors closer to arts and crafts traditions. On offer are live craft demonstrations, Handbrot from the clay oven and hot mead, as well as medieval sounds by torchlight.
