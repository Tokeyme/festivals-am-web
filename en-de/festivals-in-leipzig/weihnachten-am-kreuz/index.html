Weihnachten am Kreuz

Christmas market · Leipzig, Connewitz · 9–20 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When9–20 December 2026
WhereWERK 2 - Kulturfabrik, Kochstraße 132, Connewitz, 04277 Leipzig
TypeChristmas market
StateSaxony

"Weihnachten am Kreuz" is a creative Christmas market at the WERK 2 cultural centre by the Connewitzer Kreuz. Numerous stalls offer handmade gifts and street food, and there is a lounge for children with fairy tales and music.
