Kunsthandwerk und Genuss

Market · Lindau, Insel · 12–13 September 2026

When12–13 September 2026
WhereLöwenmole / Römerschanze, Insel, 88131 Lindau
TypeMarket
StateBaden-Württemberg

Kunsthandwerk und Genuss is an arts and crafts market set against the backdrop of Lindau's island, at the Löwenmole and on the Römerschanze. Around 40 stalls offer high-quality handicrafts with a focus on pottery, along with jewellery, clothing, accessories and decorations for home and garden. The market is complemented by culinary offerings, craft demonstrations and a small children's programme.
