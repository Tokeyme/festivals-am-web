Lindauer Jahrmarkt

Funfair · Lindau, Insel · 6–10 November 2026

When6–10 November 2026
WhereFußgängerzone, Marktplatz und Seehafenpromenade, Insel, 88131 Lindau
TypeFunfair
StateBaden-Württemberg

The Lindauer Jahrmarkt (funfair and market) is a colourful mix of fair, amusement park and traders' market on the island of Lindau. Around 140 traders and 60 showmen spread out from the pedestrian zone across the market square to the harbour promenade. Alongside stalls selling household goods, toys and handicrafts, there are rides such as a Ferris wheel and bumper cars. The market has been documented since 1652.
