Lindauer Kinderfest

Event · Lindau, Insel · 29 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When29 July 2026
WhereInsel Lindau, Insel, 88131 Lindau
TypeEvent
StateBaden-Württemberg

The long-established Lindauer Kinderfest is held every year on the last Wednesday before the Bavarian summer holidays. For the children there are parades, games and a varied supporting programme in the island town.
