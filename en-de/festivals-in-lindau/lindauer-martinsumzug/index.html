Lindauer Martinsumzug

Event · Lindau, Insel · 13 November 2026

When13 November 2026
WhereMünster Zu Unserer Lieben Frau, Stiftsplatz 1, Insel, 88131 Lindau
TypeEvent
StateBavaria

The Lindauer Martinsumzug is a traditional lantern parade for St Martin's Day, starting at the Münster Zu Unserer Lieben Frau on Lindau island. It is run by the Catholic parish office Münster Lindau. The parade is aimed above all at families and children.
