Lindauer Oktoberfest

Funfair · Lindau, Reutin · 4–6 September 2026

When4–6 September 2026
WhereFestplatz, Ludwig-Kick-Straße 19, Reutin, 88131 Lindau
TypeFunfair
StateBaden-Württemberg

The Lindauer Oktoberfest is a multi-day funfair held on the festival ground on Ludwig-Kick-Straße in the Reutin district. It is organised by the Musikverein Lindau Aeschach/Hoyren, a local music association. On offer are a festival tent with the ceremonial tapping of the first barrel (Fassanstich), live music by brass bands and party bands, and an amusement park. Sunday is set aside as a family day with its own programme for children.
