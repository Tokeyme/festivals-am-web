ORF Lange Nacht der Museen

Culture festival · Lindau · 3 October 2026

When3 October 2026
WhereCavazzen Museum, Marktplatz 6, 88131 Lindau
TypeCulture festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

For the Long Night of Museums, the Cavazzen Museum and the Kunstforum Hundertwasser in Lindau stay open until midnight. The museum team offers short guided tours of the exhibitions “Picasso.Handmade” and “Hundertwasser – Kunst der Vielfalt”. One ticket covers every participating venue.
