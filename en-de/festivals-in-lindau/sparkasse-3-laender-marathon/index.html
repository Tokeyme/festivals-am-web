Sparkasse 3-Länder-Marathon

Sports event · Lindau · 11 October 2026

When11 October 2026
WhereHafenpromenade, Hafenplatz, 88131 Lindau
TypeSports event
StateBaden-Württemberg

The marathon starts at the harbor of the island town of Lindau and follows the shore of Lake Constance through Germany, Austria and Switzerland. The flat course passes the Bregenz lake stage, runs through St. Margrethen and follows the Rhine. Spectators line the route.
