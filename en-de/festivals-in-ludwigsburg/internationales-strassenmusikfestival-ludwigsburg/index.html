Internationales Straßenmusikfestival Ludwigsburg

Street festival · Ludwigsburg · 20–21 June 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When20–21 June 2026
WhereBlühendes Barock Ludwigsburg, 71634 Ludwigsburg
TypeStreet festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

Street musicians from all over the world perform on around eleven stages over two days. In 2026, the festival was moved from its Whitsun date to the weekend of the summer solstice. Grounds open from 11 a.m., performances from midday to evening.
