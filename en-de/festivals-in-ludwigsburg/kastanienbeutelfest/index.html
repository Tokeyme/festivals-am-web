Kastanienbeutelfest

Town festival · Ludwigsburg · 11 October 2026

When11 October 2026
WhereMarktplatz / Innenstadt Ludwigsburg, 71634 Ludwigsburg
TypeTown festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

Around the chestnut tree, there is an arts and food market with shops open on Sunday, chestnut-based specialities and a children's programme. The tradition goes back to the chestnut warden David Friedrich Beutel.
