Leuchtende Traumpfade

Event · Ludwigsburg · 23 October – 6 December 2026

When23 October – 6 December 2026
WhereBlühendes Barock, Mömpelgardstraße 28, 71640 Ludwigsburg
TypeEvent
StateBaden-Württemberg

Light shows, projections and installations turn the gardens of the Blühendes Barock into an illuminated dream world. The circular route starts at the Schüsselesee in the Oberer Ostgarten, is around 2.5 kilometres long and takes roughly one to one and a half hours. Along the way there are interactive stations and places to stop for refreshments. Doors open at 5pm and the event ends at 9.30pm.
