Ludwigsburger Antikmeile

Flea market · Ludwigsburg · 26–27 September 2026

When26–27 September 2026
WhereMarktplatz, 71634 Ludwigsburg
TypeFlea market
StateBaden-Württemberg

On the baroque Marktplatz, more than 150 stallholders from all over Germany offer antique furniture, vintage technology, toys, crockery, jewellery and porcelain. Visitors can browse and haggle, and admission is free. Experts on site appraise the age and value of antiques brought along.
