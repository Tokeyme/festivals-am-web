Ludwigsburger Steillagentage

Wine festival · Ludwigsburg, Neckarweihingen · 3–5 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When3–5 July 2026
WhereSteillagen zwischen Neckarweihingen und Poppenweiler, Neckarweihingen, 71642 Ludwigsburg
TypeWine festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

On the steep slopes along the Neckar, winegrowers invite visitors to wine tastings, regional delicacies and live music. Guided tours through the vineyards are part of the event. Motto: Steilgehen, Staunen, Genießen.
