Ludwigsburger Weinlaube

Wine festival · Ludwigsburg · 6–22 August 2026

When6–22 August 2026
WhereRathaushof / Rathausplatz Ludwigsburg, 71634 Ludwigsburg
TypeWine festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

For a good two weeks, the town hall courtyard transforms into a cosy wine village where hosts present exquisite regional and international wines with live music. Daily from 5 p.m.
