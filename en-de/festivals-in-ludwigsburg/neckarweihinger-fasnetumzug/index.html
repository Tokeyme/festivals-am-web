Neckarweihinger Fasnetumzug

Carnival · Ludwigsburg, Neckarweihingen · 31 January 2027

When31 January 2027
WhereHauptstraße, Neckarweihingen, 71642 Ludwigsburg
TypeCarnival
StateBaden-Württemberg

The carnival guild 1. Fasnetzunft Neckarweihingen "Mistelhexen" hosts this procession, always held on the Sunday before Schmotziger Donnerstag. Groups from the VSAN, SBH and Hegau-Bodensee associations regularly take part, along with many other societies, showing the variety of the local tradition. Hauptstrasse forms the main route, where spectators stand several rows deep. The guild provides food and drink for participants and visitors.
