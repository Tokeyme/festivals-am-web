Ochsenfest Poppenweiler

Village & club fest · Ludwigsburg, Poppenweiler · 24–27 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When24–27 July 2026
WhereFestplatz Poppenweiler, Erdmannhäuser Straße 11, Poppenweiler, 71642 Ludwigsburg
TypeVillage & club fest
StateBaden-Württemberg

The Musikverein Poppenweiler hosts its four-day Ochsenfest, known for the ox prepared on the charcoal grill, live music, the Ochsen-Gaudi and a family programme.
