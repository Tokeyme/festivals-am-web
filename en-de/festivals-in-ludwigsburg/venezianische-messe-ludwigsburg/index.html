Venezianische Messe Ludwigsburg

Village & club fest · Ludwigsburg · 4–6 September 2026

When4–6 September 2026
WhereMarktplatz Ludwigsburg, 71634 Ludwigsburg
TypeVillage & club fest
StateBaden-Württemberg

Every two years, the baroque market square transforms into a Venetian dreamscape with more than two thousand costumed guests and international artists performing acrobatics, music, theatre, comedy and dance on around ten stages. Admission free.
