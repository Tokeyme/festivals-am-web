Weinbergfest Hoheneck

Wine festival · Ludwigsburg, Hoheneck · 11–12 July 2026

When11–12 July 2026
WhereFestwiese am Weinberghaus Rapp, Hoheneck, Hoheneck, 71642 Ludwigsburg
TypeWine festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

The Wein-, Obst- und Gartenbauverein Hoheneck e.V. is hosting its vineyard festival for the first time on the festival meadow by the Weinberghaus Rapp, with regional wine, food and drink, a children's programme, cake sales and, on Sunday, a Weißwurst breakfast.
