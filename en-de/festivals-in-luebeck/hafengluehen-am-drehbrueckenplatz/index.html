Hafenglühen am Drehbrückenplatz

Christmas market · Lübeck, Altstadt · 23 November – 29 December 2026

When23 November – 29 December 2026
WhereDrehbrückenplatz, An der Untertrave, Altstadt, 23552 Lübeck
TypeChristmas market
StateSchleswig-Holstein

At Drehbrückenplatz on the Untertrave, the Hafenglühen creates a Christmas market with a harbour atmosphere right by the water. Against the backdrop of the Drehbrücke (swing bridge) and the Media Docks, stalls invite visitors to enjoy mulled wine and wintry treats.
