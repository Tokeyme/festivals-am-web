Heiligabend am Alten Leuchtturm

Event · Lübeck, Travemünde · 24 December 2026

When24 December 2026
WhereAlter Leuchtturm, Am Leuchtenfeld 1, Travemünde, 23570 Lübeck
TypeEvent
StateSchleswig-Holstein

At the Alter Leuchtturm (old lighthouse) in Travemünde, the traditional Heiligabendpunschen (Christmas Eve punch gathering) takes place on Christmas Eve. Over mulled wine and Christmas music, Father Christmas and an angel hand out sweets; admission is free.
