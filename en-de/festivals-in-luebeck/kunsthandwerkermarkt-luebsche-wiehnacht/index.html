Kunsthandwerkermarkt Lübsche Wiehnacht

Christmas market · Lübeck, Altstadt · 21–29 November 2026

When21–29 November 2026
WhereSchuppen 6, An der Untertrave 47a, Altstadt, 23552 Lübeck
TypeChristmas market
StateSchleswig-Holstein

In the historic Schuppen 6 at the harbour, the Lübsche Wiehnacht offers arts and crafts and Christmas goods on two Advent weekends. The covered market in the listed harbour hall on the Untertrave is part of the Lübecker Weihnachtsstadt (Lübeck's Christmas City).
