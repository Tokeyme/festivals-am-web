Kunsthandwerkermarkt St. Petri

Christmas market · Lübeck, Altstadt · 24 November – 14 December 2026

When24 November – 14 December 2026
WhereSt.-Petri-Kirche, Petrikirchhof, Altstadt, 23552 Lübeck
TypeChristmas market
StateSchleswig-Holstein

In and around St. Petri church, an arts and crafts market is held at which craftspeople from Lübeck and the region present their work. The market is one of the themed markets of the Lübecker Weihnachtsstadt (Lübeck's Christmas City) and combines festive craftwork with the historic church interior.
