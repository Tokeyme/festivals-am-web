Lübecker Museumsnacht

Culture festival · Lübeck, Altstadt · 29 August 2026

When29 August 2026
WhereAltstadt, 23552 Lübeck
TypeCulture festival
StateSchleswig-Holstein

During the Lübecker Museumsnacht (Lübeck Museum Night), numerous museums and cultural institutions of the Hanseatic city open their doors in the evening and through the night. With a single ticket, visitors can experience exhibitions, activities and a varied supporting programme in one evening.
