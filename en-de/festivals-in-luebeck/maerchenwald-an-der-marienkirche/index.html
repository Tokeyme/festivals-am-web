Märchenwald an der Marienkirche

Christmas market · Lübeck, Altstadt · 23 November – 30 December 2026

When23 November – 30 December 2026
WhereMarienkirchhof, Altstadt, 23552 Lübeck
TypeChristmas market
StateSchleswig-Holstein

At the foot of the St. Marien-Kirche (St Mary's Church), the square is transformed into a Märchenwald (fairy-tale forest), a pre-Christmas attraction of the north's Weihnachtsstadt (Christmas City). Illuminated firs and fairy-tale motifs create a contemplative mood in the heart of Lübeck's old town.
