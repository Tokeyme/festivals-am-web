Maritimer Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Lübeck, Altstadt · 23 November – 29 December 2026

When23 November – 29 December 2026
WhereKoberg, Altstadt, 23552 Lübeck
TypeChristmas market
StateSchleswig-Holstein

On the Koberg in the northern part of the old town, the Maritime Weihnachtsmarkt (maritime Christmas market) invites visitors to browse amid a maritime atmosphere. As one of the themed markets of the Weihnachtsstadt des Nordens (Christmas City of the North), it offers festive food and handicrafts.
