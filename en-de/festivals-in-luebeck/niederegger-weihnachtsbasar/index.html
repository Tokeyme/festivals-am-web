Niederegger Weihnachtsbasar

Christmas market · Lübeck, Altstadt · 23 November – 24 December 2026

When23 November – 24 December 2026
WhereBreite Straße 89, Altstadt, 23552 Lübeck
TypeChristmas market
StateSchleswig-Holstein

The Niederegger Weihnachtsbasar (Christmas bazaar) takes place in the flagship house of the marzipan maker Niederegger on Breite Straße. The bazaar is one of the themed markets of the Lübecker Weihnachtsstadt (Lübeck's Christmas City) and offers festive goods centred on Lübeck marzipan.
