Travemünder Lichterzauber

Culture festival · Lübeck, Travemünde · 17–19 September 2026

When17–19 September 2026
WhereGodewindpark, Kaiserallee, Travemünde, 23570 Lübeck
TypeCulture festival
StateSchleswig-Holstein

During the Travemünder Lichterzauber, the Godewindpark is transformed into a Festival of 1000 Lights, when trees, the lake and the meadows turn into a sparkling sea of lights in the evening hours. Fire shows, live music and culinary offerings create a fairytale atmosphere.
