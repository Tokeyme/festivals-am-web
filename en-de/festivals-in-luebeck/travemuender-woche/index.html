Travemünder Woche

Funfair · Lübeck, Travemünde · 23 July – 1 August 2027

When23 July – 1 August 2027
WhereLübeck-Travemünde, Travemünde, 23570 Lübeck
TypeFunfair
StateSchleswig-Holstein

International sailing event combined with a maritime folk festival by the water in Travemünde. The festival grounds feature live music, culinary stands, and maritime atmosphere; the finale is a grand fireworks display from the Norderdam pier.
