Travemünder Woche

Funfair · Lübeck, Travemünde · 17–26 July 2026

When17–26 July 2026
WhereLübeck-Travemünde, Travemünde, 23570 Lübeck
TypeFunfair
StateSchleswig-Holstein

International sailing event combined with a maritime folk festival by the water in Travemünde. The festival grounds feature live music, culinary stands, and maritime atmosphere; the finale on July 26 is a grand fireworks display from the Norderdam pier.
