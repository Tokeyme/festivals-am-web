Weihnachtsmarkt Travemünde - Schiff Ahoi

Christmas market · Lübeck, Travemünde · 21 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

When21 November 2026 – 3 January 2027
WhereOstpreußenkai, Travemünde, 23570 Lübeck
TypeChristmas market
StateSchleswig-Holstein

On the Ostpreußenkai and in the cruise terminal in Travemünde, Schiff Ahoi creates a small maritime Christmas world. Fine arts and crafts, live music and catering, as well as a stage programme and a children's train ride, are part of the offering.
