Wichtelwunderland

Christmas market · Lübeck, Altstadt · 23 November – 30 December 2026

When23 November – 30 December 2026
WhereSt. Jakobi Kirchhof, Altstadt, 23552 Lübeck
TypeChristmas market
StateSchleswig-Holstein

On the St. Jakobi Kirchhof (St James's churchyard) in the Seefahrerviertel (seafarers' quarter), the Wichtelwunderland takes shape, a Christmas market for families and children. Visitors can play Wichtelgolf, get creative in the Wichtelwerkstatt (elves' workshop) and explore the Wichtelhütten, Wichtelladen and Wichtelschule (elves' huts, shop and school).
