Adventsfest in Luthers Haus und Hof

Christmas market · Lutherstadt Wittenberg · 11–13 December 2026

When11–13 December 2026
WhereLutherhof, Collegienstraße 54, 06886 Lutherstadt Wittenberg
TypeChristmas market
StateSaxony-Anhalt

The LutherMuseen invite visitors to an Advent festival in the Lutherhof, Martin Luther's historic home. On the third weekend of Advent there is an Advent market with carols, a light show, an art bazaar, traditional craft stalls, food and drink, puppet theatre, concerts and craft activities. Admission is free.
