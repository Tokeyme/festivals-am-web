Markt der schönen Dinge

Christmas market · Lutherstadt Wittenberg · 27–29 November 2026

When27–29 November 2026
WhereCranach-Hof, Schlossstraße 1, 06886 Lutherstadt Wittenberg
TypeChristmas market
StateSaxony-Anhalt

One of the most appealing arts and crafts markets in the region is held in the historic Cranach-Höfe. More than 40 artists and craftspeople from the region and beyond offer high-quality and imaginative work for sale. In the Christmas bakery children decorate their own gingerbread houses, and the Cranach-Café serves home-made cake and Cranach mulled wine.
