Wittenberger Reformationsfest

Village & club fest · Lutherstadt Wittenberg · 31 October 2026

When31 October 2026
WhereAltstadt Lutherstadt Wittenberg, 06886 Lutherstadt Wittenberg
TypeVillage & club fest
StateSaxony-Anhalt

Festival for Reformation Day centred on Martin Luther's posting of the theses, with a historical market spectacle, music, church services and a large procession of lights from the Lutherhaus through the old town. Admission to the market spectacle is free.
