Wittenberger Töpfermarkt

Market · Lutherstadt Wittenberg · 26–27 September 2026

When26–27 September 2026
WhereMarktplatz und Kirchhof der Stadtkirche, 06886 Lutherstadt Wittenberg
TypeMarket
StateSaxony-Anhalt

Potters and ceramic workshops from all over Germany show their craft on the Marktplatz and in the churchyard of the Stadtkirche. Visitors can watch the potters working at the wheel. By tradition the mayor opens the market on the Saturday with the potters' procession.
