Bretzenheimer Kerb

Church / parish fest · Mainz, Bretzenheim · 7–10 August 2026

When7–10 August 2026
WhereBretzenheim, 55128 Mainz
TypeChurch / parish fest
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Bretzenheimer Kerb is the church-consecration festival in the Mainz district of Bretzenheim, organised by the Vereinsring Mainz-Bretzenheim. It opens with the traditional tapping of the beer keg; on offer are fairground rides, game stalls, and food and drink.
