Finther Kerb

Church / parish fest · Mainz, Finthen · 12–16 September 2026

When12–16 September 2026
WhereRodeneckplatz, Finthen, 55126 Mainz
TypeChurch / parish fest
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Finther Kerb is the church consecration festival in the Mainz district of Finthen and is celebrated around the Rodeneckplatz. Alongside fairground rides and stalls, the local associations put together a programme; activities also take place at the parish church of St. Martin.
