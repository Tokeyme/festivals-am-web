Gonsenheimer Adventsmarkt

Christmas market · Mainz, Gonsenheim · 28–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28–29 November 2026
WherePfarrer-Grimm-Anlage, Gonsenheim, 55124 Mainz
TypeChristmas market
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Gonsenheimer Adventsmarkt is organised by the Vereinsring Gonsenheim in the Pfarrer-Grimm-Anlage. As well as stalls with food and drink, there is a craft workshop for children, and St Nicholas is expected.
