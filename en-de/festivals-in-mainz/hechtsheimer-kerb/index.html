Hechtsheimer Kerb

Church / parish fest · Mainz, Hechtsheim · 4–7 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When4–7 September 2026
WhereAlter Ortskern / An der Brausch, Hechtsheim, 55129 Mainz
TypeChurch / parish fest
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Hechtsheimer Kerb is the church consecration festival in the old village centre of Mainz-Hechtsheim. For children there is a funfair on the Brausch, while the celebrations take place in the village centre. Afterwards, the Hechtsheim winemakers invite visitors to the Winzertage, with their courtyards open.
