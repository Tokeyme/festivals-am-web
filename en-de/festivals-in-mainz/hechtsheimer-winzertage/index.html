Hechtsheimer Winzertage

Wine festival · Mainz, Hechtsheim · 11–14 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When11–14 September 2026
WhereAlter Ortskern, Hechtsheim, 55129 Mainz
TypeWine festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

At the Hechtsheimer Winzertage, the winemakers in the old village centre of Mainz-Hechtsheim open up their courtyards. Wines from the local wineries are served, along with regional culinary specialities such as the traditional Leberknödelessen.
