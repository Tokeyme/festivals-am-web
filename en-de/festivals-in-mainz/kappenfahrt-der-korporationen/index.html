Kappenfahrt der Korporationen

Carnival · Mainz, Altstadt · 9 February 2027

When9 February 2027
WhereMainzer Altstadt, Altstadt, 55116 Mainz
TypeCarnival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

On Shrove Tuesday, the Mainz Fastnacht campaign draws to a close with the Kappenfahrt der Korporationen in the old town. As the traditional conclusion of the days of revelry, the Fastnacht clubs process through the lanes.
