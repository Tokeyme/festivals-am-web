Laubenheimer Kerb

Church / parish fest · Mainz, Laubenheim · 11–14 September 2026

When11–14 September 2026
WhereLaubenheimer Park, Laubenheim, 55130 Mainz
TypeChurch / parish fest
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Laubenheimer Kerb is the church-consecration festival in the Mainz district of Laubenheim and is celebrated in the Laubenheimer Park. Alongside a children's programme, there is music, food and drink for the whole family.
