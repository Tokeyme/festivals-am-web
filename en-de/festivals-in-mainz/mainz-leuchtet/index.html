Mainz leuchtet

Culture festival · Mainz, Altstadt · 24–26 September 2026

When24–26 September 2026
WhereRheingoldhalle bis Schillerplatz, Altstadt, 55116 Mainz
TypeCulture festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

At the Late-Light-Festival Mainz leuchtet, buildings and squares in the city centre between the Rheingoldhalle and Schillerplatz are illuminated. Light art, projections and creative installations turn the evening city centre into a backdrop of light.
