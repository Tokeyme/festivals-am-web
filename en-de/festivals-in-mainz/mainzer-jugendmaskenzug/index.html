Mainzer Jugendmaskenzug

Carnival · Mainz · 6 February 2027

When6 February 2027
WhereMainzer Innenstadt, 55116 Mainz
TypeCarnival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Mainzer Jugendmaskenzug makes its way through the city centre on Fastnachtssamstag. Children and young people from schools, clubs and carnival guards take part in costume; the procession is a prelude to the main Fastnacht days.
