Mainzer Rosenmontagszug

Carnival · Mainz · 8 February 2027

When8 February 2027
WhereMainzer Innenstadt, 55116 Mainz
TypeCarnival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Mainzer Rosenmontagszug is the highlight of Mainz's Straßenfastnacht. With over a hundred procession entries, themed floats, guards, walking groups and bands make their way through the city centre from 11:11 a.m.
