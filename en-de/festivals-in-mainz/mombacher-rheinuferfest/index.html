Mombacher Rheinuferfest

Neighbourhood festival · Mainz, Mombach · 21–23 August 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When21–23 August 2026
WhereKanufreunde 1929 e.V., Rheinallee 128 b, Mombach, 55120 Mainz
TypeNeighbourhood festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Mombacher Rheinuferfest is a summer festival held by Mombach's clubs on the grounds of the Kanufreunde 1929 e.V., right on the bank of the Rhine. There is live music, a family programme and food stalls. Traditionally, the festival is opened by the Lord Mayor with the tapping of a beer barrel.
