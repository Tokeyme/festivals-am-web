Rebblütenfest

Wine festival · Mainz, Laubenheim · 17–20 July 2026

When17–20 July 2026
WhereLaubenheimer Park, Laubenheim, 55130 Mainz
TypeWine festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The traditional Rebblütenfest transforms the Laubenheimer Park into a cosy wine village. Mainz winemakers from Laubenheim vineyards serve their wines, accompanied by culinary specialities. The local Laubenheim associations help to stage the festival, and the twin community of Longchamp is represented.
