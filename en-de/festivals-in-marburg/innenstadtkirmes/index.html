Innenstadtkirmes

Funfair · Marburg · 9–12 October 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When9–12 October 2026
Where35037 Marburg
TypeFunfair
StateHesse

The Innenstadtkirmes (city-centre funfair) takes place alongside the Marburg Elisabethmarkt in the town centre. The fairground draws visitors with rides, showmen's booths and culinary offerings, including a Ferris wheel with a view over the rooftops of Marburg. It adds a family-friendly funfair to the autumn market bustle.
