Marburger Kunsthandwerkermarkt

Market · Marburg · 7–8 November 2026

When7–8 November 2026
WhereErwin-Piscator-Haus, Biegenstraße 15, 35037 Marburg
TypeMarket
StateHesse

In and in front of the Erwin-Piscator-Haus, the Marburger Kunsthandwerkermarkt (arts and crafts market) presents handmade works by artisans. Visitors find a varied range of handmade products for the home and for the senses.
