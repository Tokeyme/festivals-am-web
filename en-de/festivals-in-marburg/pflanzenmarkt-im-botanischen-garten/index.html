Pflanzenmarkt im Botanischen Garten

Market · Marburg, Lahnberge · 12–13 September 2026

When12–13 September 2026
WhereNeuer Botanischer Garten, Karl-von-Frisch-Straße, Lahnberge, 35043 Marburg
TypeMarket
StateHesse

At the autumn plant market in the New Botanical Garden on Marburg's Lahnberge, around 80 regional and international nurseries, plant breeders and collectors offer rare crop and ornamental plants. The range includes varieties of roses, perennials and woody plants, wild plants, herbs, young vegetable plants and fruit trees. Over the weekend, the garden turns into a large plant paradise.
