Weihnachtsmarkt auf dem Hofgut Dagobertshausen

Christmas market · Marburg, Dagobertshausen · 27 November – 20 December 2026

When27 November – 20 December 2026
WhereHofgut Dagobertshausen, Dagobertshausen, 35041 Marburg
TypeChristmas market
StateHesse

On the Advent weekends, the festively decorated grounds of the Hofgut Dagobertshausen are transformed into an atmospheric Christmas wonderland. Visitors stroll across the estate and find Christmas decorations, handicrafts as well as culinary offerings such as hot drinks and snacks. Admission is free.
