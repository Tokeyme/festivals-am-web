Weihnachtsmarkt im Bückingsgarten

Christmas market · Marburg · 27 November – 20 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 November – 20 December 2026
WhereBückingsgarten, Landgraf-Philipp-Straße, 35037 Marburg
TypeChristmas market
StateHesse

The Christmas market in the Bückingsgarten on Marburg's Schlossberg is regarded as the smallest and highest-lying Christmas market in the city. On the terrace with a view over the old town, visitors can expect a magical atmosphere with mulled wine and festive offerings on the Advent weekends.
