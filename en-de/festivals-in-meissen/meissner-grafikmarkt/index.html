Meißner Grafikmarkt

Market · Meißen · 12–13 September 2026

When12–13 September 2026
WhereDomplatz 1, 01662 Meißen
TypeMarket
StateSaxony

In the Albrechtsburg, around 40 artists from Meißen and the surrounding region as well as from Leipzig, Halle, Saalfeld and Berlin show and sell their work. On offer are prints, drawings, watercolours, screen prints, photographs and artists' books, with the artists themselves on hand to advise. Open from 10am to 6pm on both days, and admission is free.
