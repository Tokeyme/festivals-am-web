Meißner Weinfest

Wine festival · Meißen · 18–20 September 2026

When18–20 September 2026
Where01662 Meißen
TypeWine festival
StateSaxony

One of the largest wine festivals in Saxony, held in the historic old town between the market square, Domplatz and the banks of the Elbe, with around twenty stages, a festival parade on Sunday and a closing fireworks display.
