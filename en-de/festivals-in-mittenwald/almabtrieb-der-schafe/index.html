Almabtrieb der Schafe

Event · Mittenwald · 12 September 2026

When12 September 2026
WhereOrtszentrum, 82481 Mittenwald
TypeEvent
StateBavaria

As part of the Mittenwalder Bauernwochen (Mittenwald farmers' weeks), the sheep are driven through the village centre at the end of their summer on the alpine pastures. This Schafabtrieb (ceremonial sheep drive down from the mountain pastures) is one of the autumn folk-custom dates in the Werdenfelser Land region.
