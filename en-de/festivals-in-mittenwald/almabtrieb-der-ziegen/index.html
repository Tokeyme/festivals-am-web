Almabtrieb der Ziegen

Event · Mittenwald · 5 September 2026

When5 September 2026
WhereDekan-Karl-Platz, 82481 Mittenwald
TypeEvent
StateBavaria

To open the Mittenwalder Bauernwochen (farmers' weeks), the goats return from the summer pastures to the valley. At Dekan-Karl-Platz the animals are driven in and then judged.
