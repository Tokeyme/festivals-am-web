Bozner Markt

Historical festival · Mittenwald · 1–9 August 2026

When1–9 August 2026
WhereOrtskern, Obermarkt und Dekan-Karl-Platz, 82481 Mittenwald
TypeHistorical festival
StateBavaria

The Bozner Markt transforms the centre of Mittenwald into a historical trade and craft market from the 16th century. Market stalls, wine taverns, craftsmen, jesters, merchants and nobles, together with theatrical scenes and musical performances, bring the historical market bustle to life. The venues are the Kuranlage Puit spa gardens and the town centre with Obermarkt and Dekan-Karl-Platz.
