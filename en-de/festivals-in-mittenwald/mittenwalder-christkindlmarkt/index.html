Mittenwalder Christkindlmarkt

Christmas market · Mittenwald · 10–13 December 2026

When10–13 December 2026
WhereRund um die Pfarrkirche St. Peter und Paul, Matthias-Klotz-Straße / Ballenhausgasse, 82481 Mittenwald
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

The traditional Christkindlmarkt (Christmas market) takes place around the Baroque parish church of St. Peter and Paul and is run by around 36 stallholders. The supporting programme includes carriage rides, pony riding, children's craft stations, visits from St. Nicholas, guided tours of the local Lüftlmalerei (traditional painted facades) and daily stall concerts by local music groups.
