Stadlfest mit Schafprämierung

Event · Mittenwald · 13 September 2026

When13 September 2026
WhereSchafstadl an der Gröbl-Alm, 82481 Mittenwald
TypeEvent
StateBavaria

At the Stadlfest by the Schafstadl (sheep barn) at the Gröbl-Alm, the flocks of sheep are judged and awarded prizes after the alpine summer. This festive event is part of the Mittenwalder Bauernwochen (farmers' weeks).
