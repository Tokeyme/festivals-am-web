Münchner Christkindlmarkt am Marienplatz

Christmas market · München, Altstadt · 20 November – 24 December 2026

When20 November – 24 December 2026
WhereMarienplatz, Altstadt, 80331 München
TypeChristmas market
StateBayern

The traditional Munich Christkindl market around Marienplatz features over 130 stands, a large Christmas tree, and daily Advent music from the town hall balcony. Closed on All Souls' Day.
