Hammer Fez

Street festival · Münster, Südviertel · 1–2 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When1–2 August 2026
WhereHammer Straße, Südviertel, Südviertel, 48153 Münster
TypeStreet festival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Hammer Fez is the revival of the Hammer Street Festival along Hammer Straße in the South District. It offers programs and stands focused on the neighborhood, extending at least to St. Joseph's Church.
