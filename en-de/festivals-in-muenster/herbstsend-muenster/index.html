Herbstsend Münster

Funfair · Münster · 24 October – 1 November 2026

When24 October – 1 November 2026
WhereSchlossplatz, 48149 Münster
TypeFunfair
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Herbstsend is the last of three annual editions of the Send on Schlossplatz. The folk festival is considered the largest fair in Münsterland and attracts hundreds of thousands of visitors annually.
