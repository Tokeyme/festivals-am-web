Town festival · Münster · 28–30 August 2026

When28–30 August 2026
WhereInnenstadt (Domplatz, Salzstraße), 48143 Münster
TypeTown festival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Münster Mittendrin city festival takes place in 2026 together with the North Rhine-Westphalia Day. With multiple stages in the city center, the state anniversary is celebrated as a large public and family festival.
