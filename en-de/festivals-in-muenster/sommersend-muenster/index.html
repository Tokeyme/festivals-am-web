Sommersend Münster

Funfair · Münster · 16–20 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When16–20 July 2026
WhereSchlossplatz, 48149 Münster
TypeFunfair
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Sommersend is one of three annual editions of the traditional Send, the largest folk festival in Münsterland. On Schlossplatz, visitors can enjoy rides, stands, and food vendors.
