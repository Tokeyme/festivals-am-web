Haidhauser Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Munich, Haidhausen · 24 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When24 November – 23 December 2026
WhereWeißenburger Platz, Haidhausen, 81667 Munich
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

A traditional district Christmas market around the fountain on Weißenburger Platz in Haidhausen. Around 50 stalls offer mulled wine, food and handcrafted goods. The market has an intimate, quiet character and is run by a club from Haidhausen.
