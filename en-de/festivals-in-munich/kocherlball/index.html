Kocherlball

Village & club fest · Munich, Schwabing · 19 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When19 July 2026
WhereChinesischer Turm, Englischer Garten, Schwabing, 80538 Munich
TypeVillage & club fest
StateBavaria

Traditional early-morning folk dance at the Chinese Tower in the English Garden in commemoration of the historic Sunday balls of domestic staff. Admission is free, and dancing takes place to Bavarian folk music; many guests wear traditional clothing.
