Lange Nacht der Münchner Museen

Culture festival · Munich · 17 October 2026

When17 October 2026
Whereverschiedene Museen und Kulturorte, 80333 Munich
TypeCulture festival
StateBavaria

On a single night, around 100 Munich museums, collections, galleries and other cultural venues open their doors until the early hours of the morning. A single ticket is valid for all participating venues and the shuttle buses. On offer are exhibitions, guided tours and numerous activities into the late hours.
