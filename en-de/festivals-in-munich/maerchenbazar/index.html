Märchenbazar

Christmas market · Munich, Olympiapark · 26 November – 28 December 2026

When26 November – 28 December 2026
WhereSpiridon-Louis-Ring 100, Olympiapark, 80809 Munich
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

A winter market in the Olympiapark Sued on the summer Tollwood site. Between circus tents and fairground structures there is live music daily on several stages and acrobatics in the tents. For children, fairy tale hours, candle dipping, puppet theatre and craft activities are free.
