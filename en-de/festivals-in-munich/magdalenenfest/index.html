Magdalenenfest

Funfair · Munich, Neuhausen-Nymphenburg · 4–19 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When4–19 July 2026
WhereHirschgarten, Nymphenburg, Neuhausen-Nymphenburg, 80639 Munich
TypeFunfair
StateBavaria

One of Munich's coziest folk festivals in the Hirschgarten under old chestnut trees, with family-friendly rides, market stands, and food vendors. The festival dates back to the 18th century.
