München Marathon

Sports event · Munich · 11 October 2026

When11 October 2026
WhereStart und Ziel: Olympiapark, 80809 Munich
TypeSports event
StateBavaria

A traditional city marathon starting and finishing in the Olympiapark. Alongside the marathon there is a half marathon, a 10-kilometre run, a relay and a children's run, as well as a Runners Festival in the Olympiapark. The route runs through the city centre, past landmarks such as Marienplatz and Odeonsplatz.
