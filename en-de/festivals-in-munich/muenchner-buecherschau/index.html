Münchner Bücherschau

Culture festival · Munich, Lehel · 12–29 November 2026

When12–29 November 2026
WhereHaus der Kunst, Prinzregentenstraße 1, Lehel, 80538 Munich
TypeCulture festival
StateBavaria

A large book fair in the west wing of the Haus der Kunst, with more than 20,000 titles from around 300 publishers. Admission is free; visitors can browse every day and take part in events. The book fair sees itself as a meeting place for book lovers and supports local booksellers.
