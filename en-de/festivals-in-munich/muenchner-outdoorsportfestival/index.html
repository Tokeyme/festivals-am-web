Münchner Outdoorsportfestival

Sports event · Munich · 27 September 2026

When27 September 2026
WhereOlympiapark, 80809 Munich
TypeSports event
StateBavaria

A free sports festival in the Olympiapark where visitors can try out more than 50 classic and trending sports. Sports clubs, associations and organisations run hands-on activities at locations including Hans-Jochen-Vogel-Platz, the Olympiasee and the Theatron. There are also inclusive options for people with and without disabilities.
