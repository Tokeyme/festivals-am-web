Neuhauser Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Munich, Neuhausen · 20 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November – 23 December 2026
WhereRotkreuzplatz, Neuhausen, 80634 Munich
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

A Christmas market on the north and south sides of the Rotkreuzplatz in the Neuhausen district. On offer are Christmas tree decorations, nativity figures, arts and crafts as well as food and drink. There is also a supporting programme of puppet theatre and music.
