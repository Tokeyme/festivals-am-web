Oktoberfest

Funfair · Munich · 19 September – 4 October 2026

When19 September – 4 October 2026
WhereTheresienwiese, 80336 Munich
TypeFunfair
StateBavaria

The world's largest folk festival with beer tents from Munich breweries, festival hosts, rides, and exhibitors on Theresienwiese. Traditionally opened with the ceremonial beer tap by the mayor.
