Tanz der Marktfrauen am Viktualienmarkt

Carnival · Munich, Altstadt · 9 February 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When9 February 2027
WhereViktualienmarkt, Altstadt, 80331 Munich
TypeCarnival
StateBavaria

Carnival festivities on the Viktualienmarkt on Faschingsdienstag with the traditional Tanz der Marktfrauen. The Marktfrauen dance in costume in front of their stalls, accompanied by an appearance from the Prinzenpaar of the Narrhalla. The free spectacle is a highlight of the Munich Fasching.
