Weihnachtsdorf im Kaiserhof der Residenz

Christmas market · Munich, Altstadt · 20 November 2026 – 6 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November 2026 – 6 January 2027
WhereResidenzstraße 1, Kaiserhof der Residenz, Altstadt, 80333 Munich
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

A Christmas market in the Kaiserhof of the Munich Residenz with rustic wooden huts. Craftspeople such as goldsmiths, leatherworkers and glassblowers show their work. For children, there is a fairy-tale forest with Hänsel and Gretel as well as a puppet theatre.
