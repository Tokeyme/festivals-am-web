Weihnachtsmarkt am Chinesischen Turm

Christmas market · Munich, Schwabing · 20 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November – 23 December 2026
WhereEnglischer Garten 3, Schwabing, 80538 Munich
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

A Weihnachtsmarkt in the Englischer Garten around the Chinesischer Turm. Wooden huts offer crafts and art as well as regional food and drink. The programme includes a historic children's carousel, a children's railway, carriage rides and music from the Turmbläser.
