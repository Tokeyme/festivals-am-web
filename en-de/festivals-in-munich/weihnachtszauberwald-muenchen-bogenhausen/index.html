Weihnachtszauberwald München-Bogenhausen

Christmas market · Munich, Bogenhausen · 12 November 2026 – 10 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12 November 2026 – 10 January 2027
WhereWiese am Cosimabad, Cosimastraße, Bogenhausen, 81925 Munich
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

A Christmas market and mulled-wine festival on the meadow by the Cosimabad in Bogenhausen. The market offers a wide range of arts and crafts as well as numerous varieties of mulled wine, including alcohol-free and organic ones. The site is laid out as a wintry forest strung with lights.
