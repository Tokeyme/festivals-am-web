WinterWunderWERK im Werksviertel-Mitte

Christmas market · Munich, Berg am Laim · 12 November – 20 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12 November – 20 December 2026
WhereWerksviertel-Mitte, am Ostbahnhof, Berg am Laim, 81677 Munich
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

An urban Christmas market in the Werksviertel-Mitte by the Ostbahnhof. Among the repurposed industrial buildings, there are mulled wine, glittering lights and food stalls. The offerings include, among other things, Bavarian curling lanes and a supporting programme with live music.
