Winterzauber auf dem Viktualienmarkt

Christmas market · Munich, Altstadt · 20 November 2026 – 5 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November 2026 – 5 January 2027
WhereViktualienmarkt, Altstadt, 80331 Munich
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

A wintry market on the Viktualienmarkt in the Altstadt. Around the Maibaum, festively decorated stalls invite visitors to enjoy Glühwein and Schmankerl. The Winterzauber combines the running of the weekly market with a Christmassy atmosphere.
