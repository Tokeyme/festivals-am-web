Winterzauber Gans am Wasser

Christmas market · Munich, Sendling-Westpark · 27 November 2026 – 1 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 November 2026 – 1 January 2027
WhereCafé Gans am Wasser, Westpark (am Mollsee), Sendling-Westpark, 81377 Munich
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

A small, atmospheric Winterzauber at the Café Gans am Wasser in the Westpark. Around fire bowls there is mulled wine, crêpes and live music on a small stage. At a crafts market, small businesses and artists show their work, and there is also a fire show and a children's programme.
