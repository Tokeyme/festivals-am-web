Zamanand Festival

Town festival · Munich, Maxvorstadt · 12–13 September 2026

When12–13 September 2026
WhereLudwigstraße (zwischen Odeonsplatz und Siegestor), Maxvorstadt, 80539 Munich
TypeTown festival
StateBavaria

A free city festival on the car-free Ludwigstraße between Odeonsplatz and Siegestor. At the marketplace, much revolves around sustainability, with music, workshops, street food, hands-on activities and discussions. At the same time, the Corso Leopold takes place on Leopoldstraße.
