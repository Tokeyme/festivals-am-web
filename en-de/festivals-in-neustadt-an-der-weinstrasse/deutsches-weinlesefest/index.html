Deutsches Weinlesefest

Wine festival · Neustadt an der Weinstraße · 25 September – 12 October 2026

When25 September – 12 October 2026
WhereInnenstadt Neustadt an der Weinstraße, 67433 Neustadt an der Weinstraße
TypeWine festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The largest wine festival in the Palatinate, featuring the Winzerdorf Haiselscher, a funfair, the w.i.n.e.FESTival and the election of the Palatinate wine royalty. The highlight is the Großer Winzerfestumzug on 11 October 2026.
