Hambacher Straßenflohmarkt

Flea market · Neustadt an der Weinstraße, Hambach · 12–13 September 2026

When12–13 September 2026
WhereSchloßstraße, Hambach, 67434 Neustadt an der Weinstraße
TypeFlea market
StateRhineland-Palatinate

Along Hambacher Schloßstraße, stallholders sell odds and ends along with all sorts of things for the house, yard and garden. There is also plenty to eat, for those with a sweet tooth as well as lovers of hearty, rustic fare. The flea market is open on both days from 11am to 5pm.
