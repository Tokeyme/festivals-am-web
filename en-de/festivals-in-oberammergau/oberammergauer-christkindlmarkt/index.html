Oberammergauer Christkindlmarkt

Christmas market · Oberammergau · 29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When29 November 2026
WhereKirchplatz, 82487 Oberammergau
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

A traditional Christkindlmarkt (Christmas market) around the church square in front of the parish church of St Peter and Paul in Oberammergau. Lovingly decorated stalls offer festive specialities, mulled wine, handicrafts and gift ideas. The market is regarded as a "market with heart", as the proceeds go to charitable causes.
