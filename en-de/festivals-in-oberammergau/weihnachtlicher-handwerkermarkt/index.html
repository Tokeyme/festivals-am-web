Weihnachtlicher Handwerkermarkt

Christmas market · Oberammergau · 12–13 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12–13 December 2026
WhereAmmergauer Haus, Eugen-Papst-Straße 9a, 82487 Oberammergau
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

A Christmas arts and crafts market in the Ammergauer Haus in Oberammergau. Around 50 regional artisans present their craftsmanship, including woodcarvers, nativity-scene makers, glassblowers and porcelain painters. Some of the artisans can be watched at work.
