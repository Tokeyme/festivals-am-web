Bauern- und Käsemarkt

Market · Oberstdorf · 27 September 2026

When27 September 2026
WherePrinzenstraße bis Marktplatz, 87561 Oberstdorf, 87561 Oberstdorf
TypeMarket
StateBavaria

The Bauern- und Käsemarkt (farmers' and cheese market) presents regional produce and rural crafts from the Oberstdorf Haus along Prinzenstraße to the Marktplatz. The focus is on Allgäu cheese and other specialities from the region. The organisers are Oberstdorf Tourismus GmbH and the Bauernverband (farmers' association).
