Dorffest Oberstdorf

Village & club fest · Oberstdorf · 8 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When8 August 2026
WhereOberstdorf Haus / Kurpark, Prinzregenten-Platz 1, 87561 Oberstdorf, 87561 Oberstdorf
TypeVillage & club fest
StateBavaria

On the second Saturday in August, Oberstdorf's local clubs transform the Kurpark (spa gardens) and the parish garden into a colourful festival ground with two stages. On offer are live music, a children's programme featuring duck fishing, laser biathlon and a bouncy castle, as well as food and drinks. The festival's motto is 'Ein Dorffest - zwei Bühnen' (one village festival - two stages).
