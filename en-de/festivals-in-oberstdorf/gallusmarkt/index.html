Gallusmarkt

Market · Oberstdorf · 10 October 2026

When10 October 2026
WhereMarktplatz / Fußgängerzone, 87561 Oberstdorf, 87561 Oberstdorf
TypeMarket
StateBavaria

The long-established Gallusmarkt is a general market with around 300 stalls in and around the pedestrian zone by the church tower. On offer are goods for the household, garden and trade, as well as textiles, toys, pottery and flowers. Admission is free.
