Nebelhorn Trophy

Sports event · Oberstdorf · 23–26 September 2026

When23–26 September 2026
WhereRoßbichlstraße 2-6, 87561 Oberstdorf
TypeSports event
StateBavaria

An international figure skating competition at the Eissportzentrum Oberstdorf, held for the 58th time in 2026. As part of the ISU Challenger Series it is the highest international format after the Grand Prix events, and since 1969 it has been a stepping stone on the way to the European and World Championships. Skating takes place in the four Olympic disciplines: women's, men's, pairs and ice dance.
