Oberstdorfer Advent

Christmas market · Oberstdorf · 28 November – 19 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28 November – 19 December 2026
WhereKurpark / Oberstdorf Haus, Prinzregenten-Platz 1, 87561 Oberstdorf, 87561 Oberstdorf
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

On the Advent Saturdays, the Oberstdorfer Advent invites visitors to light the candles together on the large Advent wreath in the Kurpark (spa gardens). Afterwards a contemplative programme with music in local dialect and classical pieces awaits visitors at the Oberstdorf Haus. Sales stalls in the Kurpark round off the pre-Christmas event.
