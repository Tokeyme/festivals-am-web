Oberstdorfer Fasnachtsumzug

Carnival · Oberstdorf · 7 February 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When7 February 2027
WhereOrtszentrum, 87561 Oberstdorf, 87561 Oberstdorf
TypeCarnival
StateBavaria

The Oberstdorfer Fasnachtsumzug (carnival parade) moves through the town centre on Fasnachtssonntag (Carnival Sunday) and marks the highlight of the local Fasnacht (carnival). The parade traditionally starts near the Loft cinema. The organisers are d' Oberstdorfer Fasnachter.
