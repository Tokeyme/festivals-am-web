Oberstdorfer kleinKunsttage

Culture festival · Oberstdorf · 4–6 September 2026

When4–6 September 2026
WhereKurpark, Prinzregenten-Platz 1, 87561 Oberstdorf, 87561 Oberstdorf
TypeCulture festival
StateBavaria

On the first weekend of September, the Oberstdorfer kleinKunsttage bring cabaret, comedy, music and poetry slam to the Kurpark (spa gardens). Several performances take place each day, along with food and drinks. Admission is free on all days.
