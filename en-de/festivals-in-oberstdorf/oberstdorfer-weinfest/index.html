Oberstdorfer Weinfest

Wine festival · Oberstdorf · 28–30 August 2026

When28–30 August 2026
WhereOberstdorf Haus, Prinzregenten-Platz 1, 87561 Oberstdorf, 87561 Oberstdorf
TypeWine festival
StateBavaria

At the Oberstdorfer Weinfest (wine festival), several wineries serve their wines at the Oberstdorf Haus in the Kurpark (spa gardens), ranging from Riesling and Pinot to winemaker's sparkling wine and wine brandies. Regional specialities are served alongside, accompanied by live music on all three days. Admission is free.
