Viehscheid Oberstdorf

Funfair · Oberstdorf, Ried · 12 September 2026

When12 September 2026
WhereNordic Zentrum, Birgsauer Str. 39, 87561 Oberstdorf, Ried, 87561 Oberstdorf
TypeFunfair
StateBavaria

At the Viehscheid (ceremonial cattle drive from the alpine pastures), around 1,000 cattle return from the mountain pastures to the valley after the alpine summer. At the Nordic Zentrum in Ried, the decorated animals are handed back to their owners at the Scheidplatz in front of thousands of spectators. In the festival tent, brass music by the Musikkapelle Oberstdorf, Allgäu beer and Scheidwurst (a local sausage) create a festive atmosphere.
