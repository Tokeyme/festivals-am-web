Ferragosto

Street festival · Passau, Altstadt · 14–15 August 2026

When14–15 August 2026
WhereTheresienstraße, Altstadt, 94032 Passau
TypeStreet festival
StateBavaria

The Ferragosto is an Italian street festival on Passau's Theresienstraße. Restaurateurs and shops offer Italian food, wine and aperitifs, accompanied by live Italian music. For two days, the street turns into a Mediterranean summer festival.
