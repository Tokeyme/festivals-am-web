Herbstmarkt mit verkaufsoffenem Sonntag

Market · Passau, Innenstadt · 18 October 2026

When18 October 2026
WhereInnenstadt zwischen Nibelungenplatz und Rindermarkt, Innenstadt, 94032 Passau
TypeMarket
StateBavaria

At the autumn market in Passau's city centre, numerous traders from Bavaria, Austria and the Czech Republic offer their goods between Nibelungenplatz and Rindermarkt. The range extends from sweets to hearty savoury treats, along with live music and dance performances. On the Sunday shopping day, the shops in the city centre are also open.
