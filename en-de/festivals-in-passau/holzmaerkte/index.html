Holzmärkte

Market · Passau, Innenstadt · 3–8 December 2026

When3–8 December 2026
WhereLudwigstraße / Ludwigsplatz / Bahnhofstraße, Innenstadt, 94032 Passau
TypeMarket
StateBavaria

At the Passau Holzmärkte (wood markets), traders and craftspeople from the region offer useful, decorative and traditional items made of wood. The markets are held during Advent on two days (3 and 8 December) on Ludwigstraße, Ludwigsplatz and Bahnhofstraße.
