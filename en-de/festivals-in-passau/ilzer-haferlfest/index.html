Ilzer Haferlfest

Village & club fest · Passau, Ilzstadt · 30 July – 2 August 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When30 July – 2 August 2026
WhereIlzstadt, 94034 Passau
TypeVillage & club fest
StateBayern

Traditional folk festival in the Ilzstadt with views of the confluence of three rivers. Dating back to the 1887 harbor festival, the Ilzer Pearl (festival queen) is crowned anew each year.
