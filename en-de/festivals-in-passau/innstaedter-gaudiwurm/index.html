Innstädter Gaudiwurm

Carnival · Passau, Innstadt · 9 February 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When9 February 2027
WherePassau-Innstadt, Innstadt, 94032 Passau
TypeCarnival
StateBavaria

The Innstädter Gaudiwurm is the large Fasching carnival parade through Passau's Innstadt district, staged by the Innstädter Faschingsfreunde on Faschingsdienstag (Shrove Tuesday). Numerous costumed groups and floats move through the streets under various themes. The colourful parade draws many spectators.
