JazzFest Passau

Music festival · Passau, Altstadt · 30 July – 9 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When30 July – 9 August 2026
WhereRathaus-Innenhof (Rathausplatz) und Russenkai, Altstadt, 94032 Passau
TypeMusic festival
StateBavaria

The JazzFest Passau brings top-class jazz and world-music acts to the three-rivers city. The concerts take place in the town hall courtyard and on the Russenkai with free admission. It is organised by the Café Museum, a jazz club in Passau's old town known well beyond the region.
