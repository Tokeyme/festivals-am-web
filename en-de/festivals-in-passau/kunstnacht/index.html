Kunstnacht

Culture festival · Passau, Altstadt · 18 July 2026

When18 July 2026
WherePassauer Altstadt, Altstadt, 94032 Passau
TypeCulture festival
StateBavaria

During the Passau Kunstnacht (Art Night), the entire old town turns into a stage for art and creativity. At more than 60 exhibition venues, studios, galleries and museums open their doors and show visual art, music and performances into the night. Visitors can explore the city centre on foot and discover the wide range of contributions.
