Bornimer Herbstfest

Neighbourhood festival · Potsdam, Bornim · 12 September 2026

When12 September 2026
WherePannenbergstraße, Bornim, 14469 Potsdam
TypeNeighbourhood festival
StateBrandenburg

On the Bornim festival meadow on Pannenbergstrasse there is a varied programme for all ages from 2 to 10 p.m. Two school bands from the music school play, and in the evening the bands Die Potsdamen and Die Guten. The festival includes a flea market for which you can register in advance.
