Das Potsdamer Umweltfest

Event · Potsdam, Bornstedter Feld · 13 September 2026

When13 September 2026
WhereGeorg-Hermann-Allee 101, Bornstedter Feld, 14469 Potsdam
TypeEvent
StateBrandenburg

A day of information and hands-on activities in the Grosser Wiesenpark of the Volkspark Potsdam. Around 100 exhibitors from the region form an information market on sustainability and climate protection, alongside family theatre, talks, a science slam, expert workshops and a discovery pass.
