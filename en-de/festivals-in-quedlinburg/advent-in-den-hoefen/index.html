Advent in den Höfen

Christmas market · Quedlinburg · 28 November – 13 December 2026

When28 November – 13 December 2026
WhereHistorische Innenhöfe der Altstadt, 06484 Quedlinburg
TypeChristmas market
StateSaxony-Anhalt

On the first three Advent weekends, private and historic inner courtyards in Quedlinburg's old town open their gates. In the festively decorated courtyards, artisans offer handmade goods and rarities, along with mulled wine and regional treats. Open on Saturdays and Sundays.
