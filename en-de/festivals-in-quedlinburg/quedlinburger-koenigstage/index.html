Quedlinburger Königstage

Town festival · Quedlinburg · 22–24 May 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When22–24 May 2026
WhereMarktplatz und Altstadt, 06484 Quedlinburg
TypeTown festival
StateSaxony-Anhalt

The large town festival of the World Heritage town turns the historic old town into a festival mile for three days. On several stages, visitors can enjoy live music, a medieval spectacle, handicrafts, regional specialties and a large children's program.
