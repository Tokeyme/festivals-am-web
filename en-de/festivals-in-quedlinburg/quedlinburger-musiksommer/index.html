Quedlinburger Musiksommer

Culture festival · Quedlinburg · 20 June – 12 September 2026

When20 June – 12 September 2026
WhereMarktkirche St. Benedikti und weitere Kirchen, 06484 Quedlinburg
TypeCulture festival
StateSaxony-Anhalt

For over four decades, classical music has resounded in Quedlinburg's historic churches from June to September. The 46th season offers symphony concerts, baroque music, tower brass serenades as well as children's and family concerts; it opens in the Marktkirche St. Benedikti with the Staatskapelle Halle.
