Quedlinburger Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Quedlinburg · 25 November – 22 December 2026

When25 November – 22 December 2026
WhereMarkt, 06484 Quedlinburg
TypeChristmas market
StateSaxony-Anhalt

On the market square in front of the historic town hall, the Advent town of Quedlinburg invites visitors to stroll for around four weeks. A festively decorated half-timbered backdrop, craft stalls, a Christmas pyramid as well as mulled wine and roasted almonds create an Advent atmosphere.
