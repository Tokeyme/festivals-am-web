Bermuda - Konzerte im Alumneum

Music festival · Regensburg, Altstadt · 2–5 September 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When2–5 September 2026
WhereAm Ölberg 2, Altstadt, 93047 Regensburg
TypeMusic festival
StateBavaria

The courtyard of the Alumneum in Regensburg’s old town hosts 14 concerts over four days, all free and open to everyone. The stage offers fingerstyle guitar, funk, punk, latin, pop, hip-hop, folk and soul. From 4 p.m. there are home-made lemonades, beer, cocktails, deep-fried dumplings, coffee and cake; the music starts at 5.30 p.m.
