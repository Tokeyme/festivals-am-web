Der zauberhafte gräfliche Christkindlmarkt

Christmas market · Regensburg, Westenviertel · 19 November – 20 December 2026

When19 November – 20 December 2026
WhereDörnbergpark, Hoppestraße 3a, Westenviertel, 93049 Regensburg
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

The market lies in the Doernbergpark around the rose garden in the heart of Regensburg. It offers gift ideas and crafts along with food and drink, from Christmas market classics to new creations. Admission is free; the market stays closed on Totensonntag.
