Stoffmarkt Regensburg

Market · Regensburg, Steinweg-Pfaffenstein · 3 October 2026

When3 October 2026
WhereDultplatz, Am Europakanal, Steinweg-Pfaffenstein, 93059 Regensburg
TypeMarket
StateBavaria

On the Dultplatz-West, numerous stalls offer a wide choice of fabrics such as jersey, cotton, linen and viscose, along with matching sewing supplies. Whether for clothing, decoration or personal projects, the market is aimed at everyone who enjoys sewing and needlework.
