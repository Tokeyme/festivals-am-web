Hanse Sail Rostock

Funfair · Rostock · 6–9 August 2026

When6–9 August 2026
Where18055 Rostock
TypeFunfair
StateMecklenburg-Western Pomerania

One of Europe's largest maritime folk festivals in Rostock's city harbor and in Warnemünde. Around the traditional sailing ships, there are stage programs, maritime markets, food stands, and fireworks.
