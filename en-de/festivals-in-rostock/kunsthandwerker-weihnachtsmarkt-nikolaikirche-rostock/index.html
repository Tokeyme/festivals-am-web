Kunsthandwerker Weihnachtsmarkt Nikolaikirche Rostock

Christmas market · Rostock, Östliche Altstadt · 26–28 November 2026

When26–28 November 2026
WhereBei der Nikolaikirche 1, Östliche Altstadt, 18055 Rostock
TypeChristmas market
StateMecklenburg-Western Pomerania

A craft market in the Nikolai church in Rostock’s old town. Over three days more than 40 makers show handmade work including ceramics, jewellery and bookbinding. A small cafe serves punch and hearty food, and admission is free.
