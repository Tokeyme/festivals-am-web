Der Meistertrunk

Historical festival · Rothenburg ob der Tauber · 14–17 May 2027

When14–17 May 2027
WhereAltstadt, 91541 Rothenburg ob der Tauber
TypeHistorical festival
StateBavaria

The historical pageant "Der Meistertrunk" commemorates the legend that a courageous drink saved the town from destruction during the Thirty Years' War. At Whitsun, hundreds of participants in historical costumes bring the old town to life with performances, a military camp, and groups of horsemen and Landsknechte.
