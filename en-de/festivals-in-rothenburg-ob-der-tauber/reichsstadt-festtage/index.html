Reichsstadt-Festtage

Historical festival · Rothenburg ob der Tauber · 4–6 September 2026

When4–6 September 2026
WhereAltstadt, 91541 Rothenburg ob der Tauber
TypeHistorical festival
StateBavaria

During the Reichsstadt-Festtage, the entire old town transforms into a historical spectacle on the first weekend of September. Around 1,000 participants in costumes from various eras present camp life, crafts and knights' tournaments; the highlights are the torchlight procession on Friday and the fireworks.
