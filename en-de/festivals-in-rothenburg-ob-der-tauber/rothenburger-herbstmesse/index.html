Rothenburger Herbstmesse

Funfair · Rothenburg ob der Tauber · 31 October – 8 November 2026

When31 October – 8 November 2026
WhereSchrannenplatz, 91541 Rothenburg ob der Tauber
TypeFunfair
StateBavaria

On the Schrannenplatz, a traders' market, rides for younger children and food stalls come together. The market area offers household goods, clothing and things to eat, and sweets and toys for children. Many visitors come for the first mulled wine of the approaching cold season. On the special Sunday opening, the shops in the Altstadt open as well.
