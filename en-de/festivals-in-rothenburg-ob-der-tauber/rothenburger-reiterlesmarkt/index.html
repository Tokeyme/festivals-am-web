Rothenburger Reiterlesmarkt

Christmas market · Rothenburg ob der Tauber · 20 November – 23 December 2026

When20 November – 23 December 2026
WhereMarktplatz, 91541 Rothenburg ob der Tauber
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

The Reiterlesmarkt is Rothenburg's time-honoured Christmas market and takes place between the Marktplatz, Grünplatz and Kirchplatz. At the opening, the legendary "Reiterle" rides in; around sixty stalls offer handicrafts, regional specialities and mulled wine against a medieval backdrop.
