Taubertal-Festival

Music festival · Rothenburg ob der Tauber · 6–9 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When6–9 August 2026
WhereEiswiese, 91541 Rothenburg ob der Tauber
TypeMusic festival
StateBavaria

The Taubertal-Festival is a large open-air festival on the Eiswiese below the old town, with a focus on rock, punk and hip-hop. Over several days, numerous bands perform on several stages in front of up to 15,000 visitors; in 2026, the festival celebrates its 30th anniversary.
