Magic Bike Rüdesheim

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

TypeEvent
StateHesse

Magic Bike Rüdesheim is one of the largest bikers' gatherings in Europe and takes place around the Brömserburg on the banks of the Rhine. For four days, everything revolves around motorcycles, live music and the flair of the UNESCO World Heritage Upper Middle Rhine Valley. Exhibitors, concerts and group rides attract thousands of visitors.
