Rhein in Flammen

Event · Rüdesheim am Rhein · 3–5 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

WhereMarktplatz und Rheinufer, 65385 Rüdesheim am Rhein

During Rhein in Flammen, Rüdesheim turns into a festival mile with music, regional specialties and convivial festivities on the market square. The highlight is the large fireworks display over the Rhine that lights up the night sky. The festival spans three days.
