Rüdesheimer Weinfest

Wine festival · Rüdesheim am Rhein · 13–17 August 2026

When13–17 August 2026
WhereMarktplatz, 65385 Rüdesheim am Rhein
TypeWine festival
StateHesse

The Rüdesheim wine festival 'Summer of Riesling' traditionally takes place on the third weekend in August on the historic market square. Winemakers serve Rheingau wines, accompanied by music and regional cuisine. The festival stands for wine culture and Rheingau hospitality.
