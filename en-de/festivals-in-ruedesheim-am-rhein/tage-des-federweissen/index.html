Tage des Federweißen

Wine festival · Rüdesheim am Rhein · 23 October – 1 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When23 October – 1 November 2026
WhereMarktplatz, 65385 Rüdesheim am Rhein
TypeWine festival
StateHesse

During the Tage des Federweißen, Rüdesheim's winemakers invite guests to the market square on two weekends in October. The young, still-fermenting Federweißer is served, along with autumnal specialties. The event ushers in the autumn wine season in the Rheingau.
