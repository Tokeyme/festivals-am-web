Weihnachtsmarkt der Nationen

Christmas market · Rüdesheim am Rhein · 19 November – 23 December 2026

When19 November – 23 December 2026
WhereAltstadt, 65385 Rüdesheim am Rhein
TypeChristmas market
StateHesse

The Christmas Market of Nations in Rüdesheim brings together exhibitors from over 20 nations and four continents. In the lanes of the old town, they present goods, specialties and customs from around the world. The international market is open for several weeks and attracts many visitors.
