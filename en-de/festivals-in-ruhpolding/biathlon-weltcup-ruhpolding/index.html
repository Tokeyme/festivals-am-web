Biathlon-Weltcup Ruhpolding

Sports event · Ruhpolding · 6–10 January 2027

When6–10 January 2027
WhereChiemgau Arena, Biathlonzentrum 1, 83324 Ruhpolding, 83324 Ruhpolding
TypeSports event
StateBavaria

An international weekend of the Biathlon World Cup at the Chiemgau Arena, the national training centre for biathlon. The programme features relay, sprint and pursuit races for women and men. The arena holds up to 14,000 spectators.
