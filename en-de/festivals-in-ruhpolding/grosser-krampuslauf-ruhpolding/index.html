Großer Krampuslauf Ruhpolding

Event · Ruhpolding · 21 November 2026

When21 November 2026
WhereOrtsmitte, Hauptstraße, 83324 Ruhpolding, 83324 Ruhpolding
TypeEvent
StateBavaria

The run, organised by the Krampusgruppe Rauschberg Pass Ruhpolding, turns the town centre into a spectacle of rattling chains, ringing bells and roaring. More than 40 Krampus groups with over 600 participants move through the streets in demonic masks and elaborate costumes. The Krampuslauf (Krampus run) is an experience for all age groups.
