Großes Ruhpoldinger Dorffest

Village & club fest · Ruhpolding · 28–29 August 2026

When28–29 August 2026
WhereEissporthalle, Brander Straße 3, 83324 Ruhpolding, 83324 Ruhpolding
TypeVillage & club fest
StateBavaria

The large village festival is the annual meeting point for locals and visitors in the ice rink arena. It is celebrated with live music, culinary delicacies, beer and regional dishes. The programme also includes performances by children's and youth groups as well as the Schnalzer (traditional whip-crackers).
