Ostern in Ruhpolding - Heiliges Grab

Historical festival · Ruhpolding · 26–29 March 2027

When26–29 March 2027
WhereSchlosskapelle, Schlossstraße 2, 83324 Ruhpolding
TypeHistorical festival
StateBavaria

Over the Easter days, the Ruhpolding Heiliges Grab (Holy Sepulchre) is displayed in the Schlosskapelle: a stage-set-like depiction of the dead Jesus in the rock tomb, lit by coloured glass spheres with lights and candles. Because of the great effort involved, it is only set up every three years, and 2027 is such a year again. The organiser is the Tourismusseelsorge Chiemgauer Alpen (tourism pastoral care).
