RIDE - The Moto Festival

Event · Ruhpolding · 24–26 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When24–26 July 2026
WhereOrtsmitte, Hauptstraße, 83324 Ruhpolding, 83324 Ruhpolding
TypeEvent
StateBavaria

A three-day motorcycle festival under the motto 'Where custom meets adventure'. In the village centre along the main street, in the Kurpark (spa gardens) and in the former speedway stadium there is a traders' row, a custom-bike show featuring modified and historic machines, as well as test rides, talks and workshops. There is also live music and camping, and large parts of the festival are open to the public free of charge.
