RIDE - The Moto Festival

Event · Ruhpolding · 23–25 July 2027

When23–25 July 2027
WhereOrtsmitte, Hauptstraße, 83324 Ruhpolding, 83324 Ruhpolding
TypeEvent
StateBavaria

A three-day motorcycle festival under the motto 'Where custom meets adventure'. In the village center along the main street, in the Kurpark (spa gardens) and in the former speedway stadium there is a traders' row, a custom-bike show featuring modified and historic machines, as well as test rides, talks and workshops. There is also live music and camping, and large parts of the festival are open to the public free of charge.
