Route66 goes Ruhpolding

Event · Ruhpolding · 13 September 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When13 September 2026
WhereHauptstraße und Kurpark, 83324 Ruhpolding, 83324 Ruhpolding
TypeEvent
StateBavaria

At this motoring event, US cars and vintage classics gather in Ruhpolding. Around 50 lovingly maintained historic vehicles are displayed along the main street and in the Kurpark (spa gardens) before setting off on their five-day tour through Germany, Austria, Slovenia and Italy. The program includes live music, autograph sessions and a row of food stalls.
