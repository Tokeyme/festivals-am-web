Ruhpoldinger Rauhnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Ruhpolding · 27–29 December 2026

When27–29 December 2026
WhereKurpark, Kurhausstraße 4, 83324 Ruhpolding, 83324 Ruhpolding
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

A contemplative market held during the Rauhnächte (the traditional nights around the turn of the year) in Ruhpolding's Kurpark spa park. This Alpine-style market offers regional crafts, mulled wine and punch, as well as appearances by Perchten and Alpine sounds such as alphorn music. It invites visitors to come together quietly in the days around the turn of the year.
