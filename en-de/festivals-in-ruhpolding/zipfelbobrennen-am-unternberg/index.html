Zipfelbobrennen am Unternberg

Event · Ruhpolding · 20 February 2027

When20 February 2027
WhereUnternbergbahn, Raffner Alm 2, 83324 Ruhpolding
TypeEvent
StateBavaria

At the Zipfelbobrennen on the Unternberg, individual starters and teams of up to three people compete against each other on the small sledge. There are prizes and refreshments, and spectators are welcome too. Helmets are compulsory for all participants, and the organiser is the Unternbergbahn.
