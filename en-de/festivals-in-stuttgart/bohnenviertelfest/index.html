Bohnenviertelfest

Street festival · Stuttgart, Stuttgart-Mitte · 23–25 July 2026

When23–25 July 2026
WhereBohnenviertel, Brennerstraße / Rosenstraße, Stuttgart-Mitte, 70182 Stuttgart
TypeStreet festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

Traditional street festival in the Bohnenviertel between Charlottenplatz and Leonhardsplatz featuring music, children's activities, and food and beverage stands from local businesses. Organized by the Bohnenviertel Chamber of Commerce.
