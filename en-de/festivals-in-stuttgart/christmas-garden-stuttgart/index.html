Christmas Garden Stuttgart

Event · Stuttgart, Bad Cannstatt · 18 November 2026 – 16 January 2027

When18 November 2026 – 16 January 2027
WhereWilhelma, Neckartalstraße, Bad Cannstatt, 70376 Stuttgart
TypeEvent
StateBaden-Württemberg

A festive park of lights in the Wilhelma in Stuttgart-Bad Cannstatt. A circular route several hundred metres long leads through illuminated displays and light installations. The garden is closed on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve.
