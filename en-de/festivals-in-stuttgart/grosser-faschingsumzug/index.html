Großer Faschingsumzug

Carnival · Stuttgart · 9 February 2027

When9 February 2027
Where70173 Stuttgart
TypeCarnival
StateBaden-Württemberg

The traditional procession runs through the streets of central Stuttgart and brings the carnival revelry into the city centre. The organiser is Gesellschaft Moebelwagen 1897 e.V., the first Stuttgart carnival society.
