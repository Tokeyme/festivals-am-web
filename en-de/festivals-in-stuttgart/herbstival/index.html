Herbstival

Music festival · Stuttgart, Stuttgart-Ost · 12–13 September 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When12–13 September 2026
WhereBerger Festplatz, Stuttgart-Ost, 70190 Stuttgart
TypeMusic festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

A two-day open-air festival of electronic music and installations on the Berger Festplatz in the east of Stuttgart. It is organized by the IG Berger Festplatz, an alliance of local collectives and clubs. Instead of one large main stage, there are several small stages and a curated selection of food trucks.
