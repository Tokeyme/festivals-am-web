Herbstival

Music festival · Stuttgart, Stuttgart-Ost · 12–13 September 2026

When12–13 September 2026
WhereBerger Festplatz, Stuttgart-Ost, 70190 Stuttgart
TypeMusic festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

A two-day open-air festival of electronic music and installations on the Berger Festplatz in the east of Stuttgart. It is organised by the IG Berger Festplatz, an alliance of local collectives and clubs. Instead of one large main stage, there are several small stages and a curated selection of food trucks.
