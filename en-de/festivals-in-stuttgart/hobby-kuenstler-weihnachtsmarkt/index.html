Hobby-Künstler-Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Stuttgart, Wangen · 28 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28 November 2026
WhereUlmer Straße 347, Wangen, 70327 Stuttgart
TypeChristmas market
StateBaden-Württemberg

A Christmas market around the Treff 347 community centre on Ulmer Strasse in the Stuttgart borough of Wangen. It traditionally takes place on the first Saturday of Advent. Only home-made goods are sold, by clubs, parishes, schools and kindergartens from the district; there are no commercial traders.
