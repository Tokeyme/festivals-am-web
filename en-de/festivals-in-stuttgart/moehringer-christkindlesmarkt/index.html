Möhringer Christkindlesmarkt

Christmas market · Stuttgart, Möhringen · 28 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhereOberdorfplatz, an der Martinskirche, Möhringen, 70567 Stuttgart
TypeChristmas market
StateBaden-Württemberg

A Christkindlesmarkt held by the Bürgerverein Möhringen at Oberdorfplatz by the Martinskirche and at the district town hall in Stuttgart-Möhringen. It is regarded as the largest purely charitable Weihnachtsmarkt in Stuttgart.
