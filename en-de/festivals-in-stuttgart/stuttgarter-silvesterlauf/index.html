Stuttgarter Silvesterlauf

Sports event · Stuttgart, Weilimdorf · 31 December 2026

When31 December 2026
WhereLindenbachhalle, Solitudeallee, Weilimdorf, 70499 Stuttgart
TypeSports event
StateBaden-Württemberg

An end-of-year run in Stuttgart-Weilimdorf at the foot of Schloss Solitude. Alongside the main race of around eleven kilometres, there is also a schools' race and a shorter fun run, among others.
