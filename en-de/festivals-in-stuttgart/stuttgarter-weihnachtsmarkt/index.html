Stuttgarter Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Stuttgart, Stuttgart-Mitte · 25 November – 23 December 2026

When25 November – 23 December 2026
WhereSchlossplatz, Schillerplatz, Karlsplatz und Marktplatz, Stuttgart-Mitte, 70173 Stuttgart
TypeChristmas market
StateBaden-Württemberg

One of Europe's oldest and largest Christmas markets with approximately 250 to 300 elaborately decorated stalls in Stuttgart's city center. Festive opening in the courtyard of the Old Castle.
