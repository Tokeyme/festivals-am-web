Stuttgarter Weindorf

Wine festival · Stuttgart, Stuttgart-Mitte · 20 August – 5 September 2026

When20 August – 5 September 2026
WhereMarktplatz, Schillerplatz und Kirchstraße, Stuttgart-Mitte, 70173 Stuttgart
TypeWine festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

One of southern Germany's largest wine festivals in Stuttgart's city center with around 120 wine pavilions and up to 500 Württemberg wines. 2026 celebrates the 50th Stuttgart Wine Village. Opening on August 20.
