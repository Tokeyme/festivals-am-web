Umsonst & Draußen

Music festival · Stuttgart, Vaihingen · 7–9 August 2026

When7–9 August 2026
WhereFestplatz an der Krehlstraße, Vaihingen, 70563 Stuttgart
TypeMusic festival
StateBaden-Württemberg

A non-commercial music, politics and culture festival with free entry on the Festplatz on Krehlstraße in Stuttgart-Vaihingen. The programme features regional and international bands, readings, a children's programme and an info village.
