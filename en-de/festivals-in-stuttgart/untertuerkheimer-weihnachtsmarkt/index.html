Untertürkheimer Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Stuttgart, Untertürkheim · 5 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When5 December 2026
WhereLeonhard-Schmidt-Platz, Untertürkheim, 70327 Stuttgart
TypeChristmas market
StateBaden-Württemberg

A Weihnachtsmarkt in the centre of Stuttgart-Untertürkheim at Leonhard-Schmidt-Platz, organised by the Bürgerverein and the Handels- und Gewerbeverein. At more than 30 stalls there are handicrafts, food and drink as well as a stage programme.
