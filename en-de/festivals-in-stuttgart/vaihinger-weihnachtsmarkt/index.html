Vaihinger Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Stuttgart, Vaihingen · 28–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28–29 November 2026
WhereVaihinger Markt, am Bezirksrathaus, Vaihingen, 70563 Stuttgart
TypeChristmas market
StateBaden-Württemberg

A volunteer-run Christmas market at the Vaihinger Markt around the district town hall in Stuttgart-Vaihingen, with over 100 stalls. On offer are floral arrangements, wood carvings, Christmas tree decorations, as well as mulled wine and food.
