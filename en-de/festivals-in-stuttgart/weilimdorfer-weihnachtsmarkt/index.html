Weilimdorfer Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Stuttgart, Weilimdorf · 28–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28–29 November 2026
WhereLöwen-Markt, Weilimdorf, 70499 Stuttgart
TypeChristmas market
StateBaden-Württemberg

A Christmas market on the Löwen-Markt in Stuttgart-Weilimdorf with around 40 stalls. Local clubs put on a stage programme, and for children the little MALU-Bahn runs. Admission is free.
