Kampener Anleuchten

Event · Sylt, Kampen · 29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When29 November 2026
WhereKaamp-Hüs, Kampen, Kampen, 25999 Sylt
TypeEvent
StateSchleswig-Holstein

To open the pre-Christmas season, the Kampen community gathers at the Kaamp-Hüs and switches on the festive lights together. Over a glass of punch, with the glow of lights above the dune horizon, locals and visitors ease into a contemplative mood. The supporting programme includes a Christmas market at the Kaamp-Hüs as well as a winter concert.
