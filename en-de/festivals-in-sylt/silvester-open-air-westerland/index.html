Silvester Open Air Westerland

Event · Sylt, Westerland · 31 December 2026

When31 December 2026
WhereMusikmuschel, Westerländer Promenade, Westerland, 25980 Sylt
TypeEvent
StateSchleswig-Holstein

At the turn of the year, the Westerland promenade by the Musikmuschel band shell becomes a large open-air stage. Admission is free, and DJs play the biggest chart hits along with popular house and dance classics while thousands dance on the promenade and in the sand. Because of the many thatched roofs, a year-round ban on fireworks applies across Sylt.
