Sylter Sturmwoche

Culture festival · Sylt · 15–24 January 2027

When15–24 January 2027
Whereverschiedene Orte auf der Insel, Sylt
TypeCulture festival
StateSchleswig-Holstein

The Sturmwoche (storm week) is a winter series of events with nature and culture formats at unusual places on the island. Last year, these included sauna sessions by the sea, workshops, talks, a beach clean-up and a folding-bike race on the promenade. The events are spread across several places on the island, such as Westerland, Kampen, Rantum, Keitum and Hörnum.
