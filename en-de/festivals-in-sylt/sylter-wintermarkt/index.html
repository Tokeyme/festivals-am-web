Sylter Wintermarkt

Christmas market · Sylt, Westerland · 4 December 2026 – 2 January 2027

When4 December 2026 – 2 January 2027
WhereNeue Straße 6, Neue Mitte, Westerland, 25980 Sylt
TypeChristmas market
StateSchleswig-Holstein

During Advent, the Sylter Wintermarkt turns the Neue Mitte in Westerland into an atmospheric meeting place. In decorated Advent huts, selected exhibitors offer culinary specialities, punch and handcrafted gift ideas, while a sheltered Stadl (barn) invites visitors to linger. The concept draws on Danish hygge and offers a varied programme for all age groups.
