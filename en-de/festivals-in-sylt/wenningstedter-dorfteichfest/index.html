Wenningstedter Dorfteichfest

Village & club fest · Sylt, Wenningstedt · 25 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When25 July 2026
WhereDorfteich, Bi Kiar, Wenningstedt, 25996 Sylt
TypeVillage & club fest
StateSchleswig-Holstein

At the long-established Wenningstedter Dorfteichfest (village pond festival), the area around the village pond is transformed into a colourful festival ground. In the afternoon there is a children's programme with games and craft activities, followed by the popular LIONS duck race on the pond. Live music and food stalls serving regional dishes provide entertainment into the night.
