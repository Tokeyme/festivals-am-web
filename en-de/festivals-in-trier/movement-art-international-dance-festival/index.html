Movement Art International Dance Festival

Culture festival · Trier · 17–24 October 2026

When17–24 October 2026
WhereEuropahalle, Viehmarktplatz, 54290 Trier
TypeCulture festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The Movement Art International Dance Festival takes place across the border in Germany (Trier) and Luxembourg. It brings together international contemporary dance artists whose work explores memory, identity, resistance and human connection. The Trier programme features "IN THE BUSHES" by Léa Tirabasso and "MEGASTRUCTURE" by Sarah Baltzinger and Isaiah Wilson.
