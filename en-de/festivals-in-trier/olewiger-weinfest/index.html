Olewiger Weinfest

Wine festival · Trier, Olewig · 31 July – 3 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When31 July – 3 August 2026
WhereOlewig, 54295 Trier
TypeWine festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

One of Trier's best-known wine festivals held directly in the wine town of Olewig. Wine, food, and live music are offered in the vintners' courtyards and along the closed-off main street; traditionally, the new Trier wine queen is crowned.
