Trierer Unterwelten

Culture festival · Trier · 29 October – 21 November 2026

When29 October – 21 November 2026
Where54290 Trier
TypeCulture festival
StateRhineland-Palatinate

The festival takes place in cellars, catacombs, tunnels and other underground spaces across Trier. The programme includes performances, music, food, guided tours and talks. On 14 November 2026 there is also a Long Night of the Underworlds. Tickets are available in advance.
