Herbst-Regionalmarkt

Market · Tübingen · 10 October 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When10 October 2026
WhereAltstadt, 72070 Tübingen
TypeMarket
StateBaden-Württemberg

At Tübingen's regional market around 100 stallholders sell specialties from local fields, gardens, woodlands and traditional orchard meadows. Besides regional food there are flowers, basketware, jewelry, everyday ceramics and felt products to discover. The market takes place in the Altstadt.
