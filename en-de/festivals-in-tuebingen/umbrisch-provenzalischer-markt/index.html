Umbrisch-provenzalischer Markt

Village & club fest · Tübingen · 16–20 September 2026

When16–20 September 2026
WhereAltstadt / Marktplatz Tübingen, 72070 Tübingen
TypeVillage & club fest
StateBaden-Württemberg

Traders from the twin towns of Aix-en-Provence and Perugia transform the historic old town into a Mediterranean market village with regional specialities and arts and crafts. The finale on Sunday features the Tübinger Erbe-Lauf and a Sunday with shops open.
