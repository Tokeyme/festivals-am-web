Gässlesmarkt

Market · Ulm · 19 September 2026

When19 September 2026
WherePlatzgasse, Pfauengasse, Herrenkellergasse, Dreiköniggasse, 89073 Ulm
TypeMarket
StateBavaria

In the winding lanes of Ulm's old town, traders offer their goods in front of their own shops. The stalls are decorated and there are special promotions to go with them. Visitors can discover autumn produce, art and specialities from the region. The market opens the Ulmer Kulturnacht (Ulm's night of culture).
