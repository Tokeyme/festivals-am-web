Internationales Donaufest Ulm/Neu-Ulm

Town festival · Ulm · 3–12 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When3–12 July 2026
WhereDonauufer / Unter der Metzig, 89073 Ulm
TypeTown festival
StateBavaria

Every two years, the Donaufest transforms the riverside promenades of both Danube cities into a festival of encounter. For ten days, artists and exhibitors from the countries along the Danube present music, cuisine and crafts with free admission.
