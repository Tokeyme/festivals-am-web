Lichterserenade auf der Donau

Village & club fest · Ulm · 18 July 2026

When18 July 2026
WhereDonauufer Ulm/Neu-Ulm, 89073 Ulm
TypeVillage & club fest
StateBavaria

On the Saturday before Schwörmontag, at nightfall Ulmer Schachteln set out around 20,000 red tea lights on the Danube. The atmospheric light spectacle ushers in the Schwörwoche.
