Ulmer Marktsonntag

Market · Ulm · 4 October 2026

When4 October 2026
WhereMünsterplatz, 89073 Ulm
TypeMarket
StateBavaria

An antiques and craft market with more than 60 stalls takes place on the Münsterplatz, complemented by an autumn market with regional specialities, handicrafts and accessories. Sporting displays are put on at a show stage. For children, the Ulmer Spatzenbähnle (little road train) runs free rides. Shops in the city centre are open from 1 pm to 6 pm.
