Ulmer Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Ulm · 19 November – 22 December 2026

When19 November – 22 December 2026
WhereMünsterplatz, 89073 Ulm
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

Around Ulm Minster, more than 130 festively decorated stalls transform the Münsterplatz into a Christmas meeting place with arts and crafts, gift ideas and culinary treats. For children there are carousels and a petting zoo.
