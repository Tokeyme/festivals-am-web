Ulmer Weinfest

Wine festival · Ulm · 5–22 August 2026

When5–22 August 2026
WhereSüdlicher Münsterplatz, 89073 Ulm
TypeWine festival
StateBavaria

Right at the foot of the minster, the season's earliest wine festival invites visitors to linger for around two and a half weeks. More than 70 wines from Germany and abroad are served alongside culinary offerings from Ulm's restaurants.
