Unter Ulmern: Streetfoodmarkt

Market · Ulm · 10–13 September 2026

When10–13 September 2026
WhereSüdlicher Münsterplatz, 89073 Ulm
TypeMarket
StateBavaria

For four days the southern part of the Münsterplatz turns into an open-air meeting point for good food. Stallholders from Ulm, Neu-Ulm and the surrounding area set up on the square. What is on offer ranges from savoury to sweet and from regional to international.
