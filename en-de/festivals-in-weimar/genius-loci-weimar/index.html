Genius Loci Weimar

Culture festival · Weimar · 11–13 September 2026

When11–13 September 2026
WhereAltstadt Weimar, 99423 Weimar
TypeCulture festival
StateThuringia

Genius Loci Weimar is a festival of audiovisual art and facade projections (projection mapping) in public space. Historic buildings in Weimar's old town become projection surfaces for light and video art. This edition is dedicated to the theme 'Democracy & Society'; admission is free.
