Hofadventsmarkt im Kirms-Krackow-Haus

Christmas market · Weimar · 29 November 2026

When29 November 2026
WhereKirms-Krackow-Haus, Jakobstraße 10, 99423 Weimar
TypeChristmas market
StateThuringia

The Hofadventsmarkt (courtyard Advent market) in the historic Kirms-Krackow-Haus is a Christmas market featuring local businesses. In the courtyard of the townhouse, exhibitors present Advent goods. The market is part of the Advent programme of the Weimarer Weihnacht (Weimar Christmas).
