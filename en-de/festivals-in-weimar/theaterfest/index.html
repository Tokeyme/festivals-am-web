Theaterfest

Culture festival · Weimar · 12 September 2026

When12 September 2026
WhereTheaterplatz 2 (Deutsches Nationaltheater), 99423 Weimar
TypeCulture festival
StateThuringia

At the Theaterfest, the Deutsches Nationaltheater Weimar opens its doors and celebrates the start of the new season together with the whole city. On the Theaterplatz and in the Großes Haus there is a varied programme to watch, join in and explore. The highlights include, among others, a family concert, a costume sale, an ensemble gala and the One-Minute-Show.
