Thüringer Genuss- und Spezialmarkt

Market · Weimar · 5–6 September 2026

When5–6 September 2026
WhereTheaterplatz, 99423 Weimar
TypeMarket
StateThuringia

On Weimar's Theaterplatz, the Thüringer Genuss- und Spezialmarkt invites visitors to feast and browse. Regional vendors present culinary specialities and gourmet products. The market is held at the same time as the pottery market (Töpfermarkt) on the Marktplatz.
