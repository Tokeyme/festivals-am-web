Töpfermarkt Weimar

Market · Weimar · 5–6 September 2026

When5–6 September 2026
WhereMarktplatz, 99423 Weimar
TypeMarket
StateThuringia

The pottery market brings the Thuringian potters' guild (Töpferinnung), with around 60 ceramicists from Thuringia and all across Germany, to Weimar's market square. The exhibitors display their special variety of craftsmanship, and visitors can watch the potters at work and buy individual ceramic pieces.
