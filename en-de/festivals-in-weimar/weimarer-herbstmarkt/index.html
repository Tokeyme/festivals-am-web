Weimarer Herbstmarkt

Market · Weimar · 14–15 September 2026

When14–15 September 2026
WhereMarktplatz, 99423 Weimar
TypeMarket
StateThuringia

The Weimarer Herbstmarkt (Weimar autumn market) invites visitors to a lively market on the market square in the centre of Weimar. On offer are autumnal produce, everyday goods, arts and crafts, as well as food and drink. The market ushers in the autumn season in Weimar.
