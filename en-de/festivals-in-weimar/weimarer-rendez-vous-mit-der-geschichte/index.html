Weimarer Rendez-vous mit der Geschichte

Culture festival · Weimar · 30 October – 1 November 2026

When30 October – 1 November 2026
WhereWeimarer Innenstadt, 99423 Weimar
TypeCulture festival
StateThuringia

The Weimarer Rendez-vous mit der Geschichte is a public history festival with discussions, talks and encounters at various locations in Weimar. Experts from academia, public life and civil society enter into conversation with the audience. The theme of this edition is "Fakt oder Fake?" and deals with propaganda, enlightenment, myths and images of history.
