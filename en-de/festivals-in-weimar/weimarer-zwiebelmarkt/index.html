Weimarer Zwiebelmarkt

Funfair · Weimar · 9–11 October 2026

When9–11 October 2026
WhereInnenstadt Weimar, 99423 Weimar
TypeFunfair
StateThuringia

Thuringia's oldest and largest folk festival, traditionally held the second October weekend throughout the downtown. Stalls, onion specialties, and stage programming. October 11 features a traffic-free shopping Sunday.
