chocolART

Market · Wernigerode · 28 October – 1 November 2026

When28 October – 1 November 2026
WhereMarktplatz, Nicolaiplatz, Kohlmarkt und Breite Straße, 38855 Wernigerode
TypeMarket
StateLower Saxony

At the chocolART chocolate festival, chocolatiers, pastry chefs and chocolate makers set up their stalls in Wernigerode's old town. The market spreads across Marktplatz, Nicolaiplatz, Kohlmarkt and Breite Straße, and admission to the chocoMARKT is free. The chocoPFAD adds further offerings in restaurants, shops and cafés.
