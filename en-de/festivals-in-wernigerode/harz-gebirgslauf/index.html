Harz-Gebirgslauf

Sports event · Wernigerode, Hasserode · 10 October 2026

When10 October 2026
WhereStart und Ziel Himmelpforte, Hasserode, 38855 Wernigerode
TypeSports event
StateLower Saxony

The Harz-Gebirgslauf is a trail race through the Harz National Park, starting and finishing at the Himmelpforte in Wernigerode. Several distances are on offer, from short courses to the marathon over the Brocken, which is regarded as the toughest marathon in northern Germany. Up to 4,000 runners come to the event each year.
