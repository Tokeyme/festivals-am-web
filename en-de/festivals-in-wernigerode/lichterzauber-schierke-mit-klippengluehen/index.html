Lichterzauber Schierke mit Klippenglühen

Event · Wernigerode, Schierke · 28 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

When28 November 2026 – 3 January 2027
WhereKurpark Schierke, 38879 Wernigerode
TypeEvent
StateSaxony-Anhalt

On weekends during Advent and the Christmas season, the village of Schierke is lit in colour, including buildings, the Mäuseklippen rocks and the luge track. The supporting programme includes the opening of the Nativity scene in the Kurpark, communal Advent singing and hands-on activities. The local restaurants and cafés are open.
