Schierker Kuhball

Village & club fest · Wernigerode, Schierke · 26 September 2026

When26 September 2026
WhereQuesenbank, Schierke, 38879 Wernigerode
TypeVillage & club fest
StateLower Saxony

The Kuhball is a traditional local festival on the meadows by the Schierker Quesenbank and marks the end of summer. It has been held every year in late September for almost 30 years and welcomes locals and visitors alike. Beer and fresh Harz milk are among the things served.
