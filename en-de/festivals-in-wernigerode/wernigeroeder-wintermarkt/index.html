Wernigeröder Wintermarkt

Christmas market · Wernigerode · 25 December 2026 – 6 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When25 December 2026 – 6 January 2027
WhereAn der Blumenuhr, Klintgasse, 38855 Wernigerode
TypeChristmas market
StateLower Saxony

Once the Christmas market has ended, the Wintermarkt follows on behind the town hall at the Blumenuhr (flower clock). It stays open over the turn of the year and offers stalls with food and drink on a smaller scale.
