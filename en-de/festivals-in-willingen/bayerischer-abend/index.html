Bayerischer Abend

Village & club fest · Willingen, Usseln · 26 September 2026

When26 September 2026
WhereSportstraße 3, Usseln, 34508 Willingen
TypeVillage & club fest
StateHesse

The Burschenclub Usseln 1612 has been hosting the Bayerischer Abend (Bavarian evening) in the Bavarian-decorated Schützenhalle in Usseln since 2009. The band Donau Power provides the music, and Hermann Vogel and Julian Krause host the evening. Hundreds of guests celebrate into the early hours.
