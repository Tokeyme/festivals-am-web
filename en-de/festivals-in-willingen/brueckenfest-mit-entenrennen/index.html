Brückenfest mit Entenrennen

Village & club fest · Willingen · 8 August 2026

When8 August 2026
WhereKurgarten, Zum Kurgarten, 34508 Willingen
TypeVillage & club fest
StateHesse

The Concordia Willingen men's choir hosts the Brückenfest (bridge festival) in the Kurgarten (spa garden) with music, coffee and cake, barbecue specialities and a varied programme for the whole family. The highlight is the duck race of the Willinger Dunnerkuile, in which hundreds of yellow rubber ducks race one another along the stream in the Kurgarten. There are prizes for the first three finishers.
