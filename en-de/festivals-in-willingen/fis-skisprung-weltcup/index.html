FIS Skisprung-Weltcup

Sports event · Willingen, Stryck · 29–31 January 2027

When29–31 January 2027
WhereZur Mühlenkopfschanze 1, Stryck, 34508 Willingen
TypeSports event
StateHesse

The FIS Skisprung-Weltcup (ski jumping World Cup) takes place at the Mühlenkopfschanze, the largest large hill in the world. Over three days of competition the world's best ski jumpers leap down into the valley, and the stadium holds up to 35,000 spectators. The World Cup weekend is one of the internationally best-known winter sports events and combines top-level sport with a festival atmosphere.
