Jahresabschlusskonzert der Schützenblaskapelle

Event · Willingen · 28 December 2026

When28 December 2026
WhereAm Hagen 9-10, 34508 Willingen
TypeEvent
StateHesse

The Willingen Schützenblaskapelle gives its traditional end-of-year concert at the Besucherzentrum Willingen. The programme is a musical potpourri of opera, operetta, musicals, fairy tales and popular songs. Doors open at 6.30pm.
