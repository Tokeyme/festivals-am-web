Oldtimertreffen

Event · Willingen · 29 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When29 August 2026
WhereAm Hoppern 7, 34508 Willingen
TypeEvent
StateHesse

At the Willingen Oldtimertreffen (classic vehicle meet), numerous vintage tractors, motorcycles, lorries, cars and mopeds from various decades are on show. Visitors can view the well-kept vehicles up close and get into conversation with their owners. Music, coffee and cake, Currywurst and drinks are provided, along with a children's programme featuring pedal tractors and a bouncy castle.
