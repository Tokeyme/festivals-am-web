Schützenfest Usseln

Funfair · Willingen, Usseln · 25–27 July 2026

When25–27 July 2026
WhereUsseln, 34508 Willingen
TypeFunfair
StateHesse

A traditional Schützenfest (marksmen's festival) held by the local shooting club in Usseln, a district of Willingen. This multi-day village festival is a fixed part of the events calendar of the municipality of Willingen (Upland).
