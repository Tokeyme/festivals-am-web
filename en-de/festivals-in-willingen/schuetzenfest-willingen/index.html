Schützenfest Willingen

Funfair · Willingen · 3–5 July 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When3–5 July 2027
WhereAm Hagen 9–10, 34508 Willingen
TypeFunfair
StateHesse

The Schützengesellschaft Willingen (shooting club) celebrates its Schützenfest (marksmen's festival) on the first weekend of July at the Besucherzentrum. It begins on Saturday with an open-air concert and an ecumenical service. The highlight is the grand festival parade through the village on Sunday, and on Monday the new Schützenkönig (shooting king) is determined at the Vogelschießen (bird shoot).
