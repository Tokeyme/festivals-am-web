SPRING-Festival

Culture festival · Willingen · 29 March – 3 April 2027

When29 March – 3 April 2027
WhereKneippweg 1, 34508 Willingen
TypeCulture festival
StateHesse

SPRING is a Christian festival of the Evangelical Alliance in Germany, held in the week after Easter, which brings around 3,000 participants of all ages to Willingen. The programme combines music, talks, seminars and services with leisure activities in nature. Registration is required.
