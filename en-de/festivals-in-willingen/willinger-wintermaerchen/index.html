Willinger Wintermärchen

Christmas market · Willingen · 4–20 December 2026

When4–20 December 2026
WhereKurgarten, Zum Kurgarten 8, 34508 Willingen
TypeChristmas market
StateHesse

The Willinger Wintermärchen is a small, atmospheric Christmas market in the Kurgarten (spa garden) and a popular meeting point for families in the run-up to Christmas. On several Advent weekends (Friday to Sunday) there are mulled wine, children's punch, waffles and grilled specialities, as well as gifts and handmade items at the stalls. Live music and performances by various groups accompany the market, and admission is free.
