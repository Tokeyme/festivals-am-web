Eberspächer Rodel-Weltcup

Sports event · Winterberg · 27–29 November 2026

When27–29 November 2026
WhereVELTINS-EisArena, Kappe 3, 59955 Winterberg
TypeSports event
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The luge World Cup opens its season at the VELTINS-EisArena in Winterberg. On one of the fastest artificial ice tracks in the world, the world's best lugers compete for World Cup points. A track announcer keeps the audience informed about the racing, while video walls and food and drink stalls provide the supporting programme.
