IBSF Bob- und Skeleton-Weltcup

Sports event · Winterberg · 29–31 January 2027

When29–31 January 2027
WhereVELTINS-EisArena, Kappe 3, 59955 Winterberg
TypeSports event
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The IBSF Bob- und Skeleton-Weltcup (World Cup) comes to the VELTINS-EisArena in Winterberg. Over a single weekend, the world's best pilots compete in two-man and four-man bobsleigh, monobob and skeleton on one of the fastest artificial ice tracks in the world.
