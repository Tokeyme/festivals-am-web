IBSF Europacup Skeleton

Sports event · Winterberg · 11–17 January 2027

When11–17 January 2027
WhereVELTINS-EisArena, Kappe 3, 59955 Winterberg
TypeSports event
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

An IBSF Europe Cup in skeleton is held at the VELTINS-EisArena in Winterberg. Over several days, the skeleton athletes take to the start on the artificial ice track in this international competition.
