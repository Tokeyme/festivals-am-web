Karnevalsumzug in Züschen

Carnival · Winterberg, Züschen · 7 February 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When7 February 2027
WhereAm alten Bahnhof, Züschen, Züschen, 59955 Winterberg
TypeCarnival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Karnevalsumzug (carnival parade) makes its way through Züschen, a district of Winterberg, on carnival Sunday. It starts at the old railway station, from where the procession moves through the village. Afterwards, the celebrations continue in the Schützenhalle, the marksmen's hall, in Züschen.
