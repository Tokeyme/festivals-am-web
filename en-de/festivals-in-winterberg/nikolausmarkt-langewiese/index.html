Nikolausmarkt Langewiese

Christmas market · Winterberg, Langewiese · 6 December 2026

The Nikolausmarkt in Langewiese is a local Christmas market in the Winterberg district of Langewiese. On one Advent Sunday the market opens at the Schuetzenhalle (shooting club hall) with festive stalls and refreshments.
