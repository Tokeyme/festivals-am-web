Sparkassen Open Air Winterberg

Music festival · Winterberg · 22 July – 5 August 2026

When22 July – 5 August 2026
WhereMarktplatz, 59955 Winterberg
TypeMusic festival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

A summer open-air stage on the Marktplatz with a free music programme. Over several evenings, party and cover bands perform, accompanied by activities for families. In 2026 the festival celebrates its tenth anniversary.
