Weihnachtsmarkt in Niedersfeld

Christmas market · Winterberg, Niedersfeld · 28–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28–29 November 2026
WhereDorfhalle Niedersfeld, Niedersfeld, 59955 Winterberg
TypeChristmas market
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

The Christmas market in Niedersfeld traditionally takes place on the first Advent weekend in the village hall. Around 50 exhibitors offer homemade treats, mulled wine and festive goods inside the hall. Christmas brass music on Advent Sunday adds to the festive atmosphere.
