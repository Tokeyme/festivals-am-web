Winterberger Stadterlebnis

Town festival · Winterberg · 8–10 May 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When8–10 May 2026
WhereInnenstadt, 59955 Winterberg
TypeTown festival
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

A large town festival in Winterberg's town centre, held over three days for the first time in 2026 to mark the town's 750th anniversary. It combines a trade show and a car mile with a stage programme and live music. The motto is 'Eine Stadt - viele Welten' (one town, many worlds).
