Winterberger Winterdorf

Christmas market · Winterberg · 11 December 2026 – 6 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When11 December 2026 – 6 January 2027
WhereMarktplatz, 59955 Winterberg
TypeChristmas market
StateNorth Rhine-Westphalia

A festive winter market with decorated wooden huts on the Marktplatz in the town centre. Gluehwein, regional specialities and an ice rink invite visitors to linger into the new year. The Winterdorf is one of the best-known Christmas markets in the Sauerland.
