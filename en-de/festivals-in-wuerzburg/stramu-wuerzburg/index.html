STRAMU Würzburg

Music festival · Würzburg · 11–13 September 2026

When11–13 September 2026
WhereInnenstadt und Fußgängerzone, 97070 Würzburg
TypeMusic festival
StateBavaria

For one weekend a year, Würzburg's pedestrian zone turns into one big stage for street art. Hundreds of performers from all over the world present music, dance, acrobatics and fire and juggling shows at more than 20 locations. The festival does without fixed stages, and it is free to attend.
