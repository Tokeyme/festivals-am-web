Würzburger Weihnachtsmarkt

Christmas market · Würzburg · 27 November – 23 December 2026

When27 November – 23 December 2026
WhereOberer und Unterer Markt, Marienkapelle, Würzburg, 97070 Würzburg
TypeChristmas market
StateBavaria

Traditional Christmas market set against the Falkenhaus and Marienkapelle with stalls around the market square, Eichhornstraße, and Rathausinnenhof. Artist Christmas market in the Rathaus courtyard during advent weekends.
