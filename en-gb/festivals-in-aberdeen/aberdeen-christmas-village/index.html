Aberdeen Christmas Village

Christmas market · Aberdeen, City Centre · 19 November – 31 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When19 November – 31 December 2026
WhereBroad Street, Aberdeen city centre, City Centre, AB10 1AB Aberdeen
TypeChristmas market

Aberdeen's festive market and funfair, held on Broad Street in the city centre through the run-up to Christmas. Attractions include fairground rides, an open-air ice rink and chalets selling seasonal food, drink and gifts. It anchors the city's wider Christmas programme.
