True North

Music festival · Aberdeen, City Centre · 24–27 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When24–27 September 2026
WhereMusic Hall, His Majesty's Theatre and the Lemon Tree, City Centre, AB10 1QS Aberdeen
TypeMusic festival

Aberdeen's music festival presented by Aberdeen Performing Arts, staging concerts across the city's main venues including the Music Hall and His Majesty's Theatre. The programme spans folk, roots, soul and contemporary artists, mixing well-known names with Scottish and local talent. It usually takes place over a long weekend in early autumn.
