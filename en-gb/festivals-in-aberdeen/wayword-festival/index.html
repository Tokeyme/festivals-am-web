WayWORD Festival

Culture festival · Aberdeen, Old Aberdeen · 3–11 October 2026

When3–11 October 2026
WhereVarious venues across Aberdeen (University of Aberdeen and city centre), Old Aberdeen, AB24 3FX Aberdeen
TypeCulture festival

A literary and cross-arts festival held across Aberdeen each autumn, presented by the University of Aberdeen's WORD Centre. The programme mixes author events, workshops, panel discussions and performances, with a focus on new and unconventional writing. Most events are free and many include BSL interpretation.
