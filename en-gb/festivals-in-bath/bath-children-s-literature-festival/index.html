Bath Children's Literature Festival

Culture festival · Bath · 25 September – 4 October 2026

When25 September – 4 October 2026
WhereVarious venues, Bath city centre, BA1 1LY Bath
TypeCulture festival

One of the largest dedicated children's book festivals in Europe, presented by Bath Festivals across venues in the city centre. The programme brings authors, illustrators and storytellers together for talks, workshops and interactive events for young readers and families. It runs over ten days spanning late September and early October.
