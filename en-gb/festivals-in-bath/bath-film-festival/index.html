Bath Film Festival

Culture festival · Bath · 16–25 October 2026

When16–25 October 2026
WhereCinemas and venues across Bath city centre, BA1 1EB Bath
TypeCulture festival

An annual independent film festival, run by the charity FilmBath, screening new features, documentaries and short films across cinemas and venues in the city. The programme mixes premieres, retrospectives and filmmaker discussions, with a long-standing focus on independent and world cinema. It takes place over ten days in October.
