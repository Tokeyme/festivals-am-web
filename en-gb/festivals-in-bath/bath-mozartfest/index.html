Bath Mozartfest

Music festival · Bath · 13–21 November 2026

When13–21 November 2026
WhereBath Abbey, Assembly Rooms and other historic venues, BA1 1LT Bath
TypeMusic festival

A classical music festival held in some of Bath's finest historic settings, including Bath Abbey and the Assembly Rooms. Leading chamber ensembles, soloists and orchestras present concerts centred on Mozart and the wider classical and Romantic repertoire. The festival runs over several days each November.
