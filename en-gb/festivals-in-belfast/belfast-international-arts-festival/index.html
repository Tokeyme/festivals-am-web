Belfast International Arts Festival

Culture festival · Belfast · 14 October – 8 November 2026

When14 October – 8 November 2026
WhereVenues across Belfast, including Ulster Hall, Bedford Street, BT2 7FF Belfast
TypeCulture festival

The Belfast International Arts Festival is Northern Ireland's flagship international arts event, presenting theatre, dance, music, visual arts, literature and ideas. Held over several weeks in more than 30 venues across the city, it features a mix of local and international artists and premieres.
