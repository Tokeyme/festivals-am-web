Belfast Mela

Culture festival · Belfast · 22–30 August 2026

When22–30 August 2026
WhereBotanic Gardens and venues across the city, Belfast, BT7 1LP Belfast
TypeCulture festival

Belfast Mela is a celebration of world cultures that transforms venues across the city into hubs of international music, dance, food, art and wellbeing. Its flagship day in Botanic Gardens brings together performers and communities from around the globe for one of Northern Ireland's largest multicultural events.
