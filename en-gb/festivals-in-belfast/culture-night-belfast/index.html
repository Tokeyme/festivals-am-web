Culture Night Belfast

Street festival · Belfast, Cathedral Quarter · 18 September 2026

When18 September 2026
WhereCathedral Quarter and surrounding streets, Belfast, Cathedral Quarter, BT1 2LA Belfast
TypeStreet festival

Culture Night Belfast is a free evening celebration of arts and culture centred on the Cathedral Quarter and surrounding streets. Galleries, venues and outdoor stages host music, performance, workshops and installations as thousands of people fill the city centre.
