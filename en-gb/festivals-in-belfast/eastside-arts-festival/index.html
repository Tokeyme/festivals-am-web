EastSide Arts Festival

Culture festival · Belfast, East Belfast · 24 July – 2 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When24 July – 2 August 2026
WhereC.S. Lewis Square and venues across East Belfast, Newtownards Road, Belfast, East Belfast, BT4 1HH Belfast
TypeCulture festival

The EastSide Arts Festival is a ten-day celebration of music, comedy, theatre, visual arts, talks and family events across East Belfast. It draws on the area's cultural heritage, including its links to the writer C.S. Lewis, with many events centred on C.S. Lewis Square and nearby venues.
