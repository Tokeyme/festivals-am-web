Feile an Phobail

Culture festival · Belfast, West Belfast · 25 July – 9 August 2026

When25 July – 9 August 2026
WhereFalls Park and venues across West Belfast, Falls Road, Belfast, West Belfast, BT12 6AE Belfast
TypeCulture festival

Feile an Phobail, held mainly in West Belfast, is described as Ireland's biggest community arts festival. Over more than two weeks it stages hundreds of events, including concerts, debates, theatre, tours, sport and family activities at venues such as the Falls Park.
