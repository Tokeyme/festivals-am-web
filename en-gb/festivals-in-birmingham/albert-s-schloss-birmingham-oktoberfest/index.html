Albert's Schloss Birmingham Oktoberfest

Funfair · Birmingham, City Centre (Chamberlain Square) · 19 September – 25 October 2026

When19 September – 25 October 2026
WhereTwo Chamberlain Square, City Centre (Chamberlain Square), B3 3AX Birmingham
TypeFunfair

A five-week Bavarian Oktoberfest celebration at Albert's Schloss Birmingham, with festival biers, feasting, oompah and brass bands and live entertainment seven days a week. One of the few UK venues pouring all official Oktoberfest beers.
