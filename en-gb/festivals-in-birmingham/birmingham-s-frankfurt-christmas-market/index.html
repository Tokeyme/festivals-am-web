Birmingham's Frankfurt Christmas Market

Christmas market · Birmingham, City Centre · 5 November – 24 December 2026

When5 November – 24 December 2026
WhereVictoria Square & New Street, City Centre, B1 1BB Birmingham
TypeChristmas market

The largest authentic German Christmas market outside Germany and Austria, run in partnership with Birmingham's twin city Frankfurt. Around 80 wooden stalls line Victoria Square and New Street with bratwurst, gluehwein and handcrafted gifts.
