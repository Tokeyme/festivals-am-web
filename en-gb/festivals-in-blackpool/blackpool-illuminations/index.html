Blackpool Illuminations

Event · Blackpool · 4 September 2026 – 3 January 2027

When4 September 2026 – 3 January 2027
WherePromenade, FY1 4BJ Blackpool
TypeEvent

Blackpool Illuminations is an annual lights display along the town's Promenade. The 2026 season is switched on on Friday 4 September and runs through the autumn and Christmas period into the new year.
