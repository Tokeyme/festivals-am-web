Christmas by the Sea

Christmas market · Blackpool · 20 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November 2026 – 3 January 2027
WhereTower Festival Headland, FY1 4BJ Blackpool
TypeChristmas market

Christmas by the Sea turns the Tower Festival Headland on Blackpool's Promenade into a festive village with an outdoor ice rink, light installations and seasonal rides. Admission is free and it returns each winter.
