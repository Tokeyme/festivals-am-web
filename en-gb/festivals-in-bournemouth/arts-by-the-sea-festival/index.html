Arts by the Sea Festival

Culture festival · Bournemouth · 25–26 September 2026

When25–26 September 2026
WhereBournemouth Gardens & Town Centre, BH2 5AA Bournemouth
TypeCulture festival

Arts by the Sea is Bournemouth's festival of arts and outdoor performance, staged across the town's gardens and centre. The 2026 edition is themed 'ELEMENTAL', exploring the energies that connect people to each other and to the world around them.
