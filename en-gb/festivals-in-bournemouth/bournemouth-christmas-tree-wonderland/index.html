Christmas market · Bournemouth · 13 November 2026 – 4 January 2027

WhereLower Gardens, BH2 6AA Bournemouth

Bournemouth Christmas Tree Wonderland is a free festive light trail through the town's Lower Gardens, with illuminated trees and light installations. It forms part of Bournemouth's wider Christmas celebrations each winter.
