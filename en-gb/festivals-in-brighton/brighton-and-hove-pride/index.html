Brighton & Hove Pride

Culture festival · Brighton, Preston Park · 1–2 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When1–2 August 2026
WherePreston Park (Pride on the Park) and city-centre parade, Preston Park, BN1 6SD Brighton
TypeCulture festival

One of the UK's largest Pride celebrations, combining a colourful community parade through the city centre with the ticketed Pride on the Park event at Preston Park. The weekend features live music across multiple stages, cabaret, a village party and street celebrations across the city. It takes place at the start of August and draws very large crowds.
