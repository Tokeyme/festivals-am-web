Brighton Dome Comedy Festival

Culture festival · Brighton · 16–25 October 2026

When16–25 October 2026
WhereBrighton Dome, Church Street, and Komedia, BN1 1UE Brighton
TypeCulture festival

A comedy festival programmed by Brighton Dome, in 2026 joining forces with the Komedia venue for an expanded ten-day run. It features stand-up from leading touring comedians alongside live podcast recordings, work-in-progress shows, workshops and family-friendly events. The festival takes place across the city's established comedy venues in October.
