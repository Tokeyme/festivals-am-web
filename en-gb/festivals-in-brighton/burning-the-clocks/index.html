Burning the Clocks

Culture festival · Brighton, Seafront · 21 December 2026

When21 December 2026
WhereCity-centre procession to Brighton beach, Seafront, BN2 1TB Brighton
TypeCulture festival

A winter solstice celebration created by the arts charity Same Sky, held on the longest night of the year. Thousands take part in a lantern procession of handmade paper-and-willow sculptures that winds through the city centre to Brighton beach, where the lanterns are burned on a bonfire followed by fireworks. First held in 1993, it has become one of the city's best-loved seasonal traditions.
