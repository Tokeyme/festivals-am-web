Simple Things Festival

Music festival · Bristol · 7 November 2026

When7 November 2026
WhereBristol Beacon, Trenchard Street, and multiple city-centre venues, BS1 5AR Bristol
TypeMusic festival

A multi-venue day festival of adventurous live and electronic music staged across central Bristol, anchored by the Bristol Beacon. The programme spans experimental, alternative and club sounds, with guest artists curating parts of the line-up. It has become a fixture of the city's autumn cultural calendar.
