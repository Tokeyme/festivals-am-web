Cambridge Film Festival

Culture festival · Cambridge · 22 October – 1 November 2026

When22 October – 1 November 2026
WherePicturehouse, 38-39 St Andrew's Street, and venues across the city, CB2 3AR Cambridge
TypeCulture festival

The Cambridge Film Festival is one of the UK's longest-running film festivals, presenting new British, international and independent cinema alongside premieres, restorations, short films and filmmaker Q&As. Screenings are centred on the Picturehouse in the city centre and other venues across Cambridge.
