Cambridge Fireworks Night

Event · Cambridge · 5 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When5 November 2026
WhereMidsummer Common, Cambridge, CB4 1HB Cambridge
TypeEvent

Cambridge Fireworks Night is a large free bonfire and fireworks display on historic Midsummer Common, organised by the city council. It is one of the biggest free events in the region, drawing tens of thousands of spectators around Bonfire Night in early November. The evening also features street food and live entertainment.
