Cambridge Literary Festival

Culture festival · Cambridge · 21–22 November 2026

When21–22 November 2026
WhereCambridge Union, 9A Bridge Street, and venues across the city centre, CB2 1UB Cambridge
TypeCulture festival

The Cambridge Literary Festival is a leading UK literary event that brings authors, thinkers and readers together for talks, readings and debates. This weekend winter edition takes place at the Cambridge Union and other venues in the city centre, complementing the festival's larger spring programme.
