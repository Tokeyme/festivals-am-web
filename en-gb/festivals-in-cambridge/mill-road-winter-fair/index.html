Mill Road Winter Fair

Street festival · Cambridge, Mill Road · 5 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When5 December 2026
WhereMill Road, Cambridge, Mill Road, CB1 2AZ Cambridge
TypeStreet festival

The Mill Road Winter Fair is a lively one-day community street fair along Cambridge's diverse and independent Mill Road, usually held on the first Saturday of December. Local shops, cafes, musicians, artists and dancers fill the street and nearby venues with music stages, food stalls and family activities.
