Canterbury Christmas Market

Christmas market · Canterbury · 12 November – 24 December 2026

When12 November – 24 December 2026
WhereWhitefriars, CT1 2TF Canterbury
TypeChristmas market

Canterbury Christmas Market is a festive market spread across three areas of the city: Whitefriars, St George's Street and the Canterbury Cathedral precincts. Wooden chalets sell gifts, crafts and seasonal food and drink, and there is a Santa's grotto for children.
