Canterbury Festival

Culture festival · Canterbury · 16–31 October 2026

When16–31 October 2026
WhereVarious venues across Canterbury, CT1 2AS Canterbury
TypeCulture festival

Canterbury Festival is Kent's international arts festival, held across venues in the historic city of Canterbury. The two-week programme includes classical concerts, contemporary gigs, theatre, circus, talks and family events. In 2026 it runs from 16 to 31 October.
