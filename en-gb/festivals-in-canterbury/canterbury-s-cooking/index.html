Canterbury's Cooking

Market · Canterbury · 25–27 September 2026

When25–27 September 2026
WhereDane John Gardens, CT1 2QU Canterbury
TypeMarket

Canterbury's Cooking is a food and drink festival held in Dane John Gardens in central Canterbury. The three-day event brings together local, national and international food and craft traders alongside kitchen demonstrations, workshops, live music, children's entertainment and competitions. In 2026 it takes place from 25 to 27 September.
