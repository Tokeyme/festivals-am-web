Cardiff Music City Festival

Music festival · Cardiff · 2–17 October 2026

When2–17 October 2026
WhereVenues, clubs and pop-up spaces across Cardiff, CF10 1AH Cardiff
TypeMusic festival

A city-wide music festival spanning around two weeks across venues, clubs and pop-up spaces throughout Cardiff. The programme brings together international stars, homegrown Welsh talent, installations and one-off collaborations, and acts as an umbrella for events including Swn, Llais and the Welsh Music Prize. It runs across the first half of October.
