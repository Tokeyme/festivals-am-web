Sŵn Festival

Music festival · Cardiff · 15–17 October 2026

When15–17 October 2026
WhereMultiple city-centre venues incl. Clwb Ifor Bach, Womanby Street, CF10 1BR Cardiff
TypeMusic festival

An award-winning multi-venue festival focused on new and emerging music, staged in independent venues across central Cardiff. Founded in 2007, it showcases more than a hundred Welsh, UK and international artists across intimate stages such as Clwb Ifor Bach and Tramshed. The festival runs over three days in October.
