Cheltenham Christmas Market

Christmas market · Cheltenham · 20 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November – 23 December 2026
WhereThe Promenade, GL50 1PP Cheltenham
TypeChristmas market

Cheltenham's Christmas market lines The Promenade with chalets selling gifts, crafts and festive food and drink. It runs in the town centre in the run-up to Christmas each year.
