The Christmas Meeting

Sports event · Cheltenham, Prestbury · 11–12 December 2026

When11–12 December 2026
WhereCheltenham Racecourse, Evesham Road, Prestbury, GL50 4SH Cheltenham
TypeSports event

The Christmas Meeting is a two-day jump racing fixture at Cheltenham Racecourse, with races including the December Gold Cup and the International Hurdle. In 2026 it takes place on Friday 11 and Saturday 12 December.
