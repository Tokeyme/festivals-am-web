The November Meeting

Sports event · Cheltenham, Prestbury · 13–15 November 2026

When13–15 November 2026
WhereCheltenham Racecourse, Evesham Road, Prestbury, GL50 4SH Cheltenham
TypeSports event

The November Meeting is a three-day jump racing fixture at Cheltenham Racecourse. Its highlight is the Paddy Power Gold Cup, run on the Saturday. The 2026 meeting takes place from Friday 13 to Sunday 15 November.
