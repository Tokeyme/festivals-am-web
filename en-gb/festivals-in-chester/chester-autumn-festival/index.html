Chester Autumn Festival

Sports event · Chester · 11–12 September 2026

When11–12 September 2026
WhereChester Racecourse (The Roodee), Chester, CH1 2LY Chester
TypeSports event

The Autumn Festival is a two-day flat horse-racing meeting at Chester Racecourse, the historic Roodee, which is among the oldest racecourses still in use in Britain. The fixture combines competitive racing with hospitality and entertainment beside the River Dee.
