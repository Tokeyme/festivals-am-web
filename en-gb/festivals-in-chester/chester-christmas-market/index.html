Chester Christmas Market

Christmas market · Chester, City Centre · 18 November – 22 December 2026

When18 November – 22 December 2026
WhereTown Hall Square, Northgate Street, City Centre, CH1 2HS Chester
TypeChristmas market

Around 70 twinkling wooden cabins gather around Town Hall Square and Chester's Gothic Town Hall, selling crafts, gifts and festive food and drink.
