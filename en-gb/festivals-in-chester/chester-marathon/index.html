Chester Marathon

Sports event · Chester · 11 October 2026

When11 October 2026
WhereStart and finish at Chester Racecourse, Chester, CH1 2LY Chester
TypeSports event

The Chester Marathon is one of the UK's largest and most highly rated marathons, starting and finishing at Chester Racecourse. The scenic route is the only UK marathon to cross an international border, taking runners from England into Wales and back through Cheshire countryside.
