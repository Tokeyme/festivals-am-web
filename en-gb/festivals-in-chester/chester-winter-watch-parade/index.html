Chester Winter Watch Parade

Historical festival · Chester · 3 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When3 December 2026
WhereCity centre streets and Town Hall Square, Chester, CH1 2HS Chester
TypeHistorical festival

The Chester Winter Watch Parade is a torch-lit procession through the city's historic streets that re-enacts the medieval tradition of the City Watch guarding Chester at Christmas. Costumed characters, giant puppets, drummers and street performers lead the parade, which is usually held on a Thursday evening in early December.
