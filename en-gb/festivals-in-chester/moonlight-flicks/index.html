Moonlight Flicks

Event · Chester · 9–31 August 2026

When9–31 August 2026
WhereGrosvenor Park, Chester, CH1 1QQ Chester
TypeEvent

Moonlight Flicks is Chester's open-air cinema season, staged by Storyhouse in the Victorian surroundings of Grosvenor Park. Across August, audiences gather after dark for screenings of cult classics, musicals and recent releases in a relaxed picnic setting.
