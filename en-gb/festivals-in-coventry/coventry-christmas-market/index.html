Coventry Christmas Market

Christmas market · Coventry · 20 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 November 2026 – 3 January 2027
WhereBroadgate, CV1 1FS Coventry
TypeChristmas market

Coventry's Christmas Market is held in Broadgate in the city centre, with around 30 stalls selling seasonal food, drink and gifts. A big wheel and other festive attractions are set up nearby, and entry to the market is free.
