Festival of Christmas

Culture festival · Coventry · 21 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When21 November 2026
WhereBroadgate and Millennium Place, CV1 1FS Coventry
TypeCulture festival

The Festival of Christmas is a free family event in Coventry city centre marking the start of the festive season. Roaming performers, music and street entertainment fill Broadgate and Millennium Place, and families can meet Santa Claus.
