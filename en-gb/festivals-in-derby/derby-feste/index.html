Derby Festé

Culture festival · Derby · 26 September 2026

When26 September 2026
WhereMarket Place, Cathedral Green, Derby Market Hall and Osnabruck Square, DE1 3AH Derby
TypeCulture festival

Derby Festé is the city's flagship free outdoor arts festival, held across Derby city centre. The programme features international and regional street performers, including walkabout acts, aerial performers, roller-skating choreography and dance, building towards an evening spectacle. Performances take place at Derby Market Place, Cathedral Green, Derby Market Hall and Osnabruck Square.
