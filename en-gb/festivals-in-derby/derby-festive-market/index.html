Derby Festive Market

Christmas market · Derby · 27 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 November – 23 December 2026
WhereMarket Place, DE1 3AH Derby
TypeChristmas market

Derby's Festive Market returns to the Market Place with stalls selling crafts, gifts, food and drink; entry is free. Throughout December the nearby Derby Market Hall also hosts festive markets with a changing line-up of pop-up traders.
