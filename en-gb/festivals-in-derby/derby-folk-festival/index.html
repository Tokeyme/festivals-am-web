Derby Folk Festival

Music festival · Derby, Cathedral Quarter · 9–11 October 2026

When9–11 October 2026
WhereCathedral Quarter, Derby Market Hall, Cathedral Quarter, DE1 3AF Derby
TypeMusic festival

Derby Folk Festival is an annual weekend of folk music and dance held at venues across Derby's Cathedral Quarter, with Derby Market Hall as the festival hub. The programme includes concerts, ceilidhs, workshops and craft stalls, featuring national and local folk performers.
