St Leger Festival

Sports event · Doncaster · 9–12 September 2027

When9–12 September 2027
WhereLeger Way, DN2 6BB Doncaster
TypeSports event

Four days of Flat racing at Doncaster, from Thursday to Sunday, built around the St Leger. The course describes the St Leger Stakes as the world's oldest classic and the final leg of the British Triple Crown. The festival comes at the end of the classic season.
