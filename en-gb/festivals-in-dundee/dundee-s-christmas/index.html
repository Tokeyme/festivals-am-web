Dundee's Christmas

Christmas market · Dundee · 4–31 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When4–31 December 2026
WhereCity Square, DD1 3BG Dundee
TypeChristmas market

Dundee's Christmas turns City Square into a festive village with an ice rink, a Christmas market of local traders, an immersive Santa experience and a funfair. The attraction runs through the festive season in the city centre.
