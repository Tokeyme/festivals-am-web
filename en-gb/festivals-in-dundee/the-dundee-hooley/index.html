The Dundee Hooley

Culture festival · Dundee · 28 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28 November 2026
WhereDD1 1EN Dundee
TypeCulture festival

The Dundee Hooley is a free St Andrew's Day celebration in Dundee city centre, with live music, street theatre, a torchlight procession and a ceilidh. Activities take place across the city centre, including a main stage and a family activities zone.
