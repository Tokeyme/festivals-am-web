Edinburgh Art Festival

Culture festival · Edinburgh, Old Town · 14–30 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When14–30 August 2026
WhereCity Art Centre, 2 Market Street and galleries across the city, Old Town, EH1 1DE Edinburgh
TypeCulture festival

The UK's largest annual festival of visual art, presented across Edinburgh's galleries and public spaces each August. It brings together exhibitions from major institutions and independent spaces alongside newly commissioned works and installations. Much of the programme is free to visit.
