Edinburgh International Festival

Culture festival · Edinburgh, Old Town · 7–30 August 2026

When7–30 August 2026
WhereThe Hub, Castlehill and venues across the city, Old Town, EH1 2NE Edinburgh
TypeCulture festival

A curated programme of world-class opera, classical music, theatre and dance staged in Edinburgh each August. Founded in 1947, it brings leading international orchestras, ensembles and companies to venues such as the Usher Hall and Festival Theatre. It runs alongside the Fringe and the city's other August festivals.
