Edinburgh Jazz & Blues Festival

Music festival · Edinburgh, New Town · 17–26 July 2026

When17–26 July 2026
WhereAssembly Rooms, 54 George Street and venues across the city, New Town, EH2 2LR Edinburgh
TypeMusic festival

Scotland's leading jazz festival and one of the largest in Europe, held across Edinburgh in July. The programme features more than a hundred concerts spanning traditional jazz, blues, swing and contemporary styles, staged in concert halls, clubs and a festival marquee. It opens with carnival and street events in the city centre.
