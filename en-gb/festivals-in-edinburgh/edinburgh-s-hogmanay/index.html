Edinburgh's Hogmanay

Event · Edinburgh, New Town · 29 December 2026 – 1 January 2027

When29 December 2026 – 1 January 2027
WherePrinces Street and Princes Street Gardens, New Town, EH2 2HG Edinburgh
TypeEvent

Edinburgh's famous New Year celebration, spanning several days around the turn of the year. Highlights include the Torchlight Procession, open-air concerts and a large street party along Princes Street that counts down to midnight with fireworks over the castle. It is one of the world's best-known New Year events.
