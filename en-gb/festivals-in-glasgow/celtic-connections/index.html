Celtic Connections

Music festival · Glasgow, City Centre · 14–31 January 2027

When14–31 January 2027
WhereGlasgow Royal Concert Hall, 2 Sauchiehall Street and venues across the city, City Centre, G2 3NY Glasgow
TypeMusic festival

Glasgow's annual festival of folk, roots and world music, and one of the largest winter music festivals anywhere. Held over more than two weeks in January, it stages hundreds of concerts, collaborations and sessions across venues including the Glasgow Royal Concert Hall. The programme mixes Scottish traditional music with international artists.
