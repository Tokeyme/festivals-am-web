Glasgow Doors Open Days Festival

Historical festival · Glasgow, City Centre · 17–20 September 2026

When17–20 September 2026
WhereVarious buildings across the city (hub at Glasgow City Chambers, George Square), City Centre, G2 1DU Glasgow
TypeHistorical festival

Scotland's largest free celebration of architecture and heritage, opening the doors of buildings across Glasgow that are usually closed to the public. Over several days visitors can explore historic, civic and hidden spaces through tours, talks and activities. It forms the Glasgow strand of the nationwide Doors Open Days programme.
