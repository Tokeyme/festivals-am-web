Merchant City Festival

Street festival · Glasgow, Merchant City · 24 July – 2 August 2026

When24 July – 2 August 2026
WhereMerchant City district (Merchant Square, Candleriggs and surrounding streets), Merchant City, G1 1NY Glasgow
TypeStreet festival

A free street-arts festival that turns Glasgow's Merchant City district into an open-air stage. Circus, dance, theatre, music and a family programme move out of the theatres and onto the streets and squares, alongside food stalls and markets. Most events are free to attend.
