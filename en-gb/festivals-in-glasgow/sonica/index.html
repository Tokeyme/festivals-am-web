Sonica

Culture festival · Glasgow, Pollokshields · 24 September – 4 October 2026

When24 September – 4 October 2026
WhereTramway, 25 Albert Drive and venues across the city, Pollokshields, G41 2PE Glasgow
TypeCulture festival

A biennial festival of audiovisual art and experimental music staged across Glasgow venues. It presents installations, live performances and immersive works by artists from around the world, in spaces ranging from concert halls to galleries and historic buildings. The programme runs for around eleven days in early autumn.
