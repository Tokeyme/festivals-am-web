World Pipe Band Championships

Music festival · Glasgow, Glasgow Green · 14–15 August 2026

When14–15 August 2026
WhereGlasgow Green, Glasgow Green, G40 1AT Glasgow
TypeMusic festival

The world's premier pipe band competition, held on Glasgow Green each August. Hundreds of bands from many countries compete across grades before large crowds, in an event organised by the Royal Scottish Pipe Band Association. It forms the climax of Glasgow's piping season.
