Freedom Festival

Culture festival · Hull · 4–6 September 2026

When4–6 September 2026
WhereCity centre squares, streets and waterfront, HU1 3RA Hull
TypeCulture festival

Freedom Festival is one of the north of England's leading free outdoor arts festivals, staged across Hull's city-centre squares, streets and waterfront. The programme features live music, street theatre, dance, circus and large-scale outdoor spectacles from international and local artists. The 2026 edition takes Peace as its theme.
