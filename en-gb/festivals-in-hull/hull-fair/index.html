Hull Fair

Funfair · Hull · 9–17 October 2026

When9–17 October 2026
WhereWalton Street Fairground, Walton Street, HU3 6JU Hull
TypeFunfair

Hull Fair is one of Europe's largest travelling funfairs, held each October on Walton Street in western Hull. Entry to the fairground is free, with more than 250 rides and stalls individually priced. The fair has a history stretching back centuries and draws large crowds over its week-long run.
