Hull Minster Victorian Christmas Market

Christmas market · Hull, Old Town · 28 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28 November 2026
WhereHull Minster, Trinity Square, Old Town, HU1 1RR Hull
TypeChristmas market

The Hull Minster Victorian Christmas Market is a festive market held in and around Hull Minster in the Old Town. Stalls sell crafts and gifts, and the day includes Christmas-themed street entertainment and refreshments. It is one of several festive markets held in Hull's city centre before Christmas.
