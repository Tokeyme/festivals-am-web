Baxters Loch Ness Marathon & Festival of Running

Sports event · Inverness · 27 September 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When27 September 2026
WhereBught Park, IV3 5SQ Inverness
TypeSports event

The Baxters Loch Ness Marathon and Festival of Running is a point-to-point marathon that starts near Loch Ness and finishes at Bught Park in Inverness. The event village and pre-race registration are based at Bught Park, and the weekend also includes shorter races. Runners collect their event packs in Inverness before race day.
