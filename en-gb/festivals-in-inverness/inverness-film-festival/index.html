Inverness Film Festival

Culture festival · Inverness · 13–19 November 2026

When13–19 November 2026
WhereEden Court, Bishop's Road, IV3 5SA Inverness
TypeCulture festival

The Inverness Film Festival is an annual film event held at Eden Court in Inverness, presenting new feature films, documentaries, short films and retrospective screenings. It is the leading cinema event in the Scottish Highlands.
