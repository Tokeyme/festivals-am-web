November At Eden Court

Christmas market · Inverness · 20–22 November 2026

When20–22 November 2026
WhereEden Court, Bishop's Road, IV3 5SA Inverness
TypeChristmas market

November At Eden Court is a Christmas craft and food fair hosted by Exclusively Highlands at Eden Court, beside the River Ness in the centre of Inverness. Stalls offer handmade crafts, artwork, jewellery, textiles, ceramics and local food and drink. Admission is free and hot food refreshments are available on site.
