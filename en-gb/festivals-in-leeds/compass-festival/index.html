Compass Festival

Culture festival · Leeds · 12–21 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12–21 November 2026
WhereVarious city-centre locations, LS1 6HD Leeds
TypeCulture festival

Compass Festival is a biennial festival of live art and interactive performance staged in public spaces across Leeds, including markets, shopping centres, museums and streets. It commissions playful and participatory works intended to bring art into everyday settings. The next edition is planned for the autumn.
