Leeds International Film Festival

Culture festival · Leeds · 29 October – 15 November 2026

When29 October – 15 November 2026
WhereLeeds Town Hall, The Headrow, LS1 3AD Leeds
TypeCulture festival

Leeds International Film Festival is the largest film festival in England outside London, screening international features, documentaries, shorts and retrospectives. It runs across city-centre venues including Leeds Town Hall, the Hyde Park Picture House and city cinemas over roughly two and a half weeks each autumn.
