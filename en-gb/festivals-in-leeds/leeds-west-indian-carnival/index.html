Leeds West Indian Carnival

Street festival · Leeds, Chapeltown · 31 August 2026

When31 August 2026
WherePotternewton Park, Harehills Lane, Chapeltown, LS7 4HA Leeds
TypeStreet festival

The Leeds West Indian Carnival is a Caribbean carnival held every August bank holiday in the Chapeltown area, centred on Potternewton Park. Founded in 1967, it is one of Europe's oldest carnivals of its kind, featuring a costumed parade, sound systems, live music and Caribbean food. It draws large crowds to north-east Leeds.
