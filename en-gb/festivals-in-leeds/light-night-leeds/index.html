Light Night Leeds

Culture festival · Leeds · 28–29 October 2026

When28–29 October 2026
WhereMillennium Square and city-centre locations, LS2 3AD Leeds
TypeCulture festival

Light Night Leeds is a free annual arts and light festival that transforms streets, squares and buildings across the city centre after dark. Over two evenings, artists present illuminated installations, projections and performances. It is one of the largest events of its kind in the UK.
