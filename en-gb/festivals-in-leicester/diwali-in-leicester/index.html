Diwali in Leicester

Culture festival · Leicester, Belgrave · 8 November 2026

When8 November 2026
WhereCossington Recreation Ground, Cossington Street, Belgrave, LE4 6JE Leicester
TypeCulture festival

Diwali Day celebrations return to Leicester's Cossington Recreation Ground in the Belgrave area, long associated with the city's Diwali festivities. The free family event features stage performances, food stalls, arts and crafts and a children's funfair. The illuminated Wheel of Light and rangoli artworks are displayed on and around Belgrave Road. There is no public fireworks display.
