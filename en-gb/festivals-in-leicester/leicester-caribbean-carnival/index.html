Leicester Caribbean Carnival

Culture festival · Leicester · 1 August 2026

When1 August 2026
WhereVictoria Park, London Road, LE1 7RY Leicester
TypeCulture festival

Leicester Caribbean Carnival is an annual celebration of Caribbean culture and one of the largest carnivals of its kind in the UK. The day includes a costumed procession through the city that ends at Victoria Park, along with live music, sound systems, dancers and food stalls.
