Leicester Christmas Market

Christmas market · Leicester · 17 November – 24 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When17 November – 24 December 2026
WhereGallowtree Gate and High Street, LE1 5AD Leicester
TypeChristmas market

Leicester Christmas Market brings festive wooden stalls to the city centre along Gallowtree Gate and High Street, selling seasonal food, drink and gifts. Admission is free. The market forms part of the city's wider Christmas programme, which also includes ice skating at Jubilee Square.
