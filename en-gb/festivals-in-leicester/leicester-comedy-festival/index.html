Leicester Comedy Festival

Culture festival · Leicester · 3–21 February 2027

When3–21 February 2027
WhereVarious venues across the city, LE1 9BG Leicester
TypeCulture festival

Leicester Comedy Festival is one of the UK's longest-running comedy festivals, staged at venues across the city and county over about three weeks. The programme spans stand-up, sketch, improvised and family comedy, featuring established names alongside new acts.
