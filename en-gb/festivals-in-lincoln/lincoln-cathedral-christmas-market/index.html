Lincoln Cathedral Christmas Market

Christmas market · Lincoln, Cathedral Quarter · 3–5 December 2026

When3–5 December 2026
WhereLincoln Cathedral, Minster Yard, Cathedral Quarter, LN2 1PX Lincoln
TypeChristmas market

The Lincoln Cathedral Christmas Market is a festive market held in the grounds of Lincoln Cathedral, with stallholders offering gifts, crafts and seasonal food and drink over three days. It is a new event, replacing the historic city-wide Lincoln Christmas Market that ended in 2022. The first edition runs from Thursday 3 to Saturday 5 December 2026.
