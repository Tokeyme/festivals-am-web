Lincoln Medieval Christmas Market

Christmas market · Lincoln, High Street · 3–6 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When3–6 December 2026
WhereSt Mary's Guildhall, 385 High Street, High Street, LN5 7SN Lincoln
TypeChristmas market

The Lincoln Medieval Christmas Market is held at St Mary le Wigford Church and St Mary's Guildhall on Lincoln's High Street, hosting artisan craftspeople in a historic setting. It is one of several festive markets that take place across Lincoln in December.
