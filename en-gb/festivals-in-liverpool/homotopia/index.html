Homotopia

Culture festival · Liverpool · 5–22 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When5–22 November 2026
WhereThe Bluecoat, School Lane (and venues across the city), L1 3BX Liverpool
TypeCulture festival

Homotopia is an LGBTQ+ arts and culture festival based in Liverpool, described as the longest-running festival of its kind in the UK. Its programme brings together theatre, dance, film, visual art, cabaret and debate at venues across the city, typically in the autumn. The exact scheduling varies from year to year.
