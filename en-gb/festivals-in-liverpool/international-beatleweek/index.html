International Beatleweek

Music festival · Liverpool · 26 August – 1 September 2026

When26 August – 1 September 2026
WhereThe Cavern Club, 10 Mathew Street, L2 6RE Liverpool
TypeMusic festival

International Beatleweek is the world's largest Beatles festival, staged across Liverpool city-centre venues including the Cavern Club and the Adelphi Hotel. Hundreds of tribute bands from many countries perform alongside conventions, memorabilia sales and related events. It has run annually since the late 1970s and draws thousands of visitors.
