Liverpool Christmas Market

Christmas market · Liverpool, City Centre · 15 November – 24 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When15 November – 24 December 2026
WhereSt George's Plateau, St George's Hall, City Centre, L1 1JJ Liverpool
TypeChristmas market
