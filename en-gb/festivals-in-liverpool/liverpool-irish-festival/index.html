Liverpool Irish Festival

Culture festival · Liverpool · 15–25 October 2026

When15–25 October 2026
WhereMuseum of Liverpool, Pier Head (and venues across the city), L3 1DG Liverpool
TypeCulture festival

The Liverpool Irish Festival celebrates the cultural, historical and social ties between Liverpool and Ireland. Held over around ten days each October at venues across the city, it features music, theatre, film, talks, visual art and community events. It is one of the longest-established Irish festivals in Britain.
