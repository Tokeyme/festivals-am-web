River of Light

Culture festival · Liverpool · 23 October – 1 November 2026

When23 October – 1 November 2026
WherePier Head, Liverpool Waterfront, L3 1HN Liverpool
TypeCulture festival

River of Light is a free outdoor light festival along Liverpool's waterfront and city centre, centred on the Pier Head. Illuminated artworks and installations by local, national and international artists light up landmarks over an extended autumn run. The event is produced by Culture Liverpool.
