London Oktoberfest (Finsbury Park)

Funfair · London, Finsbury Park (Haringey) · 1–3 October 2026

When1–3 October 2026
WhereFinsbury Park, near the park cafe, Finsbury Park (Haringey), N4 2DH London
TypeFunfair

London's original Bavarian beer festival, held since 2011 in a large marquee in Finsbury Park. Features one-litre steins, oompah bands, German food and a blue-and-white Bavarian atmosphere.
