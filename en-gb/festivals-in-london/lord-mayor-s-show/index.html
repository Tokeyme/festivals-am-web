Lord Mayor's Show

Culture festival · London, City of London · 14 November 2026

When14 November 2026
WhereMansion House to the Royal Courts of Justice, City of London, EC4N 8BH London
TypeCulture festival

One of the world's oldest and largest civic processions, dating from 1215, marking the new Lord Mayor of the City of London. A three-mile parade of thousands of participants, floats and marching bands winds from Mansion House to the Royal Courts of Justice, ending with fireworks over the Thames.
