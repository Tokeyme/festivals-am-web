Notting Hill Carnival

Culture festival · London, Notting Hill (Kensington and Chelsea) · 30–31 August 2026

When30–31 August 2026
WhereStreets around Ladbroke Grove & Great Western Road, Notting Hill (Kensington and Chelsea), W11 1LU London
TypeCulture festival

Europe's largest street festival, celebrating Caribbean culture across the streets of Notting Hill each August bank holiday. Sunday is Children's Day; Monday hosts the main adults' parade with sound systems, steel bands and Mas costumes.
