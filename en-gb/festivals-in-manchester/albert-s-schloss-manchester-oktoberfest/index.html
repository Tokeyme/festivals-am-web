Albert's Schloss Manchester Oktoberfest

Funfair · Manchester, City Centre · 19 September – 25 October 2026

When19 September – 25 October 2026
WhereAlbert's Schloss, 27 Peter Street, Manchester, City Centre, M2 5QR Manchester
TypeFunfair

Manchester's flagship Oktoberfest inside the Albert's Schloss Bavarian pleasure palace on Peter Street, with litre steins, oompah bands, live acts and Bavarian feasting across a six-week run.
