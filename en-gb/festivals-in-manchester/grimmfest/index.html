Grimmfest

Culture festival · Manchester · 8–11 October 2026

When8–11 October 2026
WhereOdeon Luxe Great Northern, 235 Deansgate, M3 4EN Manchester
TypeCulture festival

Grimmfest is Manchester's international festival of horror, fantasy, science-fiction and cult cinema, held over four days each October at the Odeon Great Northern. It presents UK and world premieres, feature and short film competitions, and guest appearances by filmmakers and cast. It has grown into one of the UK's leading genre film festivals.
