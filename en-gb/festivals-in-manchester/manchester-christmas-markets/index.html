Manchester Christmas Markets

Christmas market · Manchester, City Centre (Albert Square & Piccadilly Gardens) · 7 November – 22 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When7 November – 22 December 2026
WhereAlbert Square & city centre, City Centre (Albert Square & Piccadilly Gardens), M2 5DB Manchester
TypeChristmas market

One of the UK's largest Christmas markets, spread across Albert Square, Piccadilly Gardens and the city centre. European-style chalets sell food, drink and crafts, with a Nordic-themed tipi bar and the giant Manchester Windmill at Albert Square.
