Manchester Village Pride

Culture festival · Manchester, Gay Village · 28–31 August 2026

When28–31 August 2026
WhereCanal Street, Gay Village, Gay Village, M1 3HE Manchester
TypeCulture festival

Manchester Village Pride is an LGBTQ+ celebration centred on the city's Gay Village around Canal Street, run by a community-owned organisation. The programme spans the August bank holiday weekend with a parade through the city centre, live performances across multiple stages and a candlelit vigil. It is the successor to the long-running Manchester Pride and raises funds for LGBTQ+ charities and grassroots groups.
