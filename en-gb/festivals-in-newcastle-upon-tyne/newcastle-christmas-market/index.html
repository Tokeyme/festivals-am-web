Newcastle Christmas Market

Christmas market · Newcastle upon Tyne, City Centre · 14 November – 23 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When14 November – 23 December 2026
WhereGrey's Monument, Grainger Street and Grey Street, City Centre, NE1 7AF Newcastle upon Tyne
TypeChristmas market

A festive market that fills the streets around Grey's Monument and Grainger Street in Newcastle city centre in the run-up to Christmas. Wooden chalets offer seasonal food and drink, crafts and gifts, alongside an Alpine-style bar. It is the city's main Christmas market and returns each winter.
