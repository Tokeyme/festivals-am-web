Newcastle Pride

Culture festival · Newcastle upon Tyne, City Centre · 25–26 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When25–26 July 2026
WhereTimes Square and venues across the city centre, City Centre, NE1 4EP Newcastle upon Tyne
TypeCulture festival

One of the UK's largest LGBTQ+ festivals, filling Newcastle city centre with a parade, live music and community events. The programme includes a main arena at Times Square along with market, family and youth zones across the city. The weekend celebrates and supports the region's LGBTQ+ communities.
