Newport Christmas Market

Christmas market · Newport · 28 November – 24 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28 November – 24 December 2026
WhereJohn Frost Square, NP20 1PA Newport
TypeChristmas market

Newport's Christmas market sets up traditional cabins in John Frost Square in the city centre, with stalls selling festive food, drink and gifts. In recent years the festive offer has also included a real ice rink and funfair rides, coordinated by Newport City Council and the Newport Now business improvement district.
