Newport Food Festival

Street festival · Newport · 3–4 October 2026

When3–4 October 2026
WhereHigh Street & John Frost Square, NP20 1PA Newport
TypeStreet festival

The Newport Food Festival is a free festival organised by Newport City Council in the city centre. It features a food market with street entertainment, chef demonstrations in Newport Market and a vegan and vegetarian village in John Frost Square, with live music and street food on the High Street.
