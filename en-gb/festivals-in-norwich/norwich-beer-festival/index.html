Norwich Beer Festival

Event · Norwich · 26–31 October 2026

When26–31 October 2026
WhereSt Andrew's and Blackfriars' Halls (The Halls), St Andrews Plain, Norwich, NR3 1AU Norwich
TypeEvent

The Norwich Beer Festival, run by the local branch of CAMRA, is one of the largest and longest-established real ale festivals in the UK. Held in the medieval St Andrew's and Blackfriars' Halls, it offers hundreds of cask ales, ciders and perries alongside food and live music.
