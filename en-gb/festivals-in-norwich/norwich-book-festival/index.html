Norwich Book Festival

Culture festival · Norwich · 24–30 October 2026

When24–30 October 2026
WhereThe Forum, Millennium Plain, and partner venues, Norwich, NR2 1TF Norwich
TypeCulture festival

The Norwich Book Festival is a celebration of reading and writing in the UNESCO City of Literature, bringing together authors, readers, bookshops and literary organisations. Held mainly at The Forum and partner venues, the programme features author talks, workshops and family events for every kind of reader.
