Norwich Film Festival

Culture festival · Norwich · 6–15 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When6–15 November 2026
WhereCinema City, St Andrews Street, and venues across the city, Norwich, NR2 4AD Norwich
TypeCulture festival

Norwich Film Festival is a BAFTA- and BIFA-qualifying short film festival held each November in Norwich. Alongside screenings of local, national and international short films, the programme includes panels, workshops and feature presentations at venues across the city.
