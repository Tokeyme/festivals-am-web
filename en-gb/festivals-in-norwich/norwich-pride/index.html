Norwich Pride

Street festival · Norwich · 25 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When25 July 2026
WhereMarch from City Hall to Chapelfield Gardens, Chapelfield Road, Norwich, NR2 1RP Norwich
TypeStreet festival

Norwich Pride is one of the city's largest free events, celebrating the LGBTQ+ community with a colourful march through the city centre followed by a gathering in Chapelfield Gardens. The parade runs from City Hall to the gardens, where stalls, speeches, music and performances continue through the afternoon.
