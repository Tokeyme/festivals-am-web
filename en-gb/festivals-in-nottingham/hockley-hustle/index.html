Hockley Hustle

Music festival · Nottingham, Hockley · 25 October 2026

When25 October 2026
WhereVarious venues across the Hockley quarter, Nottingham, Hockley, NG1 3HN Nottingham
TypeMusic festival

Hockley Hustle is a one-day multi-venue music and arts festival in Nottingham's creative Hockley quarter, run to raise money for local and national charities. Dozens of stages across bars, cafes and independent venues showcase a wide range of live music, DJs and performance.
