Nottingham Comedy Festival

Culture festival · Nottingham · 30 October – 8 November 2026

When30 October – 8 November 2026
WhereVarious venues across Nottingham city centre, NG1 2DP Nottingham
TypeCulture festival

The Nottingham Comedy Festival is an annual celebration of comedy staged across venues throughout the city. Its programme spans stand-up, sketch, improv and new-act showcases, featuring both established names and emerging talent over more than a week of shows.
