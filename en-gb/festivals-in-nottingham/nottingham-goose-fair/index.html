Nottingham Goose Fair

Funfair · Nottingham · 25 September – 4 October 2026

When25 September – 4 October 2026
WhereForest Recreation Ground, Gregory Boulevard, Nottingham, NG7 6HB Nottingham
TypeFunfair

Nottingham Goose Fair is one of Europe's oldest travelling fairs, held on the Forest Recreation Ground. Dating back more than 700 years, the ten-day fair fills the site with over 500 rides, stalls and attractions, and admission is free.
