Splendour Festival

Music festival · Nottingham, Wollaton · 18–19 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When18–19 July 2026
WhereWollaton Park, Wollaton, Nottingham, Wollaton, NG8 2AE Nottingham
TypeMusic festival

Splendour is one of Nottingham's biggest music festivals, staged in the grounds of Wollaton Park around the Elizabethan Wollaton Hall. The line-up mixes major headline acts with local artists, and the site also offers a children's area, comedy, a silent disco, funfair rides and an independent market.
