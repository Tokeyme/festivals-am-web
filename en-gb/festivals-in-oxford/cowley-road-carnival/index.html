Cowley Road Carnival

Culture festival · Oxford, Cowley Road · 20 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20 September 2026
WhereCowley Road, East Oxford, Cowley Road, OX4 1JE Oxford
TypeCulture festival

A vibrant multicultural community carnival on Oxford's Cowley Road, one of the city's most diverse districts. The event features a costumed procession, live music, dance, world food and market stalls celebrating the area's many cultures. After recent interruptions it is set to return as a focused community event in the autumn ahead of plans for a full-scale relaunch the following year.
