Oxford International Song Festival

Music festival · Oxford · 9–24 October 2026

When9–24 October 2026
WhereHolywell Music Room and various venues, OX1 3SD Oxford
TypeMusic festival

An internationally renowned festival devoted to art song and recital, formerly known as the Oxford Lieder Festival. Over two weeks it presents concerts, masterclasses and study events in venues including the Holywell Music Room, one of the oldest purpose-built concert halls in Europe. Leading singers and pianists gather in Oxford each October.
