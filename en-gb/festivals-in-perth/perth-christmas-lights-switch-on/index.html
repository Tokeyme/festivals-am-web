Perth Christmas Lights Switch-On

Town festival · Perth · 21–22 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When21–22 November 2026
WhereTay Street, PH1 5TF Perth
TypeTown festival

The Perth Christmas Lights Switch-On is an annual festive weekend in Perth city centre, with stages on Tay Street and King Edward Street, market stalls, live entertainment and a parade through the streets. It marks the start of the city's winter festival season.
