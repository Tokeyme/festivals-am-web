Perth Farmers' Market

Market · Perth · 3 October 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When3 October 2026
WhereSouth Inch Car Park, Shore Road, PH2 8BW Perth
TypeMarket

Perth Farmers' Market is a regular market held in central Perth, selling produce from local Perthshire growers and makers including meat, baking, preserves and drinks. It takes place on the first Saturday of most months from 9am to 2pm.
