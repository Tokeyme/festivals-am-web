British Firework Championships

Event · Plymouth, The Hoe · 12–13 August 2026

When12–13 August 2026
WherePlymouth Hoe, The Hoe, PL1 3DE Plymouth
TypeEvent

The British Firework Championships is a free event held on Plymouth Hoe over two evenings, in which leading UK pyrotechnic companies compete with ten-minute firework displays over Plymouth Sound. Alongside the displays there is live music and entertainment on the Hoe, with crowds watching from the Hoe and along Hoe Road.
