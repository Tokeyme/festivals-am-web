Plymouth Christmas Market

Christmas market · Plymouth · 26 November – 20 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When26 November – 20 December 2026
WhereArmada Way and New George Street, city centre, PL1 1LD Plymouth
TypeChristmas market

The Plymouth Christmas Market fills the city centre around Armada Way and New George Street with wooden chalets selling artisan crafts, gifts, seasonal treats and international street food. A German-style bar serves mulled wine and there is family entertainment. Admission is free.
