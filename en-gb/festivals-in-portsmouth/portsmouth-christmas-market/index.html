Portsmouth Christmas Market

Christmas market · Portsmouth · 21 November – 30 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When21 November – 30 December 2026
WhereCommercial Road, PO1 1DS Portsmouth
TypeChristmas market

Portsmouth's Christmas market brings wooden chalets with local makers and seasonal food and drink to the city centre in the run-up to Christmas. It forms part of the city's festive programme each winter.
