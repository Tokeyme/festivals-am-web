Reading Pride

Culture festival · Reading · 5 September 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When5 September 2026
WhereKing's Meadow, RG1 8AA Reading
TypeCulture festival

Reading Pride is an annual LGBT+ parade and festival. The parade sets off from near Reading Station and reaches King's Meadow around midday, where the free festival offers entertainment stages, a funfair, a family area and market stalls.
