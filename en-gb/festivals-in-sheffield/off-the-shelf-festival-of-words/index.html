Off the Shelf Festival of Words

Culture festival · Sheffield · 9–25 October 2026

When9–25 October 2026
WhereSheffield City Hall, Barker's Pool (and city venues), S1 2JA Sheffield
TypeCulture festival

Off the Shelf is Sheffield's festival of words, presenting hundreds of literary and spoken-word events across the city over several weeks each October. The programme includes author talks, readings, workshops, screenings and performances for all ages. It is coordinated by the University of Sheffield in partnership with city venues.
