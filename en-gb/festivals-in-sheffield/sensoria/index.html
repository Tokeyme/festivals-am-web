Sensoria

Culture festival · Sheffield · 22–25 October 2026

When22–25 October 2026
WhereShowroom Cinema, 15 Paternoster Row, S1 2BX Sheffield
TypeCulture festival

Sensoria is Sheffield's festival of film, music and digital culture, held at city-centre venues each October. The programme combines music documentaries and film screenings with live performances, talks and audiovisual events. It is one of the UK's established festivals dedicated to the crossover of music and the moving image.
