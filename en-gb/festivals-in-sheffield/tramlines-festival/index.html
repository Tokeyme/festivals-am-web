Tramlines Festival

Music festival · Sheffield, Hillsborough · 24–26 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When24–26 July 2026
WhereHillsborough Park, Penistone Road, Hillsborough, S6 2AB Sheffield
TypeMusic festival

Tramlines is Sheffield's largest music festival, held over a weekend each July at Hillsborough Park. It features major headline acts alongside emerging artists across multiple stages, plus comedy, family activities and local food and drink. Around 40,000 people attend each day.
