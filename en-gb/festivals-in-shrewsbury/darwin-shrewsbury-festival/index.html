DarwIN Shrewsbury Festival

Culture festival · Shrewsbury · 6–20 February 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When6–20 February 2027
WhereVarious venues across Shrewsbury, SY1 1LH Shrewsbury
TypeCulture festival

The DarwIN Shrewsbury Festival celebrates Charles Darwin, who was born in Shrewsbury in 1809. Held across venues in the town, its programme of talks, guided tours, exhibitions and family activities runs during February.
