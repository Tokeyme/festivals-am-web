Shrewsbury Christmas Market

Christmas market · Shrewsbury · 18–19 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When18–19 December 2026
WhereThe Square, SY1 1LA Shrewsbury
TypeChristmas market

The Shrewsbury Christmas Market is a festive market held in The Square in the centre of Shrewsbury, with stalls selling handmade gifts, food and drink such as mince pies and mulled wine. It is organised as part of the town's Christmas programme.
