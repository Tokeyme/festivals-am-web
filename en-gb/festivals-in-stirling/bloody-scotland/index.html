Bloody Scotland

Culture festival · Stirling · 18–20 September 2026

When18–20 September 2026
WhereFK8 1BD Stirling
TypeCulture festival

Bloody Scotland is Scotland's international crime writing festival, held in Stirling with events at venues including the Golden Lion Hotel, the Albert Halls and Holy Trinity Church. The programme features leading crime authors in talks, panels and fringe events.
