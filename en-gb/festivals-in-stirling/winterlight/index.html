Winterlight

Music festival · Stirling · 27–31 December 2026

When27–31 December 2026
WhereAlbert Halls, Dumbarton Road, FK8 2QL Stirling
TypeMusic festival

Winterlight is a winter music festival at the Albert Halls in Stirling, presenting live Scottish music across several evenings. The 2026 programme includes acts such as Mànran, Talisk, Siobhan Miller Band and RURA, plus a daytime music event and a family ceilidh.
