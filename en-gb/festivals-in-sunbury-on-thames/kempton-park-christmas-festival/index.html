Kempton Park Christmas Festival

Sports event · Sunbury-on-Thames, Kempton Park · 26–27 December 2026

When26–27 December 2026
WhereStaines Road East, Kempton Park, TW16 5AQ Sunbury-on-Thames
TypeSports event

Kempton Park stages a two-day jump racing festival over the Boxing Day weekend. The King George VI Chase is the headline contest on the first day. It is a traditional outing for racegoers in the days after Christmas.
