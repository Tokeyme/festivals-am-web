Swansea International Jazz Festival

Music festival · Swansea · 3–6 September 2026

When3–6 September 2026
WhereOystermouth Road, SA1 3RD Swansea
TypeMusic festival

The Swansea International Jazz Festival is presented by Swansea Council in partnership with Swansea Jazz Club, showcasing local and international jazz artists and bands. Performances take place across multiple city venues, including the National Waterfront Museum, Swansea Grand Theatre and Swansea Minster.
