Swansea Victorian Christmas Fayre

Christmas market · Swansea · 19–22 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When19–22 November 2026
WhereOxford Street, SA1 3AE Swansea
TypeChristmas market

The Swansea Victorian Christmas Fayre transforms Oxford Street, Princess Way and Portland Street into a Victorian-style market with artisan stalls, costumed traders, street food and live entertainment. It is held over several days in the run-up to Christmas in the city centre.
