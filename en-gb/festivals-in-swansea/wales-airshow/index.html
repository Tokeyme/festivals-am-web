Wales Airshow

Event · Swansea · 25–26 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When25–26 July 2026
WhereSA1 3SN Swansea
TypeEvent

The Wales Airshow is a free two-day event staged over Swansea Bay, with aerial displays and seaside entertainment along the seafront. Ground displays and attractions run on both days, and the 2026 edition adds an evening programme of displays on the Saturday. It is one of the biggest free events in Wales and regularly draws large crowds to the Swansea seafront.
