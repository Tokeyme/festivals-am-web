Wrexham City Carnival

Town festival · Wrexham · 18 July 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When18 July 2026
WhereBellevue Park, LL13 7BP Wrexham
TypeTown festival

The Wrexham City Carnival (Carnifal Dinas Wrecsam) is a free family day at Bellevue Park with a parade, funfair, stalls and entertainment. It is followed in the evening by Wrexham Proms in the Park at the same location.
