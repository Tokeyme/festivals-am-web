Wrexham Victorian Christmas Market

Christmas market · Wrexham · 26–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When26–29 November 2026
WhereHigh Street, LL13 8LS Wrexham
TypeChristmas market

Wrexham's Victorian Christmas Market fills the city centre around St Giles' Church and High Street with wooden cabins selling festive food, drink, crafts and gifts. Victorian-themed entertainment, live music and family activities feature across the event, with a fair on Queen's Square.
