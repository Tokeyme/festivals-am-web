Aesthetica Short Film Festival

Culture festival · York · 4–8 November 2026

When4–8 November 2026
WhereCity Screen Picturehouse, 13-17 Coney Street (and city venues), YO1 9QL York
TypeCulture festival

The Aesthetica Short Film Festival is a BAFTA-qualifying festival held across venues in the historic city of York. It screens hundreds of short films alongside masterclasses, industry sessions, games and virtual-reality showcases each November. It draws filmmakers and audiences from around the world.
