York Beer and Cider Festival

Funfair · York · 16–19 September 2026

When16–19 September 2026
WhereSt Lawrence Church, Lawrence Street, YO10 3BP York
TypeFunfair

The York Beer and Cider Festival is organised by the local branch of the Campaign for Real Ale and held at St Lawrence Church near the city walls. It offers more than 200 real ales and ciders alongside wines, meads, food and live music over several days each September. Admission is free for CAMRA members.
