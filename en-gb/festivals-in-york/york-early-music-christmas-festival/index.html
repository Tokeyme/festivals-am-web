York Early Music Christmas Festival

Music festival · York · 4–13 December 2026

When4–13 December 2026
WhereNational Centre for Early Music, St Margaret's Church, Walmgate, YO1 9TL York
TypeMusic festival

The York Early Music Christmas Festival presents historically informed performances of medieval, Renaissance and Baroque music in York's historic churches and the National Centre for Early Music. Held each December, it features leading early-music ensembles and soloists in an intimate seasonal setting. It is organised by the National Centre for Early Music.
