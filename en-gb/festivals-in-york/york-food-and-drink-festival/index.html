York Food and Drink Festival

Market · York · 18–27 September 2026

When18–27 September 2026
WhereParliament Street, YO1 8SS York
TypeMarket

The York Food and Drink Festival is a ten-day celebration of regional food and drink, centred on Parliament Street and other city-centre locations. It brings together producers, street food, cookery demonstrations, tastings and live entertainment, with most of the market free to enter. It is a not-for-profit community event showcasing Yorkshire produce.
