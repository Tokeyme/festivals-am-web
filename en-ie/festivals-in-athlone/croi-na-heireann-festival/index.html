Croí na hÉireann Festival

Town festival · Athlone · 17 March 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When17 March 2027
WhereCustume Barracks, Magazine Road, Athlone
TypeTown festival

Athlone's St Patrick's Day celebration, billed as the Heart of Ireland festival. Its main event is a parade from Custume Barracks on Magazine Road to Golden Island, with community groups, marching bands, floats and dancers. A sensory-friendly zone on John Broderick Street lets participants march more quietly.
