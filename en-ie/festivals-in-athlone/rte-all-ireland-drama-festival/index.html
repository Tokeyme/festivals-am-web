RTÉ All Ireland Drama Festival

Culture festival · Athlone · 29 April – 7 May 2027

When29 April – 7 May 2027
WhereDean Crowe Theatre, Chapel Street, N37 X213 Athlone
TypeCulture festival

A national competition for amateur theatre groups, staged at the Dean Crowe Theatre in Athlone under the auspices of the Amateur Drama Council of Ireland. The festival has been running for over 70 years, and RTÉ has been involved since 2004. Groups and supporters travel from all over Ireland, and the gala awards follow on Saturday 8 May at the Radisson Blu Hotel.
