Christmas in Clifden

Christmas market · Clifden · 4–13 December 2026

When4–13 December 2026
WhereChrist Church, Church Hill, Clifden
TypeChristmas market

A two-weekend Christmas festival in Clifden, Connemara, with a market of handmade crafts, gifts and local design. Live music, baked treats and children's activities are part of the programme. The market is held around Christ Church and the Town Hall.
