Christmas in Clifden

Christmas market · Clifden · 27 November – 13 December 2026

When27 November – 13 December 2026
WhereThe Square / Town Hall, Clifden
TypeChristmas market

A three-weekend Christmas festival in Clifden, Connemara, running on 27-29 November, 5-6 December and 11-13 December 2026. It opens on 27 November with the Christmas lights countdown on The Square. Christmas markets are held at the Station House Theatre on 5 December and at the Town Hall on 6 December, and a Christmas carol concert takes place at Christ Church on 12 December.
