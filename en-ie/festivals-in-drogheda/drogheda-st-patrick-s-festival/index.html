Drogheda St. Patrick's Festival

Town festival · Drogheda · 17 March 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When17 March 2027
WhereWest Street (parade start), Drogheda
TypeTown festival

Drogheda's St Patrick's Day parade sets off from West Street at midday on the national holiday, with live music in the town afterwards. The parade has a different theme each year.
