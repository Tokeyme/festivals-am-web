Cúirt International Festival of Literature

Culture festival · Galway · 20–25 April 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20–25 April 2027
Where47 Dominick Street Lower, H91 P08F Galway
TypeCulture festival

A week-long literature festival in Galway covering fiction, non-fiction and poetry, with programming for all ages. It began in 1985 as a three-day poetry festival and calls itself one of Europe's oldest book festivals.
