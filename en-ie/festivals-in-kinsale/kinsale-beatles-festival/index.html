Kinsale Beatles Festival

Culture festival · Kinsale · 29 January – 1 February 2027

When29 January – 1 February 2027
WhereBlue Haven Hotel, Pearse Street, Kinsale
TypeCulture festival

A Beatles-themed weekend in Kinsale with talks, panel discussions, an art exhibition, a walking tour and live music. Events take place in hotels, a bookshop and pubs around the town, and most are free, while the ticketed talks are booked through Eventbrite. The festival debuted in 2025 and returned for a second year in 2026.
