Kinsale St Patrick's Maritime Festival

Town festival · Kinsale · 16–17 March 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When16–17 March 2027
WhereKinsale Yacht Club, Pier Road, Kinsale
TypeTown festival

Kinsale's St Patrick's festival opens with an evening in the harbour, with illuminated boats and fireworks, and continues with a street parade on the national holiday. The 2026 edition was the twenty-sixth and marked a visit from the town's twin, Newport in the United States.
