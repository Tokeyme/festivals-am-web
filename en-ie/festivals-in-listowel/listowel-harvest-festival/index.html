Listowel Harvest Festival

Sports event · Listowel · 20–26 September 2027

When20–26 September 2027
WhereThe Island, V31 YW54 Listowel
TypeSports event

A seven-day September race meeting at Listowel Racecourse in County Kerry, described by the course as one of Ireland's showcase racing events. Racing at Listowel dates back to 1858, and the course marked its 150th year in 2008. The 2027 festival runs from Monday 20 to Sunday 26 September.
