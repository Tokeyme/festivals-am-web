Mullingar Wild Beer & Cask Festival

Event · Mullingar · 24 April 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When24 April 2027
Where11 Dominick Street, N91 Mullingar
TypeEvent

A one-day beer festival at Smiddy's pub in Mullingar, focused on wild beers brewed with natural yeast. Breweries from Ireland, the UK and Europe have taken part in past editions. The event has been held in the town for several years.
