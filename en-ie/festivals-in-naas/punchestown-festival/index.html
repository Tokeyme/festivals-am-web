Punchestown Festival

Sports event · Naas, Punchestown · 27 April – 1 May 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 April – 1 May 2027
WherePunchestown Racecourse, W91 VCX4 Naas
TypeSports event

Five days of National Hunt racing in County Kildare that close the Irish jump season. Gold Cup Day and Champion Hurdle Day are among the highlights, and the week finishes with a Finale Day. Fashion competitions and entertainment add to the racing.
