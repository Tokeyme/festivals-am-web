Dunamaise Christmas Market

Christmas market · Portlaoise · 27–28 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27–28 November 2026
WhereChurch Street, Portlaoise
TypeChristmas market

Dunamaise Arts Centre holds a Christmas market in late November, with a small group of artists and makers selling handmade gifts, local food and festive decorations. Mulled wine and seasonal treats are on offer, and there are family crafts and a Santa meet-and-greet. The market marks the start of the arts centre's festive season.
