Tullamore Street Feast

Street festival · Tullamore · 16 May 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When16 May 2027
WhereO'Connor Square, R35 Tullamore
TypeStreet festival

A one-day street event in O'Connor Square in Tullamore, organised by local business and community groups. It offered food samples from a local pub, children's activities and live music from TradFest performers. It was held for a second year in 2026.
