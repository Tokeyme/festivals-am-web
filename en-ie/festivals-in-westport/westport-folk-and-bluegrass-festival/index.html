Westport Folk & Bluegrass Festival

Music festival · Westport · 10–13 June 2027

When10–13 June 2027
WhereTown Hall Theatre, Shop Street, Westport
TypeMusic festival

A folk, old-time and bluegrass festival spread across about ten venues in Westport, with ticketed concerts in the Town Hall Theatre and free sessions in pubs. The 2026 edition marked its twentieth anniversary and featured acts from the United States and Ireland. Workshops, a record fair and a gospel concert are also on the programme.
