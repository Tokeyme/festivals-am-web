Westport St Patrick's Day Parade

Street festival · Westport · 17 March 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When17 March 2027
WhereThe Octagon, Westport
TypeStreet festival

Westport marks the national holiday with a community parade through the town. The 2026 parade was led by the musician Matt Molloy, a member of The Chieftains, and started in the early afternoon.
