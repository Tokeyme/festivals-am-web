Imnarja

Funfair · Rabat, Buskett · 29–30 June 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When29–30 June 2027
WhereBuskett Gardens, RBT Rabat
TypeFunfair

A traditional folk celebration at Buskett Gardens, linked to the feast of Saints Peter and Paul. Crowds gather there on the evening before for a rabbit stew cooked in wine and live folk music. The following morning brings an agricultural show.
