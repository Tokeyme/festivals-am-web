Il-Karnival ta' Malta

Carnival · Valletta · 5–9 February 2027

When5–9 February 2027
WhereCity centre, VLT Valletta
TypeCarnival

The traditional Maltese carnival, held in the days before Lent, with large floats, grotesque masks and choreographed dances. The main celebrations take place in Valletta and Floriana, with smaller events in other towns and villages. Since 1926 the festivities have been centred on Valletta.
