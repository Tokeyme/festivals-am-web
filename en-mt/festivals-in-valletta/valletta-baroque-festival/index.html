Valletta Baroque Festival

Music festival · Valletta · 7–24 January 2027

When7–24 January 2027
WhereTeatru Manoel, Old Theatre Street 115, VLT Valletta
TypeMusic festival

A festival of baroque and early music, held every January in historic buildings in Valletta and across the Maltese islands. Launched in 2013, it is run by Festivals Malta under the artistic direction of Kenneth Zammit Tabona. The 2025 edition reportedly drew more than 5,000 attendees to 32 concerts.
