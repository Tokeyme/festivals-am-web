Alkmaar Ontzet

Town festival · Alkmaar, Centrum · 8 October 2026

When8 October 2026
WhereCentrum Alkmaar, Victoriepark, Canadaplein, Centrum, 1811-1815 Alkmaar
TypeTown festival

Annual celebration of the resistance against the Spanish siege (1573). Program: cheese competitions, a children's parade, a lantern procession, a traditional sauerkraut meal and an evening spectacle.
