Kaasmarkt Alkmaar

Market · Alkmaar, Centrum · 27 March – 25 September 2026

When27 March – 25 September 2026
WhereWaagplein, Alkmaar, Centrum, 1811 JP Alkmaar
TypeMarket

Traditional Dutch cheese market every Friday from 10:00–13:00; from July to August additionally on Tuesday evenings 19:00–21:00. One of the oldest cheese markets in Europe with a thousand-year history.
