KNUS Vintagemarkt

Market · Alkmaar, Centrum · 5 September 2026

When5 September 2026
WhereLaat en Koorstraat, Centrum, 1811 Alkmaar
TypeMarket

KNUS is a vintage and sustainability market that turns the Laat and Koorstraat streets into a large market for second-hand fashion, accessories and home accessories. Selected vendors offer sustainable and reusable finds in a relaxed atmosphere. Admission is free.
