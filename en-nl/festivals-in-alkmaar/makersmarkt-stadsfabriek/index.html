Makersmarkt Stadsfabriek

Market · Alkmaar, Overdie · 5 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When5 December 2026
WhereMarconistraat 5, Overdie, 1821 Alkmaar
TypeMarket

For the Makersmarkt, the Stadsfabriek on Marconistraat opens its doors as a creative meeting point for local makers and designers. Visitors can attend workshops, view exhibitions and studios, and buy handmade products such as ceramics, leatherwork and woodwork. There is also live music and activities for children; admission is free.
