Museumnacht Alkmaar

Culture festival · Alkmaar, Centrum · 26 September 2026

When26 September 2026
WhereCanadaplein 1 e.o., Centrum, 1811 Alkmaar
TypeCulture festival

During Museumnacht (museum night), several museums and cultural venues in Alkmaar's town centre open their doors together for one evening. The participating locations include the Stedelijk Museum Alkmaar, the Grote Kerk, Podium Victorie, the beer museum, HAL25 and Filmhuis Alkmaar. The programme ranges from workshops and light art through live music, DJ sets and short films to comedy and interactive art experiences.
