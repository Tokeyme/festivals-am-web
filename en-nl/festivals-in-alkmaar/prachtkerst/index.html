Prachtkerst

Christmas market · Alkmaar, Centrum · 19 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When19 December 2026
WhereWaagplein, Canadaplein e.o., Centrum, 1811 Alkmaar
TypeChristmas market

During Prachtkerst the centre of Alkmaar is entirely given over to Christmas. On the Waagplein, fire baskets burn and Christmas music plays, singer-songwriters perform in the shopping streets, and there is a Kerstcircus (Christmas circus) for children. Visitors can watch an ice sculpture being created, take a photo at the Santa's Selfie Spot and join in the Kerst Sing-a-long; the traditional cheese Christmas tree stands on the Canadaplein. For refreshment there is mulled wine, hot chocolate and poffertjes.
