Sinterklaasintocht Alkmaar

Funfair · Alkmaar, Centrum · 14 November 2026

When14 November 2026
WhereBinnenstad / Waagplein, Centrum, 1811 Alkmaar
TypeFunfair

At the Sinterklaasintocht (arrival of Sinterklaas), Sinterklaas arrives by boat across the water into Alkmaar town centre and then proceeds through the city with his Pieten. Pepernoten (small spiced biscuits) are handed out along the route, and for children the arrival is the highlight of the run-up to Christmas. The event traditionally marks the start of the Sinterklaas season.
