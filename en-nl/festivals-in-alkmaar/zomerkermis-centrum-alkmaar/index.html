Zomerkermis Centrum Alkmaar

Funfair · Alkmaar, Centrum · 21–30 August 2026

When21–30 August 2026
WhereKanaalkade, Paardenmarkt, Kerkplein, Canadaplein, Alkmaar, Centrum, 1811-1814 Alkmaar
TypeFunfair

Funfair with 50–100 attractions spread across several squares. One of the oldest funfairs in North Holland, with a history dating back to 1339; from family rides to thrill rides.
