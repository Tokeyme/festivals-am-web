Affordable Art Fair Amsterdam

Market · Amsterdam, Noord · 25–29 November 2026

When25–29 November 2026
WhereDe Kromhouthal, Gedempt Hamerkanaal 231, Noord, 1021 KP Amsterdam
TypeMarket

The Affordable Art Fair Amsterdam showcases affordable contemporary art at the Kromhouthal in Amsterdam-Noord. The 20th edition presents thousands of artworks from more than 65 Dutch and international galleries. All works are offered at fixed, transparent prices.
