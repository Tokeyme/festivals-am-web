Amsterdam Art Week

Culture festival · Amsterdam · 18–22 November 2026

When18–22 November 2026
Where Amsterdam
TypeCulture festival

Amsterdam Art Week brings together galleries, project spaces, museums and collections in a city-wide programme of contemporary art. From 2026 the event takes place in November, coinciding with the Rijksakademie's Open Studios. The programme includes a Gallery Night as well as curated routes through various districts of the city.
