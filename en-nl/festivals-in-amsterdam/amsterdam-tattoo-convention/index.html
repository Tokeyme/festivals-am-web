Amsterdam Tattoo Convention

WhereNDSM-Loods, TT Neveritaweg 15, Noord, 1033 WB Amsterdam
TypeEvent

The Amsterdam Tattoo Convention is an international tattoo gathering held in the NDSM-Loods in Amsterdam-Noord. More than 350 national and international tattoo artists present their work. This is the 20th edition of the event.
