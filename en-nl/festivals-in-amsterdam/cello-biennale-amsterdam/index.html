Cello Biënnale Amsterdam

Music festival · Amsterdam, Centrum · 29 October – 7 November 2026

When29 October – 7 November 2026
WhereMuziekgebouw aan 't IJ, Piet Heinkade 1, Centrum, 1019 BR Amsterdam
TypeMusic festival

The Cello Biënnale Amsterdam is the largest cello festival in the world and takes place at the Muziekgebouw and the BIMHUIS. The 11th edition has the theme 'Shifting Borders'. The programme includes more than 85 concerts, masterclasses, the Nationale Cello-Concours and an extensive supporting programme.
