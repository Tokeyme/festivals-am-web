Jordaanfestival

Street festival · Amsterdam, Jordaan · 12–15 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12–15 September 2026
WhereAppeltjesmarkt, kruising Marnixstraat / Elandsgracht, Jordaan, 1016 Amsterdam
TypeStreet festival

The Jordaanfestival is a traditional street party in Amsterdam's Jordaan district, centred on the Appeltjesmarkt at the junction of Marnixstraat and Elandsgracht. The district's history is celebrated with Amsterdam sentimental ballads (smartlappen), street theatre and concerts on the Elandsplein. The festival has been running since 1975, and most of the outdoor activities are free.
