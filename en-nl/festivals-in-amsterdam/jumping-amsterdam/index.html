Jumping Amsterdam

Sports event · Amsterdam, Zuid · 28–31 January 2027

When28–31 January 2027
WhereRAI Amsterdam, Europaplein 22, Zuid, 1078 GZ Amsterdam
TypeSports event

Jumping Amsterdam is an international equestrian tournament at the RAI Amsterdam, with top-level dressage and show-jumping competitions, including qualifiers for the FEI World Cup. The event runs over four days.
