Open Monumentendag Amsterdam

Historical festival · Amsterdam · 12–13 September 2026

When12–13 September 2026
Where Amsterdam
TypeHistorical festival

On Open Monumentendag, around 50 monuments in Amsterdam open their doors to the public free of charge. The 2026 edition is the 40th time the event has been held. Visitors can view historic buildings that are otherwise closed or only accessible on a limited basis.
