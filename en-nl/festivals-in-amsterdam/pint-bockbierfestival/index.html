PINT Bockbierfestival

Event · Amsterdam, West · 2–3 October 2026

When2–3 October 2026
WhereDe Hallen Studio's, Hannie Dankbaarpassage 18, West, 1053 RT Amsterdam
TypeEvent

The PINT Bockbierfestival is a beer festival devoted to Dutch bock beers. The 44th edition takes place at De Hallen Studio's in Amsterdam-West and features more than 40 different bock beers. After being held for many years at the Beurs van Berlage, the festival has been relaunched in a smaller format.
