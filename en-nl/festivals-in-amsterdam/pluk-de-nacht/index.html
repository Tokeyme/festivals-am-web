Pluk de Nacht

Culture festival · Amsterdam, Centrum · 19–29 August 2026

When19–29 August 2026
WhereStenen Hoofd, Westerdoksdijk, Centrum, 1013 Amsterdam
TypeCulture festival

Pluk de Nacht is a free open-air film festival at the Stenen Hoofd on the banks of the IJ, not far from the Central Station. Short films and feature films are shown under the open sky, complemented by a programme of drinks and music. The festival also takes place at the same time in Utrecht.
