Prinsengrachtconcert

Music festival · Amsterdam, Centrum · 22 August 2026

When22 August 2026
WherePrinsengracht, voor Hotel Pulitzer, Centrum, 1016 GZ Amsterdam
TypeMusic festival

The Prinsengrachtconcert is an annual open-air classical music concert on a floating stage in the Prinsengracht in front of the Hotel Pulitzer. It is regarded as the largest free-to-attend classical concert in the Netherlands. This edition puts the piano centre stage, with the Norwegian pianist Leif Ove Andsnes, the cellist Sol Gabetta and the mezzo-soprano Maria Warenberg.
