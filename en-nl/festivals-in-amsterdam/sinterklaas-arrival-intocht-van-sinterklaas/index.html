Sinterklaas Arrival / Intocht van Sinterklaas

Funfair · Amsterdam, Centrum · 15 November 2026

When15 November 2026
WhereCity-wide, starts Marineterrein via Amstel to Leidseplein, Centrum, 1012 Amsterdam
TypeFunfair

The world's largest Sinterklaas arrival parade attracting 300,000-350,000 spectators. Arrival by steamship on the Amstel, followed by a horse-drawn procession and key presentation ceremony.
