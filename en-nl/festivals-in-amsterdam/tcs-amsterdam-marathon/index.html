TCS Amsterdam Marathon

Event · Amsterdam, Diverse locaties · 17–18 October 2026

When17–18 October 2026
WhereOlympic Stadium and city routes, Diverse locaties, 1076 Amsterdam
TypeEvent

World-class platinum marathon event renowned for its fast and flat course. Start and finish at the 1928 Olympic Stadium, with the route passing through Vondelpark, the Rijksmuseum, and along the Amstel River.
