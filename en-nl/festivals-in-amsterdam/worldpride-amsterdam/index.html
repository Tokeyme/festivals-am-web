WorldPride Amsterdam

Culture festival · Amsterdam, Centrum / diverse locaties · 25 July – 8 August 2026

When25 July – 8 August 2026
WhereCity-wide, Canal Parade: Prinsengracht, Centrum / diverse locaties, 1010 Amsterdam
TypeCulture festival

Two-week WorldPride celebration with canal parade on August 1 (80 festively decorated boats, approximately 500,000 spectators). First WorldPride in Amsterdam, celebrating 25 years of marriage equality.
