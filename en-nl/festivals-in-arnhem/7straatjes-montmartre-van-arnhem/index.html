7straatjes, Montmartre van Arnhem

Street festival · Arnhem, Centrum · 29 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When29 August 2026
Where7 straatjes, binnenstad, Centrum, Arnhem
TypeStreet festival

On this day, the 7 straatjes (seven little streets) in the center of Arnhem turn into the Montmartre of Arnhem. The shop windows and alleys are lavishly decorated with flowers in the Parisian style. Visitors can expect a French ambience with evocative chansons, portrait artists and terraces. Admission is free.
