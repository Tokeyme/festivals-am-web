Arnhem Zingt TOP2000

Music festival · Arnhem, Centrum · 31 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When31 December 2026
WhereJansplein, Centrum, Arnhem
TypeMusic festival

At Arnhem Zingt TOP2000, people sing together on the Jansplein in Arnhem town centre. A vocal band with several members performs the most popular hits from the TOP2000, while the lyrics appear on large screens for singing along. The event takes place at the end of the year and is free to attend.
