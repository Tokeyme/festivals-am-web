Every Buddy Happy Festival

Culture festival · Arnhem, Sint Marten · 23 August 2026

When23 August 2026
WhereDe Nieuwe Hommel, De Wiltstraat 6, Sint Marten, 6821 CE Arnhem
TypeCulture festival

The Every Buddy Happy Festival is hosted by Buddy to Buddy Arnhem at the De Nieuwe Hommel neighbourhood centre. It offers music, good food and various cultural activities such as a poetry stage, a percussion workshop and typing on old typewriters. The initiative connects newcomers with residents from every part of the city. Admission is free.
