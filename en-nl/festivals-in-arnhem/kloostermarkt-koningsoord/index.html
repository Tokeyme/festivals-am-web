Kloostermarkt Koningsoord

Market · Arnhem · 1 August 2026

When1 August 2026
WhereAbdij Koningsoord, Johannahoeveweg 79, 6816 VG Arnhem
TypeMarket

The annual Kloostermarkt (monastery market) at the Abdij Koningsoord welcomes visitors onto the grounds of the Cistercian abbey on the edge of Arnhem. The stalls offer handmade products from the Trappist sisters and their friends, as well as monastic produce to taste. Visitors can light a candle, take part in workshops or enjoy the quiet atmosphere on the abbey grounds. The monastery shop is open too.
