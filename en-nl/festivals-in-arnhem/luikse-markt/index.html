Luikse Markt

Flea market · Arnhem · 26 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When26 July 2026
WhereParkeerterrein GelreDome, Batavierenweg 25, 6841 HN Arnhem
TypeFlea market

The Luikse Markt is a large open-air flea market in the car park of the GelreDome. At around 400 stalls, visitors can find bric-a-brac, curiosities and antiques. The market is a popular destination for bargain hunters and collectors. Parking is free.
