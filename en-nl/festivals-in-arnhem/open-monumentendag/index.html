Open Monumentendag

Historical festival · Arnhem · 12–13 September 2026

When12–13 September 2026
Wherediverse monumenten in de binnenstad, Arnhem
TypeHistorical festival

On the weekend of Open Monumentendag (Heritage Day), numerous monuments and historic buildings in Arnhem open their doors free of charge. This nationwide cultural event is organised in Arnhem by a local committee and takes place on the second weekend of September. Visitors can view monuments that are otherwise not accessible.
