Sinterklaasintocht Arnhem

Funfair · Arnhem, Centrum · 14 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When14 November 2026
WhereRijnkade, Centrum, Arnhem
TypeFunfair

At the Sinterklaasintocht (arrival of Sinterklaas), Sinterklaas arrives by boat at the Rijnkade in Arnhem. After his arrival, he moves through the town centre in a parade together with his Pieten. Traditionally, many families with children line the route. The event is free to attend.
