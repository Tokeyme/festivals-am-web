Winter in het Openluchtmuseum

Culture festival · Arnhem · 5 December 2026 – 17 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When5 December 2026 – 17 January 2027
WhereNederlands Openluchtmuseum, Hoeferlaan 4, 6816 SG Arnhem
TypeCulture festival

In winter the Nederlands Openluchtmuseum (open-air museum) in Arnhem transforms into a festive winter world. On the museum grounds visitors can find a historical winter market modelled on the late 19th century, a Winterkermis (winter fair) with a carousel from 1890 and a covered ice rink. Old Dutch Christmas and New Year traditions from various decades are also on show.
