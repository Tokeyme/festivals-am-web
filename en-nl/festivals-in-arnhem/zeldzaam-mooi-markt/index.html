Zeldzaam Mooi Markt

Market · Arnhem · 19 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When19 July 2026
WhereSonsbeekpark, Arnhem
TypeMarket

The Zeldzaam Mooi Markt in Sonsbeekpark brings together especially beautiful products from local and regional artists, designers and creative entrepreneurs. All goods are handmade, self-designed or produced in small runs, alongside artisanal treats. Live music and a bubble artist with giant soap bubbles provide entertainment. Admission is free.
