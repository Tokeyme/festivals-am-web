Sinterklaas Intocht (Ankunft des Nikolaus)

Funfair · Assen, Centrum · 14 November 2026

When14 November 2026
WhereKop van de Vaart en Centrum Assen, Centrum, 9401 Assen
TypeFunfair

Sinterklaas arrives by steamboat and moves through the town centre with Pieten, decorated floats and a brass band. Celebrations then continue on the Koopmansplein and in the Winkelcentrum Marsdijk.
