PopMonument

Music festival · Bergen op Zoom, Centrum · 12 September 2026

When12 September 2026
WhereDiverse Monumente im Centrum (Theater De Maagd, Markiezenhof, Gebouw-T), Centrum, 4611 Bergen op Zoom
TypeMusic festival

11th edition of a music festival where pop, hip-hop and experimental music are performed at historic monument sites. Artists: Bazart, Fresku, Marathon, Meral Polat and others. Admission: €40 (discount for under-27s).
