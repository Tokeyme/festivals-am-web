Braderie Teteringen

Street festival · Breda, Teteringen · 29 August 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When29 August 2026
WhereWillem Alexanderplein, Teteringen, 4847 Breda
TypeStreet festival

The Braderie Teteringen takes place on and around the Willem Alexanderplein in Teteringen, a district of Breda. The relaxed street market is combined with the Kermis (funfair); traders and vendors present their wares at market stalls. Admission is free.
