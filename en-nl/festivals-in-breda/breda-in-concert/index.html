Breda in Concert

Music festival · Breda, Chasséveld · 18 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When18 July 2026
WhereChasséveld, Claudius Prinsenlaan, Chasséveld, 4811 Breda
TypeMusic festival

Breda in Concert is West Brabant's Dutch-language music festival and takes place on the Chasséveld. Past editions have featured performers including Snollebollekes, Jan Smit, Nick & Simon and Wolter Kroes. The festival runs on the Saturday from midday until late in the evening.
