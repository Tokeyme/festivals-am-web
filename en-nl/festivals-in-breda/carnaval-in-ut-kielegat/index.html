Carnaval in ut Kielegat

Carnival · Breda · 6–9 February 2027

When6–9 February 2027
WhereBinnenstad / Grote Markt, 4811 Breda
TypeCarnival

During carnival, Breda is known as 'ut Kielegat'. For four days there is celebrating in the city centre and the pubs, with the Grote Optocht (grand parade) as the highlight. The motto of this edition is 'K'ebbut te pakke!', and each day the festivities begin around midday.
