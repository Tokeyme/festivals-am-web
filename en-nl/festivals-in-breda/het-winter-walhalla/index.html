Het Winter Walhalla

Funfair · Breda · 17 December 2026 – 3 January 2027

When17 December 2026 – 3 January 2027
WhereBreepark, Bavelseparklaan 7, 4817 Breda
TypeFunfair

The Winter Walhalla transforms the Breepark event hall into a wintry pleasure paradise (“Winterpaleis van het Zuiden”). On offer are an ice rink with skate hire, curling and a funfair, along with food and drink and entertainment, with a family programme during the day and parties in the evening. The event runs over the Christmas and New Year period on the Bavelseparklaan in Breda.
