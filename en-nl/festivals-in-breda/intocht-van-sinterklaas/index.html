Intocht van Sinterklaas

Event · Breda · 14 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When14 November 2026
WhereGrote Markt, 4811 Breda
TypeEvent

At the Intocht van Sinterklaas, Sinterklaas arrives in Breda's town centre and is welcomed on the Grote Markt. From there a procession with music makes its way through the town centre before Sinterklaas gives his address from the town hall balcony. The route runs along Reigerstraat, Havermarkt, Lange Brugstraat and Eindstraat, among others.
