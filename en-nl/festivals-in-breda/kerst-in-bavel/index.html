Kerst in Bavel

Christmas market · Breda, Bavel · 12 December 2026 – 2 January 2027

When12 December 2026 – 2 January 2027
Where't Klooster, Jack van Gilsplein 1, Bavel, 4854 Breda
TypeChristmas market

'Kerst in Bavel' is the Christmas programme in Bavel, a district of Breda. At the Winterfair in 't Klooster, exhibitors offer winter decorations, handicrafts, Christmas wreaths and gifts; there is a dedicated area for children selling their own goods. The programme is rounded off by a Kerststallenroute (nativity-scene trail) through the village.
