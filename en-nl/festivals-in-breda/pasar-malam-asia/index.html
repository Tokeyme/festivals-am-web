Pasar Malam Asia

Market · Breda · 9–11 October 2026

When9–11 October 2026
WhereBreepark, Bavelseparklaan 7, 4817 Breda
TypeMarket

The Pasar Malam Asia is an Asian market at the Breepark event venue and is billed as the largest and most affordable Pasar Malam in the Netherlands. It takes place over three days from Friday to Sunday on the Bavelseparklaan in Breda.
