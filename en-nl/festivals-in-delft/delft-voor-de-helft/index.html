Delft voor de Helft

Market · Delft, Centrum · 29 August 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When29 August 2026
WhereMarkt, Centrum, 2611 Delft
TypeMarket

Delft voor de Helft is an outlet market held on the Markt square in the centre of Delft. Numerous Delft shops sell their goods at steep discounts, ranging from clothing and home accessories to bags and perfume. The market is complemented by an 'Autoplaza', where regional car dealers present their latest models.
