Halloween Tanthof Oost

Neighbourhood festival · Delft, Tanthof-Oost · 24 October 2026

When24 October 2026
WhereTanthof-Oost, Tanthof-Oost, 2623 Delft
TypeNeighbourhood festival

Halloween Tanthof Oost is a Halloween neighbourhood celebration in the Tanthof-Oost district of Delft. Across the neighbourhood, streets and houses are given autumnal and spooky decorations, and children in costume roam through the area.
