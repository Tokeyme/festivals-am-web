Kerstmarkt Bikolaan

Christmas market · Delft, Tanthof-West · 12 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When12 December 2026
WhereBikoplein / Bikolaan, Tanthof-West, 2622 Delft
TypeChristmas market

The Kerstmarkt Bikolaan is a Christmas market with a Braderie (street market) on and around the Bikolaan and the Bikoplein in the Tanthof-West district. Residents of the Tanthof neighborhood can take part with their own stalls. The market is organized by the Stichting Welbevinden Delft together with the local community.
