Kerstmarkt

Christmas market · Delft, Centrum · 19 December 2026

When19 December 2026
WhereMarkt, Centrum, 2611 Delft
TypeChristmas market

A Christmas market is held on the Markt square in Delft's old town. Between the Stadhuis (city hall) and the Nieuwe Kerk, stalls offer festive goods.
