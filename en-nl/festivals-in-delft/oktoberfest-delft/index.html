Oktoberfest Delft

Funfair · Delft, Centrum · 9–11 October 2026

When9–11 October 2026
WhereHeilige Geestkerkhof (Brouwplein), Centrum, 2611 Delft
TypeFunfair

Oktoberfest Delft brings a Bavarian atmosphere to the Brouwplein at the Heilige Geestkerkhof and to the Delfts Brouwhuis. At long tables, beer is served by the litre, alongside schnitzel and other dishes. Live music and several DJs provide the entertainment.
