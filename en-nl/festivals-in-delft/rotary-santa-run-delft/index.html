Rotary Santa Run Delft

Sports event · Delft, Centrum · 13 December 2026

When13 December 2026
WhereBinnenstad (start en finish), Centrum, 2611 Delft
TypeSports event

The Rotary Santa Run Delft is a festive 3 km run through the decorated town center of Delft. Hundreds of participants run in Santa costumes across canals and bridges, right in the middle of the shopping district. The proceeds benefit local good causes, including the Jessehof and the Buurtfabriek Delft.
