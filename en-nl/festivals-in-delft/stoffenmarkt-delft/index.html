Stoffenmarkt Delft

Market · Delft, Centrum · 3 October 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When3 October 2026
WhereMarkt, Centrum, 2611 Delft
TypeMarket

During the Stoffenmarkt, the market square in the centre of Delft turns into a sales venue for textiles. The stalls offer fashion and clothing fabrics as well as home and upholstery fabrics and matching accessories. The stalls are open during the day from 10 am to 4.30 pm.
