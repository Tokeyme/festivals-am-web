Van Marken Dag

Neighbourhood festival · Delft, Agnetapark · 30 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When30 August 2026
WhereAgnetapark, Agnetapark, 2614 Delft
TypeNeighbourhood festival

Van Marken Dag is a festive day with many activities for young and old in the Agnetapark. It carries on the community day that Jacques and Agneta van Marken once organised each year for the residents of the workers' quarter. On offer are performances by clubs, theatre groups and creative entrepreneurs, music from the Koninklijke Harmoniekapel Delft among others, as well as an entertainment programme for children. There are also exhibitions by the Stichting Museum Van Marken.
