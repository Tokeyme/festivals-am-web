Vreugdevuur Tanthof

Event · Delft, Tanthof · 31 December 2026

When31 December 2026
WhereAmazoneweg / Kalfjeslaan / Abtswoude, Tanthof, 2623 Delft
TypeEvent

The Vreugdevuur Tanthof is a traditional New Year's bonfire in the Tanthof district of Delft. A woodpile is set alight near Amazoneweg, Kalfjeslaan and Abtswoude, accompanied by music and a sociable neighbourhood get-together. The fire requires a permit and may not be lit when the wind is too strong.
