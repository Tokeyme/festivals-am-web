Dickens Festijn

Culture festival · Deventer, Bergkwartier · 12–13 December 2026

When12–13 December 2026
WhereBergkwartier, Bergkwartier, 7411 Deventer
TypeCulture festival

Unique festival with over 950 people in costumes from Charles Dickens' works. It transforms the Bergkwartier into a Victorian English town with market stalls, carolers and a Christmas atmosphere. Admission EUR 6.
