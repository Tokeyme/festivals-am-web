Open Monumentendag

Culture festival · Deventer, Centrum · 12–13 September 2026

When12–13 September 2026
WhereDiverse Orte in der Gemeinde Deventer, Centrum, 7411 Deventer
TypeCulture festival

40th edition of the nationwide day with free access to over 45 monuments in the municipality. Guided tours, exhibitions and activities on the theme of cultural heritage under the motto 'Veerkrachtig erfgoed'.
