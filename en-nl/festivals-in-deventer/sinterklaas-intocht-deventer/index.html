Sinterklaas Intocht Deventer

Funfair · Deventer, Centrum · 5 December 2026

When5 December 2026
WhereWellekade, Centrum, 7411 Deventer
TypeFunfair

Traditional Dutch Saint Nicholas arrival celebration with a procession through the city center. Tens of thousands of visitors welcome the Christmas saint, with pepernoten and gingerbread handed out.
