ASML Marathon Eindhoven

Sports event · Eindhoven, Centrum · 10–11 October 2026

When10–11 October 2026
WhereStart/finish Stadhuisplein, centrum, Centrum, 5611 Eindhoven
TypeSports event

The ASML Marathon Eindhoven is the largest two-day sporting event in the province of Noord-Brabant. The programme includes the marathon over 42.195 kilometres, a half marathon and a quarter marathon, among others. The start and finish are in the centre of Eindhoven.
