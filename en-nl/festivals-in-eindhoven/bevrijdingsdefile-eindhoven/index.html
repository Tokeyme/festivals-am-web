Bevrijdingsdefilé Eindhoven

Historical festival · Eindhoven, Centrum · 18 September 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When18 September 2026
WhereStadhuisplein, Centrum, 5611 Eindhoven
TypeHistorical festival

On 18 September each year, Eindhoven commemorates its liberation in the Second World War. On the Stadhuisplein there is a memorial ceremony and the lighting of the liberation fire, followed by a torchlight procession through the center. The Stichting 18 September organizes the commemoration in honor of the victims and the Allied liberators.
