Carnaval in Eindhoven

Carnival · Eindhoven, Centrum · 5–9 February 2027

When5–9 February 2027
WhereCentrum (o.a. Markt, 18 Septemberplein), Centrum, 5611 Eindhoven
TypeCarnival

During the days of Carnaval, Eindhoven transforms into 'Lampegat', the realm of fools. Almost the entire city is given over to the celebration, with parades, music and costumes. The highlight is the great Carnaval parade through the centre.
