Hidden Garden Festival

Music festival · Eindhoven, Tongelre · 29 August 2026

When29 August 2026
WhereRecreatieplas de IJzeren Man, Tongelre, 5641 Eindhoven
TypeMusic festival

Hidden Garden is a boutique festival set in nature on the shore of the IJzeren Man in Eindhoven. Hidden among the trees are stages, food spots and surprising acts. The event is open to those aged 18 and over.
