Hit The City

Music festival · Eindhoven, Centrum · 27–29 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When27–29 August 2026
WhereDiverse locaties in de binnenstad (o.a. Effenaar, Klokgebouw, Dynamo), Centrum, 5611 Eindhoven
TypeMusic festival

Hit The City is a free city festival that puts new music and well-known acts centre stage. Over a hundred performances are spread across numerous stages and venues in the centre of Eindhoven, including Effenaar, Klokgebouw and Dynamo. Admission is free.
