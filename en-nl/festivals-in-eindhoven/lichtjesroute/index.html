Lichtjesroute

Historical festival · Eindhoven · 18 September – 11 October 2026

When18 September – 11 October 2026
WhereRoute door de stad, Eindhoven
TypeHistorical festival

The Lichtjesroute commemorates the liberation of Eindhoven on 18 September 1944, eight months before the rest of the Netherlands. Along a route through the city, numerous ornately illuminated ornaments and light objects are on display. The route is free and can be explored on foot, by bike or by car.
