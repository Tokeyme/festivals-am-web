Maker Days Eindhoven

Culture festival · Eindhoven, Strijp-S · 12–13 September 2026

When12–13 September 2026
WhereKlokgebouw, Klokgebouw 50, Strijp-S, Strijp-S, 5617 Eindhoven
TypeCulture festival

Maker Days Eindhoven is a festival centred on innovation, creativity and technology. Makers, artists and inventors show off creative inventions, workshops and interactive activities for young and old alike. The event takes place in the Klokgebouw at Strijp-S and expects several thousand visitors.
