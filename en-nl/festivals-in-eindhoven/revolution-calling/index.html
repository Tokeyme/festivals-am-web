Revolution Calling

Music festival · Eindhoven, Strijp-S · 20–21 November 2026

When20–21 November 2026
WhereKlokgebouw, Klokgebouw 50, Strijp-S, Strijp-S, 5617 Eindhoven
TypeMusic festival

Revolution Calling is a hardcore punk festival at the Klokgebouw in Eindhoven. Influential bands from the hardcore and punk scene perform on several stages. The festival is open to all age groups.
