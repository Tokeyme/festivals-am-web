Brugger-Festijn

Neighbourhood festival · Enschede, Glanerbrug · 13 September 2026

When13 September 2026
WhereGronausestraat, Glanerbrug, 7534 Enschede
TypeNeighbourhood festival

The Brugger-Festijn is the annual village festival of Glanerbrug on the Gronausestraat. Between the market stalls run by local residents, businesses and clubs there is a catering area offering food, drinks and live music by local artists. Admission is free.
