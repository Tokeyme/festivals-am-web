Garage Sale op het Van Heekplein

Flea market · Enschede, Binnenstad · 2 August 2026

When2 August 2026
WhereVan Heekplein, Binnenstad, 7511 Enschede
TypeFlea market

At the Garage Sale, the Van Heekplein turns into a large open-air flea market full of second-hand finds, vintage items, clothing and toys. Visitors can browse the stalls or sell their own goods. The regular Zondagmarkt (Sunday market) runs alongside it, with food stalls and children's activities; admission is free.
