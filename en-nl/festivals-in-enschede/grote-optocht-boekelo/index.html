Grote Optocht Boekelo

Carnival · Enschede, Boekelo · 7 February 2027

When7 February 2027
WhereBoekelo (dorpscentrum), Boekelo, 7548 Enschede
TypeCarnival

The Grote Optocht (grand parade) is the large carnival parade in Boekelo, organised by the Carnavalsvereniging De Soaltkloet'ns. On Carnival Sunday the parade moves through the village and forms the highlight of the Boekelo carnival.
