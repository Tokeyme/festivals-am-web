Kerstmarkt Boekelo

Christmas market · Enschede, Boekelo · 13 December 2026

When13 December 2026
WhereBoekelo (dorpscentrum), 7548 Enschede
TypeChristmas market

The Kerstmarkt Boekelo is the Christmas market in the village of Boekelo, organized by Ondernemend Boekelo. During Advent there are stalls with gift ideas, food and drinks.
