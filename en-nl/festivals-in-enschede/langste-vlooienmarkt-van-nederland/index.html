Langste Vlooienmarkt van Nederland

Flea market · Enschede, Enschede · 18–19 July 2026

When18–19 July 2026
WhereVliegveld Twenthe, Vliegveldweg 345, Enschede, 7524 PT Enschede
TypeFlea market

Holland's longest flea market with approx. 3 km of market stalls on the runway of Twenthe airport; over 300 vendors with antiques, clothing and furniture.
