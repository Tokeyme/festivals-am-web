Military Boekelo-Enschede

Sports event · Enschede, Boekelo · 1–4 October 2026

When1–4 October 2026
WhereLandgoed Het Everlo, Boekelo, Boekelo, 7548 Enschede
TypeSports event

The Military Boekelo-Enschede is one of the largest eventing competitions in international equestrian sport and takes place on the grounds near Boekelo. Over four days, top riders compete in the disciplines of dressage, cross-country and show jumping. Around the sport there is a large programme for spectators and supporting events.
