Oktoberfest op het Van Heekplein

Funfair · Enschede, Binnenstad · 27 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When27 September 2026
WhereVan Heekplein, Binnenstad, 7511 Enschede
TypeFunfair

At the Oktoberfest, the Van Heekplein turns into a beer garden during the Zondagmarkt (Sunday market), with bratwurst, beer and German-Dutch Schlager music. Various Dutch and German bands provide the entertainment. Admission is free.
