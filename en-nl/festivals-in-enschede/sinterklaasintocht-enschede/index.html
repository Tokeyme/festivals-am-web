Sinterklaasintocht Enschede

Event · Enschede, Binnenstad · 14 November 2026

When14 November 2026
WhereBinnenstad, Binnenstad, 7511 Enschede
TypeEvent

At the Sinterklaasintocht (arrival of Sinterklaas), Sinterklaas arrives by boat in the harbour of Enschede and is welcomed there by children and families. He then moves through the town centre in a parade and is received by the mayor at the town hall. The public event is free.
