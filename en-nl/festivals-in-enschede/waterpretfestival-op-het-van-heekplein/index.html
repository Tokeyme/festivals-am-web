Waterpretfestival op het Van Heekplein

Event · Enschede, Binnenstad · 9 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When9 August 2026
WhereVan Heekplein, Binnenstad, 7511 Enschede
TypeEvent

At the Waterpretfestival (water fun festival), the Van Heekplein turns into a large water paradise with huge inflatable water attractions and a foam party. Children, families and friends can cool off among the various water activities. The event is part of the Zondagmarkt (Sunday market) and is free of charge.
