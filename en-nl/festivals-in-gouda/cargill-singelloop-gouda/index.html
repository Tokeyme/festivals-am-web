Cargill Singelloop Gouda

Sports event · Gouda, Binnenstad · 5–6 September 2026

When5–6 September 2026
WhereMarkt (start en finish), Binnenstad, 2801 JG Gouda
TypeSports event

The Cargill Singelloop Gouda is a running event through the historic town centre, starting and finishing on the Markt. On Saturday, 5 September 2026, the Singelloop by Night takes place with lighting and music. On Sunday, 6 September 2026, the fast courses follow, with distances of 3.5, 7 and 10 kilometres, as well as children's runs.
