Cargill Singelloop Gouda

Sports event · Gouda, Binnenstad · 5–6 September 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When5–6 September 2026
WhereMarkt (start en finish), Binnenstad, 2801 JG Gouda
TypeSports event

The Cargill Singelloop Gouda is a running event through the historic town center, starting and finishing on the Markt. On Saturday, 5 September 2026, the Singelloop by Night takes place with 5 and 10 km runs. On Sunday, 6 September 2026, 1 and 2 km family runs, a 5 km walking route, and 5 and 10 km runs follow.
