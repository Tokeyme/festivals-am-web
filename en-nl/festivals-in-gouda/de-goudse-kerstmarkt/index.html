De Goudse Kerstmarkt

Christmas market · Gouda · 19 December 2026

When19 December 2026
WhereGoudse Schouwburg, Boelekade 67, 2806 AE Gouda
TypeChristmas market

In the Goudse Schouwburg, more than 50 artists from Gouda present and sell their creations, ranging from painting, jewelry and illustration to ceramics, textiles and design. Throughout the day there are live acts, music and children's performances. Admission is free.
