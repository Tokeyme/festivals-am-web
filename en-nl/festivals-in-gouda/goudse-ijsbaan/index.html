Goudse IJsbaan

Event · Gouda, Binnenstad · 16 December 2026 – 10 January 2027

When16 December 2026 – 10 January 2027
WhereMarkt, Binnenstad, 2801 JG Gouda
TypeEvent

During the winter season, the Goudse IJsbaan is set up on the Markt square, an ice rink overlooking the historic town hall. Stalls around the rink offer hot chocolate and snacks (Koek & Zopie). There are also programme highlights such as Disco-Schaatsen (disco skating).
