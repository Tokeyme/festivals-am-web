Open Monumentendag Gouda

Historical festival · Gouda, Binnenstad · 12–13 September 2026

When12–13 September 2026
WhereDiverse monumenten; informatiepunt Arti Legi, Markt 27, Binnenstad, 2801 JG Gouda
TypeHistorical festival

On Open Monumentendag (Heritage Day) in Gouda, around 55 monuments open their doors free of charge, many of them with guided tours, stories and activities. The central information point is Arti Legi at Markt 27. The 2026 edition takes place on Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 September, from 10am to 5pm on Saturday and from 12pm to 5pm on Sunday.
