Pianofestival Gouda

Music festival · Gouda, Binnenstad · 23 July 2026

When23 July 2026
WhereHoutmansplantsoen en Sint-Janskerk, Binnenstad, Gouda
TypeMusic festival

At the Pianofestival Gouda, the town centre turns into a lively stage during the so-called Pianocircuit, where amateur pianists from all over the Netherlands play at several public locations. Performances take place in the green Houtmansplantsoen and in the Sint-Janskerk, among other venues, complemented by a family concert and a closing concert. On Thursday, 23 July 2026, the festival's Zomerspecial (summer special) takes place.
