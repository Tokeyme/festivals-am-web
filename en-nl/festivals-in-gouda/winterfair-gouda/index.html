Winterfair Gouda

Christmas market · Gouda, Binnenstad · 11–12 December 2026

When11–12 December 2026
WhereSint-Janskerk, Achter de Kerk 2, Binnenstad, 2801 JW Gouda
TypeChristmas market

A cosy Winterfair with stalls takes place inside the Goudse Sint-Jan (Sint-Janskerk); the church can be visited free of charge on both days. It runs on Friday, 11 December 2026, during Gouda bij Kaarslicht (Gouda by candlelight), and on Saturday, 12 December 2026.
