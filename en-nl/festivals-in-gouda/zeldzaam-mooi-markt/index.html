Zeldzaam Mooi Markt

Market · Gouda, Binnenstad · 4 October 2026

When4 October 2026
WhereMarkt, Binnenstad, 2801 JG Gouda
TypeMarket

At the Zeldzaam Mooi Markt, the central market square (de Markt) turns into a creative market with handmade products by local and regional artists and designers, along with live music and artisanal treats. All products are handmade, self-designed or produced locally in small runs. On Sunday 4 October 2026 admission is free, open from 12pm to 6pm.
