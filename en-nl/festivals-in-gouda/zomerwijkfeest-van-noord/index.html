Zomerwijkfeest Van Noord

Neighbourhood festival · Gouda, Gouda Noord · 5 September 2026

When5 September 2026
WhereOntmoetingscentrum Van Noord, Lekkenburg 1, Gouda Noord, 2804 XA Gouda
TypeNeighbourhood festival

Around the Ontmoetingscentrum Van Noord in Gouda Noord, the square and grass field turn into a festival ground with live music, activities and a clothing and jumble market (Rommelmarkt). There are creative workshops, sports activities and a programme for children. No registration is needed and almost everything is free.
