Eurosonic Noorderslag

Music festival · Groningen · 13–16 January 2027

When13–16 January 2027
WhereDiverse locaties (o.a. De Oosterpoort, Trompsingel 27), 9724 Groningen
TypeMusic festival

ESNS (Eurosonic Noorderslag) is a showcase festival with a conference for new European music. During the day, music professionals gather for the conference at the Oosterpoort, while in the evening numerous up-and-coming artists present their music in venues spread across the city. The festival draws around 40,000 visitors to Groningen each year.
