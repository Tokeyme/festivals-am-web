Groninger Museumnacht

Culture festival · Groningen · 19 September 2026

When19 September 2026
WhereDiverse musea (o.a. Groninger Museum, Museumeiland 1), 9711 Groningen
TypeCulture festival

During the Groninger Museumnacht, several museums keep their doors open until after midnight. A single ticket gives access to seven museums at six locations, including the Groninger Museum, Forum Groningen and Museum aan de A. It is an evening full of art and culture.
