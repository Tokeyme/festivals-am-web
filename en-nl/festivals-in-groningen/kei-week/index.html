KEI-week

Event · Groningen · 10–14 August 2026

When10–14 August 2026
WhereBinnenstad, diverse locaties, Groningen
TypeEvent

The KEI-week is the introduction week for first-year students in Groningen and is regarded as the oldest and largest introduction week in the Netherlands. New students get to know clubs, sports and cultural organisations as well as the city itself. The programme includes music, markets and numerous activities all over the city.
