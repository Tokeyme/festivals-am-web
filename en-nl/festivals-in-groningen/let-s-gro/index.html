Let's Gro

Culture festival · Groningen, Binnenstad · 6–8 November 2026

When6–8 November 2026
WhereForum Groningen, Nieuwe Markt 1 e.o., Binnenstad, 9712 Groningen
TypeCulture festival

Let's Gro is a free festival about the future of Groningen. Over three days there are more than a hundred programme items such as talks, workshops, films, music and excursions at various locations around the city. Residents and organisations help to shape the programme.
