TakeRoot Festival

Music festival · Groningen, Oosterpoort · 7 November 2026

When7 November 2026
WhereDe Oosterpoort, Trompsingel 27, Oosterpoort, 9724 Groningen
TypeMusic festival

TakeRoot is a festival of American roots music at the Oosterpoort in Groningen. From intimate singer-songwriters to loud bands, it offers a musical journey through blues, country, folk, soul and rock. The festival takes place every autumn.
