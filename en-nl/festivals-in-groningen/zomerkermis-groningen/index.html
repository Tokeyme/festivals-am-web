Zomerkermis Groningen

Funfair · Groningen, Binnenstad · 20–30 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When20–30 August 2026
WhereGrote Markt en omgeving, Binnenstad, 9711 Groningen
TypeFunfair

Around the celebrations of Gronings Ontzet at the end of August, the Groninger Kermis takes place on and around the Grote Markt. The time-honoured funfair offers numerous rides and attractions in the heart of the city and marks the start of the Ontzet festivities.
