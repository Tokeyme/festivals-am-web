Haarlem Classic Film Festival

Culture festival · Haarlem, Centrum · 5–8 September 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When5–8 September 2026
WhereGrote Markt / FilmKoepel / Schuur, Centrum, 2011 RD Haarlem
TypeCulture festival

A film festival that brings restored film classics to the big screen. The opening is a free open-air screening on the Grote Markt, after which the program continues at the FilmKoepel and the Schuur. One focus is on Italian neorealism, with works by De Sica, Rossellini and Visconti, as well as a new compilation of the early Lumière films.
