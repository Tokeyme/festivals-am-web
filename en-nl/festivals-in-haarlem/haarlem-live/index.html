Haarlem Live

Town festival · Haarlem, Centrum · 12–16 August 2026

When12–16 August 2026
WhereGrote Markt en omgeving, Centrum, 2011 RD Haarlem
TypeTown festival

A new city festival that transforms Haarlem's city centre into one big festival for five days. At squares, terraces and indoor venues there are dozens of performances with live music ranging from classical and jazz through blues and soul to rock, indie and pop. Stages are set up at the Grote Markt, the Botermarkt and the Klokhuisplein, among others. The festival was initiated by Patronaat, PHIL and Pletterij.
