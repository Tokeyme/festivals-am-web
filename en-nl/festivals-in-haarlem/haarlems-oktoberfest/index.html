Haarlems Oktoberfest

Funfair · Haarlem · 19 September 2026

When19 September 2026
WhereVeerplas, Haarlem
TypeFunfair

An Oktoberfest-style beer festival at the Veerplas. A large marquee with room for around 5,000 visitors houses traditional beer tables, a big dance floor, a catwalk and live entertainment. A ticket grants access to all areas, and reserving a beer table is optional. The event is organised by the Haarlem event-makers, father and son Visser.
