Halve van Haarlem

Sports event · Haarlem, Centrum · 27 September 2026

When27 September 2026
WhereGrote Markt, Centrum, 2011 RD Haarlem
TypeSports event

An annual running event organised by SportSupport Kennemerland, with the start and finish at the Grote Markt. On offer are a half marathon over 21.1 km as well as 10 km and 5 km runs, plus a KidsRun, Kabouterloop and a BusinessRun. The route runs through Haarlem's neighbourhoods and the dune-rich surrounding area. 2026 sees the 40th edition.
