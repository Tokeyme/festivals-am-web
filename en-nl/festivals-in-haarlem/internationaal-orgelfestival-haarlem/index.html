Internationaal Orgelfestival Haarlem

Music festival · Haarlem, Centrum · 18 July – 1 August 2026

When18 July – 1 August 2026
WhereGrote Markt 22 (Grote of St.-Bavokerk), Centrum, 2011 RD Haarlem
TypeMusic festival

The oldest and internationally most renowned organ festival, held in Haarlem since 1951. Over two weeks it comprises a summer academy with 22 courses, the International Improvisation Competition and an extensive concert programme. The opening takes place in the Grote of St.-Bavokerk with its historic Christian Müller organ. 2026 also marks the celebration of its 75th anniversary.
