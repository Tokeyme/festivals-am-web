Kerstsamenzang Haarlem

Event · Haarlem, Centrum · 24 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When24 December 2026
WhereGrote Markt / Nieuwe Kerksplein, Centrum, 2011 RD Haarlem
TypeEvent

A traditional communal Christmas sing-along in Haarlem's town centre. On the Grote Markt there is first a Silent Disco, followed by singing with a live band and guest appearances. On the Nieuwe Kerksplein a brass band accompanies classic Dutch Christmas songs against the backdrop of the Nieuwe Kerk. Over mulled wine, visitors sing together.
