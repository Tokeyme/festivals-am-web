Kunstlijn Haarlem

Culture festival · Haarlem · 7–15 November 2026

When7–15 November 2026
Wherediverse locaties in Haarlem en omstreken, Haarlem
TypeCulture festival

The region's largest open studio route, held on the first two weekends of November. More than 200 selected artists open their studios, complemented by group exhibitions at around 200 locations in Haarlem and the surrounding area. In 2026 the art event celebrates its 40th anniversary.
