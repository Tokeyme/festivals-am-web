Lichtjeswandeling Haarlem

Sports event · Haarlem · 20 November 2026

When20 November 2026
Whereomgeving Henk van Turnhoutpad 1, 2034 JL Haarlem
TypeSports event

An evening walking event through Haarlem, organised by SportSupport Kennemerland. On a 6 km family route and a 10 km evening route, the way leads past illuminated monuments. Along the way, music, live acts and refreshment stations create the atmosphere.
