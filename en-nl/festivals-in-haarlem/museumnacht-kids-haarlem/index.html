Museumnacht Kids Haarlem

Culture festival · Haarlem, Centrum · 17 October 2026

When17 October 2026
Wherediverse musea (o.a. Frans Hals Museum, Teylers Museum), Centrum, 2011 HE Haarlem
TypeCulture festival

A cultural evening for families with children between 3 and 12 years old. After closing time, museums and cultural venues in Haarlem open their doors in the dark and offer workshops, creative activities and performances. Around 15 institutions take part, including the Frans Hals Museum and the Teylers Museum. It all ends with an afterparty.
