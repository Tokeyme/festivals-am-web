Zomermarkt Hoorn

Market · Hoorn, Binnenstad · 22 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When22 July 2026
WhereBinnenstad Hoorn, Binnenstad, 1621 Hoorn
TypeMarket

Large summer market in Hoorn's historic Binnenstad, also known as the Weversmarkt. More than 300 stalls with craft and needlework supplies, handicrafts and market goods wind through the old town's alleys. The market takes place on Wednesday, 22 July 2026, from 10:00 to 17:00; admission is free.
