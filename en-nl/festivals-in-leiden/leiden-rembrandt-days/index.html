Leiden Rembrandt Days

Culture festival · Leiden, Pieterswijk · 11–12 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When11–12 July 2026
WherePieterskerk / Pieterswijk / Rapenburg, Pieterswijk, 2311 Leiden
TypeCulture festival

Two-day event where Leiden's old town is transported back to the 17th century with several hundred actors. Historical performances take place around Rembrandt's birthplace, through the historic Pieterswijk and along the Rapenburg.
