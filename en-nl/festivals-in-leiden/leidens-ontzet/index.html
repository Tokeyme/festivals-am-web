Leidens Ontzet

Funfair · Leiden, Centrum · 2–3 October 2026

When2–3 October 2026
WhereCentrum Leiden (Beestenmarkt, Schuttersveld, Lammermarkt, Steenstraat, Stationsplein), Centrum, 2312 Leiden
TypeFunfair

Annual celebration of Leiden's liberation from Spanish siege in 1574. Includes Luna Park funfair with over 150 attractions, street theatre, grand parade, memorial service and herring distribution.
