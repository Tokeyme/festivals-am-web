De Stadsparade

Historical festival · Maastricht, Binnenstad · 13 September 2026

When13 September 2026
WhereMarkt, Binnenstad, 6211 CL Maastricht
TypeHistorical festival

De Stadsparade is the large historic festival parade held as part of the Maastrichtjaar. Inspired by the 1905 parade under Fons Olterdissen, special performances in the city centre bring the history of Maastricht back to life. At the end of the day, the performers move in a joint procession to the Markt square.
