JekerJazz Festival

Music festival · Maastricht · 9–11 October 2026

When9–11 October 2026
Where Maastricht
TypeMusic festival

The JekerJazz Festival is a free jazz festival in central Maastricht. Over three days, musicians play in cafés, restaurants and hotels, so visitors can move from one performance to the next. The organising foundation aims to make music and art accessible to everyone.
