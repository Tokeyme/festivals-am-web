Musica Sacra Maastricht

Culture festival · Maastricht, Binnenstad · 18–20 September 2026

When18–20 September 2026
WhereVrijthof, Binnenstad, 6211 LD Maastricht
TypeCulture festival

Musica Sacra Maastricht is a festival of spiritually inspired music, built each year around a changing theme. At several venues across the city, the programme combines music, theatre, visual art and film. This edition is dedicated to the theme of Extase (ecstasy).
