Nederlandse Dansdagen

Culture festival · Maastricht, Binnenstad · 2–8 October 2026

When2–8 October 2026
WhereVrijthof, Binnenstad, 6211 LD Maastricht
TypeCulture festival

The Nederlandse Dansdagen (Dutch Dance Days) celebrate dance in all its forms. Around the Theater aan het Vrijthof and a dance tent on the Vrijthof, the festival brings together audiences, dance makers, ensembles as well as amateurs and professionals through more than a hundred activities.
