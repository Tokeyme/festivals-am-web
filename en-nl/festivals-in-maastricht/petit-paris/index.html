Petit Paris

Culture festival · Maastricht, Jekerkwartier · 19–20 September 2026

When19–20 September 2026
WhereJekerkwartier, Jekerkwartier, 6211 JH Maastricht
TypeCulture festival

At Petit Paris, the Jekerkwartier is transformed into a slice of 1960s Paris. Music, theatre and art performances fill the streets of the quarter to create a Parisian atmosphere. The public programme is part of the Maastrichtjaar (Maastricht year).
