Sinterklaasintocht Maastricht

Event · Maastricht, Binnenstad · 14 November 2026

When14 November 2026
WhereKesselskade, Binnenstad, 6211 EN Maastricht
TypeEvent

At the Sinterklaasintocht (arrival of Sinterklaas), Sinterklaas is welcomed in Maastricht. He traditionally arrives by boat along the Maas and then proceeds through the town centre in a festive parade. The event is aimed above all at children and families.
