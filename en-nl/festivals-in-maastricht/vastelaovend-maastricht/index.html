Vastelaovend Maastricht

Carnival · Maastricht, Binnenstad · 7–9 February 2027

When7–9 February 2027
WhereVrijthof, Binnenstad, 6211 LD Maastricht
TypeCarnival

Vastelaovend (Limburg carnival) is the highlight of the Maastricht carnival and is celebrated over three days. Throughout the town centre, parades, the zaate hermeniekes (brass bands) and boisterous street carnival create the atmosphere. The Vrijthof and the Markt are the central locations.
