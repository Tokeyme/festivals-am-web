Zate Hermeniekesconcours

Carnival · Maastricht, Binnenstad · 9 February 2027

When9 February 2027
WhereVrijthof, Binnenstad, 6211 LD Maastricht
TypeCarnival

At the Zaate Hermeniekesconcours on Shrove Tuesday, the zaate hermeniekes, carnival-style brass bands, compete against each other on the Vrijthof. The bands move through the centre and play in front of a jury and the audience on the Vrijthof.
