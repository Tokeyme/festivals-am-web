Kermis Middelburg

Funfair · Middelburg, Centrum · 7–13 August 2026

When7–13 August 2026
WhereMarkt en Plein 1940, Middelburg, Centrum, 4331 Middelburg
TypeFunfair

Annual fair with over 35 attractions: roller coasters, claw machines, nostalgic carousels and cotton candy stands. Centrally located on the Markt with the Gothic town hall as a backdrop. Reduced-stimulus ride day for sensitive visitors.
