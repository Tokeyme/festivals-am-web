Bridge to Liberation Experience

Historical festival · Nijmegen, Centrum · 18 September 2026

When18 September 2026
WhereWaalbrug (John Frost-brug) / Waalkade, Centrum, 6511 Nijmegen
TypeHistorical festival

At the foot of the Waal Bridge (John Frost Bridge), Bridge to Liberation commemorates the liberation of Nijmegen and Operation Market Garden. On a floating stage, music, dance, film and personal stories come together in a memorial event. The freely accessible event is also shown via livestream.
