Kerstmarkt Kolpinghuis

Christmas market · Nijmegen, Centrum · 16 December 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When16 December 2026
WhereKolpinghuis, Smetiusstraat 1, Centrum, 6511 ER Nijmegen
TypeChristmas market

The Kolpingvereniging organises a Christmas market at the Kolpinghuis on Smetiusstraat, with stalls, Christmas decorations and a craft corner for children. Choirs sing Christmas carols, and there is pea soup and hot chocolate; the proceeds go to charitable projects.
