Nieuwjaarsduik Spiegelwaal

Event · Nijmegen, Lent · 1 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When1 January 2027
WhereSpiegelwaal, Lent, 6663 Nijmegen
TypeEvent

At the start of the year, participants jump into the cold water of the Spiegelwaal, the side channel of the Waal near Lent. The Nieuwjaarsduik (New Year's dive) is a recurring New Year tradition at the waterside north of the city centre.
