Nijmeegse Snuffelmarkt

Flea market · Nijmegen, Nijmegen-Nieuw-West · 6 September 2026

When6 September 2026
WhereJan Massinkhal, Nieuwe Dukenburgseweg, Nijmegen-Nieuw-West, 6541 AH Nijmegen
TypeFlea market

The Nijmeegse Snuffelmarkt is a recurring flea market in the Jan Massinkhal on the Nieuwe Dukenburgseweg. Numerous stalls offer bric-a-brac, collectors' items and second-hand goods.
