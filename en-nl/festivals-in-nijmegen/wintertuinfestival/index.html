Wintertuinfestival

Culture festival · Nijmegen, Centrum · 23–28 November 2026

When23–28 November 2026
WhereBinnenstad, Centrum, 6511 Nijmegen
TypeCulture festival

For six days, the Wintertuinfestival fills the city center of Nijmegen with literature, performance, art, music and encounters. Established and new voices from the Netherlands and abroad present narratives and unusual forms. The festival celebrates outspoken stories in every form.
