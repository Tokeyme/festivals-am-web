Zevenheuvelenloop

Sports event · Nijmegen, Heyendaal · 14–15 November 2026

When14–15 November 2026
WhereStart/finish omgeving Radboud Universiteit / Heyendaal, Heyendaal, 6525 Nijmegen
TypeSports event

The Zevenheuvelenloop is one of the largest road races in the Netherlands, running 15 kilometres from Nijmegen through the seven hills near Groesbeek and back. On the eve of the race, the Zevenheuvelennacht is held over 7 kilometres. Thousands of runners and spectators line the route.
