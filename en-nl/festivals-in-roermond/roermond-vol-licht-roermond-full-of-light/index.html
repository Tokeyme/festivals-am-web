Roermond Vol Licht (Roermond Full of Light)

Christmas market · Roermond, Binnenstad · 20 November 2026 – 7 January 2027

When20 November 2026 – 7 January 2027
WhereDowntown city center, Markt, Munsterplein, multiple locations, Roermond, Binnenstad, 6041 Roermond
TypeChristmas market

Major winter festival with thousands of lights, video shows, XXL photo spots, heated terraces, and festive shopping. Includes light projections on Town Hall, Ferris wheel, and winter entertainment from late November through early January.
