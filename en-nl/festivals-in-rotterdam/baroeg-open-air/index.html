Baroeg Open Air

Music festival · Rotterdam, Zuiderpark · 11–12 September 2026

When11–12 September 2026
WhereZuiderpark, Zuiderpark, 3083 Rotterdam
TypeMusic festival

Baroeg Open Air is the open-air festival of Rotterdam's Baroeg music venue and takes place in the Zuiderpark. The programme features heavier guitar styles such as metal, punk and related music. In 2026 the festival is being extended to two festival days for the first time.
