CAMERA JAPAN Festival

Culture festival · Rotterdam, Wilhelminapier · 23–27 September 2026

When23–27 September 2026
WhereLantarenVenster, Otto Reuchlinweg 996, Wilhelminapier, 3072 Rotterdam
TypeCulture festival

The CAMERA JAPAN Festival is the largest Japanese film festival in the Benelux countries and takes place in Rotterdam, including at LantarenVenster and the Fenix. It screens Japanese films, complemented by a programme of cultural events and a market. The festival draws several thousand visitors each year.
