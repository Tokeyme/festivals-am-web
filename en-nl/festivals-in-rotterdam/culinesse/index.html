CuliNESSE

Music festival · Rotterdam, Nesselande · 27–29 August 2026

When27–29 August 2026
WhereOeverpark, Nesselande, Nesselande, 3059 Rotterdam
TypeMusic festival

CuliNESSE is a multi-day culinary music festival in the Oeverpark by the lake in Rotterdam-Nesselande. The programme combines live music on several stages with food trucks, culinary stalls and relaxation areas. The line-up features well-known Dutch music acts.
