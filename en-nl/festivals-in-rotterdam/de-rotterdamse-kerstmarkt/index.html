De Rotterdamse Kerstmarkt

Christmas market · Rotterdam, Centrum · 19–23 December 2026

When19–23 December 2026
WhereOude Haven, Centrum, 3011 Rotterdam
TypeChristmas market

De Rotterdamse Kerstmarkt is a Christmas market at the Oude Haven in the centre of Rotterdam with over 100 stalls. On offer are Christmas decorations, gifts and treats, as well as an après-ski bar, mulled wine and fire pits. Choirs and live acts perform on a stage.
