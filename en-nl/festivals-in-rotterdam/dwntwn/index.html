DWNTWN

Music festival · Rotterdam, Centrum · 11–12 September 2026

When11–12 September 2026
WhereInnenstadt (u.a. WORM), Centrum, 3012 Rotterdam
TypeMusic festival

DWNTWN (Festival Downtown) is a progressive festival at several locations in Rotterdam's city centre, including at WORM. Under the motto 'Free by day, cheap by night', it combines music and art with programme elements that are free to attend during the day.
