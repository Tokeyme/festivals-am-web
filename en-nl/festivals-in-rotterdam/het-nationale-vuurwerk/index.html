Het Nationale Vuurwerk

Event · Rotterdam, Centrum · 31 December 2026 – 1 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When31 December 2026 – 1 January 2027
WhereErasmusbrug / Boompjeskade, Centrum, 3011 Rotterdam
TypeEvent

Het Nationale Vuurwerk is the largest New Year's Eve firework display in the Netherlands and is set off over the Erasmusbrug in Rotterdam. As the year turns, spectators gather on the quays, including the Boompjeskade, to watch the spectacle. Music and screens along the banks accompany the show.
