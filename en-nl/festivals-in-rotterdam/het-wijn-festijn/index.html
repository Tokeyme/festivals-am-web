Het Wijn Festijn

Wine festival · Rotterdam, Centrum · 14–15 August 2026

When14–15 August 2026
WhereGrotekerkplein, Centrum, 3011 Rotterdam
TypeWine festival

Het Wijn Festijn turns the Grotekerkplein in the centre of Rotterdam into a wine festival. Visitors can taste more than 100 different wines, served with matching snacks and dishes. Admission gives access to a convivial festival all about wine and enjoyment.
