International Film Festival Rotterdam

Culture festival · Rotterdam, Centrum · 28 January – 7 February 2027

When28 January – 7 February 2027
Wherede Doelen, Schouwburgplein 50, Centrum, 3012 Rotterdam
TypeCulture festival

The International Film Festival Rotterdam (IFFR) is one of the largest film festivals in the Netherlands and presents international, independent and experimental cinema. Feature films, short films and installations are shown at various venues in the city centre. The central venue is de Doelen.
