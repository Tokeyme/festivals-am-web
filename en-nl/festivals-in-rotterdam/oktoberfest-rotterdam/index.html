Oktoberfest Rotterdam

Funfair · Rotterdam, Overschie · 10 October 2026

When10 October 2026
WhereVan Nelle Fabriek, Van Nelleweg 1, Overschie, 3044 Rotterdam
TypeFunfair

The Oktoberfest Rotterdam is a Bavarian-inspired beer festival held in the iconic Van Nelle Fabriek. On offer are beer, music and an exuberant Oktoberfest atmosphere. For this edition the festival is moving to the larger Van Nelle Fabriek.
