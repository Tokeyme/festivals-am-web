TAPT Festival Rotterdam

Event · Rotterdam, Centrum · 4–5 September 2026

When4–5 September 2026
WhereMuseumpark, Centrum, 3015 Rotterdam
TypeEvent

The TAPT Festival is a festival of drinks and indulgence in the Museumpark in Rotterdam. Alongside beer, wine and cocktails, there is food, music, dancing and games. The festival sees itself as a convivial gathering centred on eating, drinking and entertainment.
