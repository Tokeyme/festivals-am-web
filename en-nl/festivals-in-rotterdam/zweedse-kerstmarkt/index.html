Zweedse Kerstmarkt

Christmas market · Rotterdam, Centrum · 28–29 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When28–29 November 2026
WhereScots International Church, Schiedamse Vest 121, Centrum, 3012 Rotterdam
TypeChristmas market

The Zweedse Kerstmarkt (Swedish Christmas market) takes place in the historic Scots International Church in Rotterdam. Visitors can discover Scandinavian traditions, from handmade jewellery and textiles to culinary specialities. Over coffee and cinnamon buns, they can settle in for a 'Fika'.
