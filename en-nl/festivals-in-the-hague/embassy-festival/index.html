Embassy Festival

Culture festival · The Hague · 5 September 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When5 September 2026
WhereLange Voorhout 37, 2514 EC The Hague
TypeCulture festival

At the Embassy Festival on the Lange Voorhout in The Hague, numerous embassies and cultures present themselves with music, dance, art and culinary offerings from all over the world. The one-day festival is free to attend and invites visitors to discover the international side of the Hofstad.
