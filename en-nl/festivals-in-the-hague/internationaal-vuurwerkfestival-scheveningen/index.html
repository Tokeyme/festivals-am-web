Internationaal Vuurwerkfestival Scheveningen

Event · The Hague, Scheveningen · 14–15 August 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When14–15 August 2026
WhereStrand Scheveningen (bij de Pier), Scheveningen, 2586 The Hague
TypeEvent

At the Internationaal Vuurwerkfestival Scheveningen, teams from various countries compete against each other with elaborate firework displays over the North Sea. The fireworks are set off from pontoons just off the coast and can be seen from the beach, the boulevard and the pier. Held on several evenings in August, the free spectacle draws large crowds.
