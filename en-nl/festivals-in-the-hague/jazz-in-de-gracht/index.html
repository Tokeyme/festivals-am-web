Jazz in de Gracht

Music festival · The Hague · 27–29 August 2026

When27–29 August 2026
WhereBierkade e.o. (grachten binnenstad), 2512 The Hague
TypeMusic festival

At Jazz in de Gracht, jazz musicians perform on floating stages in the canals of The Hague. From afternoon until evening a varied programme rings out along the water, complemented by food and drink. The festival makes the most of the canals' special atmosphere and is free to attend.
