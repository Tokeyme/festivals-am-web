Museumnacht Den Haag

Culture festival · The Hague · 10 October 2026

When10 October 2026
Whereverschillende musea in de binnenstad, The Hague
TypeCulture festival

During Museumnacht Den Haag, more than 30 museums and cultural venues open their doors together for the evening. A single ticket gives access to the various locations, which offer a special evening programme alongside their exhibitions.
