Prinsjesfestival

Culture festival · The Hague · 9–15 September 2026

When9–15 September 2026
WhereHofvijver / Lange Vijverberg, 2513 AC The Hague
TypeCulture festival

The Prinsjesfestival is an annual event held in the week leading up to and on Prinsjesdag, celebrating democracy in the centre of Den Haag around the Hofvijver. The programme combines substantive contributions with festive activities and is aimed at a broad audience, from those with an interest in politics to families. Admission is free.
