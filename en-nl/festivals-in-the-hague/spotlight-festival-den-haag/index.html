Spotlight Festival Den Haag

Culture festival · The Hague · 4–5 September 2026

When4–5 September 2026
Wherebinnenstad, verschillende locaties, The Hague
TypeCulture festival

The Spotlight Festival opens the new cultural season in Den Haag. At various locations across the city centre, more than 80 cultural organisations from Den Haag present previews, performances and workshops spanning music, dance, theatre, comedy and film. In the evening, the Cultuurnacht invites visitors to catch short previews at several venues; admission is free.
