Unox Nieuwjaarsduik Scheveningen

Sports event · The Hague, Scheveningen · 1 January 2027

When1 January 2027
WhereStrandweg (bij de Pier), Scheveningen, Scheveningen, 2586 The Hague
TypeSports event

The Unox Nieuwjaarsduik is the best-known New Year's dip in the Netherlands, held on the beach at Scheveningen. Thousands of participants plunge together into the cold North Sea water and afterwards receive the traditional pea soup (snert) and the orange Unox hat. The spectacle draws large crowds of spectators on New Year's Day.
