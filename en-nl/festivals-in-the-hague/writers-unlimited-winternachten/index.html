Writers Unlimited Winternachten

Culture festival · The Hague · 21–24 January 2027

When21–24 January 2027
WhereSpui 187 (Theater aan het Spui / Filmhuis Den Haag) e.a., 2511 BN The Hague
TypeCulture festival

Writers Unlimited (Winternachten) is an international literature festival in Den Haag. In the city's theatres, museums, libraries and schools, writers, poets and thinkers from around the world come together for readings, discussions and performances. The highlight is the Saturday Night Unlimited at Theater aan het Spui and Filmhuis Den Haag.
