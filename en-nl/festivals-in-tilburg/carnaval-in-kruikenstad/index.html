Carnaval in Kruikenstad

Carnival · Tilburg, Binnenstad · 5–9 February 2027

When5–9 February 2027
WhereBinnenstad Tilburg, Binnenstad, 5038 Tilburg
TypeCarnival

The Carnaval in Tilburg, which during Carnival is known as “Kruikenstad”, is a multi-day public festival across the whole city. The highlight is the large street parade D'n Opstoet, one of the best-attended processions in the Netherlands. In the town centre people celebrate with music and in costume.
