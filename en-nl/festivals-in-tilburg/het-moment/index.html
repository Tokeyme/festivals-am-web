Het Moment

Music festival · Tilburg, Spoorzone · 25 July 2026

When25 July 2026
WhereSpoorpark / Spoorzone, Tilburg, Spoorzone, 5017 Tilburg
TypeMusic festival

Het Moment is a Dutch hip-hop festival in the Spoorzone district of Tilburg. It brings together different generations of hip-hop fans, from classic boom-bap to high-energy performances. The festival features both established artists and up-and-coming talents from the Dutch hip-hop scene.
