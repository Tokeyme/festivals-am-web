Het Wijn Festijn

Wine festival · Tilburg, Spoorzone · 28–29 August 2026

When28–29 August 2026
WhereSpoorboulevard, Tilburg, Spoorzone, 5038 Tilburg
TypeWine festival

Het Wijn Festijn is a travelling wine festival that stops for two days on the Spoorboulevard in Tilburg. Visitors can taste a wide selection of wines. The festival travels through several Dutch cities.
