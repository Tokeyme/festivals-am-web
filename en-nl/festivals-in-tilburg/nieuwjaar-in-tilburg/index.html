Nieuwjaar in Tilburg

Event · Tilburg, Binnenstad · 31 December 2026 – 1 January 2027

When31 December 2026 – 1 January 2027
WhereStadsforum, Tilburg, Binnenstad, 5038 Tilburg
TypeEvent

Nieuwjaar in Tilburg is the municipality of Tilburg's low-fireworks New Year's Eve celebration. At locations such as the Stadsforum and in the Reeshofpark there are shows combining theatre, music, lasers and light, with a limited amount of fireworks. Across the city's districts, numerous neighbourhood activities round out the programme.
