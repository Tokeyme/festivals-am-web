Oogstfeest

Neighbourhood festival · Tilburg, Tilburg-Noord · 18 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When18 July 2026
WhereWagnerplein, Tilburg, Tilburg-Noord, 5011 Tilburg
TypeNeighbourhood festival

The Oogstfeest is a neighbourhood festival in the north of Tilburg and the successor to the former Zomerfeest. A colourful parade (Optocht) sets off from the Wagnerplein. Around the square there is a sociable programme for the neighbourhood.
