Roze Maandag

Funfair · Tilburg, Binnenstad · 20 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When20 July 2026
WhereBinnenstad Tilburg (Jupiler Bierhal Plein, Korte Heuvel u.a.), Binnenstad, 5038 Tilburg
TypeFunfair

Roze Maandag is the best-known themed day of the Tilburgse Kermis and has for decades been regarded as one of the largest LGBT+ events in the Netherlands. Tens of thousands of visitors celebrate in the centre of Tilburg. The day begins with a Pride Walk from the railway station and an official opening at the Jupiler Bierhal Plein.
