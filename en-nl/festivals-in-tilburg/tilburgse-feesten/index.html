Tilburgse Feesten

Town festival · Tilburg, Binnenstad · 26–27 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When26–27 September 2026
WhereVerschiedene Horecapleinen, Binnenstad Tilburg, Binnenstad, 5038 Tilburg
TypeTown festival

The Tilburgse Feesten is a city festival held on various hospitality squares in the centre of Tilburg. Over a single weekend, the squares with their bars and restaurants in the Binnenstad come alive with celebration.
