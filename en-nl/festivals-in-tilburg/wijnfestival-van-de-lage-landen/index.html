Wijnfestival van de Lage Landen

Wine festival · Tilburg, Spoorzone · 24 October 2026

When24 October 2026
WhereDe Koepelhal, Burgemeester Brokxlaan 2, Tilburg, Spoorzone, 5041 Tilburg
TypeWine festival

The Wijnfestival van de Lage Landen takes place in the Koepelhal in Tilburg and showcases the best Belgian and Dutch wines. Visitors can taste wines, take part in blind tastings and talk with winemakers. The programme also includes masterclasses and matching food.
