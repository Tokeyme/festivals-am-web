Winter in Tilburg

Christmas market · Tilburg, Binnenstad · 21 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When21 November 2026 – 3 January 2027
WhereWillemsplein / Binnenstad, Tilburg, Binnenstad, 5038 Tilburg
TypeChristmas market

Winter in Tilburg transforms the city centre into an atmospheric winter garden during the winter months. On the redesigned Willemsplein, a pop-up Christmas market springs up with a carousel, oliebollen and hot chocolate by the fireside. In the shopping streets and on the terraces, festive street entertainment sets the mood.
