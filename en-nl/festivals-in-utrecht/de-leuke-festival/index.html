De Leuke Festival

Music festival · Utrecht · 18 July 2026

When18 July 2026
WhereVoorveldse Polder, Utrecht
TypeMusic festival

De Leuke Festival is a one-day festival of disco, house and funk in the Voorveldse Polder in Utrecht. Alongside the music, visitors can expect food trucks, art installations and light shows. Admission is for those aged 18 and over.
