Gaudeamus Festival

Music festival · Utrecht · 9–13 September 2026

When9–13 September 2026
WhereTivoliVredenburg, Vredenburg 10 (en andere locaties), 3511 BD Utrecht
TypeMusic festival

The Gaudeamus Festival is an international festival of new music in Utrecht. At various locations around the city, with TivoliVredenburg as the central venue, there are concerts, interdisciplinary performances and sound installations by young composers and sound artists.
