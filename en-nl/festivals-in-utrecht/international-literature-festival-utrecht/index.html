International Literature Festival Utrecht

Culture festival · Utrecht · 19 September – 3 October 2026

When19 September – 3 October 2026
Whereverschillende locaties, o.a. TivoliVredenburg, Utrecht
TypeCulture festival

The International Literature Festival Utrecht (ILFU) is the largest literature festival in the Netherlands. Over the course of around two weeks, international authors and new talents appear at various locations across the city, with TivoliVredenburg as the central venue. The programme comprises readings, talks and performances.
