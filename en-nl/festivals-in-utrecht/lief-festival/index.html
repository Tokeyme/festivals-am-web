Lief Festival

Music festival · Utrecht · 5 September 2026

When5 September 2026
WhereStrijkviertel (recreatiegebied), Utrecht
TypeMusic festival

The Lief Festival is a dance festival in the Strijkviertel recreation area on the edge of Utrecht. Across several stages, DJs present electronic music in a summery open-air setting.
