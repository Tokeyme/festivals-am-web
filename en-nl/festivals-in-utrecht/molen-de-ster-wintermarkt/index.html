Molen de Ster Wintermarkt

Christmas market · Utrecht, Maarssen · 12–13 December 2026

When12–13 December 2026
WhereMolenpark 3, Maarssen, 3531 Utrecht
TypeChristmas market

Utrecht's coziest winter market in a historic mill featuring over 50 stalls by local artists and craftspeople. Homemade pea soup, hot chocolate with whipped cream, and mill tours.
