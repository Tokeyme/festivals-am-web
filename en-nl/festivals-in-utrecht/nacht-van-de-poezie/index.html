Nacht van de Poëzie

Culture festival · Utrecht · 3 October 2026

When3 October 2026
WhereTivoliVredenburg, Vredenburg 10, 3511 BD Utrecht
TypeCulture festival

The Nacht van de Poëzie at TivoliVredenburg in Utrecht brings together poetry and music over one long evening. Numerous poets recite their work; visitors receive a free volume of poetry, and there is also a book market with signing sessions. The event traditionally marks the close of the ILFU.
