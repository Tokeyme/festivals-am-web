Nacht van de Utrechtse Geschiedenis

Historical festival · Utrecht · 10 October 2026

When10 October 2026
Wherebinnenstad (verschillende historische locaties), 3512 JE Utrecht
TypeHistorical festival

The Nacht van de Utrechtse Geschiedenis is organised by the Oud-Utrecht history society in the centre of the city. At various historic locations, visitors can expect a programme of short talks, guided tours, music, workshops and an escape game. Each edition has its own theme.
