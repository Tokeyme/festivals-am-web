Singelloop Utrecht

Sports event · Utrecht · 4 October 2026

When4 October 2026
WhereWilhelminapark en singels, 3581 Utrecht
TypeSports event

The Singelloop Utrecht is regarded as the oldest city run in the Netherlands. On flat, car-free courses along Utrecht's Singels and through the Wilhelminapark, various distances as well as children's runs are offered. The running event attracts thousands of participants and spectators every year.
