Sinterklaasintocht Utrecht

Event · Utrecht · 15 November 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When15 November 2026
WhereOudegracht / Domplein, 3512 JE Utrecht
TypeEvent

At the Sinterklaasintocht, Sinterklaas and his entourage make their way through the center of Utrecht. From the Ledig Erf, they travel by boat along the Oudegracht, before the procession ends at the Domplein with music and a celebration for children. The event is free to attend.
