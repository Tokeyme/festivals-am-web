Sinterklaasintocht Utrecht

Event · Utrecht · 15 November 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When15 November 2026
WhereOudegracht / Domplein, 3512 JE Utrecht
TypeEvent

At the Sinterklaasintocht, Sinterklaas and his entourage make their way through the centre of Utrecht. From the Ledig Erf, they travel by boat along the Oudegracht, before the procession ends at the Domplein with music and a celebration for children. The event is free to attend.
