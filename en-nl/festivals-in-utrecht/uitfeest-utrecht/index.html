UITfeest Utrecht

Culture festival · Utrecht · 11–12 September 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When11–12 September 2026
Whereverschillende pleinen in de binnenstad, Utrecht
TypeCulture festival

The UITfeest marks the opening of the cultural season in Utrecht. In various squares across the city centre, cultural institutions offer music, dance, theatre and previews of the coming season's programme. The event is free to attend.
