Utrecht Science Week

Event · Utrecht · 25 September – 4 October 2026

When25 September – 4 October 2026
WhereUtrecht Science Park, 3584 CS Utrecht
TypeEvent

The Utrecht Science Week is a public knowledge festival at the Utrecht Science Park. At numerous venues there are talks, interactive workshops, guided tours and interviews all about science. The programme includes the Betweter Festival and the Weekend van de Wetenschap, among others.
