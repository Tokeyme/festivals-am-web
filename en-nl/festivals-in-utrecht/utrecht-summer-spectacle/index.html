Utrecht Summer Spectacle

Funfair · Utrecht, Zuilen · 2 July – 2 August 2026

When2 July – 2 August 2026
WhereJaarbeursplein 6, Zuilen, 3521 Utrecht
TypeFunfair

Holland's largest summer festival with over 25,000 m² of activities, entertainment, and food and beverages. Six themed worlds including an adventure tree, dinosaur area, disco roller skating, and beach bar.
