Christmas Fair (Kasteeltuinen Arcen)

Christmas market · Venlo, Arcen · 25–29 November 2026

When25–29 November 2026
WhereKasteeltuinen Arcen, Lingsforterweg 26, 5944BE Venlo
TypeChristmas market

Christmas fair in the castle gardens of Arcen with Christmas gifts, decorations, fashion, care products, live entertainment, Christmas lights, poffertjes, mulled wine and soup. The castle can be toured in a historic Christmas atmosphere. Open daily from 11 am to 9 pm, on Sundays until 7 pm; dogs are not allowed.
