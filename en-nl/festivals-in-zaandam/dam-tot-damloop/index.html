Dam tot Damloop

Sports event · Zaandam · 19–20 September 2026

When19–20 September 2026
WherePeperstraat / Dam tot Dam Park, Zaandam
TypeSports event

The Dam tot Damloop is a traditional running event over 10 English miles (around 16 kilometres) from Amsterdam to Zaandam. The finish is on the Peperstraat in Zaandam, where a Finish Festival takes place in the Dam tot Dam Park. On the evening before there is the Damloop by Night, along with a Mini Dam tot Damloop for children in Zaandam.
