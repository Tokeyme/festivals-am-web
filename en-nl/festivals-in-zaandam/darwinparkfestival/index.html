Darwinparkfestival

Culture festival · Zaandam · 5 September 2026

When5 September 2026
WhereDarwinpark, De Weer 31, 1504 AH Zaandam
TypeCulture festival

The Darwinparkfestival is a free open-air festival in the Darwinpark aimed at a broad Zaan audience. It offers music, theatre and free creative workshops as well as activities for the whole family. Stalls serve food from various cuisines; the focus is on art, culture and bringing residents together.
