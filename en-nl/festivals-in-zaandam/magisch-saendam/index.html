Magisch Saendam

Event · Zaandam · 7–8 November 2026

When7–8 November 2026
WhereInnenstadt Zaandam (u.a. Cultureel Centrum Bullekerk, Westzijde 75), 1506 ED Zaandam
TypeEvent

Over this weekend, the centre of Zaandam turns into 'Magisch Saendam'. Starting from the railway station, a magical tour leads through the centre, where visitors solve puzzles and collect stamps, with the Bullekerk serving as a school of magic. This imaginatively designed weekend is organised by the Ondernemersvereniging BIZ Stadshart Zaandam (local business association) together with the Cultureel Centrum Bullekerk (Bullekerk cultural centre).
