Sinterklaasintocht Zaandam

Event · Zaandam · 14 November 2026

When14 November 2026
WhereDe Burcht (Burcht 1A), Zaandam
TypeEvent

At the Sinterklaasintocht, Sinterklaas arrives by boat in Zaandam and is welcomed at the Burcht. On a festival square with a marquee there are, among other things, funfair rides and a children's programme. The public celebration begins in the afternoon and draws many families.
