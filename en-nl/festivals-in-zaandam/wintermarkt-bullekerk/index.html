Wintermarkt Bullekerk

Christmas market · Zaandam · 11–13 December 2026

When11–13 December 2026
WhereCultureel Centrum Bullekerk, Westzijde 75, 1506 ED Zaandam
TypeChristmas market

In and around the Bullekerk (Westzijderkerk), decorated for Christmas, a festive winter market takes place. The stalls offer gift items and handicrafts, along with food, drinks and live music. Outside, children can roast marshmallows over a fire.
