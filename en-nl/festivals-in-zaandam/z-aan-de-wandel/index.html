[Z]aan de Wandel

Sports event · Zaandam, Peldersveld · 27 September 2026

When27 September 2026
WhereZwembad De Slag, Koningsgeelstraat 3, Peldersveld, 1503 NA Zaandam
TypeSports event

[Z]aan de Wandel is an annual walking day through Zaanstad and the surrounding area; the start is at the Zwembad De Slag swimming pool in Zaandam. The proceeds go towards the fight against cancer and support research projects of the Antoni van Leeuwenhoek hospital in Amsterdam.
