Nationale Bokbierdag

Funfair · Zutphen, Centrum · 11 October 2026

When11 October 2026
WhereGroenmarkt, Houtmarkt, Zaadmarkt, 's Gravenhof, Basseroord, Centrum, 7201 Zutphen
TypeFunfair

29th Nationale Bokbierdag on Sunday: bock beer markets at five locations in the center of Zutphen (12:00-19:00), live music (13:00-19:00) and a large nostalgic festive parade through the city center (13:00-14:30).
