Into The Summer Festival

Music festival · Zwolle, Herfte · 18 June 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When18 June 2026
WhereWijthmenerplas, strand- en recreatiegebied, Herfte, 8017 Zwolle
TypeMusic festival

Large music festival with almost 50 artists on 4 stages (Mainstage, Hardstyle, Techno, Drum'n Bass) and a silent disco at the Wijthmenerplas lake. Easily reachable by bike with guarded bicycle parking and shuttle buses.
