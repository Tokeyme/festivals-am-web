KETI KOTI Festival Zwolle

Culture festival · Zwolle, Centrum · 1 July 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

When1 July 2026
WhereBurgemeester Drijbersingel 7, Poppodium Hedon, Centrum, 8021DA Zwolle
TypeCulture festival

Festival commemorating the abolition of slavery with a colorful Prodo Waka procession through the city center, followed by concerts, traditional clothing and dishes from Suriname, Java and East Africa. Free admission with registration.
