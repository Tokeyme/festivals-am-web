Champions Day

Sports event · Auckland, Ellerslie · 6 March 2027

When6 March 2027
Where100 Ascot Avenue, Ellerslie, Auckland
TypeSports event

Champions Day is the main event of Auckland's early-March racing carnival at Ellerslie Racecourse. The card combines New Zealand's richest race with the New Zealand Derby and the Auckland Cup. A national racewear fashion final and a free family area with activities for children round out the day, followed by an evening party for adults.
