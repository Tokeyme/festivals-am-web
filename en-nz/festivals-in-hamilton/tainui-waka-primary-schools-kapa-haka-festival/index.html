Tainui Waka Primary Schools Kapa Haka Festival

Culture festival · Hamilton, Claudelands · 1–2 December 2026

When1–2 December 2026
WhereCorner of Brooklyn Road and Heaphy Terrace, Claudelands, 3214 Hamilton
TypeCulture festival

Over two days, primary school kapa haka groups compete at Claudelands in a festival run for the Tainui community. Entry is by koha, and parking is free. The listing calls it an inclusive community event.
