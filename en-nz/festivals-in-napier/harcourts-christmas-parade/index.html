Harcourts Christmas Parade

Street festival · Napier · 6 December 2026

When6 December 2026
WhereClive Square, 4110 Napier
TypeStreet festival

Starting and finishing at Clive Square, this street parade runs through the city centre with over 30 floats and displays, bands, marching troupes, costumed mascots and Santa himself. The celebration carries on afterwards at the Christmas Fiesta in Lower Emerson Street and Clive Square.
