The Extravaganza Fair

Funfair · Napier, Taradale · 26–29 March 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When26–29 March 2027
WhereAnderson Park, Kennedy Road, Taradale, 4112 Napier
TypeFunfair

A travelling family fair with market stalls, handmade crafts, street food, kids' rides and games, and live music. Puppet shows and displays on tiny homes and solar living add to the programme, and kids enter free while adults pay a gold-coin donation.
