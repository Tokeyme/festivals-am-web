Winter Deco Weekend

Historical festival · Napier · 16–18 July 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When16–18 July 2027
WhereVarious venues in the Napier city centre, 4110 Napier
TypeHistorical festival

A winter weekend in central Napier celebrating the city's Art Deco era, run by the Art Deco Trust, with jazz, classic films and vintage car displays. The programme also includes vintage cocktail evenings, fashion and fine dining.
