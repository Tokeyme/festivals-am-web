Founders Annual Book Fair

Market · Nelson · 5–13 June 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When5–13 June 2027
WhereFounders Heritage Park, 7010 Nelson
TypeMarket

A second-hand book sale spread across Founders Heritage Park, covering fiction, cookbooks, history and art, along with DVDs and vinyl. It began in 1988 to keep usable books out of landfill, raises money for the park and relies on hundreds of volunteers.
