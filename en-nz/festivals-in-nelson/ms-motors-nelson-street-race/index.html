MS Motors Nelson Street Race

Sports event · Nelson · 2 January 2027

When2 January 2027
WherePort Nelson, 7010 Nelson
TypeSports event

An annual motorcycle race meeting at Port Nelson, billed as the pinnacle motorsport event for Nelson Tasman, with several race classes and top riders from across New Zealand. Spectators get public access to the pit area to meet riders and see the bikes up close.
