Festival of Cultures

Culture festival · Palmerston North · 27 February 2027

When27 February 2027
WhereTe Marae o Hine – The Square, 4410 Palmerston North
TypeCulture festival

A free community festival in The Square that showcases the food, music and customs of the city's many cultures. Families can sample dishes from around the world and catch live performances. Activities start at 10am.
