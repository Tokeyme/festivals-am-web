The Magic of Christmas Night Market

Christmas market · Palmerston North, Awapuni · 1–2 December 2026

When1–2 December 2026
WhereRacecourse Road (off Maxwells Line), Awapuni, 4412 Palmerston North
TypeChristmas market

A curated boutique market at Awapuni Racecourse with a festive theme, running from 3pm to 10pm over two days. The organiser presents it as a place for gift shopping and for gathering with friends and family.
