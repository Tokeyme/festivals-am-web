Wellington Cup Day

Sports event · Upper Hutt, Trentham · 30 January 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

When30 January 2027
Where10 Racecourse Road, Trentham, 5018 Upper Hutt
TypeSports event

Wellington Cup Day closes the Wellington Cup Carnival, a series of January Saturday meetings at Trentham Racecourse run by the Wellington Racing Club. The Wellington Cup is a long-distance race and the day also features a fashion competition. It is the main summer race day in the Wellington region.
