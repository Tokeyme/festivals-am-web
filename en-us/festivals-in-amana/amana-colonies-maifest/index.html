Amana Colonies Maifest

Funfair · Amana · May 1–2, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 1–2, 2027
WhereHistoric Amana Village, 52203 Amana
TypeFunfair

Maifest is the Amana Colonies' traditional German celebration of spring, held in villages founded by German Radical Pietist immigrants, with a Saturday parade and Maypole dancers performing throughout the weekend. It is consistently held the first full weekend of May: May 4-5 (2024), May 3-4 (2025) and May 2-3 (2026). Following that pattern, the 2027 festival should fall on Saturday-Sunday, May 1-2, though organizers had not yet published the exact 2027 date at the time of research.
