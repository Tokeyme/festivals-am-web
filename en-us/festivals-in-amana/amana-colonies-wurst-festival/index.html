Amana Colonies Wurst Festival

Funfair · Amana · June 18–19, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJune 18–19, 2027
WhereAmana Colonies Market Barn, 707 46th Ave, 52203 Amana
TypeFunfair

Summer celebration of German sausage in the Amana Colonies, held each June at the Market Barn. The program includes sausage vendors, live music, yard games, a dachshund derby, and a parade honoring the villages' heritage.
