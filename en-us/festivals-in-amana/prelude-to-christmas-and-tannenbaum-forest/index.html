Prelude to Christmas & Tannenbaum Forest

Christmas market · Amana, Amana Village · December 4–6, 2026

WhenDecember 4–6, 2026
WhereAmana Colonies, Amana, IA 52203, Amana Village, 52203 Amana
TypeChristmas market

Prelude to Christmas and the Tannenbaum Forest open the holiday season across the seven Amana villages, an Iowa communal settlement founded by German Pietist immigrants in 1855. Weekend events include a lit, walk-through forest of decorated trees, craft vendors, and Old World holiday traditions carried on by the Amana Colonies. The celebration runs on Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays for most of December.
