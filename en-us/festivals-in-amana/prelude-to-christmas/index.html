Prelude to Christmas

Christmas market · Amana · December 4–20, 2026

WhenDecember 4–20, 2026
WhereAmana Colonies (mehrere Standorte in den sieben Dörfern), 52203 Amana
TypeChristmas market

The Amana Colonies, seven villages founded by German communal settlers in Iowa, hold Prelude to Christmas across three weekends. Shops, restaurants and craft studios in all seven villages open for holiday shopping, tastings and seasonal displays.
