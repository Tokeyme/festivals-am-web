German Christmas Market NJ

Christmas market · Augusta (New Jersey) · December 4–6, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenDecember 4–6, 2026
WhereSussex County Fairgrounds, 37 Plains Rd, 07822 Augusta (New Jersey)
TypeChristmas market

This German Christmas market has run for 25 years at the Sussex County Fairgrounds in the small hamlet of Augusta. It offers artisanal crafts, traditional German flavors, and a festive atmosphere over three days.
