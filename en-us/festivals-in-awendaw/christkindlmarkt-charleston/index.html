CHRISTkindlmarkt Charleston

Christmas market · Awendaw · December 4–13, 2026

WhenDecember 4–13, 2026
Where4461 US-17, Awendaw, SC 29429, 29429 Awendaw
TypeChristmas market

CHRISTkindlmarkt Charleston is an outdoor German-style Christmas market held on private grounds in Awendaw, just north of Charleston, across two weekends in December. Vendors sell handcrafted goods, including items imported from Germany, alongside festive food and drink. The market runs family-friendly daytime sessions and separate adults-only evening sessions each day, with parking and shuttle service from the Awendaw site.
