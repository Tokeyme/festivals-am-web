Bandera County Fair

Funfair · Bandera, Mansfield Park · October 8–10, 2026

WhenOctober 8–10, 2026
Where2886 TX-16, Mansfield Park, 78003 Bandera
TypeFunfair

The Bandera County Fair is a three-day county fair in Bandera, self-styled 'Cowboy Capital of the World', with a rodeo, carnival rides, livestock exhibits, a pageant and live music at Mansfield Park. It has no ethnic or German theme and was chosen as a control because Bandera is a comparably small Hill Country town to the German-heritage festival sites in this dataset.
