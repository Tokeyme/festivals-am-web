Family Fall Fest & Craft Brew Garden

Funfair · Bear Mountain · October 10–12, 2026

WhenOctober 10–12, 2026
WhereBear Mountain State Park, Seven Lakes Drive, 10911 Bear Mountain
TypeFunfair

A three-day fall festival at Bear Mountain State Park with live music, local makers, seasonal food and a craft beer garden built around New York breweries, daily from 11 am to 5 pm, rain or shine. It replaces the Bear Mountain Oktoberfest, which the organizers ended after 34 years.
