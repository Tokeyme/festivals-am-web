Bloomsburg Fair

Event · Bloomsburg · September 25 – October 3, 2026

WhenSeptember 25 – October 3, 2026
WhereBloomsburg Fairgrounds, 17815 Bloomsburg
TypeEvent

The Bloomsburg Fair is a large general agricultural county fair held annually in the small town of Bloomsburg, running from late September into early October. It features rides, music, food, motorsports, livestock and agricultural exhibits, and a demolition derby, with no ethnic or German theme.
