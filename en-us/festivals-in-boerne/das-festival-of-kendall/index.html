Das Festival of Kendall

Funfair · Boerne, Main Plaza · June 18–20, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJune 18–20, 2027
WhereMain Plaza, Downtown Boerne, Main Plaza, 78006 Boerne
TypeFunfair

Das Festival of Kendall is a downtown Boerne festival explicitly reviving the German-heritage traditions of the former Berges Fest (1967-2021), with Dachshund races, stein-holding contests, local vendors and live music; proceeds benefit Kendall County non-profits.
