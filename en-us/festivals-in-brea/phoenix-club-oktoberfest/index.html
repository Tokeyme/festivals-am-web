Phoenix Club Oktoberfest

Funfair · Brea, Phoenix Club · October 3–25, 2026

WhenOctober 3–25, 2026
Where375 W. Central Ave., Brea, CA 92821, Phoenix Club, 92821 Brea
TypeFunfair

The Phoenix Club, a German-American cultural club in Brea, California, hosts what it calls Orange County's longest-running and most authentic Oktoberfest. The 2026 celebration spans four consecutive weekends: Saturdays October 3, 10, 17 and 24 (5-10pm, 21+) and Sundays October 4, 11, 18 and 25 (noon-5pm, all ages). Brea is a small Orange County city of about 46,000 residents.
