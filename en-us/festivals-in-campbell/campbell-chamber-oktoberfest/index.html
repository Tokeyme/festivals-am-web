The Campbell Chamber Oktoberfest, organized by the Campbell Chamber of Commerce, is a community fall festival in downtown Campbell, California. It runs Saturday, October 17 (10am-6pm) and Sunday, October 18, 2026 (10am-5pm). Campbell is a small South Bay city with about 43,000 residents.
