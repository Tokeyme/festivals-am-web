St. Louis Day

Church / parish fest · Castroville, Koenig Park · August 21–22, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenAugust 21–22, 2027
Where610 Madrid St., Koenig Park, 78009 Castroville
TypeChurch / parish fest

St. Louis Day is Castroville's annual parish feast-day celebration, promoted locally as the 'granddaddy of church picnics', with a Biergarten, performances by the Liederkranz Choir of San Antonio, live music, food and raffles at Koenig Park. Castroville was founded by Alsatian settlers who kept close cultural and linguistic ties to German-speaking Alsace, and the festival's Biergarten and choir give it an explicit German flavor.
