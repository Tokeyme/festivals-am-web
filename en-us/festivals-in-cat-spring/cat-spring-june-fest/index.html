Cat Spring June Fest

Village & club fest · Cat Spring · June 5, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJune 5, 2027
Where13035 Hall Rd, 78933 Cat Spring
TypeVillage & club fest

The Cat Spring Agricultural Society, the oldest agricultural society in Texas, hosts its annual June Fest at its historic 1902 hall, built for the area's German and Czech farming families. Cat Spring was settled by German immigrants beginning in the 1830s. The society also holds a larger antiques fair each September.
