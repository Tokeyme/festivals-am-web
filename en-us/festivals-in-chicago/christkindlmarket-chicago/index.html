Christkindlmarket Chicago

Christmas market · Chicago, Loop · November 20 – December 24, 2026

WhenNovember 20 – December 24, 2026
WhereDaley Plaza, 50 W Washington St, Loop, 60602 Chicago
TypeChristmas market

Chicago's flagship German-style holiday market and one of the largest in the U.S., modeled on Nuremberg's Christkindlesmarkt with wooden vendor huts, Gluehwein and the traditional collectible boot mug.
