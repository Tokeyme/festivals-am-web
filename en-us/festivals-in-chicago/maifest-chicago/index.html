Maifest Chicago

Funfair · Chicago, Lincoln Square · May 28–30, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 28–30, 2027
WhereLincoln Ave & Western Ave, Lincoln Square, 60625 Chicago
TypeFunfair

Spring street festival organized by Lincoln Square's German-American community, run to benefit local clubs such as the Rheinischer Verein, the American Aid Society and DANK Haus. The multi-day event offers German food, beer, live music and folk dancing in the heart of Chicago's historic German neighborhood. It usually takes place around the last weekend of May, though an exact 2027 date had not yet been published.
