Feast of the Flowering Moon

Culture festival · Chillicothe · May 28–30, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 28–30, 2027
WhereYoctangee Park, 45601 Chillicothe
TypeCulture festival

Downtown Chillicothe's Yoctangee Park hosts this Native American heritage festival every year over the Memorial Day weekend, running since 1984. The free three-day program includes traditional music and dance, a mountain-men encampment with craft demonstrations, and an arts and crafts show with dozens of exhibitors. Organizers also run a stage of continuous entertainment plus food and games for families.
