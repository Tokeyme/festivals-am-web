Cincinnati Christkindlmarkt

Christmas market · Cincinnati, The Banks · November 22 – December 28, 2026

WhenNovember 22 – December 28, 2026
WhereSmale Riverfront Park (Cincinnati Lager House to Carol Ann's Carousel), Mehring Way, The Banks, 45202 Cincinnati
TypeChristmas market

A German-style Christmas market strung along Smale Riverfront Park, with glass chalet vendors, Gluehwein, bratwurst, carolers and live German bands running from the Cincinnati Lager House to Carol Ann's Carousel.
