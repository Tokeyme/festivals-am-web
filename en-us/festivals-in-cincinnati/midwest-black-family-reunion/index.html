Midwest Black Family Reunion

Culture festival · Cincinnati, Downtown · August 13–16, 2026

WhenAugust 13–16, 2026
WhereSawyer Point, 705 E Pete Rose Way, Downtown, 45202 Cincinnati
TypeCulture festival

One of the nation's longest-running Black family reunion celebrations, held at Sawyer Point with a parade, concerts, pavilions and food. The 2026 edition marks its 38th year.
