Oktoberfest Zinzinnati

Funfair · Cincinnati, Downtown · September 17–20, 2026

WhenSeptember 17–20, 2026
WhereSawyer Point & Yeatman's Cove, E Pete Rose Way, Downtown, 45202 Cincinnati
TypeFunfair

Billed as the largest Oktoberfest in the United States, this free four-day festival fills Cincinnati's riverfront at Sawyer Point and Yeatman's Cove with German beer, bratwurst, a 300-foot fest tent and the famous 'Running of the Wieners' dachshund dash.
