NoFoberfest

Funfair · Cleveland, NoFo Brew Co taproom · September 19, 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

WhenSeptember 19, 2026
Where1939 Helen Hwy, Cleveland, GA 30528, NoFo Brew Co taproom, 30528 Cleveland
TypeFunfair

NoFoberfest is an Oktoberfest-themed event held simultaneously at all three NoFo Brew Co taproom locations in Cumming, Gainesville and Cleveland, Georgia on Saturday, September 19, 2026. It features German-style beers, steins, German food specials and games such as stein holding and sausage tossing. The Cleveland taproom sits on Helen Highway in a small White County town of about 3,500 people, close to the Bavarian-themed town of Helen.
