Corn Bible Academy German Feast & Auction

Event · Clinton (Oklahoma), Corn Bible Academy · April 3, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenApril 3, 2027
Where22387 E 1070 Rd, Clinton, OK 73601, Corn Bible Academy, 73601 Clinton (Oklahoma)
TypeEvent

Corn Bible Academy, a Mennonite school founded by German-Russian Mennonite settlers near Corn, Oklahoma, holds an annual German Feast and Auction to fund the school. The event centers on a traditional German-Mennonite noon meal with dishes such as Verenika, German sausage and sauerkraut, followed by a live auction. It is normally held on a Saturday in early April.
