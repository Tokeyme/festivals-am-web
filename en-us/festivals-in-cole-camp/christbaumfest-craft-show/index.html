Christbaumfest Craft Show

Christmas market · Cole Camp · November 21, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenNovember 21, 2026
WhereLutheran School Auditorium and Jaycee House, 65325 Cole Camp
TypeChristmas market

Cole Camp's Christbaumfest is an annual late-autumn German-style Christmas craft show, held for more than twenty years at the town's Lutheran School Auditorium and Jaycee House. It is one of three German-heritage festivals the small town hosts each year, alongside Maifest and Oktoberfest. The event features dozens of holiday vendors for local Christmas shopping.
