Cole Camp holds a small downtown Christkindlmarket in early December, drawing on the town's German heritage identity as the self-styled 'Festival City.' It follows the town's Oktoberfest and Christbaumfest in the annual German-themed events calendar. It is listed as an upcoming event on the town's official community website.
