Christmas in Comfort

Event · Comfort, Historic District · November 27, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenNovember 27, 2027
WhereHistoric District, Comfort, TX 78013, Historic District, 78013 Comfort
TypeEvent

Christmas in Comfort celebrates the holiday season in a Texas Hill Country town founded in 1854 by German Freethinker immigrants, with roughly 125 arts and craft vendors filling the historic district. The day culminates in a nighttime parade of lighted floats through Comfort's well-preserved nineteenth-century downtown. The event is organized by the local chamber of commerce as the town's main annual gathering.
