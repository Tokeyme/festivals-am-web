July 4th Celebration

Street festival · Comfort · July 4, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJuly 4, 2027
WhereComfort Park, 78013 Comfort
TypeStreet festival

Comfort marks Independence Day with a children's bicycle parade followed by a larger procession of antique vehicles, floats, fire engines and motorcycles through the historic downtown. The celebration continues at Comfort Park with barbecue, live music, a pie-eating contest, craft and vendor booths, a silent auction and water-based games for kids.
