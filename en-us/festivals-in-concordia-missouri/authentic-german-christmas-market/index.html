Authentic German Christmas Market

Christmas market · Concordia (Missouri), Downtown · December 11–12, 2026

WhenDecember 11–12, 2026
Where205 S. Main Street, Concordia, MO 64020, Downtown, 64020 Concordia (Missouri)
TypeChristmas market

Concordia's Authentic German Christmas Market brings vendors, food, and craft stalls celebrating the town's German heritage to Main Street for a December weekend. The market is organized by the City of Concordia, whose Central Park district also hosts the town's long-running fall German Heritage Festival. Concordia was settled predominantly by German immigrants in the nineteenth century and still keeps strong German-American traditions.
