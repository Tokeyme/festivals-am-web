Christkindlesmarkt

Christmas market · Concordia (Missouri) · December 11–12, 2026

WhenDecember 11–12, 2026
Where205 S. Main Street, 64020 Concordia (Missouri)
TypeChristmas market

The City of Concordia, a small Missouri town with a Lutheran German heritage, puts on an authentic German Christmas Market downtown. A figure called the Christkind, an angel-like character, greets attendees and gives gifts to children as part of the tradition.
