Concordia Fall Festival

Funfair · Concordia (Missouri) · September 7–11, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenSeptember 7–11, 2027
Where64020 Concordia (Missouri)
TypeFunfair

The Concordia Fall Festival grew out of a public market day established in 1898 and is now a multi-day September celebration held the week after Labor Day. Highlights include a queen contest, three parades, carnival rides, and contests and bake sales for children and adults. The town's German roots show in the outdoor Heidelberg Gardens, where visitors find German-style food, live band dancing, and a country store of handcrafted goods.
