Cottage Grove Oktoberfest

Funfair · Cottage Grove, Downtown / The Armory · September 26, 2026

WhenSeptember 26, 2026
WhereDowntown Cottage Grove (starting at the Armory), Cottage Grove, OR 97424, Downtown / The Armory, 97424 Cottage Grove
TypeFunfair

Cottage Grove Oktoberfest is a community festival held in downtown Cottage Grove, Oregon, starting at the historic Armory. The 2026 edition is the fifth annual and takes place on Saturday, September 26. Cottage Grove is a small Lane County town of about 10,000 residents.
