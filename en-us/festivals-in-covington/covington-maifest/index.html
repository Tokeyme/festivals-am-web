Covington Maifest

Funfair · Covington · May 15–17, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 15–17, 2027
WhereGoebel Park, MainStrasse Village, Covington, 41011 Covington
TypeFunfair

Covington Maifest is a vibrant celebration of German origins held each May at Goebel Park in historic MainStrasse Village. The festival features spring celebration with food, beer, live music, and traditional maypole dancing, celebrating Covington's rich German heritage.
