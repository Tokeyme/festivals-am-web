Covington Oktoberfest

Funfair · Covington · September 11–13, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenSeptember 11–13, 2027
WhereMainStrasse Village, Covington, 41011 Covington
TypeFunfair

Covington's annual Oktoberfest celebration of German heritage, food, and culture features German-inspired food, beer, live music, and festival competitions at MainStrasse Village. The festival includes stein-holding contests and traditional German festivities celebrating Covington's German heritage.
