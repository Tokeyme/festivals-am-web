Cullman Christkindlmarkt

Christmas market · Cullman · November 19 – December 23, 2026

WhenNovember 19 – December 23, 2026
WhereDepot Park, 107 Arnold St NW, 35055 Cullman
TypeChristmas market

Cullman, Alabama was founded by German immigrant John G. Cullmann in 1873, and its Christkindlmarkt draws on that heritage. The open-air market at Depot Park runs for over a month and blends German tradition with Southern charm, with more than 40 vendors, German-style food and drink, and a Christmas pyramid.
