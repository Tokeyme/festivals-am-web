Dayton Germanfest Picnic

Funfair · Dayton · August 14–16, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenAugust 14–16, 2027
WhereSt. Anne's Hill, Dayton, 45402 Dayton
TypeFunfair

The Dayton Liederkranz-Turner hosts an annual Germanfest Picnic at St. Anne's Hill, a celebration of German heritage and culture. The club maintains traditional German singing and social traditions, hosting concerts and special events throughout the year.
