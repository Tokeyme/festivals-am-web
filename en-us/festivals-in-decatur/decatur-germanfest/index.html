Decatur Germanfest

Funfair · Decatur · May 29, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 29, 2027
WhereMadison Street Plaza, Downtown Decatur, 46733 Decatur
TypeFunfair

Decatur Germanfest features traditional German food including bratwurst, sauerkraut, potato salad, and hot Bavarian pretzels, along with games, live music, and German beer. Held annually on Saturday evening from 5-10 PM on Madison Street Plaza in downtown Decatur.
