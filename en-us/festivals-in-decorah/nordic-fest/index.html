Nordic Fest

Culture festival · Decorah, Downtown Decorah · July 22–24, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJuly 22–24, 2027
WhereDowntown Decorah, Downtown Decorah, 52101 Decorah
TypeCulture festival

Nordic Fest is an annual three-day festival in Decorah, Iowa celebrating the town's Norwegian-American heritage, home to the Vesterheim Norwegian-American Museum and Luther College. The event draws thousands of visitors each July and has no German cultural connection, serving here as a size-and-season comparison to German heritage events.
