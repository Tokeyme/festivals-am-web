Oktoberfest at Ruttger's Bay Lake Resort

Event · Deerwood · October 16–18, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenOctober 16–18, 2026
WhereRuttger's Bay Lake Resort, 56444 Deerwood
TypeEvent

Ruttger's Bay Lake Resort in the small Brainerd-area town of Deerwood hosts an annual Oktoberfest weekend with scheduled programming on Friday afternoon and Saturday. The 2026 event runs Friday, October 16 through Sunday, October 18. As a resort-hosted event rather than a townwide civic festival, it fits the German-Bavarian Oktoberfest category but on private resort grounds.
