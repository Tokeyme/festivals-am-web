Delphos Canal Days

Town festival · Delphos · September 17–20, 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

WhenSeptember 17–20, 2026
Where310 N. Main St, 45833 Delphos
TypeTown festival

Delphos Canal Days is a four-day, end-of-summer community festival held each September along the historic Miami-Erie Canal, explicitly described by organizers as a general family festival without any particular ethnic theme. It features carnival rides, live music, food trucks, a 5K run and a parade, and marked its 68th year in 2026.
