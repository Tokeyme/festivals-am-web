Christkindlmarkt at Dill's Tavern

Christmas market · Dillsburg · December 11–12, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenDecember 11–12, 2026
Where227 North Baltimore St., 17019 Dillsburg
TypeChristmas market

The Northern York County Historical and Preservation Society hosts a Christkindlmarkt on the historic grounds of Dill's Tavern. The two-day market features over 100 vendors and artisans, Christmas ambience, shopping, food, children's activities and music. It regularly draws more than 6,000 shoppers to this small Pennsylvania town.
