Elberta German Sausage Festival

Village & club fest · Elberta (Alabama) · March 27, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMarch 27, 2027
WhereElberta Town Park, Main St & State St (Co Rd 83 / US Hwy 98), 36530 Elberta (Alabama)
TypeVillage & club fest

Elberta, a small Baldwin County town founded by German immigrants, hosts the German Sausage Festival at Elberta Town Park. The event features homemade German sausage, sauerkraut, craft vendors and live music, and is run twice a year by the nonprofit Elberta Community Partners. The spring edition traditionally falls on the last Saturday of March.
