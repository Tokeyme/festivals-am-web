Eureka German-Russian Schmeckfest

Funfair · Eureka · September 19–20, 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenSeptember 19–20, 2026
WhereEureka, SD (downtown/community grounds), 57437 Eureka
TypeFunfair

Eureka's German Schmeckfest celebrates the town's German-Russian heritage with pioneer demonstrations, a songfest, and a traditional German meal. Eureka was settled by German-Russian farmers unable to find land further south, and was once known as the world's wheat capital. The date given here (third weekend of September) is the stated recurring pattern; the exact 2026 date was not separately confirmed by the organizer for this year.
