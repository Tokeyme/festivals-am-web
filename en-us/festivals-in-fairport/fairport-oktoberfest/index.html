Fairport Oktoberfest

Funfair · Fairport, Fairport Junction · September 24–26, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenSeptember 24–26, 2026
Where9 Liftbridge Lane East, Fairport, NY 14450, Fairport Junction, 14450 Fairport
TypeFunfair

Fairport Oktoberfest, now in its 17th year, is held at the Fairport Junction festival site along the Erie Canal in the village of Fairport, New York. The 2026 event runs September 24-26 and features German food and beer, polka bands and traditional German dancing. Fairport is a small canal-side village of around 5,400 residents near Rochester.
