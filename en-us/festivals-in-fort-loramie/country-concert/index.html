Country Concert

Music festival · Fort Loramie · July 8–10, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJuly 8–10, 2027
Where7103 State Rte 66, 45845 Fort Loramie
TypeMusic festival

Country Concert is a camping and live-music weekend held at the Hickory Hill Lakes grounds outside the village, running since the early 1980s. National touring country acts perform on an outdoor stage across the three-day event. On-site camping and RV parking let attendees, often numbering in the tens of thousands, stay for the full run.
