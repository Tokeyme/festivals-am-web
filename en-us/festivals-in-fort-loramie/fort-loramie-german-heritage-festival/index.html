Fort Loramie German Heritage Festival

Funfair · Fort Loramie · September 25–26, 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

WhenSeptember 25–26, 2026
WhereDowntown Fort Loramie, 45845 Fort Loramie
TypeFunfair

Fort Loramie's German Heritage Festival is held each fall in a downtown park and is billed as a German celebration with food, music and plenty of beer, served inside a large tent. The menu includes stuffed cabbage, German potato salad, apple dumplings and Reuben bites, alongside a car show. The event ties into the area's history of German immigrants who worked on the Miami-Erie Canal.
