Liberty Days

Funfair · Fort Loramie · July 2–4, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJuly 2–4, 2027
Where45845 Fort Loramie
TypeFunfair

Liberty Days is Fort Loramie's own Independence Day celebration, built around amusement rides, food vendors, and live entertainment at the village park. A fireworks display on the Friday evening is the traditional highlight of the weekend. Organizers frame it as a small-town alternative to larger regional Fourth of July events.
