Summer Music Fest

Music festival · Frankenmuth · August 11–14, 2027

WhenAugust 11–14, 2027
WhereHarvey Kern Pavilion, 601 Weiss St, 48734 Frankenmuth
TypeMusic festival

This event brings traditional polka entertainment to Heritage Park, mixing German and Polish tunes for fans of Eastern European music. Alongside the heritage polka acts, the lineup also includes contemporary performers. Food and drinks are sold on site, and parking on the festival grounds is free.
