Volksläufe - The People's Race

Sports event · Frankenmuth · July 4, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJuly 4, 2027
WhereHarvey Kern Pavilion, 601 Weiss St, 48734 Frankenmuth
TypeSports event

Hosted by the Frankenmuth Jaycees, this road race is usually held around the Fourth of July and offers several distances, from a short fun run up to a half marathon. The course leads runners through the town's Bavarian-style streets, past open countryside, and along the Cass River. Proceeds normally help pay for the local fireworks display.
