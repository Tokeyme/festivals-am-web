Wein & Stein

Event · Frankenmuth · June 16, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJune 16, 2027
Where48734 Frankenmuth
TypeEvent

On this progressive tasting evening, visitors move between participating restaurants and shops around downtown Frankenmuth to sample beer, wine, spirits, and appetizers. It is organized by the local chamber of commerce and usually held on a weekday evening in June. Each stop offers a small pour and a bite for ticket holders.
