World Expo of Beer

Funfair · Frankenmuth · May 15–16, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 15–16, 2027
WhereHarvey Kern Pavilion, 601 Weiss St, 48734 Frankenmuth
TypeFunfair

Billed as Michigan's largest beer sampling event, this tasting festival at Harvey Kern Pavilion pours close to 300 different beers from breweries around the world. It usually runs over a weekend in mid-to-late May and pairs the beer stations with food vendors and live music. It is not a specifically German-themed event, unlike most other Frankenmuth festivals.
