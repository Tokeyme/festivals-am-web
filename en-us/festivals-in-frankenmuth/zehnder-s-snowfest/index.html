Zehnder's Snowfest

Event · Frankenmuth, Downtown · February 3–7, 2027

WhenFebruary 3–7, 2027
Where730 S Main St, Frankenmuth, MI 48734, Downtown, 48734 Frankenmuth
TypeEvent

Zehnder's Snowfest brings professional snow and ice carvers from around the world to Frankenmuth, Michigan's self-styled Bavarian town, for a week of sculpture competitions, fireworks, and family activities. It is hosted by the Zehnder's and Bavarian Inn hospitality businesses that anchor the town's German-themed downtown. The festival has run annually since the 1990s as one of Frankenmuth's signature winter draws alongside its Bavarian Festival and Christmas market.
