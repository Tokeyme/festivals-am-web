Das Bock (New York's Original Lager Festival)

Event · Franklin Square · May 15, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 15, 2027
WherePlattduetsche Park, 1132 Hempstead Turnpike, 11010 Franklin Square
TypeEvent

Das Bock is a lager and bock-beer festival held at Plattduetsche Park, a historic German-American social club in Franklin Square, New York. More than 45 breweries pour German-style lagers and bocks alongside traditional German food, live band music and a stein-holding contest.
