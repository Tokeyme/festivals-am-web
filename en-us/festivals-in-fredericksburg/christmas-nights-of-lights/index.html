Christmas Nights of Lights

Christmas market · Fredericksburg · November 24, 2026 – January 5, 2027

WhenNovember 24, 2026 – January 5, 2027
WhereMarktplatz, 78624 Fredericksburg
TypeChristmas market

Every evening for several weeks, downtown Fredericksburg fills with holiday music starting at five o'clock. A short recorded program shares the story of the town's German settlers before carols continue to play. Each night builds toward a countdown that lights both the tall community Christmas tree and a hand-carved German Christmas pyramid brought over from Germany in 2009.
