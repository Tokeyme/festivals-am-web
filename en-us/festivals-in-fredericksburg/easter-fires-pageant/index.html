Easter Fires Pageant

Historical festival · Fredericksburg, Gillespie County Fairgrounds · March 27, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMarch 27, 2027
Where530 Fair Drive, Fredericksburg, TX 78624, Gillespie County Fairgrounds, 78624 Fredericksburg
TypeHistorical festival

The Easter Fires Pageant reenacts a German-Texan legend that explains bonfires seen on the hills around Fredericksburg on Easter Eve, tying the story to an 1847 peace treaty with the Comanche. Performed after dusk at the Gillespie County Fairgrounds, it combines a costumed retelling of the legend with real hillside bonfires. The pageant has been staged since the 1930s as an expression of the town's German settler heritage.
