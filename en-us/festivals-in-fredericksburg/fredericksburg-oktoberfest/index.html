Fredericksburg Oktoberfest

Funfair · Fredericksburg · October 2–4, 2026

WhenOctober 2–4, 2026
WhereMarktplatz, 126 W Main St, 78624 Fredericksburg
TypeFunfair

Held on the Marktplatz of the German-founded Hill Country town of Fredericksburg, this three-day festival celebrates Texas Gemütlichkeit with polka and oompah bands, German and craft beer, and local Hill Country wine.
