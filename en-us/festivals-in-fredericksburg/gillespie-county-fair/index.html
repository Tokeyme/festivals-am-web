Gillespie County Fair

Funfair · Fredericksburg · August 26–29, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenAugust 26–29, 2027
Where530 Fair Drive, 78624 Fredericksburg
TypeFunfair

Fredericksburg's county fair fills the fairgrounds with livestock judging, agricultural exhibits and carnival rides over four days. Evenings bring live music and dancing, and the closing weekend features pari-mutuel horse racing. A parade moves down Main Street, and visitors can watch a 42 domino tournament, goat roping, a washer pitching tournament and the crowning of the fair queen, plus displays from local FFA and 4-H members. The fair is always held in late August; the exact 2027 dates had not been announced yet.
