Kinderfest

Event · Fredericksburg, Pioneer Museum, Main Street · December 9, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenDecember 9, 2026
Where325 West Main Street, Pioneer Museum, Main Street, 78624 Fredericksburg
TypeEvent

Kinderfest, hosted by the Pioneer Museum in Fredericksburg, reenacts the German tradition of children putting out their shoes on the eve of St. Nicholas Day; well-behaved children find nuts, fruit and candy, naughty ones a switch or coal. This is a separate, smaller event from the town's much larger October Oktoberfest.
