Schmeckfest

Funfair · Freeman · April 9–10, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenApril 9–10, 2027
WhereFreeman Academy, 57029 Freeman
TypeFunfair

Schmeckfest in Freeman is an annual 'festival of tasting' celebrating the heritage of Plautdietsch-speaking Mennonites from the Russian Empire who settled the area from the 1870s. Begun in 1959, it is held every spring at Freeman Academy alongside a musical theater production.
