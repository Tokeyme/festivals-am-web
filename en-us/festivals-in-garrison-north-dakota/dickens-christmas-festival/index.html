Dickens Christmas Festival

Historical festival · Garrison (North Dakota) · November 27 – December 5, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenNovember 27 – December 5, 2026
Where52 N. Main Street, 58540 Garrison (North Dakota)
TypeHistorical festival

Garrison, a small North Dakota town, turns its main street into a Victorian-era village for the Dickens Christmas Festival, themed on the era of Charles Dickens. Starting in 2026 the festival runs over two weekends, Thanksgiving weekend and the following weekend, a change from its previous three-weekend format.
