Germantown Oktoberfest

Funfair · Germantown (Wisconsin) · September 26–27, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenSeptember 26–27, 2026
WhereDheinsville Historic Park, 53022 Germantown (Wisconsin)
TypeFunfair

Germantown was settled in the 1840s chiefly by German immigrants from Bavaria, Hesse, the Rhineland, Pomerania and Prussia, which gave the town its name. Its annual Oktoberfest is held at Dheinsville Historic Park, an 1840s German pioneer settlement, with classic German dishes, live traditional music, a classic car show and the Dachshund Dash. The 2026 festival runs Saturday, September 26 and Sunday, September 27.
