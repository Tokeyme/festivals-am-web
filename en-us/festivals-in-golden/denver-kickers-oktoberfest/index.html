Denver Kickers Oktoberfest

Funfair · Golden, Denver Kickers Sports Club · September 18–19, 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

WhenSeptember 18–19, 2026
Where16776 W. 50th Ave., Golden, CO 80403, Denver Kickers Sports Club, 80403 Golden
TypeFunfair

The Denver Kickers Oktoberfest is a free, family-friendly community festival at the Denver Kickers Sports Club in Golden, Colorado, featuring German food and drinks, live music and performances by the club's own Schuhplattler dance troupe. It runs September 18-19, 2026, Friday 5-9pm and Saturday 1-9pm. Golden is a small city of roughly 20,000 residents west of Denver.
