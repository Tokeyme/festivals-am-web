Lumpenball

Carnival · Grand Island (Nebraska) · February 6, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenFebruary 6, 2027
Where401 W 1st St, 68801 Grand Island (Nebraska)
TypeCarnival

The Grand Island Liederkranz hosts an annual Lumpenball, a German Mardi Gras party marking the final celebration of the Fasching season, at which guests traditionally wear casual or ragged clothing rather than formal attire. The club's hall serves the city's historic German community with live music, dancing, food and drinks. The date shifts each year with the church calendar.
