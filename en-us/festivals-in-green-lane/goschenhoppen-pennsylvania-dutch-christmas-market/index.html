Goschenhoppen Pennsylvania Dutch Christmas Market

Christmas market · Green Lane · December 5–6, 2026

WhenDecember 5–6, 2026
WhereRed Men's Hall, 116 Gravel Pike (Route 29), 18054 Green Lane
TypeChristmas market

The Goschenhoppen Historians, a Pennsylvania German folklife organization, hold their 21st annual Pennsylvania Dutch Christmas Market at the Red Men's Hall in Green Lane each December. The two-day market features handcrafted and vintage items, a bake sale of Pennsylvania Dutch treats, roasted chestnuts, and live music.
