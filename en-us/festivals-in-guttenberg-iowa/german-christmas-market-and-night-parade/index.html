German Christmas Market & Night Parade

Christmas market · Guttenberg (Iowa) · November 28, 2026

Where323 S River Park Drive, 52052 Guttenberg (Iowa)
TypeChristmas market

Guttenberg, a Mississippi River town named after the German printer Gutenberg, holds a German Christmas Market and night parade organized by its Chamber of Commerce. Local vendors sell handmade crafts and treats, and the town serves traditional German holiday cuisine alongside the parade.
