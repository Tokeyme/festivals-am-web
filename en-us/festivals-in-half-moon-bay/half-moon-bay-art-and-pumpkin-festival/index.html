Half Moon Bay Art & Pumpkin Festival

Town festival · Half Moon Bay, Downtown / Main Street · October 17–18, 2026

WhenOctober 17–18, 2026
WhereMain Street, Half Moon Bay, CA 94019, Downtown / Main Street, 94019 Half Moon Bay
TypeTown festival

The 54th annual Half Moon Bay Art & Pumpkin Festival takes place October 17-18, 2026 on Main Street in downtown Half Moon Bay, California. It combines an arts-and-crafts fair with pumpkin-themed activities, following the town's Safeway World Championship Pumpkin Weigh-Off on the preceding Columbus Day. Half Moon Bay is a small coastal town of about 11,800 residents with no German or specific ethnic festival theme, used here as a size-matched, non-German control.
