Harmony German Christmas Market

Christmas market · Harmony (Pennsylvania), Downtown Harmony · November 14–15, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenNovember 14–15, 2026
Where218 Mercer St, Downtown Harmony, 16037 Harmony (Pennsylvania)
TypeChristmas market

The Harmony Museum hosts an annual open-air German Christmas Market benefiting the museum in this small Butler County borough founded by the German Harmony Society. Promotional material uses the founders' own greeting, 'Frohe Weihnachten,' to tie the market to the town's Harmonist heritage. Proceeds support the museum's preservation work.
