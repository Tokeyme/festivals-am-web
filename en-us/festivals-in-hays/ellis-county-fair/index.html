Ellis County Fair

Funfair · Hays · July 13–16, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJuly 13–16, 2027
Where1344 Fairground Rd, 67601 Hays
TypeFunfair

The Ellis County Fair is a multi-day community fair held each July at the county fairgrounds south of Hays. It features 4-H exhibits, livestock shows, and family activities typical of a rural county fair. The week draws residents from across the county for shared agricultural and community life.
