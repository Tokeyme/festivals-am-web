Volga German Society Oktoberfest

Funfair · Hays · October 2–3, 2026

WhenOctober 2–3, 2026
WhereMunicipal Park, 100 Main St, 67601 Hays
TypeFunfair

The Volga German Society hosts an annual two-day Oktoberfest at Municipal Park in Hays, timed to Fort Hays State University's Homecoming weekend. The festival celebrates the heritage of the Volga Germans who settled Ellis County, and is run by the Volga German Centennial Association, founded in 1974. It features traditional German food, beer, crafts, and live music.
