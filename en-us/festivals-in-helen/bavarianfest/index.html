Bavarianfest

Funfair · Helen · May 29, 2027

WhenMay 29, 2027
WhereHelen Festhalle, 1074 Edelweiss St., 30545 Helen
TypeFunfair

Bavarianfest takes place at the Helen Festhalle over Memorial Day weekend. The evening opens with a color guard ceremony honoring military service members, followed by a Bavarian-style celebration for the whole family in Helen's alpine-themed village.
