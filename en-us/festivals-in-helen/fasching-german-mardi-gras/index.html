Fasching (German Mardi Gras)

Carnival · Helen, Festhalle · February 6, 2027

WhenFebruary 6, 2027
Where1074 Edelweiss Strasse, Helen, GA 30545, Festhalle, 30545 Helen
TypeCarnival

Helen's Fasching is a German Mardi Gras evening at the town's Festhalle, with a live band, dancing, and a costume contest. It is organized by the local Faschingsverein and the Helen Chamber of Commerce as part of this Alpine-themed town's year-round German calendar. Food and drink are sold at the venue during the event.
