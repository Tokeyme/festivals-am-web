Fasching Sidewalk Parade

Carnival · Helen · February 6, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenFebruary 6, 2027
WhereHelendorf River Inn, 33 Munich Strasse, Helen, GA 30545, 30545 Helen
TypeCarnival

Helen, a Bavarian-themed town in the north Georgia mountains, runs several weeks of Fasching events each winter organized in part by the Faschingsverein Helen club. Activities move between local businesses such as the Heidelberg restaurant, the Hofbrauhaus and Cowboys and Angels, culminating in the Fasching Sidewalk Parade, which assembles at the Helendorf River Inn before marching down Main Street. The parade follows the pre-Lenten Karneval season observed in southern Germany and Austria.
