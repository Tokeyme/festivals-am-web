Helen Oktoberfest

Funfair · Helen · September 10 – November 1, 2026

WhenSeptember 10 – November 1, 2026
WhereHelen Festhalle, 1074 Edelweiss Strasse, 30545 Helen
TypeFunfair

The longest-running Oktoberfest in the United States, staged for weeks in the Bavarian-style alpine village of Helen and its riverside Festhalle with German bands, dancing, and beer.
