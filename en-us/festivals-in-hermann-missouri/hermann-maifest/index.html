Hermann Maifest

Funfair · Hermann (Missouri) · May 15, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 15, 2027
WhereDowntown Hermann / Volksplatz, Hermann, MO 65041, 65041 Hermann (Missouri)
TypeFunfair

Hermann's Maifest is an old-fashioned celebration of spring and the town's German heritage, with Maypole dancers, a Volksplatz marketplace, beer gardens, German music and food. The festival includes craft demonstrations and children's activities and ends with a parade on the final afternoon. It is one of Missouri's oldest German festivals, having run for over 70 years.
