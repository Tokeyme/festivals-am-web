46th Annual Hermann Wurstfest

Funfair · Hermann · March 19–21, 2027

WhenMarch 19–21, 2027
WhereHermannhof Festhalle, 237 East 1st Street (and multiple downtown venues), 65041 Hermann
TypeFunfair

Hermann's Wurstfest is a citywide March celebration of sausage-making, held at venues across this German-founded Missouri river town, including the Hermannhof Festhalle and Stone Hill Winery. The 2027 event is the 46th annual edition. It features sausage tastings and competitions, a kraut-making class, and the Braunschweiger Ball.
