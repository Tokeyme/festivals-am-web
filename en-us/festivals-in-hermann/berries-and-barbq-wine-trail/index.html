Berries & BarBQ Wine Trail

Wine festival · Hermann · July 24, 2027

WhenJuly 24, 2027
Where150A Market St (Hermann Wine Trail wineries), 65041 Hermann
TypeWine festival

Six wineries on the Hermann Wine Trail open their tasting rooms for a joint summer weekend built around berry dishes and barbecue. Visitors travel between participating cellars in and around Hermann, sampling food and wine pairings at each stop. It is one of several themed weekend events the trail organizes across the year.
