Christkindl Markt at Hermannhof

Christmas market · Hermann · December 12–13, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenDecember 12–13, 2026
WhereHermannhof, 237 East 1st Street, 65041 Hermann
TypeChristmas market

Hermann is a small Missouri River town settled by German immigrants, and its riverfront Festhalle at Hermannhof turns into a European-style Christmas market for one December weekend. Visitors can browse artisan crafts and sample local wines against a backdrop the town describes as deeply rooted in German heritage.
