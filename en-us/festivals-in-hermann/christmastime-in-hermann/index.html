Christmastime in Hermann

Christmas market · Hermann, Downtown · November 28, 2026

WhenNovember 28, 2026
WhereMarket Street, Hermann, MO 65041, Downtown, 65041 Hermann
TypeChristmas market

Christmastime in Hermann opens with a lantern parade through the German-settled Missouri river town just after Thanksgiving and runs on weekends through New Year's Eve. Downtown shops and wineries take part with holiday markets, decorated storefronts, and a bell-ringing ceremony in the Clock Tower on New Year's Eve. The season complements Hermann's other German heritage festivals, Maifest and Oktoberfest, held earlier in the year.
