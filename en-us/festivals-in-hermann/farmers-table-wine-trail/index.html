Farmers' Table Wine Trail

Wine festival · Hermann · June 12, 2027

WhenJune 12, 2027
Where150A Market St (Hermann Wine Trail wineries), 65041 Hermann
TypeWine festival

This Hermann Wine Trail weekend pairs the six member wineries with area farmers, offering food and wine combinations built around regional produce. Guests move from winery to winery sampling dishes made with local ingredients. It forms part of the wine trail's rotating calendar of seasonal tasting weekends.
