Hermann Maifest

Culture festival · Hermann, Downtown Hermann · May 15–16, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 15–16, 2027
WhereHistoric Downtown Hermann, Downtown Hermann, 65041 Hermann
TypeCulture festival

Hermann, Missouri, founded in 1837 by the German Settlement Society of Philadelphia, celebrates its heritage each spring with Maifest, featuring Maypole dancing, German music and a Volksplatz market. The town traditionally holds Maifest on the third weekend of May. Hermann also hosts a separate Wurstfest and a multi-weekend Oktoberfest later in the year.
