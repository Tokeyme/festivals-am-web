Kristkindl Markt at Stone Hill Winery

Christmas market · Hermann · December 5–6, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenDecember 5–6, 2026
WhereStone Hill Winery, 1110 Stone Hill Highway, 65041 Hermann
TypeChristmas market

Stone Hill Winery, in the German-settled town of Hermann, Missouri, hosts its own Kristkindl Market the weekend before Hermann's larger Christkindlmarkt. The craft vendor market features mulled wine, carolers, and a Highland-cow photo opportunity.
