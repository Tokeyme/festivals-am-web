Hickory Oktoberfest

Funfair · Hickory · October 9–11, 2026

WhenOctober 9–11, 2026
WhereUnion Square, Downtown Hickory, 28601 Hickory
TypeFunfair

One of the Southeast's largest street festivals, filling roughly four blocks of Union Square in downtown Hickory with beer tents, German food, craft vendors, and live music.
