pFriemtoberfest

Funfair · Hood River, Waterfront / Portway Ave · September 26, 2026

WhenSeptember 26, 2026
Where650 Portway Ave, Hood River, OR 97031, Waterfront / Portway Ave, 97031 Hood River
TypeFunfair

pFriem Family Brewers hosts its Oktoberfest, in partnership with Grasslands Barbecue, at its brewery on the Columbia River waterfront in Hood River, Oregon, on Saturday, September 26, 2026. The event pairs German-inspired food with the brewery's own beers, traditional music and games. Hood River is a small Columbia River Gorge town of about 8,300 residents.
