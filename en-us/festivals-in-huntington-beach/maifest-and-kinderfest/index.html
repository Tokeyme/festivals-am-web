Maifest and Kinderfest

Funfair · Huntington Beach · May 2, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 2, 2027
Where7561 Center Ave., 92647 Huntington Beach
TypeFunfair

Maifest and Kinderfest is the centerpiece day of Old World Huntington Beach's Spring Fest, a multi-weekend outdoor celebration in the venue's Biergarten. The day combines traditional German May customs with children's activities and family entertainment. It complements the same Bavarian village's larger Oktoberfest held each fall.
