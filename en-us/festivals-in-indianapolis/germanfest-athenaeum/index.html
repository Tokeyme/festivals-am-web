Germanfest (Athenaeum)

Funfair · Indianapolis · October 3, 2026

WhenOctober 3, 2026
Where401 East Michigan Street, 46204 Indianapolis
TypeFunfair

German-American cultural festival centered on the historic Athenaeum building in downtown Indianapolis, distinct from the city's Oktoberfest at German Park. The all-ages event features beer, bratwurst and wiener dogs, dachshund races, a brat-eating contest, children's activities and two stages of live entertainment.
