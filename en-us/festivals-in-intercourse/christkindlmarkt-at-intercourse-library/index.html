Christkindlmarkt at Intercourse Library

Christmas market · Intercourse · December 4–5, 2026

WhenDecember 4–5, 2026
WhereIntercourse Library, 31 Center St, 17534 Intercourse
TypeChristmas market

The Intercourse Merchants Association hosts this Christkindlmarkt at the small local library in the heart of Pennsylvania Dutch country. It offers a traditional European-style Christmas market with artisan shopping, live entertainment and seasonal food.
