Jasper Strassenfest

Street festival · Jasper · August 5–8, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenAugust 5–8, 2027
WhereDowntown Jasper, 47546 Jasper
TypeStreet festival

Strassenfest is organized by the Jasper Deutscher Verein (German Club) to preserve the town's German cultural heritage, including its sister-city partnership with Pfaffenweiler, Germany. It is a multi-day downtown street festival with German food, music and a parade; the 2026 edition was the 48th annual celebration. The official festival site already lists the following year's event.
