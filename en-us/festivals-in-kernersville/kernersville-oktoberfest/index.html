Kernersville Oktoberfest

Funfair · Kernersville, Körner's Folly · October 3, 2026

WhenOctober 3, 2026
Where401 South Main St., Kernersville, NC 27284, Körner's Folly, 27284 Kernersville
TypeFunfair

The 16th Annual Kernersville Oktoberfest is held on the front lawn of the historic Körner's Folly house museum in Kernersville, North Carolina, on Saturday, October 3, 2026, from 1:30 to 6pm. It traditionally showcases regional breweries, vineyards, chefs and restaurants each first Saturday of October. Kernersville is a small town of about 26,000 residents near Winston-Salem.
