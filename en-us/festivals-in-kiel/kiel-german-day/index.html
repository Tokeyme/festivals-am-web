Kiel German Day

Funfair · Kiel · June 11, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJune 11, 2027
WhereKiel City Park, Kiel, WI 53042, 53042 Kiel

German Day in Kiel, Wisconsin is a one-day community festival held at Kiel City Park, running from late morning into the evening. It celebrates the town's German heritage with food, music and family activities organized by local community groups.
