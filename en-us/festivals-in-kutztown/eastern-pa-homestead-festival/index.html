Eastern PA Homestead Festival

Culture festival · Kutztown, Kutztown Fairgrounds · July 2–5, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJuly 2–5, 2027
Where450 Wentz Street, Kutztown, PA 19530, Kutztown Fairgrounds, 19530 Kutztown
TypeCulture festival

The Eastern PA Homestead Festival debuted in 2025 on the Kutztown Fairgrounds as a successor to the discontinued Kutztown Folk Festival, bringing back Pennsylvania Dutch craftsmen, folk music, and traditional food over the July Fourth weekend. It continues the region's decades-old tradition of showcasing Pennsylvania German folklife after America's oldest folklife festival closed in 2024. The event runs for four days at the same fairgrounds that hosted the original festival.
