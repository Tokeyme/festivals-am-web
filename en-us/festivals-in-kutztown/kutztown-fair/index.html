Kutztown Fair

Funfair · Kutztown · August 9–14, 2027

WhenAugust 9–14, 2027
Where450 Wentz Street, 19530 Kutztown
TypeFunfair

The Kutztown Fair is a traditional county-style agricultural fair held each August at the Kutztown fairgrounds, marking well over a century of local tradition. The program combines livestock and produce judging with a midway, grandstand entertainment and demolition derbies. Community groups and local clubs run the food stands throughout the week-long run. It ranks among the longer-running fairs in the region.
