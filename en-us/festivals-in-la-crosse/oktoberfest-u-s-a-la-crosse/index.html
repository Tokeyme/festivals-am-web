Oktoberfest U.S.A. (La Crosse)

Funfair · La Crosse, Southside Fest Grounds · September 24–27, 2026

WhenSeptember 24–27, 2026
WhereSouthside Oktoberfest Grounds (2nd & La Crosse St), Southside Fest Grounds, 54601 La Crosse
TypeFunfair

One of the oldest and largest Oktoberfest celebrations in the United States, held on the Mississippi riverfront with beer gardens, torchlight and Maple Leaf parades, carnival rides, and traditional German music.
