Christkindlmarkt Celebration

Christmas market · Lake Worth (Florida) · December 12–13, 2026

WhenDecember 12–13, 2026
Where5111 Lantana Rd, 33463 Lake Worth (Florida)
TypeChristmas market

The American German Club in Lake Worth hosts this Christkindlmarkt with Gluhwein, German beer and food, and performances by local school choirs and bands. It is a long-running signature event of the club's clubhouse grounds.
