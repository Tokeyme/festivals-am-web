Gaufest

Culture festival · Lancaster (Pennsylvania), Downtown · July 8–11, 2027

WhenJuly 8–11, 2027
WhereLancaster County Convention Center, 25 S Queen St, Downtown, 17603 Lancaster (Pennsylvania)
TypeCulture festival

The 31. Gaufest is a four-day reunion of Schuhplattler and Trachten (folk costume) clubs from across North America, hosted this year by G.T.V. Edelweiss of Reading, Pennsylvania. It brings together dancers, musicians and guest clubs for competitions, folk dance performances and music at the Lancaster County Convention Center. The Gaufest is held every two years and rotates among member clubs of the Gauverband Nordamerika.
