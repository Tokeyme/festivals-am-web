Hands-On History Day & Fall Institute

Historical festival · Lancaster (Pennsylvania), Landis Valley · November 5–8, 2026

WhenNovember 5–8, 2026
Where2451 Kissel Hill Road, Lancaster, PA 17601, Landis Valley, 17601 Lancaster (Pennsylvania)
TypeHistorical festival

Hands-On History Day at Landis Valley Village & Farm Museum lets visitors try Pennsylvania German textile crafts firsthand, running alongside the museum's Fall Institute program on turning flax and fleece into fabric. The museum preserves a nineteenth-century Pennsylvania Dutch farmstead and village north of Lancaster. It is one of dozens of events the museum runs each year to keep Pennsylvania German rural traditions alive.
