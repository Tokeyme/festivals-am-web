Harvest Days at Landis Valley Museum

Culture festival · Lancaster (Pennsylvania) · October 10–11, 2026

WhenOctober 10–11, 2026
Where2451 Kissel Hill Road, 17601 Lancaster (Pennsylvania)
TypeCulture festival

Harvest Days is the signature autumn weekend at Landis Valley Village & Farm Museum, an open-air museum dedicated to rural Pennsylvania German life. Costumed interpreters demonstrate period trades such as blacksmithing, weaving and tin-smithing across the historic farmstead buildings. Visitors can take a wagon ride, taste food made from heirloom recipes and meet the museum's heritage-breed farm animals. Pumpkin painting and other harvest games round out the program for children.
