Lanesboro Rhubarb Festival

Event · Lanesboro · June 5, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJune 5, 2027
WhereSylvan Park, 202 Parkway Ave S, Lanesboro, MN 55949, 55949 Lanesboro
TypeEvent

Lanesboro's Rhubarb Festival celebrates the town's title as the 'Rhubarb Capital of Minnesota,' officially recognized by the state legislature in 2008. Held at Sylvan Park, the one-day event features games, contests, rhubarb tasting, a fashion show and live entertainment. It has no German cultural connection.
