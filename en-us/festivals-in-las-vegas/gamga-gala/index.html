GAMGA Gala

Carnival · Las Vegas · January 22–23, 2027

WhenJanuary 22–23, 2027
WhereTuscany Suites & Casino, 255 E Flamingo Rd, Las Vegas, NV 89109, 89109 Las Vegas
TypeCarnival

The German American Mardi Gras Association (GAMGA) holds its annual Karneval Gala at the Tuscany Suites and Casino in Las Vegas, drawing German-American carnival clubs from across the country for a weekend of Rhineland-style Karneval festivities, costumes and dancing. The 2027 gathering is the association's 41st annual gala. GAMGA's member clubs in other cities hold their own local Karneval balls throughout the same pre-Lenten season.
