Hofbräuhaus Las Vegas Oktoberfest

Funfair · Las Vegas, Paradise · September 11 – October 31, 2026

WhenSeptember 11 – October 31, 2026
WhereHofbräuhaus Las Vegas, 4510 Paradise Rd, 89169 Las Vegas
TypeFunfair

Held at the Las Vegas replica of Munich's legendary Hofbräuhaus, this weeks-long celebration features imported Hofbräu bier, celebrity keg tappings, stein-holding contests, and nightly live entertainment.
