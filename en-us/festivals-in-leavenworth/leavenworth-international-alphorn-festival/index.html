Leavenworth International Alphorn Festival

Music festival · Leavenworth · May 29–31, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 29–31, 2027
Where98826 Leavenworth
TypeMusic festival

The Leavenworth International Alphorn Festival brings together alphorn players from across the country and abroad for concerts and demonstrations in the Bavarian-themed village. The program usually falls on Memorial Day weekend and includes group performances, workshops, and instrument displays for visitors. It celebrates the alpine musical tradition tied to Leavenworth's Bavarian heritage.
