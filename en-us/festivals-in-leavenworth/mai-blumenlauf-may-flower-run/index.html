Mai BlumenLauf (May Flower Run)

Sports event · Leavenworth · May 9, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 9, 2027
Where7335 Icicle Rd, 98826 Leavenworth
TypeSports event

The Mai BlumenLauf, or May Flower Run, sends runners on a 5k, a two-loop 10k, and a four-loop half marathon through downtown Leavenworth and along its riverfront trails. Proceeds benefit the Cascade Medical Foundation. The race takes place each spring during the town's Maifest season and offers a family-friendly 5k option alongside the longer distances.
