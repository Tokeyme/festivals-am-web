Washington State Autumn Leaf Festival

Culture festival · Leavenworth · September 26, 2026

WhenSeptember 26, 2026
WhereFront Street Park and Gazebo, 820 Front Street, 98826 Leavenworth
TypeCulture festival

The Washington State Autumn Leaf Festival celebrates the fall foliage season around Leavenworth with a weekend of community activities centered on Front Street Park. A Grand Parade brings floats, marching bands, and community groups through the Bavarian-style downtown. The event highlights the surrounding mountains as their leaves turn color each autumn.
