Montana Winter Fair

Event · Lewistown (Montana) · January 22–24, 2027

WhenJanuary 22–24, 2027
WhereCentral Montana Fairgrounds, Lewistown, MT 59457, 59457 Lewistown (Montana)
TypeEvent

Lewistown, a small city in central Montana, has hosted the Montana Winter Fair every January since 2003, showcasing the state's agricultural production during the winter lull before calving and lambing season. The fair includes livestock and craft exhibits and community events at the Central Montana Fairgrounds. It is a general agricultural fair with no German heritage theme.
