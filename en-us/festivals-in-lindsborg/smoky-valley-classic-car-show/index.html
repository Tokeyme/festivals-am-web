Smoky Valley Classic Car Show

Sports event · Lindsborg · August 7, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenAugust 7, 2027
WhereSwensson Park, 400 N Main Street, 67456 Lindsborg
TypeSports event

Held each first Saturday of August in Swensson Park, this show gathers more than a hundred classic and vintage automobiles under the park's shade trees. Vendors set up alongside the vehicles for visitors browsing through the morning and early afternoon. It has become a regular fixture on Lindsborg's summer calendar.
