St. Lucia Festival

Culture festival · Lindsborg · December 12, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenDecember 12, 2026
WhereDowntown / Bethany Lutheran Church, 67456 Lindsborg
TypeCulture festival

Lindsborg, known as Little Sweden USA, celebrates its Swedish heritage with the St. Lucia Festival on the second Saturday of December. Swedish folk dancers perform downtown and on the Bethany Lutheran Church lawn, a Lucia figure and Star Boys serve coffee and ginger cookies, and a church service honors Saint Lucia.
