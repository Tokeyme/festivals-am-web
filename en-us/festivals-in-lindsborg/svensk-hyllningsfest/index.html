Svensk Hyllningsfest

Culture festival · Lindsborg · October 15–16, 2027

WhenOctober 15–16, 2027
WhereHeritage Square, Downtown Lindsborg, 67456 Lindsborg
TypeCulture festival

Svensk Hyllningsfest is Lindsborg's biennial tribute to the Swedish pioneers who settled the Smoky Valley in the late 1860s, held in October of odd-numbered years. Downtown streets fill with folk dancing, ethnic food, arts and crafts, and a parade, followed by a smorgasbord and evening entertainment. Local schoolchildren spend months beforehand rehearsing the traditional dances performed during the festival.
