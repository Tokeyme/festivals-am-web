Lititz Craft Beer Fest

Event · Lititz · September 26, 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

WhenSeptember 26, 2026
WhereLititz Springs Park (town center), 17543 Lititz
TypeEvent

The Lititz Craft Beer Fest is a general craft beer festival held annually in the small borough of Lititz, featuring more than 180 brews from 75 craft breweries alongside live music and food trucks. It has no German or ethnic theme, serving as a broad community beer and food event.
