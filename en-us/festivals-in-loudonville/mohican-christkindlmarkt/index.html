Mohican Christkindlmarkt

Christmas market · Loudonville · December 4–12, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenDecember 4–12, 2026
WhereDowntown Loudonville, 44842 Loudonville
TypeChristmas market

The Mohican Christkindlmarkt in Loudonville runs on the first two Fridays and Saturdays of December each year and describes itself as an old-world holiday market in the style of a German Christkindlmarkt.
