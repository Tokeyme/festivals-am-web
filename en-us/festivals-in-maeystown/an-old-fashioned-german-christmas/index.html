An Old Fashioned German Christmas

Christmas market · Maeystown · December 6, 2026

WhenDecember 6, 2026
Where62256 Maeystown
TypeChristmas market

Holiday open house through the historic village of Maeystown, combining a German Christmas carol sing with a small Christkindlmarkt and quilt show. Horse-drawn carriage rides carry visitors between the village's historic stone buildings.
