Apple Butter Making

Event · Maeystown · September 25–26, 2026

WhenSeptember 25–26, 2026
WhereZeitinger's Rock Mill, 62256 Maeystown
TypeEvent

Two-day heritage demonstration at the restored 1859 Zeitinger's Rock Mill in Maeystown. Apples are peeled by hand on the first day, and the butter is stirred over an open kettle fire the next, continuing a tradition of the historic German settlement.
