Fastnacht

Carnival · Maeystown · February 9, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenFebruary 9, 2027
WhereSt. John's Church, 62256 Maeystown
TypeCarnival

German-style Shrove Tuesday supper at St. John's Church hall in Maeystown, always held on the Tuesday before Ash Wednesday. Pancakes and sausage are served, and children take part in the traditional practice of grebbling for doughnuts.
