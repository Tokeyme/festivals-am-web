Maeystown's Springfest, also called Fruehlingsfest, is a one-day spring event held in this small, well-preserved 19th-century German village in southern Illinois. Maeystown is a National Historic District settled by German immigrants and is otherwise best known for its autumn Apple Butter Making Festival.
