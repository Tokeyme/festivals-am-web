Lancaster Liederkranz Christkindlmarkt

Christmas market · Manheim · December 13, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenDecember 13, 2026
Where722 South Chiques Road, 17545 Manheim
TypeChristmas market

The Lancaster Liederkranz, a German cultural club near Manheim, holds a one-day Christkindlmarkt each December at its clubhouse on South Chiques Road. The same venue hosts the club's separately dated annual Oktoberfest each September with the Alpenrose Schuhplattler Dancers.
