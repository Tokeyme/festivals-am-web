Manheim Community Farm Show

Funfair · Manheim · October 5–9, 2026

WhenOctober 5–9, 2026
Where502 Adele Avenue, 17545 Manheim
TypeFunfair

The Manheim Community Farm Show is a five-day agricultural fair held each fall next to Manheim Central High School, continuing a tradition that goes back more than seventy years. Local farm families exhibit livestock, produce and handiwork on the show grounds. Food stands operate nearby along the avenue near the athletic field and the town's Veterans Memorial Park. Admission and parking are free for the whole run.
