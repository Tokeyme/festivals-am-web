Kinderfest

Funfair · Manning (Iowa) · June 19, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJune 19, 2027
WhereMain Street und City Park, Manning, IA 51455, 51455 Manning (Iowa)
TypeFunfair

Kinderfest in Manning, Iowa is billed as the state's oldest annual heritage celebration, held on the Saturday of Father's Day weekend each June. It features a parade down Manning's historic brick Main Street followed by games, inflatables, food concessions, a petting zoo and horse rides in City Park. The name comes from the German word for children's festival, reflecting the town's German-Danish heritage.
