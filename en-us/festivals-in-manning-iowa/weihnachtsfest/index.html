Weihnachtsfest

Christmas market · Manning (Iowa) · November 27, 2026

WhenNovember 27, 2026
WhereMain Street, Manning, IA 51455, 51455 Manning (Iowa)
TypeChristmas market

Weihnachtsfest is Manning's annual German-American Christmas celebration, held on the Friday after Thanksgiving on Main Street. The evening includes a lighted parade, a Christmas tree lighting, Santa's arrival, food and live music. It is organized by the Manning Heritage Foundation to mark the town's German-American culture.
