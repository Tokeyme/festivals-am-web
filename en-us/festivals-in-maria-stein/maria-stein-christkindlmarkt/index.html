Maria Stein Christkindlmarkt

Christmas market · Maria Stein · December 11–12, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenDecember 11–12, 2026
Where2365 St. John's Road, 45860 Maria Stein
TypeChristmas market

The Maria Stein Christkindlmarkt is held at the Maria Stein Retreat Center in this small German-Catholic settled village in western Ohio. More than 30 selected vendors sell from custom-built, German-style Christmas booths, alongside traditional German food and drink, a Santa visit, train rides and a Christmas concert.
