Maria Stein Country Fest

Village & club fest · Maria Stein · June 25–27, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJune 25–27, 2027
WhereMaria Stein Shrine of the Holy Relics, 45860 Maria Stein
TypeVillage & club fest

Maria Stein, an unincorporated Mercer County community at the center of Ohio's 'Land of the Cross-Tipped Churches' built by German Catholic settlers, hosts its annual Country Fest on the grounds of the Shrine of the Holy Relics. The weekend event celebrates the community's rural and religious roots with activities including tractor square dancing and a religious procession.
