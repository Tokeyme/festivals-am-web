Marienville Oktoberfest

Funfair · Marienville · September 25–27, 2026

WhenSeptember 25–27, 2026
WhereDowntown Marienville, 16239 Marienville
TypeFunfair

Marienville, a small village near the Allegheny National Forest, holds a free four-day Oktoberfest each September. The event features craft and food vendors, live music, and fall-themed activities such as pumpkin bowling in the town's downtown.
