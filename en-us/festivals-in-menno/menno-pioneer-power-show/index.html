Menno Pioneer Power Show

Event · Menno · September 26, 2026

WhenSeptember 26, 2026
WherePioneer Acres, 57045 Menno
TypeEvent

The Menno Pioneer Power Show is an agricultural-heritage event at Pioneer Acres near Menno, South Dakota, featuring antique tractors, a historic flour mill, an old schoolhouse, and rural-life demonstrations. Although Menno was itself settled by Germans from Russia, the Power Show is framed purely around farm-equipment heritage with no ethnic or German branding. It is listed as an official upcoming event on the town's community website.
