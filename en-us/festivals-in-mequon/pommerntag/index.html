Pommerntag

Culture festival · Mequon, Freistadt · June 27, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJune 27, 2027
Where12351 N. Granville Rd, Freistadt, 53097 Mequon
TypeCulture festival

Pommerntag is a free annual festival organized by the Pommerscher Verein Freistadt celebrating the Pomeranian German heritage of the historic Freistadt settlement within Mequon. Freistadt was one of the earliest Pomeranian German settlements in Wisconsin, founded in the 1840s. The festival is traditionally held the last Sunday in June.
