Mifflinburg Oktoberfest

Funfair · Mifflinburg · October 2, 2026

WhenOctober 2, 2026
WhereMifflinburg Community Park, 131 N. Fifth Street, 17844 Mifflinburg
TypeFunfair

The Mifflinburg Heritage and Recreation Association runs the borough's 21st annual Oktoberfest at Mifflinburg Community Park, celebrating the town's German heritage. The event features imported German beer and local Rusty Rail Oktoberfest beer alongside oompah-style music and dancing.
