Isartaler Stiftungsfest

Culture festival · Millvale · May 29, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 29, 2027
WhereSchuetzen Park, 80 Schuetzen Park Rd, 15209 Millvale
TypeCulture festival

The G.T.E.V. D'Lustigen Isartaler, a Bavarian Schuhplattler and Tracht club based in the Pittsburgh area, holds its annual Stiftungsfest anniversary celebration at Schuetzen Park in Millvale. The evening combines a German dinner buffet with a dance featuring a live German band and formal folk-dance performances by the club and guest groups. The club is a registered non-profit dedicated to preserving German cultural traditions.
