German Fest Milwaukee

Funfair · Milwaukee · July 30 – August 1, 2027

WhenJuly 30 – August 1, 2027
WhereHenry Maier Festival Park, 200 N Harbor Dr, 53202 Milwaukee
TypeFunfair

Dozens of German-American cultural societies gather on Milwaukee's lakefront for a weekend of polka and folk music, dance performances, and traditional food and beer. Visitors can browse genealogy resources, cultural exhibits, and a children's area across the festival grounds. Local German-American organizations run the event together.
