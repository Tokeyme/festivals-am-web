Minden Mardi Gras Fasching Parade

Carnival · Minden (Louisiana), Downtown / Main Street District · January 30, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJanuary 30, 2027
WhereMain Street Historic District, Minden, LA 71055, Downtown / Main Street District, 71055 Minden (Louisiana)
TypeCarnival

Minden's Fasching Karneval & Parade ties the town to the nearby 19th-century Germantown Colony, a settlement founded by German Utopian immigrants. Organized by Minden Main Street, the event includes a downtown parade and free shuttle service to the Germantown Colony Museum, where volunteers in period German dress display historic artifacts. It is held annually on a Saturday in late January.
