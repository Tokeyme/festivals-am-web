Mount Airy Oktoberfest

Funfair · Mount Airy · October 3, 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

WhenOctober 3, 2026
WhereMain Street (110 S Main St), 21771 Mount Airy
TypeFunfair

The Mount Airy Main Street Association hosts an annual Oktoberfest on Main Street in downtown Mount Airy each October, with road closures for the event. It is a free, family-oriented festival typical of the small Carroll County town's Main Street event series.
