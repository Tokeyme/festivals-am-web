Fastnacht Sale

Carnival · Mount Penn · February 6–7, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenFebruary 6–7, 2027
Where2711 Grant St, Mount Penn, PA 19606, 19606 Mount Penn
TypeCarnival

The Mount Penn Fire Company holds its fastnacht sale on the Saturday and Sunday before Fat Tuesday every year, selling the traditional Pennsylvania Dutch fried potato-dough pastries as a fundraiser. Sales run out of the fire station on Grant Street and from a stand at 23rd Street and Perkiomen Avenue. The sale has run for decades and is one of several fire-company fastnacht fundraisers held across Berks County each pre-Lenten season.
