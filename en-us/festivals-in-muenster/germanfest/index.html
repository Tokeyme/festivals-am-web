Germanfest

Funfair · Muenster, Heritage Park · April 23–25, 2027

WhenApril 23–25, 2027
Where301 North Ash Street, Heritage Park, 76252 Muenster
TypeFunfair

Germanfest is Muenster's flagship three-day celebration of its German-Catholic heritage, with live music, dancing, a beer garden and traditional food such as sausage and apple strudel. It includes contests like tug of war, wiener dog races and stein hoisting plus a 5K fun run. Muenster was founded in 1889 by German immigrants and still calls itself a distinctly German community in North Texas.
