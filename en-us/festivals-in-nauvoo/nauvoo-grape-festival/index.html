Nauvoo Grape Festival

Event · Nauvoo · September 3–5, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenSeptember 3–5, 2027
WhereNauvoo State Park, 62354 Nauvoo
TypeEvent

The Nauvoo Grape Festival celebrates the town's viticulture, planted by French Icarian settlers after 1849 and continued today at Baxter's Vineyards, Illinois' oldest winery; it has no German theme. It is held every year on the Friday, Saturday and Sunday of Labor Day weekend and includes a parade, competitions and the Festival on Wheels car show. Its 88th annual edition places the 2027 festival on September 3-5, though the organizers had not yet published the exact 2027 date at the time of research.
