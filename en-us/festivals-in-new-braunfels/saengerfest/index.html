Saengerfest

Music festival · New Braunfels · May 1, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 1, 2027
Where500 Main Plaza, 78130 New Braunfels
TypeMusic festival

This free, one-day festival brings live music and family activities to downtown New Braunfels. Inspired by the German tradition of a singers' gathering, it pairs local food vendors with wine and craft beer sampling. A second stage hosts acoustic singer-songwriter sets, and local artists give hands-on demonstrations nearby. The event is presented annually on a Saturday in early May, though the exact 2027 date had not been announced yet.
