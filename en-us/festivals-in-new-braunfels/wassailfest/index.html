Wassailfest

Christmas market · New Braunfels · December 3, 2026

WhenDecember 3, 2026
Where500 Main Plaza, 78130 New Braunfels
TypeChristmas market

This downtown gathering opens the holiday season with free tastings of wassail, a warm spiced drink. Local businesses, nonprofits and other community groups each prepare their own recipe to share with visitors. Strings of holiday lights and songs performed by area school choirs fill Main Plaza for the evening.
