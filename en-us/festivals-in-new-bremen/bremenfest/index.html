Bremenfest

Village & club fest · New Bremen, Downtown New Bremen · August 20–22, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenAugust 20–22, 2027
WhereDowntown New Bremen, Downtown New Bremen, 45869 New Bremen
TypeVillage & club fest

New Bremen, Ohio, a village founded by German immigrants and named for the German city of Bremen, has held its community Bremenfest since 1974. The annual festival on the third weekend of August includes food, live music, a 5K run/walk, games, and a parade.
