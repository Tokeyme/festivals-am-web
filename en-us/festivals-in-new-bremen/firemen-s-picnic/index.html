Firemen's Picnic

Funfair · New Bremen · July 24, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJuly 24, 2027
Where45869 New Bremen
TypeFunfair

The New Bremen volunteer fire department has hosted this community picnic since 1921, making it one of the village's oldest recurring gatherings. It traditionally falls on the fourth Saturday of July and offers food and neighborly get-togethers rather than a large commercial midway. It sits alongside Bremenfest and Pumpkinfest as one of New Bremen's three named annual community events.
