Polkafest

Music festival · New Glarus · June 11–12, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJune 11–12, 2027
WhereDowntown New Glarus, 1st St, 53574 New Glarus
TypeMusic festival

This free downtown gathering honors longtime local musician Roger Bright with an evening of polka music and dancing. A festival tent is set up along 1st Street, and local beer is sold on site. It usually runs on a Friday and Saturday evening in June.
