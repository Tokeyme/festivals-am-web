Kunstfest

Culture festival · New Harmony · September 19–20, 2026

WhenSeptember 19–20, 2026
WhereMain Street, 47631 New Harmony
TypeCulture festival

Kunstfest is New Harmony's largest annual event and is described by its organizers as the town's German heritage and arts festival. More than 300 vendors line Main Street with arts, crafts, antiques and historical craft demonstrations. The festival also serves German food alongside other regional fare, plus live entertainment.
