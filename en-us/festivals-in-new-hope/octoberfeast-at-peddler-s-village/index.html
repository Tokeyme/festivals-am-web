OctoberFEAST at Peddler's Village

Funfair · New Hope, Peddler's Village · October 10–11, 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

WhenOctober 10–11, 2026
Where2400 Street Road, Peddler's Village, 18938 New Hope
TypeFunfair

Peddler's Village near New Hope hosts its 10th annual OctoberFEAST each October, featuring Oktoberfest-inspired food, live music, activities, and a beer garden. The event coincides with the village's long-running Scarecrows in the Village display. Admission and parking are free.
