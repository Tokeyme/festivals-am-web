Weihnachts Markt: New Ulm Holiday Market

Christmas market · New Ulm (Minnesota), German Park · November 27, 2026

WhenNovember 27, 2026
WhereGerman Park, 200 N German Street, German Park, 56073 New Ulm (Minnesota)
TypeChristmas market

New Ulm, founded by German immigrants, holds its second annual Weihnachts Markt in German Park on the same day as its Parade of Lights. The market features local vendors, food trucks, live reindeer and the lighting of the town's 40-foot upside-down Christmas tree, a German tradition dating to the Middle Ages.
