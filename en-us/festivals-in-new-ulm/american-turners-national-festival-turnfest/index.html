American Turners National Festival (Turnfest)

Sports event · New Ulm, Downtown · July 27–31, 2027

WhenJuly 27–31, 2027
WhereNew Ulm Turner Hall, 102 S State St, Downtown, 56073 New Ulm
TypeSports event

The New Ulm Turnverein hosts the 57th American Turners National Festival, a multi-day gathering of Turner gymnastics and cultural clubs from across the United States. The festival returns to the same Turner Hall venue that hosted the 56th edition in 2023. New Ulm's Turnverein, founded by German immigrants, has anchored the city's athletic and cultural life since the 19th century.
