Bavarian Blast

Funfair · New Ulm · July 15–18, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJuly 15–18, 2027
WhereBrown County Fairgrounds, 56073 New Ulm
TypeFunfair

New Ulm's largest summer party mixes German tradition with regional music acts, dancing, beer and bratwurst. The multi-day program includes a parade and stein-holding contests, and is always held in mid-July at the fairgrounds.
