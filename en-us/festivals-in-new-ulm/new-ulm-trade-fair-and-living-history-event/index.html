New Ulm Trade Fair and Living History Event

Historical festival · New Ulm, Downtown · March 13–14, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMarch 13–14, 2027
WhereDowntown New Ulm, New Ulm, MN 56073, Downtown, 56073 New Ulm
TypeHistorical festival

The New Ulm Trade Fair and Living History Event brings costumed reenactors, period craft demonstrations, and trade-good vendors to town for a mid-March weekend. It recreates daily commerce and craftwork from New Ulm's nineteenth-century German and German-Bohemian settler era. The event runs alongside the town's other German heritage festivals such as Bavarian Blast and Bock Fest.
