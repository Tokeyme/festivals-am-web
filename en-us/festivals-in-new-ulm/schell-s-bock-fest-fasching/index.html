Schell's Bock Fest (Fasching)

Event · New Ulm · March 6, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMarch 6, 2027
Where1860 Schell Road, 56073 New Ulm
TypeEvent

Bock Fest, a German-style Fasching celebration, has been held on the grounds of the August Schell Brewing Company in New Ulm every March since 1987. New Ulm is a city where a 2002 U.S. Census Bureau report found a higher share of residents with German ancestry, 65.85%, than any other city in the country. The 2027 event is scheduled for Saturday, March 6.
