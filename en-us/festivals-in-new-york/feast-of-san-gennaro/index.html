Feast of San Gennaro

Street festival · New York, Manhattan · September 17–27, 2026

WhenSeptember 17–27, 2026
WhereMulberry Street, between Canal and Houston Street, Manhattan, 10013 New York
TypeStreet festival

An 11-day Italian-American street festival down Mulberry Street in Little Italy, famous for cannoli, sausage-and-peppers, a saint's procession and a cannoli-eating contest; 2026 marks its 100th year.
