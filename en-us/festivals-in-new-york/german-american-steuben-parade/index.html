German-American Steuben Parade

Culture festival · New York, Manhattan · September 19, 2026

WhenSeptember 19, 2026
WhereFifth Avenue, 68th to 86th Street, Manhattan, 10028 New York
TypeCulture festival

The largest German-American celebration in the U.S., an annual parade up Manhattan's Fifth Avenue with marching bands, floats and traditional Dirndl and Lederhosen honoring New York's German heritage.
