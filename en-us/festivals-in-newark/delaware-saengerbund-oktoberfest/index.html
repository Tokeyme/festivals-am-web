Delaware Saengerbund Oktoberfest

Funfair · Newark · September 18–20, 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

WhenSeptember 18–20, 2026
Where49 Salem Church Rd, 19713 Newark
TypeFunfair

The Delaware Saengerbund, a German singing and cultural society, holds its annual Oktoberfest over three days each September at its clubhouse near Newark. The event features a live band from Germany, an opening parade, the Enzian Volkstanzgruppe Bavarian dance group in traditional costume, and German food specialties such as bratwurst, weisswurst, and rollmops.
