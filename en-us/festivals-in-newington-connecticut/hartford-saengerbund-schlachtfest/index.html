Hartford Saengerbund Schlachtfest

Where719 N. Mountain Road, 06111 Newington (Connecticut)

The Hartford Saengerbund, a German singing and social club whose hall is actually located in Newington, holds an annual Schlachtfest, a traditional German feast historically tied to autumn pig butchering. The club also hosts a Christkindlmarkt later the same month at the same hall.
