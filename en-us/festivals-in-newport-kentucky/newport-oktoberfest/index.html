Newport Oktoberfest

Funfair · Newport (Kentucky) · September 11–13, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenSeptember 11–13, 2027
WhereNewport Festival Park, Newport, 41071 Newport (Kentucky)
TypeFunfair

Newport Oktoberfest offers three days of Bavarian-inspired food, live entertainment, drinks, games, and family fun with Cincinnati skyline and Ohio River backdrop. Features traditional Oktoberfest activities including Stein Hoist, Log Sawing, Hammerschlagen, and free admission.
