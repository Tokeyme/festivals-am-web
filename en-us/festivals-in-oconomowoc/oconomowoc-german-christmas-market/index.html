Oconomowoc German Christmas Market

Christmas market · Oconomowoc · November 25–29, 2026

WhenNovember 25–29, 2026
Where104 East Wisconsin Avenue, 53066 Oconomowoc
TypeChristmas market

This German-style Christmas market runs over the Thanksgiving weekend in downtown Oconomowoc, opening with an evening program and running full days through the following weekend. It is listed by Travel Wisconsin as a themed holiday shopping event with German food and crafts.
