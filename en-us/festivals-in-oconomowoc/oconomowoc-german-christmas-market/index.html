Oconomowoc German Christmas Market

Christmas market · Oconomowoc · November 25–29, 2026

WhenNovember 25–29, 2026
Where100 East Wisconsin Avenue, 53066 Oconomowoc
TypeChristmas market

This German-style Christmas market takes place in downtown Oconomowoc. On Wednesday, November 25, only the entertainment tent is open from 5 to 10 pm; the market is closed on Thanksgiving, Thursday, November 26. It reopens on Friday and Saturday from 10 am to 10 pm and on Sunday from 10 am to 4 pm. Travel Wisconsin lists it as a themed holiday shopping event with German food and crafts.
