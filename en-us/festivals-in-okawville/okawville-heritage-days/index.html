Okawville Heritage Days

Historical festival · Okawville · June 12–13, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJune 12–13, 2027
Where114 W. Walnut St., Okawville, IL 62271, 62271 Okawville
TypeHistorical festival

Heritage Days in Okawville, Illinois is held at the Schlosser Museum property and hosted by the Heritage House Museum Association. The two-day event features traditional German foods such as bratwurst, spaetzle and German-style potato salad, alongside hands-on crafts like butter churning and bread kneading. Okawville was renamed by German immigrants who settled the town in the mid-1870s.
