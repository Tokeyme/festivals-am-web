Oldenburg Freudenfest

Funfair · Oldenburg · July 16–17, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJuly 16–17, 2027
WhereTown of Oldenburg, Franklin County, 47036 Oldenburg
TypeFunfair

The Oldenburg Freudenfest celebrates German heritage with traditional music, beer garden, and family-friendly activities. Founded in 1976, the festival preserves Oldenburg's distinctive German Catholic heritage dating back to 1837. Features include sauerkraut balls, dachshund races, and authentic German food.
