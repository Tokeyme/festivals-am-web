Oktoberfest Fall Festival

Funfair · Olmsted Township (Ohio) · October 17, 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

WhenOctober 17, 2026
Where7370 Columbia Rd, 44138 Olmsted Township (Ohio)
TypeFunfair

This single-evening fall festival at the Donauschwaben German-American Cultural Center offers a shorter counterpart to the center's August Sommer Oktoberfest. Visitors can expect the same style of German food, beer, and live music that the center's other Oktoberfest-style events feature. It runs on the club grounds in Olmsted Township alongside other seasonal club programming.
