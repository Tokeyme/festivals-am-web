Sommer Oktoberfest

Funfair · Olmsted Township (Ohio) · August 20–22, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenAugust 20–22, 2027
Where7370 Columbia Rd, 44138 Olmsted Township (Ohio)
TypeFunfair

Lenau Park at the Donauschwaben German-American Cultural Center hosts a summer edition of Oktoberfest, distinct from the center's fall celebration. The multi-day weekend program centers on German food, imported and domestic beer, and live polka and brass-band music. It reflects the ongoing social calendar of the local Danube Swabian community that runs the grounds.
