Putnam County Fair

Funfair · Ottawa · June 21–26, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJune 21–26, 2027
Where1490 E. 2nd St., 45875 Ottawa
TypeFunfair

The Putnam County Fair is the county's traditional agricultural fair, run for about a week each late June at its own fairgrounds. Recent editions carried a yearly theme and included livestock competitions, exhibits, and family entertainment across the grounds. It is organized by the county fair board and draws visitors from around Putnam County.
