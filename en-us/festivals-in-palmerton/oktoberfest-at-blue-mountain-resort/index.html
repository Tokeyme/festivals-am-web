Oktoberfest at Blue Mountain Resort

Funfair · Palmerton · September 26–27, 2026

Blue Mountain Resort in Palmerton holds a free annual Oktoberfest with live German music, traditional dancers, and authentic German cuisine. The event includes a beer garden, hayrides to a pumpkin patch, and competitions such as stein hoisting and keg rolling over a September weekend.
