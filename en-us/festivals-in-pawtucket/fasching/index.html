Fasching

Carnival · Pawtucket · February 20, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenFebruary 20, 2027
Where78 Carter Ave, Pawtucket, RI 02861, 02861 Pawtucket
TypeCarnival

The German American Cultural Society of Rhode Island holds an annual Fasching celebration at its clubhouse on Carter Avenue in Pawtucket, combining German carnival costumes and music with the club's regular social programming. The 2026 celebration was held on a Saturday in late February. The society also hosts an autumn Oktoberfest at the same clubhouse.
