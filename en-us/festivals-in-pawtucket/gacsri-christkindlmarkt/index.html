GACSRI Christkindlmarkt

Christmas market · Pawtucket · November 28, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenNovember 28, 2026
Where78 Carter Ave., 02861 Pawtucket
TypeChristmas market

The German American Cultural Society of Rhode Island hosts this Christkindlmarkt at its clubhouse. The market runs for an afternoon with vendors, food and holiday atmosphere at the same club that also runs the town's autumn Oktoberfest.
