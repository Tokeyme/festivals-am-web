MaiBacchusFest

Funfair · Pawtucket · May 1, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 1, 2027
Where78 Carter Ave, 02861 Pawtucket
TypeFunfair

MaiBacchusFest pairs the German tradition of Maibock beer with the Roman revelry of Bacchus, god of wine and song. The evening in the club's Ratskeller and Biergarten features German and Italian food alongside DJ music for dancing. Guests may wear togas or traditional German attire, and proceeds have supported the club's tree renovation project.
