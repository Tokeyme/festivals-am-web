Sommerfest

Funfair · Pawtucket · June 26, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJune 26, 2027
Where78 Carter Ave, 02861 Pawtucket
TypeFunfair

Sommerfest is the German American Cultural Society's midsummer celebration, spread across its outdoor Biergarten, indoor Ratskeller bar, and upstairs Bierhalle. The gathering typically features live music, German food and beer, and family activities. It ranks among the club's largest events outside of its autumn Oktoberfest.
