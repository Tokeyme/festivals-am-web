Laid Back on I-55 Truck Show

Sports event · Perryville (Missouri) · August 13–14, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenAugust 13–14, 2027
WhereDowntown Perryville, 63775 Perryville (Missouri)
TypeSports event

This two-day gathering in downtown Perryville brings together more than a hundred custom big rigs for a weekend centered on trucking culture. The show pairs the truck displays with live music and an evening truck light show, plus family-oriented activities. It closes with a convoy that passes the town's Veterans Memorial Wall.
