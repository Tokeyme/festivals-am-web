Cannstatter Volksfest

Funfair · Philadelphia, Northeast Philadelphia · September 5–7, 2026

WhenSeptember 5–7, 2026
Where9130 Academy Road, Northeast Philadelphia, 19114 Philadelphia
TypeFunfair

One of America's oldest German-American festivals, hosted since 1873 by the Cannstatter Volksfest-Verein over Labor Day weekend with traditional food, beer and Bavarian entertainment.
