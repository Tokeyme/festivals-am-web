Maifest at Cannstatter Volksfest-Verein

Funfair · Philadelphia · May 15, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 15, 2027
Where9130 Academy Road, 19114 Philadelphia
TypeFunfair

Maifest is the spring gathering of the Cannstatter Volksfest-Verein, one of Philadelphia's oldest German-American clubs, founded in 1873. The one-day event typically falls in mid-May and brings members and visitors together for live music, dancing and traditional German food and beer. It takes place on the same club grounds that later host the Labor Day weekend Volksfest and a seasonal outdoor biergarten.
