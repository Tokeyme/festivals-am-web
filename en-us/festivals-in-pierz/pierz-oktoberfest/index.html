Pierz Oktoberfest

TypeFunfair

Pierz Oktoberfest celebrates the town's German heritage with a parade, polka and waltz performances, dancers in dirndls and lederhosen, and a town-wide medallion hunt. Nearly 80 percent of Pierz residents are of German descent, and the festival is organized by the local Lions Club each late summer. Activities center on Pierz Park, running from a late-morning parade through an afternoon of music and family games.
