Sommerfest at Teutonia Männerchor

Funfair · Pittsburgh, Deutschtown · July 9–18, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJuly 9–18, 2027
Where857 Phineas Street, Deutschtown, 15212 Pittsburgh
TypeFunfair

Sommerfest is the summer open house of the Teutonia Männerchor, a German singing society founded in 1854 in Pittsburgh's Deutschtown neighborhood. For about nine days each July the normally private social club opens its historic 1888 hall to the public. Guests can tour the building, order German food and beer, and hear music performed mostly in German.
