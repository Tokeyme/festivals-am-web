Poteet Strawberry Festival

The Poteet Strawberry Festival celebrates the local strawberry harvest with a parade, carnival rides, food and music in this small South Texas town. It has no German cultural connection; the 2026 edition ran April 10-12 and organizers are already preparing for a milestone 80th annual festival.
