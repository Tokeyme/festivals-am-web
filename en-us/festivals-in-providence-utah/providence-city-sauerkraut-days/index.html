Providence City Sauerkraut Days

Village & club fest · Providence (Utah), Zollinger Park · August 9–14, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenAugust 9–14, 2027
WhereZollinger Park, 300 North & Main Street, Providence, UT 84332, Zollinger Park, 84332 Providence (Utah)
TypeVillage & club fest

Providence, a small Cache Valley city, holds an annual Sauerkraut Days celebration each August that traces back to the town's early-1900s German and Swiss settlers. The multi-day event includes a parade to Zollinger Park, a fun run, a pancake breakfast and a sauerkraut dinner, organized by Providence City. Recent editions ran in the second week of August.
