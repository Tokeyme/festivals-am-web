Fasching

Carnival · Pueblo · February 6, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenFebruary 6, 2027
WhereUnion Depot, 500 B St, Pueblo, CO 81003, 81003 Pueblo
TypeCarnival

The Pueblo German American Club hosts its annual Fasching celebration at the city's historic Union Depot, with doors opening in the evening for dancing to a live band. The event follows the German pre-Lenten Karneval tradition and is one of the club's two main social gatherings of the year, alongside its autumn Oktoberfest. Pueblo's German-American community has organized cultural events through the club since the late 1960s.
