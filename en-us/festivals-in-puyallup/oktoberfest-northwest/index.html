Oktoberfest Northwest

Funfair · Puyallup, Washington State Fair Event Center · October 9–11, 2026

WhenOctober 9–11, 2026
Where110 9th Avenue SW, Puyallup, WA 98371, Washington State Fair Event Center, 98371 Puyallup
TypeFunfair

Oktoberfest Northwest, billed as Washington's largest Munich-style Oktoberfest, is held at the Washington State Fair Event Center in Puyallup on October 9-11, 2026. The three-day event features German food, beer served in traditional steins, oompah music and dance. Puyallup is a mid-sized Washington town of about 42,000 residents south of Seattle.
