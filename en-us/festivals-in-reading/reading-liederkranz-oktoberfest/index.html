Reading Liederkranz Oktoberfest

Funfair · Reading · September 30 – October 4, 2026

WhenSeptember 30 – October 4, 2026
Where143 Spook Lane, 19606 Reading
TypeFunfair

The Reading Liederkranz German singing society hosts one of Pennsylvania's most authentic Oktoberfests, with imported beer, German cuisine, live bands and folk dancing.
