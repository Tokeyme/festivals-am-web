German-American Society Oktoberfest

Funfair · Robbinsville · September 25–27, 2026

WhenSeptember 25–27, 2026
Where215 Uncle Pete's Rd, 08691 Robbinsville
TypeFunfair

The German-American Society, described by itself as the largest German club of central New Jersey, holds a three-day Oktoberfest each September at its grounds on Uncle Pete's Road in Robbinsville Township (mailing address Trenton). It is a long-running annual tradition of the club.
