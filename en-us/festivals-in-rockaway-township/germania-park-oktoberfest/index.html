Germania Park Oktoberfest

Funfair · Rockaway Township, Germania Park · September 19–20, 2026

WhenSeptember 19–20, 2026
Where56 Conger Street, Germania Park, 07801 Rockaway Township
TypeFunfair

Germania Park, a German-American cultural club (Deutscher Schul- und Gesangverein) founded in 1895, hosts a two-day Oktoberfest each September at its grounds in Rockaway Township. The 2026 event features German-American bands including Bavarski and Dave Betz, with a full bar and German food; admission is $10.
