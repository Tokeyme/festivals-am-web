GermanFest at the Michael Braun House

Historical festival · Salisbury, Historic Michael Braun House, Rowan County · September 19, 2026

This event is over – it has already taken place. This page remains available for reference.

WhenSeptember 19, 2026
Where770 Old Stone House Road, Salisbury, NC 28146, Historic Michael Braun House, Rowan County, 28146 Salisbury
TypeHistorical festival

GermanFest is hosted by the Rowan Museum at the historic 1766 Michael Braun House in rural Rowan County, North Carolina, on Saturday, September 19, 2026. The event focuses specifically on the German heritage of the county's original settlers, with hands-on history and culture activities. The site lies outside Salisbury, the county seat, which has a population of roughly 35,000.
