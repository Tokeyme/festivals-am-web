Saratoga Race Course Summer Meet

Sports event · Saratoga Springs · July 15 – September 6, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJuly 15 – September 6, 2027
Where267 Union Ave, 12866 Saratoga Springs
TypeSports event

Saratoga Race Course is one of the oldest sporting venues in the United States and runs a traditional summer meet that draws racegoers from across the Northeast. After three years of a modified calendar, the track returns to its classic 40-day format. The meet is a popular summer outing, with picnic areas, a historic grandstand and the Travers Stakes as its highlight.
