Sheboygan Brat Days

Event · Sheboygan · August 6–7, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenAugust 6–7, 2027
Where53081 Sheboygan
TypeEvent

Sheboygan, nicknamed the 'Bratwurst Capital of the World' and home to a German-American population of roughly 40% of residents, has held Brat Days since 1953 as a Sheboygan Jaycees fundraiser including the Johnsonville World Bratwurst Eating Championship. The 2026 edition ran Friday, July 31 and Saturday, August 1. Organizers had not yet published exact 2027 dates at the time of research; based on the event's usual early-August timing, the first weekend of August 2027 (August 6-7) is the likely date.
