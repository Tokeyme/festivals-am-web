Shinerfest

Music festival · Shiner, K. Spoetzl Brewery · October 24, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenOctober 24, 2026
Where603 E Brewery Street, K. Spoetzl Brewery, 77984 Shiner
TypeMusic festival

Shinerfest is a country-music and craft-beer festival held at the K. Spoetzl Brewery headquarters in the small town of Shiner, running noon to 10pm with rising country artists, food vendors and included beer. The 2026 edition is the fourth annual. It carries no German or ethnic theme and was chosen here as a control against the German-heritage festivals in similarly small towns.
