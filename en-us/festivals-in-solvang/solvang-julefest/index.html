Solvang Julefest

Christmas market · Solvang · November 27, 2026 – January 3, 2027

WhenNovember 27, 2026 – January 3, 2027
WhereSolvang Park, 93463 Solvang
TypeChristmas market

Solvang's Julefest is a Danish-inspired holiday season running from late November through early January. Signature moments include a tree-lighting ceremony in Solvang Park and a parade with several hundred participants down the town's main street. The celebration also includes markets, light displays, and family entertainment drawing on Nordic Christmas traditions.
