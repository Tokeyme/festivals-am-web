Springboro Oktoberfest

Funfair · Springboro · September 11–12, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenSeptember 11–12, 2027
WhereSpringboro United Church of Christ, 5 West Mill Street, 45066 Springboro
TypeFunfair

Springboro Oktoberfest is a two-day celebration of authentic German food, beer, wine, and entertainment for the whole family. Features include bratwurst, schnitzel, wiener dog races, stein holding, singing and dancing, with proceeds going to local and national charities.
