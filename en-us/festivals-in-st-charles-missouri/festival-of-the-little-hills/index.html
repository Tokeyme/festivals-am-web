Festival of the Little Hills

Culture festival · St. Charles (Missouri) · August 20–22, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenAugust 20–22, 2027
WhereFrontier Park and Main Street, 63301 St. Charles (Missouri)
TypeCulture festival

Held annually since 1971 on the third full weekend of August, this arts and crafts show fills Main Street and Frontier Park in historic St. Charles. More than 300 craft vendors set up alongside food booths run by area nonprofit organizations. Live entertainment and a children's area round out the free, three-day event.
