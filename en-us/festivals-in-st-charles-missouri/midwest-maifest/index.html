Midwest Maifest

Funfair · St. Charles (Missouri), New Town · May 8, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMay 8, 2027
WhereNew Town Amphitheater, St. Charles, MO 63301, New Town, 63301 St. Charles (Missouri)
TypeFunfair

Midwest Maifest is a one-day, 21-and-over festival celebrating the arrival of spring and German heritage, held at the New Town Amphitheater in the New Town neighborhood of St. Charles, Missouri. It features samples from local craft breweries, wineries and spirit distilleries alongside live German entertainment.
