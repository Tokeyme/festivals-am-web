Deutsche Tage

Culture festival · St. Paul · June 12–13, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJune 12–13, 2027
Where301 Summit Ave, 55102 St. Paul
TypeCulture festival

One of St. Paul's longest-running ethnic festivals, held on the grounds of the Germanic-American Institute. The weekend features authentic German and European food, Paulaner beer, choral and folk-dance performances, sing-alongs and family activities.
