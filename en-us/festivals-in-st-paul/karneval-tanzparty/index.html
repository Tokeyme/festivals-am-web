Karneval Tanzparty

Carnival · St. Paul · February 6, 2027

WhenFebruary 6, 2027
Where301 Summit Ave, Saint Paul, MN 55102, 55102 St. Paul
TypeCarnival

The Germanic-American Institute in Saint Paul hosts a Karneval Tanzparty, a German carnival dance party with a Europop disco set to German Karneval hits, at its historic building on Summit Avenue. The 2027 party runs from 8 p.m. to 1 a.m. It is one of the Institute's main winter social events, alongside its year-round German-language and cultural programming.
