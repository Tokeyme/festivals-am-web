Sugar Mountain Resort Oktoberfest

Funfair · Sugar Mountain, Sugar Mountain Resort · October 10–11, 2026

WhenOctober 10–11, 2026
WhereSugar Mountain Resort, Sugar Mountain, NC 28604, Sugar Mountain Resort, 28604 Sugar Mountain
TypeFunfair

Sugar Mountain Resort in the North Carolina High Country hosts its 36th annual Oktoberfest on October 10-11, 2026, from 10am to 5pm both days. The mountain-town celebration features Bavarian food, live music and arts-and-crafts vendors. Sugar Mountain is a tiny incorporated village of only a few hundred year-round residents near Banner Elk.
