Syracuse GermanFest

Funfair · Syracuse · July 10, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenJuly 10, 2027
WhereOtoe County Fairgrounds, 68446 Syracuse
TypeFunfair

Syracuse's GermanFest recalls the town's 1870s German immigrant settlement with wiener-dog races, folk dancing, a parade, a market, and a two-block beer garden at the Otoe County Fairgrounds. The event is stated to occur every second Saturday of July and reached its 37th annual edition in a recent year. The exact 2027 date given is computed from this rule and was not separately confirmed for that year.
