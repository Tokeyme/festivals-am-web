Tomball German Heritage Festival (Spring)

Street festival · Tomball, Old Town / Market Street · March 26–28, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenMarch 26–28, 2027
Where201 S. Elm St., Old Town / Market Street, 77375 Tomball
TypeStreet festival

The spring Tomball German Heritage Festival is a street festival in Old Town Tomball with four stages of live music, an open-air market, German food and beer, and antique shops in the surrounding streets. This spring edition is distinct from the town's separate December German Christmas Market.
