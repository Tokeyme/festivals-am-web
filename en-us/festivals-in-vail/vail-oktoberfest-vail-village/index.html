Vail Oktoberfest (Vail Village)

Vail Oktoberfest is a two-weekend Bavarian-style celebration held in Lionshead Village and Vail Village, featuring Paulaner beer, oompah bands and costume contests. The Vail Village weekend runs September 18-20, 2026, Friday-Saturday noon-10pm and Sunday noon-6pm. Vail is a small Colorado ski town with a year-round population of only about 5,500.
