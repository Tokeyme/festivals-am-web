Wurstbraten

Event · Walburg, Zion Lutheran Church · November 1–2, 2026

Note: the 2026 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenNovember 1–2, 2026
Where6001 FM 1105, Zion Lutheran Church, 78626 Walburg
TypeEvent

Wurstbraten is Zion Lutheran Church's annual drive-thru smoked-sausage supper in the small German settlement of Walburg, serving sausage, sweet potatoes, sauerkraut, green beans, bread and pickles; ten percent of proceeds go to other local non-profits.
