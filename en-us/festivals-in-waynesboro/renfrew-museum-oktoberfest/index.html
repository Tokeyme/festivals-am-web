Renfrew Museum Oktoberfest

Funfair · Waynesboro · September 26, 2026

WhenSeptember 26, 2026
Where1010 E. Main Street, 17268 Waynesboro
TypeFunfair

The Renfrew Museum and Park in Waynesboro hosts an annual Oktoberfest honoring the German roots of the Royer family who farmed the land. The 2026 event features Bavarian brass band music from the Dockenspielers, German-themed food, a biergarten, and displays of Pennsylvania German folk artifacts.
