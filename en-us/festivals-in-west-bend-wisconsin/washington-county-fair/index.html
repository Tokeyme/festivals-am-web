Washington County Fair

Funfair · West Bend (Wisconsin) · July 20–26, 2027

WhenJuly 20–26, 2027
Where3000 Hwy PV, 53095 West Bend (Wisconsin)
TypeFunfair

Over six days, the fairgrounds fill with livestock shows, carnival rides, grandstand entertainment, and local food vendors. The Washington County Agricultural & Industrial Society runs the event, which also includes 4-H exhibits and agricultural competitions typical of a Wisconsin county fair. It has no connection to the city's German-heritage festivals.
