West Bend GERMANfest

Funfair · West Bend (Wisconsin), Regner Park · August 26–29, 2027

Note: the 2027 date is not yet officially confirmed – please check with the organiser before visiting.

WhenAugust 26–29, 2027
WhereRegner Park, West Bend, WI 53095, 53095 West Bend (Wisconsin)
TypeFunfair

West Bend GERMANfest fills Regner Park with German food, beer, and live bands over a late-August weekend, one of Wisconsin's long-running German-American community festivals outside Milwaukee. The event has run for around forty years and draws visitors from across the region for polka music and biergarten-style dining. It is organized by a local nonprofit committee dedicated to preserving the area's German cultural heritage.
