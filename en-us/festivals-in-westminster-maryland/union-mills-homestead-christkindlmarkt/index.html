Union Mills Homestead Christkindlmarkt

Christmas market · Westminster (Maryland) · November 21, 2026

WhenNovember 21, 2026
Where3311 Littlestown Pike, 21158 Westminster (Maryland)
TypeChristmas market

Union Mills Homestead, an 1797 mill and residence built by German immigrant brothers Andrew and David Shriver, hosts an annual Christkindlmarkt inside its historic Grist Mill and Tannery buildings. Vendors sell handcrafted and unique gifts alongside food and drinks. The homestead now operates as a museum of rural German-American life in Carroll County.
