Oglebayfest

Funfair · Wheeling, Oglebay Park · October 2–4, 2026

WhenOctober 2–4, 2026
Where465 Lodge Drive, Oglebay Park, 26003 Wheeling
TypeFunfair

Wheeling's Oglebay Park Resort hosts Oglebayfest each October, which organizers call their own unique version of Oktoberfest. It features an artisan market, a German Rathskeller and beer garden with seasonal and domestic brews, live music, a parade and fireworks.
