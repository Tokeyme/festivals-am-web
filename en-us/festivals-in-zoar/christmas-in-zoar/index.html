Christmas in Zoar

Christmas market · Zoar, Historic Zoar Village · December 5, 2026

WhenDecember 5, 2026
Where198 Main St, Historic Zoar Village, 44697 Zoar
TypeChristmas market

Historic Zoar Village hosts its annual Christmas in Zoar celebration, with horse-drawn wagon rides through the village, a church service and tree lighting, and visits from Kristkind, the German Belsnickel figure, and Santa. Zoar was founded in 1817 by German Separatist immigrants who built a communal religious society along the Tuscarawas River. The village operates today as a museum of its German utopian history.
